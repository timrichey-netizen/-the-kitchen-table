SAUCE · BÉCHAMEL DAUGHTER SAUCE

Mustard Sauce

Béchamel sharpened with Dijon mustard for pork, chicken, veal, fish, and vegetables.

15 minMakes about 2 cups

Photo: Chef Cuisto

Ingredients

  • 2 cups prepared Béchamel
  • 2 to 3 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp whole-grain mustard, optional
  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • Salt and black pepper

Preparation

  1. Warm the Béchamel gently over low heat.
  2. Whisk in Dijon and whole-grain mustard if using.
  3. Simmer 1 to 2 minutes without boiling hard.
  4. Remove from heat and whisk in butter and lemon juice.
  5. Taste and adjust mustard, salt, and pepper.
