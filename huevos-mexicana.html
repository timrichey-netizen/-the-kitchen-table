BREAKFAST · MEXICAN

Huevos a la Mexicana

Soft scrambled eggs with tomato, white onion, serrano chile, and cilantro—the colors of the Mexican flag.

20 minServes 4

Photo: Santoku Knives

Ingredients

  • 8 eggs
  • 2 Roma tomatoes, diced
  • 1/2 white onion, diced
  • 1 serrano, minced
  • 1 tbsp butter or oil
  • 1/4 cup cilantro
  • Salt and pepper
  • Warm tortillas

Preparation

  1. Cook onion and serrano in butter or oil until softened.
  2. Add tomato and cook 2 minutes.
  3. Beat eggs with salt and pepper and add to pan.
  4. Scramble gently until softly set.
  5. Finish with cilantro and serve with tortillas.
