Huevos Divorciados

Two fried eggs served side by side—one with salsa roja, one with salsa verde—with refried beans between them.

25 minServes 2

Photo: Tierra Brava

Ingredients

  • 4 corn tortillas
  • 2 eggs
  • 3/4 cup salsa roja
  • 3/4 cup salsa verde
  • 1 cup frijoles refritos
  • 1/2 avocado
  • Queso fresco
  • Oil and salt

Preparation

  1. Warm tortillas, both salsas, and beans.
  2. Fry eggs to preference.
  3. Place two tortillas on each plate and top with eggs.
  4. Spoon salsa roja over one egg and salsa verde over the other.
  5. Place beans between them and garnish with avocado and queso fresco.
