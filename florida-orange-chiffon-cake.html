Florida Orange Chiffon Cake

Light, airy chiffon cake perfumed with fresh Florida orange juice and zest.

American (Florida)Florida, United States2 hr

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cake flour
  • 1 1/2 cups sugar, divided
  • 1 tbsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 7 eggs, separated
  • 3/4 cup fresh orange juice
  • 1 tbsp orange zest
  • 1/2 cup neutral oil
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1/2 tsp cream of tartar

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 325°F. Whisk flour, 1 cup sugar, baking powder, and salt. Add yolks, orange juice, zest, oil, and vanilla; whisk smooth.
  2. Beat whites with cream of tartar to soft peaks; gradually add remaining sugar and beat to glossy firm peaks.
  3. Fold whites gently into batter. Pour into an ungreased tube pan.
  4. Bake 50–60 minutes until cake springs back. Invert pan until completely cool, then release cake.
