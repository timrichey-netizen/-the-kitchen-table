Veal Broth

Delicate gelatin-rich veal broth, especially useful for classical sauces.

Broth & StockMeat

Ingredients

  • 4 lb veal bones
  • 1 onion
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 celery ribs
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste, optional
  • 1 bay leaf
  • Thyme
  • 4 qt water

Preparation

  1. For brown broth, roast bones and vegetables; for white broth, skip roasting.
  2. Cover with cold water and slowly bring to a simmer.
  3. Cook 4–6 hours, skimming frequently.
  4. Strain through a fine sieve and chill.
