Ricotta and Parmesan Gnudi

Tender ricotta dumplings with Parmigiano, spinach, brown butter, and crisp sage.

45 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 15 oz whole-milk ricotta, well drained
  • ¾ cup finely grated Parmigiano-Reggiano
  • 1 egg
  • ½ cup cooked spinach, squeezed dry and chopped
  • ½ cup flour, plus more for shaping
  • Salt and nutmeg
  • 6 tbsp butter
  • 12 sage leaves

Preparation

  1. Mix ricotta, Parmigiano, egg, spinach, flour, salt, and a pinch of nutmeg.
  2. Shape into small oval dumplings and dust lightly with flour.
  3. Poach gently in salted water until they float, then cook 1 minute more.
  4. Brown butter in a skillet, add sage until crisp, and gently toss the gnudi in the butter.
  5. Serve with additional Parmigiano.
