Pescado a la Veracruzana

White fish simmered in a Veracruz-style tomato sauce with olives, capers, garlic, herbs, and chiles.

45 minServes 4

Photo: Mexico in My Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 4 white fish fillets, 6 oz each
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 small onion, sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 4 Roma tomatoes, chopped
  • 1/3 cup green olives, sliced
  • 2 tbsp capers
  • 1 jalapeño or pickled chile, sliced
  • 1 tsp Mexican oregano
  • 1 bay leaf
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Lime wedges

Preparation

  1. Season fish with salt and pepper.
  2. Cook onion in olive oil until soft; add garlic.
  3. Add tomatoes, olives, capers, chile, oregano, and bay leaf and simmer 15 minutes.
  4. Nestle fish into the sauce, cover, and cook gently until just opaque.
  5. Remove bay leaf and adjust seasoning.
  6. Serve with rice and lime.
