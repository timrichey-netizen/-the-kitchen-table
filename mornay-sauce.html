SAUCE · BÉCHAMEL DAUGHTER SAUCE

Mornay

Béchamel enriched with Gruyère and Parmesan for a smooth, savory cheese sauce.

20 minMakes about 2 cups

Photo: Finnish in Ireland

Ingredients

  • 2 cups prepared Béchamel
  • 3/4 cup finely grated Gruyère
  • 1/4 cup finely grated Parmesan
  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter
  • Pinch of nutmeg
  • White pepper and salt, to taste

Preparation

  1. Warm the Béchamel over low heat until steaming but not boiling.
  2. Add Gruyère and Parmesan a handful at a time, whisking until fully melted before adding more.
  3. Remove from heat and whisk in the butter.
  4. Season lightly with nutmeg, white pepper, and salt.
  5. If needed, thin with a splash of warm milk and serve immediately.
