Moules Marinières

The French bistro classic: mussels steamed with shallot, garlic, white wine, butter, and parsley.

25 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 2 lb mussels, scrubbed and debearded
  • 3 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 2 shallots, thinly sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup dry white wine
  • 1 bay leaf
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • 1/4 cup chopped flat-leaf parsley
  • 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice, optional
  • Crusty bread or fries, for serving

Preparation

  1. Discard cracked mussels and any open mussels that do not close when tapped.
  2. Melt 2 tablespoons butter in a large covered pot. Add shallots, garlic, and bay leaf and cook gently until softened without browning.
  3. Add white wine and black pepper and bring to a brisk simmer.
  4. Add mussels, cover, and steam 4 to 6 minutes, shaking the pot occasionally, until the shells open.
  5. Discard unopened mussels. Swirl in the remaining butter, parsley, and lemon juice if using. Serve immediately.

Notes

For moules-frites, serve with Belgian French fries and plenty of broth.
