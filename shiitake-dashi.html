Shiitake Dashi

Vegan Japanese stock built from dried shiitake, optionally combined with kombu.

Broth & StockJapanese

Ingredients

  • 4 cups water
  • 6 dried shiitake mushrooms
  • 1 piece kombu, optional

Preparation

  1. Rinse mushrooms quickly.
  2. Soak mushrooms and optional kombu in cold water 4 hours or overnight.
  3. Remove kombu before boiling; gently warm the mushroom infusion.
  4. Strain; reserve rehydrated mushrooms for another dish.
