Alaska Reindeer Sausage

Smoky Alaska-style reindeer sausage browned and served hot, reflecting a familiar local diner and street-food specialty.

American (Alaska)Alaska, United States25 min

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb prepared Alaska reindeer sausage links
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • 4 sturdy rolls, optional
  • 1/4 cup mustard

Preparation

  1. Heat skillet or grill over medium-high heat.
  2. Brown sausage links on all sides until heated through to the manufacturer's safe serving temperature, about 8–12 minutes.
  3. Meanwhile sauté onion in oil until browned and tender, 8–10 minutes.
  4. Slice sausages or place whole in rolls if using.
  5. Serve hot with onions and mustard.
