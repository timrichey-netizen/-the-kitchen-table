APPETIZER · PERUVIAN

Papas a la Huancaína

Boiled potatoes covered in a creamy ají amarillo and queso fresco sauce, garnished with egg, olives, and lettuce.

35 minServes 6

Photo: 196 Flavors

Ingredients

  • 2 lb Yukon Gold or yellow potatoes
  • 8 oz queso fresco
  • 1/2 cup evaporated milk
  • 3 tbsp ají amarillo paste
  • 4 saltine crackers
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • Salt to taste
  • 4 hard-boiled eggs, halved
  • Black olives
  • Lettuce leaves

Preparation

  1. Boil potatoes in salted water until tender; cool slightly and slice.
  2. Blend queso fresco, evaporated milk, ají amarillo, crackers, oil, and salt until smooth and creamy.
  3. Adjust thickness with a little milk if needed.
  4. Arrange potatoes over lettuce and spoon sauce generously over them.
  5. Garnish with hard-boiled eggs and black olives.
