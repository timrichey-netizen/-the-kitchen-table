Carlota de Limón

No-bake Mexican lime icebox cake layered with cookies and tangy lime cream.

Ingredients

  • 1 can sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 can evaporated milk
  • 3/4 cup fresh lime juice
  • Zest of 2 limes
  • 2 packages María cookies

Method

  1. Blend condensed milk, evaporated milk, lime juice, and zest until thickened.
  2. Layer María cookies and lime cream in a dish.
  3. Repeat, ending with cream.
  4. Chill at least 6 hours before slicing.
