Canadian (New Brunswick)

Fiddleheads

A New Brunswick, Canada side centered on fiddlehead ferns, butter, lemon, prepared in its recognizable Canadian regional style.

Canadian (New Brunswick)New Brunswick, Canada45 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lb fresh fiddleheads, cleaned and trimmed
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • salt and black pepper

Preparation

  1. Rinse fiddleheads thoroughly in several changes of cold water.
  2. Boil in plenty of water for 15 minutes, or steam for at least 10–12 minutes, until fully cooked and tender; drain well.
  3. Toss with butter, lemon juice, salt, and pepper. Serve hot.
