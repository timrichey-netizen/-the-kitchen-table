Salsa Verde

A bright tomatillo salsa with serrano or jalapeño, onion, garlic, cilantro, and lime.

25 minMakes about 2 cups

Photo: Umami Recipes

Ingredients

  • 1 lb tomatillos, husked and rinsed
  • 1 to 2 serrano or jalapeño chiles
  • 1/4 white onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1/2 cup cilantro
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil, optional

Preparation

  1. Roast or boil tomatillos and chiles until softened.
  2. Blend with onion, garlic, cilantro, lime, and salt.
  3. For a cooked salsa, heat oil in a saucepan and simmer the blended salsa 5 minutes.
  4. Taste and adjust acidity, salt, and chile heat.
  5. Serve warm or chilled.
