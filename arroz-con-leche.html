Arroz con Leche

Creamy Mexican rice pudding perfumed with cinnamon and vanilla.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup long-grain rice
  • 2 cups water
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 4 cups whole milk
  • 1 can sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • Ground cinnamon

Method

  1. Simmer rice, water, and cinnamon until most water is absorbed.
  2. Add milk and cook gently, stirring often, until the rice is tender.
  3. Stir in condensed milk and vanilla; simmer until creamy.
  4. Serve warm or chilled with cinnamon.
