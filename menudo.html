BREAKFAST · MEXICAN

Menudo

Traditional Mexican tripe and hominy soup in a red chile broth, served with onion, oregano, lime, and tortillas.

4 hrServes 8

Photo: Maricruz Avalos

Ingredients

  • 3 lb cleaned beef tripe, cut into pieces
  • 2 lb beef feet or marrow bones, optional
  • 2 cans hominy, drained
  • 6 guajillo chiles
  • 2 ancho chiles
  • 5 garlic cloves
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • Salt
  • Lime, onion, cilantro, crushed chile for serving

Preparation

  1. Rinse tripe thoroughly.
  2. Simmer tripe with onion, garlic, and bones if using until tender, about 3 hours.
  3. Toast and soak chiles, then blend with garlic and broth; strain.
  4. Add chile sauce and hominy to the pot and simmer 30 to 45 minutes.
  5. Season and serve with lime, onion, oregano, and chile.
