Salsa Borracha

A roasted chile salsa finished with tequila, beer, or pulque for a deep, smoky, slightly boozy flavor.

30 minMakes about 2 cups

Photo: Gastrolab

Ingredients

  • 4 Roma tomatoes
  • 2 ancho or pasilla chiles
  • 2 chiles de árbol
  • 1/4 white onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1/4 cup tequila, light beer, or pulque
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 2 tbsp chopped cilantro

Preparation

  1. Toast dried chiles briefly, then soak until softened.
  2. Roast tomatoes, onion, and garlic until charred.
  3. Grind or blend with chiles and salt to a rustic texture.
  4. Stir in tequila, beer, or pulque and lime juice.
  5. Finish with cilantro and serve with grilled meats.
