DESSERT · BRITISH

Spotted Dick

Traditional British steamed suet pudding studded with currants and served warm with custard.

1 hr 45 minServes 6

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups self-rising flour
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 cup shredded beef suet or vegetarian suet
  • 1/3 cup granulated sugar
  • 3/4 cup currants or raisins
  • 1 tsp finely grated lemon zest
  • 2/3 cup whole milk, plus more if needed
  • Butter, for the basin
  • Warm vanilla custard, for serving

Preparation

  1. Butter a 1-quart pudding basin.
  2. Mix flour, salt, suet, sugar, currants, and lemon zest. Stir in enough milk to make a soft, thick dough.
  3. Spoon into the basin and cover with a pleated circle of parchment and foil, tying securely with kitchen string.
  4. Set on a trivet in a large pot and pour in boiling water halfway up the basin. Cover and steam gently for about 1 1/2 hours, adding water as needed.
  5. Unmold carefully and serve warm with vanilla custard.
