Nashville Hot Chicken Sandwich

A Nashville-style sandwich of crisp buttermilk-fried chicken brushed with fiery cayenne-spiced hot oil and served on a soft bun with dill pickles.

American (Nashville / Southern)Nashville, Tennessee, United States2 hr 40 min

Ingredients

  • For 4 sandwiches:
  • For the chicken:
  • 4 boneless, skinless chicken thighs, about 5–6 oz (140–170 g) each
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • 2 tbsp hot sauce
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp freshly ground black pepper
  • For the coating:
  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp freshly ground black pepper
  • Neutral frying oil, as needed
  • For the Nashville hot coating:
  • 1/3 cup hot frying oil, carefully reserved after frying
  • 2 tbsp cayenne pepper, or to taste
  • 1 tbsp packed light brown sugar
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp chili powder
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • For assembly:
  • 4 soft sandwich buns, split
  • 12–16 dill pickle chips

Preparation

  1. Place the chicken thighs between sheets of parchment and gently pound the thickest portions so each piece is an even thickness and fits a sandwich bun.
  2. Whisk together the buttermilk, hot sauce, 1 teaspoon salt, and 1/2 teaspoon black pepper. Add the chicken, cover, and refrigerate for at least 2 hours or up to overnight.
  3. In a shallow dish, combine the flour, 1 teaspoon salt, paprika, garlic powder, and 1/2 teaspoon black pepper.
  4. Heat 1 1/2 to 2 inches of neutral oil in a heavy pot or deep skillet to 350°F (175°C).
  5. Remove one chicken thigh from the buttermilk mixture, allowing excess to drip off. Dredge thoroughly in the seasoned flour, pressing the flour onto the chicken to create a craggy coating. Repeat with the remaining chicken.
  6. Fry the chicken in batches without crowding the pan, maintaining the oil near 325–350°F (165–175°C), until deeply golden, crisp, and the thickest part reaches at least 165°F (74°C), about 6–8 minutes depending on thickness. Drain on a wire rack.
  7. Carefully measure 1/3 cup of the hot frying oil into a heatproof bowl. Whisk in the cayenne, brown sugar, paprika, garlic powder, chili powder, and salt. The mixture should form a loose, vivid red chile-oil paste.
  8. While the chicken is still hot, generously brush or spoon the Nashville hot mixture over all sides of each fried thigh.
  9. Place each hot chicken thigh on a soft bun and top with 3–4 dill pickle chips. Close the sandwiches and serve immediately while the coating is crisp and hot.
