Sage & Mushroom Chicken Skillet

Golden chicken breasts with mushrooms, shallots, sage, and a savory pan sauce.

40 minServes 4

MUSHROOM TIP

For better browning: use a wide hot pan, avoid crowding, and let released moisture evaporate before adding sauce ingredients.

See the Mushroom Cooking Guide →

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless chicken breasts
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 12 oz mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 shallots, sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 10 sage leaves
  • ½ cup dry white wine
  • ¾ cup chicken stock

Preparation

  1. Season chicken with salt and pepper and sear in olive oil until golden; remove.
  2. Add butter, mushrooms, and shallots and cook until browned.
  3. Add garlic and sage, then deglaze with wine and reduce by half.
  4. Add stock and return chicken to the skillet.
  5. Simmer until chicken is cooked through and sauce is lightly reduced.
