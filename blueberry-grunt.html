Blueberry Grunt

A Nova Scotia, Canada dessert centered on blueberries, biscuit dumplings, prepared in its recognizable Canadian regional style.

Canadian (Nova Scotia)Nova Scotia, Canada1 hr

Ingredients

  • 5 cups blueberries
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 1/2 cups flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 3/4 cup milk

Preparation

  1. Combine blueberries, sugar, water, and lemon in a wide covered pot; bring to a simmer.
  2. Mix flour, baking powder, and salt; cut in butter and stir in milk to make soft dough.
  3. Drop spoonfuls over simmering berries. Cover tightly and cook on low 15 minutes without lifting lid, until dumplings are cooked through. Serve warm with berry sauce.
