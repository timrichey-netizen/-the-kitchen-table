Bread Pudding

Comforting baked bread pudding with custard, raisins, cinnamon, and a crisp golden top.

1 hr 10 minServes 8

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

Ingredients

  • 10 cups day-old brioche or challah cubes
  • 4 large eggs
  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 3/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp nutmeg
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 cup raisins, optional
  • 2 tbsp melted butter

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 350°F and butter a 9-by-13-inch baking dish.
  2. Spread bread cubes in the dish and scatter raisins over them if using.
  3. Whisk eggs, milk, cream, sugar, vanilla, cinnamon, nutmeg, salt, and melted butter.
  4. Pour custard over bread and press gently so the bread absorbs the liquid. Let stand 20 minutes.
  5. Bake 40 to 50 minutes until puffed, golden, and just set in the center. Serve warm.
