Cod Tongues

A Newfoundland and Labrador, Canada side centered on cod tongues, flour, salt pork or butter, prepared in its recognizable Canadian regional style.

Newfoundland and LabradorNewfoundland and Labrador, Canada45 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lb cleaned cod tongues
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • salt and black pepper
  • 4 oz salt pork, diced, or 3 tbsp butter

Preparation

  1. Pat cod tongues dry and season. Dredge lightly in flour.
  2. Render salt pork in skillet until crisp; remove pieces, leaving fat. If using butter, melt it over medium heat.
  3. Pan-fry cod tongues 2–3 minutes per side until golden and just cooked through.
  4. Serve hot with crisp salt pork pieces if used.
