Italian-American (Sicilian-style)

Sicilian Pizza Dough

A high-hydration, olive-oil-enriched Sicilian-style pan dough that bakes thick, airy, and crisp-bottomed with an open, breadlike crumb.

Italian-American (Sicilian-style)Sicily-inspired / United States4–24 hr

Ingredients

  • For one 13 x 18-inch pan:
  • 500 g bread flour
  • 375 g water (75% hydration)
  • 10 g fine sea salt
  • 5 g instant dry yeast
  • 25 g extra-virgin olive oil, plus 30 g for the pan

Preparation

  1. Combine flour, yeast, and salt. Add water and 25 g olive oil; mix until a sticky, cohesive dough forms.
  2. Rest 15 minutes, then perform several stretch-and-folds in the bowl until the dough gains strength.
  3. Cover and rise 1–2 hours at room temperature, or refrigerate 12–24 hours for more flavor.
  4. Coat a 13 x 18-inch rimmed pan generously with 30 g olive oil. Transfer dough to the pan and turn once to coat.
  5. Gently stretch toward the corners. Rest 15 minutes if it resists, then continue until the pan is filled without deflating the dough.
  6. Cover and proof 1–2 hours until very puffy and visibly aerated.
  7. Add toppings gently. Bake at 475–500°F (245–260°C), preferably on a lower rack or preheated stone, for about 15–20 minutes until deeply golden underneath and crisp at the edges.
