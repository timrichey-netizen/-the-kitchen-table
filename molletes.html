Molletes

Split bolillos spread with refried beans, covered with melted cheese, and topped with pico de gallo.

20 minServes 4

Photo: Recetas Mexas

Ingredients

  • 4 bolillos, split
  • 2 cups frijoles refritos
  • 8 oz Oaxaca or Monterey Jack cheese
  • 1 1/2 cups pico de gallo
  • Butter, optional

Preparation

  1. Toast bolillo halves lightly.
  2. Spread generously with refried beans.
  3. Top with cheese.
  4. Broil until bubbling and lightly browned.
  5. Top with pico de gallo and serve immediately.
