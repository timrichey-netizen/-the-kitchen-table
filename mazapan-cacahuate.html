Mazapán de Cacahuate

Crumbly Mexican peanut candy made with roasted peanuts and confectioners' sugar.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups roasted unsalted peanuts
  • 1 1/2 cups confectioners' sugar
  • Pinch salt

Method

  1. Process peanuts until very finely ground but not yet peanut butter.
  2. Pulse in sugar and salt until the mixture holds when pressed.
  3. Pack firmly into small molds.
  4. Unmold carefully and store airtight.
