Artichokes with Aioli

Whole California artichokes steamed until tender and served with a garlicky lemon aioli for dipping.

American (California)California, United States50 min

Ingredients

  • 4 large globe artichokes
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 large egg yolk
  • 1 garlic clove, finely grated
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 3/4 cup neutral oil or mild olive oil
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice

Preparation

  1. Trim artichoke stems and top inch; snip sharp leaf tips and rub cut surfaces with lemon.
  2. Steam artichokes stem-side up over simmering salted water until a leaf pulls free easily and the heart is tender, 30–40 minutes.
  3. For aioli, whisk egg yolk, garlic, mustard, and lemon juice. Slowly whisk in oil drop by drop, then in a thin stream, until thick. Season with salt.
  4. Serve warm or room-temperature artichokes with aioli.
