American

Stovetop Creamy Mac and Cheese

A United States pasta built around macaroni, sharp cheddar, prepared in its recognizable regional style.

AmericanUnited States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 1 lb elbow macaroni
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 1/4 cup flour
  • 3 cups whole milk
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 4 cups sharp cheddar
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • salt and black pepper
  • 1 cup panko and 2 tbsp melted butter for baked versions

Preparation

  1. Cook macaroni in salted water until just tender; drain.
  2. Melt butter, whisk in flour 1 minute, then gradually whisk in milk and cream. Simmer until thickened.
  3. Remove from heat and melt in cheese and Dijon.
  4. Fold in macaroni, season, and serve immediately.
