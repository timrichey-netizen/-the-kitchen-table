Yuca con Chile y Limón

Crisp yuca fries tossed with chile, lime, salt, and herbs.

40 minServes 4

Photo: Traveler

Ingredients

  • 2 lb yuca, peeled and cut into fries
  • Neutral oil
  • 1 tsp chile powder or Tajín
  • Juice of 2 limes
  • Kosher salt
  • Cilantro, optional

Preparation

  1. Boil yuca until just tender; drain and cool.
  2. Fry at 350°F until crisp and golden.
  3. Drain and toss with chile, lime, salt, and cilantro.
