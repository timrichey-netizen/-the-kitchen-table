Tacos de Ostiones

Quick-seared oysters with garlic, chile, lime, cabbage, and avocado in warm tortillas.

25 minMakes 8 tacos

Photo: Serious Eats

Ingredients

  • 24 shucked oysters
  • Butter
  • Garlic
  • Serrano
  • Lime
  • 8 tortillas
  • Cabbage
  • Avocado
  • Cilantro
  • Salt

Preparation

  1. Pat oysters dry.
  2. Sauté garlic and serrano in butter.
  3. Add oysters and sear briefly until edges curl.
  4. Finish with lime and salt.
  5. Serve with cabbage, avocado, and cilantro.
