Polvorones Mexicanos

Tender, crumbly Mexican shortbread cookies with vanilla and a snowy sugar finish.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1/2 cup confectioners' sugar
  • 1 cup butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • Pinch salt
  • Extra confectioners' sugar

Method

  1. Cream butter, sugar, vanilla, and salt.
  2. Mix in flour just until a soft dough forms.
  3. Shape into small rounds and bake at 325°F until set but pale.
  4. Cool slightly and coat generously with confectioners' sugar.
