Tacos de Camarón a la Plancha

Shrimp seared quickly on a hot plancha with lime and chile, served with avocado and salsa verde.

20 minMakes 10 tacos

Photo: Muy Delish

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb shrimp
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1/2 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • 10 tortillas
  • Avocado
  • Cilantro
  • Salsa verde

Preparation

  1. Pat shrimp dry and toss with oil, chili powder, cumin, and salt.
  2. Heat a plancha or cast-iron skillet until very hot.
  3. Sear shrimp in a single layer until browned and just cooked.
  4. Finish with lime.
  5. Serve in tortillas with avocado, cilantro, and salsa verde.
