Scrapple with Eggs

A Pennsylvania main preparation centered on scrapple, eggs, butter, presented in its recognizable regional style.

American (Pennsylvania)Pennsylvania, United States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 1 lb scrapple, sliced 1/4-inch thick
  • 1 tbsp flour, optional
  • 1 tbsp butter or oil
  • 8 eggs
  • salt and pepper

Preparation

  1. Chill scrapple well and slice. Dust lightly with flour if desired.
  2. Fry in butter or oil over medium heat without moving until crisp, 4–5 minutes; turn and crisp second side.
  3. Fry or scramble eggs to preference.
  4. Serve hot eggs alongside crisp scrapple.
