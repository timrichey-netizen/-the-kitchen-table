CHICKEN · MEXICAN

Enchiladas Verdes

Corn tortillas filled with chicken and covered in roasted tomatillo-serrano salsa with crema, cheese, onion, and cilantro.

45 minServes 4

Photo: Directo al Paladar México

Ingredients

  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 3 cups cooked shredded chicken
  • 1 lb tomatillos
  • 2 serrano chiles
  • 1/4 white onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1/2 cup cilantro
  • 1 cup chicken stock
  • Neutral oil
  • Mexican crema
  • Crumbled queso fresco
  • Sliced onion

Preparation

  1. Roast or boil tomatillos and serranos until soft.
  2. Blend with onion, garlic, cilantro, stock, and salt. Simmer salsa 8 to 10 minutes.
  3. Warm tortillas briefly in a little oil until pliable.
  4. Fill with chicken and roll.
  5. Cover generously with salsa verde and finish with crema, queso fresco, and onion.
