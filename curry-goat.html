Caribbean (Jamaica)

Curry Goat

A Jamaica main built around goat, jamaican curry, scotch bonnet, thyme, potato, prepared in its recognizable regional style.

Caribbean (Jamaica)Jamaica1 hr

Ingredients

  • 3 lb goat meat
  • 3 tbsp Jamaican curry powder
  • 1 onion
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 scallions
  • 1 Scotch bonnet
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 1 tsp allspice
  • 4 cups water or stock
  • 2 potatoes
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • salt

Preparation

  1. Season goat with curry, aromatics, thyme, allspice, and salt; marinate 4 hours.
  2. Brown in oil. Add water and Scotch bonnet, cover, and simmer 1 1/2–2 hours until nearly tender.
  3. Add potatoes and cook 25–30 minutes until goat is tender and gravy thick.
