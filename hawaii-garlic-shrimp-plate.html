Hawaiʻi Garlic Shrimp Plate

North Shore-style garlic shrimp sautéed shell-on in abundant garlic butter and served as a Hawaiʻi plate with rice.

Hawaiʻi Local (North Shore)Oʻahu, Hawaiʻi, United States35 min

Ingredients

  • 2 lb large shell-on shrimp, deveined
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 10 garlic cloves, minced
  • 6 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 cups cooked white rice
  • lemon wedges

Preparation

  1. Pat shrimp dry. Toss flour with paprika, salt, and pepper and lightly coat shrimp.
  2. Melt butter with olive oil over medium heat; add garlic and cook gently until fragrant and pale golden, 1–2 minutes. Remove some garlic butter to prevent burning.
  3. Increase heat and sauté shrimp in batches until pink, lightly crisp, and cooked through, 2–3 minutes per side.
  4. Return reserved garlic butter and toss.
  5. Serve immediately with white rice and lemon wedges.
