Tacos de Atún con Aguacate

Seared or raw-style tuna with avocado, lime, cilantro, and chile in warm tortillas.

20 minMakes 8 tacos

Photo: Feasting at Home

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb sushi-grade tuna
  • 2 avocados
  • Juice of 2 limes
  • 1 serrano
  • Cilantro
  • Olive oil
  • 8 tortillas
  • Salt
  • Pico de gallo

Preparation

  1. Sear tuna briefly or use raw only if appropriate for raw consumption.
  2. Dice or slice tuna.
  3. Mix avocado with lime, serrano, cilantro, oil, and salt.
  4. Warm tortillas.
  5. Fill with tuna and avocado; add pico de gallo if desired.
