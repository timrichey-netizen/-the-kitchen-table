Canadian (British Columbia)

Date Squares

A British Columbia, Canada dessert centered on dates, oats, brown sugar, prepared in its recognizable Canadian regional style.

Canadian (British Columbia)British Columbia, Canada1 hr

Ingredients

  • Filling: 2 cups chopped dates
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice. Crumble: 1 1/2 cups rolled oats
  • 1 1/4 cups flour
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 3/4 cup melted butter

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 350°F. Simmer dates, water, brown sugar, and lemon until thick and jammy, 8–10 minutes; cool slightly.
  2. Mix oats, flour, brown sugar, baking soda, salt, and melted butter until crumbly.
  3. Press half into 8-inch pan, spread date filling evenly, and scatter remaining crumble over top.
  4. Bake 30–35 minutes until golden. Cool completely before cutting.
