Tiramisu

Classic tiramisu with espresso-soaked ladyfingers, mascarpone cream, cocoa, and dark chocolate.

45 min + chillServes 8

Ingredients

  • 6 large egg yolks
  • ¾ cup sugar
  • 1 cup mascarpone
  • 1½ cups heavy cream
  • 2 cups strong espresso, cooled
  • 2 tbsp coffee liqueur, optional
  • 24–30 ladyfingers
  • Unsweetened cocoa powder
  • Dark chocolate, grated

Preparation

  1. Whisk yolks and sugar over a gentle bain-marie until pale and thick; cool slightly.
  2. Beat mascarpone smooth. Whip cream to soft peaks and fold into mascarpone, then fold in yolk mixture.
  3. Combine espresso and optional liqueur. Dip ladyfingers briefly and arrange in a dish.
  4. Layer with mascarpone cream, repeat, and chill at least 6 hours.
  5. Dust with cocoa and grated chocolate before serving.
