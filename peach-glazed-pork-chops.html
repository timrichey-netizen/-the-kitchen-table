Peach-Glazed Pork Chops

Seared pork chops lacquered with a sweet-tangy Georgia peach glaze.

Southern American (Georgia)Georgia, United States35 min

Ingredients

  • 4 bone-in pork chops, 1 inch thick
  • salt and pepper
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 cup diced ripe peaches
  • 1/2 cup peach preserves
  • 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp butter

Preparation

  1. Season chops. Sear in oil over medium-high heat until browned, 3–4 minutes per side. Reduce heat.
  2. Combine peaches, preserves, vinegar, and mustard; add to skillet.
  3. Simmer, spooning glaze over chops, until pork reaches 145°F, 4–8 minutes.
  4. Remove chops and rest 3 minutes. Swirl butter into glaze and spoon over pork.
