American (Michigan)

Detroit-Style Pizza

A Michigan main centered on pizza dough, wisconsin brick cheese, pepperoni, tomato sauce, prepared in its recognizable Midwestern regional style.

American (Michigan)Michigan, United States45 min

Ingredients

  • 1 Detroit-style pizza dough
  • 12 oz Wisconsin brick cheese, cubed
  • 6 oz pepperoni
  • 1 1/2 cups thick tomato sauce

Preparation

  1. Oil a 10 x 14-inch dark metal pan and stretch dough to corners; proof until airy.
  2. Arrange pepperoni on dough and spread cheese all the way to the pan edges.
  3. Spoon tomato sauce in stripes over the cheese.
  4. Bake at 500°F for 12–16 minutes until the bottom is crisp and cheese edges are deeply caramelized. Loosen, lift from pan, and cut into squares.
