Baked Beans with Maple Syrup

A Quebec, Canada side centered on navy beans, maple syrup, salt pork, prepared in its recognizable Canadian regional style.

QuébécoisQuebec, Canada45 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lb dried navy beans, soaked overnight
  • 6 oz salt pork, diced
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1/2 cup pure maple syrup
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp dry mustard
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 3 cups hot water

Preparation

  1. Drain soaked beans and simmer in fresh water until barely tender, 45–60 minutes; drain.
  2. Heat oven to 300°F. Combine beans, salt pork, and onion in a bean pot.
  3. Stir together maple syrup, brown sugar, mustard, salt, and hot water and pour over beans.
  4. Cover and bake 5–6 hours, uncovering for the last hour, until very tender and dark.
