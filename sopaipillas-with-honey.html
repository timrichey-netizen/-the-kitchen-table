New Mexican

Sopaipillas with Honey

Hot, hollow New Mexican sopaipillas served with honey drizzled into their crisp, steaming centers.

New MexicanNew Mexico, United States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup whole wheat flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 2 tsp shortening
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • about 1/2 cup lukewarm water
  • frying oil
  • 1/2 cup honey

Preparation

  1. Mix flours, salt, and baking powder; work in shortening, then add milk and water to form soft dough. Knead briefly and rest 30 minutes.
  2. Roll 1/4 inch thick and cut.
  3. Fry at 400°F, spooning hot oil over pieces until fully puffed; turn and brown lightly.
  4. Drain. Tear a corner from each hot sopaipilla and drizzle honey into the hollow center.
