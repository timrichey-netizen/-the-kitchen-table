Alaska Smoked Salmon Dip

Creamy Alaska smoked salmon dip balanced with lemon, dill, scallion, and a touch of horseradish.

American (Alaska)Alaska, United States15 min

Ingredients

  • 8 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 6 oz Alaska smoked salmon, flaked
  • 1/4 cup sour cream
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp prepared horseradish
  • 2 tbsp chopped fresh dill
  • 2 scallions, finely sliced
  • black pepper
  • crackers for serving

Preparation

  1. Beat cream cheese and sour cream until smooth.
  2. Mix in lemon juice and horseradish.
  3. Fold in smoked salmon, dill, and scallions, leaving visible salmon flakes.
  4. Season with black pepper. Chill 30 minutes if time permits. Serve with crackers.
