Pho Broth

Clear aromatic Vietnamese beef broth with charred onion, ginger, and warm spices.

Broth & StockVietnamese

Ingredients

  • 5 lb beef bones
  • 1 onion, halved
  • 4 inches ginger
  • 3 star anise
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 4 cloves
  • 1 tbsp coriander seed
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce
  • 5 qt water

Preparation

  1. Blanch bones, drain, and rinse thoroughly.
  2. Char onion and ginger; toast spices.
  3. Combine with fresh water and simmer very gently 5–6 hours.
  4. Strain; season with fish sauce and salt.
