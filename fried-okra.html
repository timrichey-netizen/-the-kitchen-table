Fried Okra

Crisp Southern-style fried okra with a seasoned buttermilk coating and crunchy cornmeal crust.

35 minServes 4 to 6

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

Ingredients

  • 1 lb fresh okra, cut into ½-inch rounds
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • 1 large egg
  • ¾ cup fine cornmeal
  • ½ cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ¼ tsp cayenne pepper, optional
  • Neutral oil, for frying
  • Flaky salt, for finishing

Preparation

  1. Whisk buttermilk and egg in a bowl. Add the okra and let stand 10 minutes.
  2. In a shallow dish, combine cornmeal, flour, salt, black pepper, garlic powder, and cayenne.
  3. Drain the okra lightly, then toss in the cornmeal mixture until evenly coated.
  4. Heat 1½ to 2 inches of oil to 350°F. Fry the okra in batches until crisp and golden, about 3 to 4 minutes.
  5. Drain on a rack or paper towels, season with flaky salt, and serve hot.
