Shrimp Creole

Tender shrimp simmered briefly in a bright tomato sauce with onion, celery, bell pepper, herbs, and Creole spice.

45 minServes 4–6

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 green bell pepper, diced
  • 2 celery ribs, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 cup seafood or chicken stock
  • 1 tbsp Creole seasoning
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 1/2 lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • Hot sauce, to taste
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Cooked white rice, scallions, and parsley, for serving

Preparation

  1. Heat butter and olive oil in a saucepan. Cook onion, bell pepper, and celery until softened.
  2. Add garlic and cook briefly, then add tomatoes, stock, Creole seasoning, Worcestershire, thyme, and bay leaf.
  3. Simmer uncovered for 20 to 25 minutes until the sauce is rich and slightly reduced.
  4. Add shrimp and cook just until opaque, about 4 to 5 minutes.
  5. Season with hot sauce, salt, and pepper.
  6. Serve over white rice with scallions and parsley.
