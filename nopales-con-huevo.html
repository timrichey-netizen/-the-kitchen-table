Nopales con Huevo

Tender nopales scrambled with eggs, tomato, onion, and chile for a bright, savory breakfast.

25 minServes 4

Photo: SnapCalorie

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked nopales, diced
  • 8 eggs
  • 1/2 onion, diced
  • 2 Roma tomatoes, diced
  • 1 jalapeño, minced
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and pepper
  • Cilantro
  • Warm tortillas

Preparation

  1. Cook onion and jalapeño in oil until soft.
  2. Add tomato and nopales and cook until hot.
  3. Beat eggs with salt and pepper and add to pan.
  4. Scramble gently until just set.
  5. Finish with cilantro and serve with tortillas.
