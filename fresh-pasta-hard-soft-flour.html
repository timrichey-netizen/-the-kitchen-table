Fresh Pasta with Hard + Soft Flour

Fresh egg pasta made with a blend of durum-style hard flour and soft wheat flour for structure and tenderness.

1 hr 15 minServes 4

PASTA GUIDE

Recommended pasta: Tagliatelle, fettuccine, ravioli, or lasagna sheets

See pasta shapes, sauces & pairings →

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups 00 flour
  • 1 cup fine semolina or durum flour
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 1 tsp olive oil
  • Pinch of salt
  • Semolina for dusting

Preparation

  1. Combine the flours on a work surface and make a wide well in the center.
  2. Add eggs, yolk, olive oil, and salt to the well and gradually incorporate the flour.
  3. Knead 8–10 minutes until smooth and elastic. Wrap and rest 30 minutes.
  4. Divide dough and roll through a pasta machine to desired thickness.
  5. Cut into tagliatelle, fettuccine, or sheets and dust lightly with semolina.
