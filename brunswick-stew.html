Southern American (Georgia)

Brunswick Stew

Georgia-style Brunswick stew combining smoked pork and chicken with tomatoes, corn, lima beans, and barbecue seasoning.

Southern American (Georgia)Georgia, United States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 3 cups shredded smoked pork
  • 2 cups shredded cooked chicken
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 3 cups chicken stock
  • 2 cups corn
  • 2 cups lima beans
  • 1/3 cup barbecue sauce
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire
  • salt and pepper

Preparation

  1. Melt butter and cook onion until soft; add garlic for 30 seconds.
  2. Add tomatoes, stock, barbecue sauce, and Worcestershire and bring to a simmer.
  3. Add pork, chicken, corn, and lima beans.
  4. Simmer uncovered 35–45 minutes, stirring occasionally, until thick and cohesive. Season to taste.
