SAUCE · MEXICAN

Salsa Xni-Pec

A Yucatecan fresh salsa of tomato, red onion, habanero, cilantro, and sour orange or lime.

15 minMakes about 2 cups

Photo: Woodland Foods

Ingredients

  • 4 Roma tomatoes, diced
  • 1/2 red onion, finely diced
  • 1 habanero, very finely minced
  • 1/3 cup chopped cilantro
  • 1/4 cup sour orange juice, or orange-lime blend
  • 1 tsp kosher salt

Preparation

  1. Combine tomatoes, red onion, habanero, and cilantro.
  2. Add sour orange juice and salt.
  3. Toss gently and let rest 10 minutes.
  4. Taste carefully and adjust citrus or salt.
  5. Serve fresh with grilled meats, seafood, or tacos.
