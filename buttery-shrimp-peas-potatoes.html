Buttery Shrimp with Peas and Potatoes

Tender shrimp, baby potatoes, and sweet peas tossed in a glossy garlic-herb butter sauce.

35 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 1 lb baby potatoes, halved
  • 1½ cups frozen peas
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • Salt and black pepper

Preparation

  1. Boil potatoes in salted water until just tender, 10–12 minutes; drain.
  2. Heat olive oil and 1 tbsp butter in a large skillet. Sauté shrimp until just pink; remove.
  3. Add remaining butter and garlic. Cook 30 seconds, then add potatoes and peas and toss until hot.
  4. Return shrimp to the skillet. Add lemon juice, parsley, salt, and pepper and toss to coat.
