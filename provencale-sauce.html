SAUCE · TOMATO SAUCE FAMILY

Provençale

A southern French tomato sauce with garlic, olive oil, herbs, and often olives or capers.

35 minMakes about 3 cups

Photo: NEWS.ru

Ingredients

  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 4 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 tsp herbes de Provence
  • 1/3 cup black olives, sliced
  • 1 tbsp capers, optional
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley or basil
  • Salt and black pepper

Preparation

  1. Cook onion in olive oil until softened.
  2. Add garlic and cook until fragrant without browning.
  3. Add tomatoes and herbes de Provence and simmer for 20 minutes.
  4. Stir in olives and capers and simmer 5 minutes more.
  5. Finish with parsley or basil and adjust seasoning.
