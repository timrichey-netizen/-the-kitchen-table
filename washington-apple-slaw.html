American (Washington)

Washington Apple Slaw

Crisp Washington apples and cabbage tossed in a bright cider dressing for a fresh sweet-tart slaw.

American (Washington)Washington, United States20 min

Ingredients

  • 2 crisp Washington apples, julienned
  • 4 cups shredded green cabbage
  • 1 cup shredded red cabbage
  • 1 carrot, julienned
  • 3 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • black pepper

Preparation

  1. Whisk vinegar, olive oil, honey, mustard, salt, and pepper.
  2. Combine apples, green cabbage, red cabbage, and carrot.
  3. Toss thoroughly with dressing.
  4. Rest 10 minutes for flavors to blend while retaining crunch, then serve.
