American (Rhode Island)

Stuffed Quahogs

A classic Rhode Island main featuring quahogs, breadcrumbs, onion, celery, prepared in its recognizable regional style.

American (Rhode Island)Rhode Island, United States45 min

Ingredients

  • 8 large quahogs
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 onion, minced
  • 1 celery rib, minced
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1/2 tsp paprika
  • 1 tbsp parsley
  • reserved clam liquor
  • lemon

Preparation

  1. Steam quahogs just until opened; remove meat, reserve shells and liquor, and finely chop meat.
  2. Cook onion and celery in butter. Stir in chopped quahog, breadcrumbs, paprika, parsley, and enough clam liquor to moisten.
  3. Pack mixture into scrubbed half shells.
  4. Bake at 400°F for 15–20 minutes until browned. Serve with lemon.
