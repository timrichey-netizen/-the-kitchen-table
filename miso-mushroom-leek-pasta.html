Miso Mushroom and Leek Pasta

Savory pasta with caramelized mushrooms and leeks in a silky miso-butter sauce.

35 minServes 4

MUSHROOM TIP

For better browning: use a wide hot pan, avoid crowding, and let released moisture evaporate before adding sauce ingredients.

See the Mushroom Cooking Guide →

PASTA GUIDE

Recommended pasta: Tagliatelle or fettuccine

See pasta shapes, sauces & pairings →

Ingredients

  • 12 oz spaghetti or linguine
  • 12 oz mixed mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 leeks, cleaned and sliced
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp white miso
  • ½ cup pasta water, plus more as needed
  • 2 tsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil, optional
  • 2 green onions, sliced
  • Black pepper

Preparation

  1. Cook pasta until al dente, reserving pasta water.
  2. Brown mushrooms in olive oil and 1 tbsp butter. Add leeks and cook until tender.
  3. Whisk miso with ½ cup hot pasta water, soy sauce, and remaining butter.
  4. Add pasta and miso mixture to the skillet and toss until glossy.
  5. Finish with green onion, black pepper, and optional sesame oil.
Pasta-water technique: Before draining, reserve at least 1 cup of the hot, starchy cooking water. Add it to the sauce a tablespoon or splash at a time while tossing the pasta. The starch helps emulsify fat, cheese, and sauce so it clings to the pasta and becomes glossy rather than watery.
