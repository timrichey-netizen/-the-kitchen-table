Native Hawaiian

Laulau

Traditional Hawaiian laulau of pork and salted fish wrapped in lūʻau/taro leaves and steamed until exceptionally tender.

Native HawaiianHawaiʻi, United States4 hr

Ingredients

  • 2 lb pork shoulder, 2-inch chunks
  • 4 oz salted butterfish or salted cod, small pieces
  • 24 large taro leaves, stems removed
  • 8 ti leaves or parchment/foil for outer wrapping
  • 1 tsp Hawaiian sea salt

Preparation

  1. Wash taro leaves thoroughly and remove tough stems and ribs.
  2. Divide pork and salted fish into 4 portions; season pork lightly with sea salt.
  3. Stack 5–6 taro leaves per bundle, place pork and fish in center, and fold leaves securely around filling.
  4. Wrap each bundle in ti leaves and tie; if unavailable, use parchment and foil as a home adaptation.
  5. Steam bundles over simmering water 3–4 hours until pork is fall-apart tender and taro leaves are completely soft and cooked.
  6. Unwrap outer covering carefully and serve hot.
