American (Pennsylvania)

Whoopie Pies

A Pennsylvania dessert preparation centered on chocolate cakes, marshmallow cream filling, presented in its recognizable regional style.

American (Pennsylvania)Pennsylvania, United States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1/2 cup cocoa
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 cup shortening or butter
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1 cup marshmallow creme
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1 1/2 cups powdered sugar

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 350°F. Mix dry ingredients. Cream fat and sugar; beat in egg and vanilla, then alternate dry ingredients with buttermilk.
  2. Scoop rounds and bake 10–12 minutes; cool.
  3. Beat marshmallow creme, butter, and powdered sugar until fluffy.
  4. Sandwich filling between paired cakes.
