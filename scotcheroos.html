American (Minnesota)

Scotcheroos

A Minnesota dessert centered on rice cereal, peanut butter, butterscotch, chocolate, prepared in its recognizable Midwestern regional style.

American (Minnesota)Minnesota, United States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 6 cups crisp rice cereal
  • 1 cup light corn syrup
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 cup peanut butter
  • 1 cup semisweet chocolate chips
  • 1 cup butterscotch chips

Preparation

  1. Place cereal in a large bowl. Heat corn syrup and sugar just until mixture begins to boil; remove from heat and stir in peanut butter.
  2. Fold into cereal and press into greased 9 x 13-inch pan.
  3. Melt chocolate and butterscotch chips together and spread over base.
  4. Cool until topping sets, then cut into bars.
