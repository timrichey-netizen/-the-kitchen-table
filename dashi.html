BROTH & STOCK · JAPANESE

Dashi

Classic Japanese kombu-and-katsuobushi dashi: light, smoky, and deeply savory.

Broth & StockJapanese

Ingredients

  • 4 cups water
  • 1 piece kombu, about 4 inches
  • 1 cup loosely packed katsuobushi

Preparation

  1. Soak kombu in water 30 minutes.
  2. Heat slowly and remove kombu just before simmering.
  3. Bring liquid briefly to a boil, turn off heat, and add katsuobushi.
  4. Steep 5 minutes and strain without pressing.
