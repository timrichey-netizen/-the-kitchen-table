Pozole Broth

Red-chile pork broth base for pozole, built to carry hominy and garnishes.

Broth & StockMexican

Ingredients

  • 3 lb pork shoulder and neck bones
  • 3 qt water
  • 1 onion
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 4 guajillo chiles
  • 2 ancho chiles
  • 1 tsp Mexican oregano

Preparation

  1. Simmer pork, onion, and garlic until tender.
  2. Toast, soak, and blend chiles with some broth; strain.
  3. Stir chile puree into broth and simmer 30 minutes.
  4. Season with salt; add hominy when making finished pozole.
