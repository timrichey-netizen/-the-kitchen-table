Tacos de Camarón Capeado

Shrimp in a light, crisp capeado batter with cabbage, crema, lime, and salsa verde.

35 minMakes 10 tacos

Photo: Muy Delish

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 3/4 cup flour
  • 1/4 cup cornstarch
  • 1 egg
  • 3/4 cup cold sparkling water or beer
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Neutral oil, for frying
  • 10 corn tortillas
  • Shredded cabbage
  • Mexican crema
  • Salsa verde
  • Lime wedges

Preparation

  1. Whisk flour, cornstarch, egg, cold liquid, and salt into a light batter.
  2. Heat oil to 350°F.
  3. Dip shrimp in batter and fry in batches until crisp and just cooked, 2 to 3 minutes.
  4. Drain briefly.
  5. Serve in tortillas with cabbage, crema, salsa verde, and lime.
