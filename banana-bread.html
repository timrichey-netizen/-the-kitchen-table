Banana Bread

Moist banana bread with ripe bananas, brown sugar, vanilla, and warm spice.

1 hr 15 minMakes 1 loaf

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

Ingredients

  • 3 very ripe bananas, mashed
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, melted
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 3/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/2 cup chopped walnuts or pecans, optional

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 350°F and grease a 9-by-5-inch loaf pan.
  2. Whisk mashed bananas, melted butter, brown sugar, eggs, and vanilla.
  3. Whisk flour, baking soda, salt, and cinnamon separately, then fold into the wet ingredients just until combined.
  4. Fold in nuts if using. Transfer to pan.
  5. Bake 50 to 60 minutes until a tester comes out with a few moist crumbs. Cool before slicing.
