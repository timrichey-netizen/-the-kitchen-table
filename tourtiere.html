Québécois

Tourtière

A Quebec, Canada main centered on ground pork, ground beef, pastry, potato, warm spices, prepared in its recognizable Canadian regional style.

QuébécoisQuebec, Canada45 min

Ingredients

  • 1 double-crust 9-inch pie pastry
  • 1 lb ground pork
  • 1/2 lb ground beef
  • 1 onion, finely diced
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 1 small potato, peeled and grated
  • 1/2 cup beef stock
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp cloves
  • 1/4 tsp allspice
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 egg beaten with 1 tbsp water

Preparation

  1. Cook pork, beef, and onion in a skillet over medium heat, breaking meat finely, until no longer pink. Add garlic, grated potato, stock, spices, salt, and pepper. Simmer 10–15 minutes until moist but not wet; cool.
  2. Heat oven to 400°F. Line pie plate with pastry, add filling, cover with top crust, seal and vent. Brush with egg wash.
  3. Bake 15 minutes, reduce to 375°F, and continue 30–35 minutes until deeply golden. Rest 15 minutes before slicing.
