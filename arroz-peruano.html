RICE · PERUVIAN

Arroz Peruano

Peruvian-style white rice cooked with garlic and oil until fluffy and aromatic.

35 minServes 6

Photo: Eat Peru

Ingredients

  • 2 cups long-grain white rice
  • 3 tbsp neutral oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 3 cups water
  • 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt

Preparation

  1. Rinse rice until water runs mostly clear and drain well.
  2. Heat oil in a saucepan and cook garlic briefly until fragrant.
  3. Add rice and stir for 1 minute to coat the grains.
  4. Add water and salt and bring to a boil.
  5. Cover, reduce heat to low, and cook 15 to 18 minutes. Rest 10 minutes before fluffing.
