Ramen Chicken Broth

Clear but rich chicken ramen broth designed to be seasoned with a separate tare.

Broth & StockJapanese

Ingredients

  • 4 lb chicken backs and wings
  • 1 onion
  • 1 leek
  • 2 inches ginger
  • 6 garlic cloves
  • 1 piece kombu, optional
  • 4 qt water

Preparation

  1. Blanch chicken briefly and rinse.
  2. Cover with fresh water and maintain a gentle simmer 4 hours.
  3. Add aromatics for the final hour.
  4. Strain; season each bowl with tare rather than heavily salting the stock.
