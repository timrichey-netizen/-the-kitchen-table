Pennsylvania Dutch Chicken Corn Soup

A Pennsylvania soup preparation centered on chicken, sweet corn, egg noodles, eggs, presented in its recognizable regional style.

Pennsylvania DutchPennsylvania, United States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken
  • 10 cups water
  • 1 onion
  • 2 celery ribs
  • 4 cups sweet corn
  • 2 cups fine egg noodles or rivels
  • 2 hard-cooked eggs, chopped
  • parsley
  • salt and pepper

Preparation

  1. Simmer chicken with water, onion, celery, salt, and pepper until tender, about 1 hour. Remove chicken and strain broth.
  2. Shred chicken and return with corn. Simmer 10 minutes.
  3. Add noodles or rivels and cook until tender.
  4. Stir in chopped hard-cooked eggs and parsley; adjust seasoning.
