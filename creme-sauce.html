SAUCE · BÉCHAMEL DAUGHTER SAUCE

Crème Sauce

A richer Béchamel daughter sauce finished with heavy cream for an extra-silky texture.

15 minMakes about 2 cups

Photo: Chefkoch

Ingredients

  • 2 cups prepared Béchamel
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter
  • Salt and white pepper
  • Pinch of nutmeg

Preparation

  1. Bring the Béchamel to a gentle simmer over low heat.
  2. Whisk in the heavy cream and cook 3 to 5 minutes until the sauce is smooth and slightly reduced.
  3. Remove from heat and whisk in butter.
  4. Season with salt, white pepper, and nutmeg.
  5. Use immediately over vegetables, poultry, fish, or pasta.
