American (Illinois)

Chicago-Style Hot Dog

A Illinois main centered on all-beef hot dog, poppy seed bun, mustard, relish, onion, tomato, sport peppers, prepared in its recognizable Midwestern regional style.

American (Illinois)Illinois, United States45 min

Ingredients

  • 8 all-beef hot dogs
  • 8 poppy seed buns
  • yellow mustard
  • neon-green sweet relish
  • 1 onion, minced
  • 2 tomatoes, cut in wedges
  • 8 dill pickle spears
  • 16 sport peppers
  • celery salt

Preparation

  1. Steam or simmer hot dogs until heated through. Steam buns until soft.
  2. Place each dog in a bun.
  3. Top in classic Chicago style with mustard, relish, onion, tomato wedges, pickle spear, and sport peppers.
  4. Finish with celery salt; do not add ketchup.
