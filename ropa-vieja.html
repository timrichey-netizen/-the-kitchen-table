Caribbean (Cuba)

Ropa Vieja

A Cuba main built around shredded beef, tomato, bell pepper, onion, prepared in its recognizable regional style.

Caribbean (Cuba)Cuba1 hr

Ingredients

  • 3 lb flank or chuck beef
  • 1 onion
  • 2 bell peppers
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 can crushed tomatoes
  • 1 cup beef stock
  • 1/2 cup dry white wine
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • salt and pepper

Preparation

  1. Brown beef, then add stock and simmer covered until shreddable, 2–3 hours.
  2. Shred beef. Sauté onion, peppers, and garlic in oil; add tomato, wine, cumin, and oregano.
  3. Return beef and simmer 20–30 minutes until saucy and tender.
