Mexican Caldo de Res

Hearty Mexican beef broth with shank, corn, cabbage, and root vegetables.

Broth & StockMexican

Ingredients

  • 3 lb beef shank or short ribs
  • 1 onion
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 potatoes
  • 2 ears corn
  • 1/2 cabbage
  • Cilantro
  • 4 qt water

Preparation

  1. Simmer beef, onion, garlic, and salt until beef is nearly tender, 2–3 hours.
  2. Skim fat and foam.
  3. Add potatoes, carrots, and corn; add cabbage near the end.
  4. Serve with cilantro, lime, and warm tortillas.
