Caribbean (Puerto Rico)

Arroz con Gandules

A Puerto Rico main built around rice, pigeon peas, sofrito, annatto, prepared in its recognizable regional style.

Caribbean (Puerto Rico)Puerto Rico1 hr

Ingredients

  • 2 cups medium-grain rice
  • 1 can pigeon peas
  • 1/2 cup sofrito
  • 1/2 cup tomato sauce
  • 3 tbsp annatto oil
  • 3 cups stock
  • 1/2 cup green olives
  • 1 tsp adobo seasoning

Preparation

  1. Sauté sofrito in annatto oil. Add tomato sauce, pigeon peas, olives, and adobo.
  2. Stir in rice and stock; bring to a boil.
  3. Cook uncovered until liquid is mostly absorbed, then cover and cook on low 20 minutes. Fluff and rest.
