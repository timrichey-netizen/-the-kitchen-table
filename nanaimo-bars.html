Canadian (British Columbia)

Nanaimo Bars

A Nanaimo, British Columbia, Canada dessert centered on chocolate, graham crumbs, coconut, custard filling, prepared in its recognizable Canadian regional style.

Canadian (British Columbia)Nanaimo, British Columbia, Canada1 hr

Ingredients

  • Base: 1/2 cup butter
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 5 tbsp cocoa
  • 1 egg
  • 1 3/4 cups graham crumbs
  • 1 cup shredded coconut
  • 1/2 cup chopped walnuts. Filling: 1/2 cup butter
  • 2 tbsp custard powder
  • 2 cups powdered sugar
  • 3 tbsp cream. Topping: 4 oz semisweet chocolate
  • 2 tbsp butter

Preparation

  1. Melt butter with sugar and cocoa; whisk in egg and cook gently until thickened. Stir in crumbs, coconut, and walnuts; press into 8-inch pan and chill.
  2. Beat filling ingredients until light and spread over base; chill until firm.
  3. Melt chocolate with butter, cool slightly, and spread over filling. Chill until just set, then score and cut into bars.
