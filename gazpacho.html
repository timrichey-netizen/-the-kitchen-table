SOUP · SPANISH

Gazpacho

A cold Andalusian-style tomato soup with cucumber, pepper, garlic, sherry vinegar, and olive oil.

20 min + chillServes 6

Ingredients

  • 2 lb very ripe tomatoes, cored and chopped
  • 1 small cucumber, peeled and chopped
  • 1 red bell pepper, seeded and chopped
  • 1 small garlic clove
  • 1 small slice day-old country bread, soaked and squeezed dry
  • 3 tbsp sherry vinegar
  • 1/3 cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 tsp kosher salt, plus more to taste
  • Cold water as needed
  • Finely diced tomato, cucumber, and pepper, for garnish

Preparation

  1. Blend tomatoes, cucumber, bell pepper, garlic, bread, vinegar, and salt until very smooth.
  2. With the blender running, slowly stream in the olive oil until the soup is emulsified and creamy.
  3. Thin with a little cold water if needed, then taste and adjust salt and vinegar.
  4. Strain for an especially smooth texture, if desired.
  5. Chill at least 2 hours. Serve very cold with diced vegetables and a drizzle of olive oil.

Notes

The flavor depends on ripe tomatoes. Chill thoroughly before serving.
