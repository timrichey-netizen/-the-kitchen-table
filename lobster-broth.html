Lobster Broth

Luxurious roasted lobster-shell broth for bisques, sauces, and risotto.

Broth & StockSeafood

Ingredients

  • Shells from 2 lobsters
  • 1 onion
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 celery rib
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1/2 cup brandy or white wine
  • 2 qt water

Preparation

  1. Crack and roast shells until deeply aromatic.
  2. Sauté vegetables and tomato paste; add shells.
  3. Deglaze, add water, and simmer 45–60 minutes.
  4. Strain very well and chill.
