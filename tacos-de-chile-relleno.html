Tacos de Chile Relleno

Roasted poblano strips stuffed or folded with cheese, then tucked into tortillas with ranchera or roja sauce.

50 minMakes 8 tacos

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

Ingredients

  • 4 poblano peppers
  • 8 oz Oaxaca or Monterey Jack cheese
  • 8 corn tortillas
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 1/2 cups salsa ranchera or salsa roja
  • Sliced onion
  • Cilantro
  • Lime wedges

Preparation

  1. Char poblanos, steam 10 minutes, peel, seed, and cut into broad strips.
  2. Place cheese inside pepper strips and warm until melted.
  3. Warm tortillas.
  4. Fill with chile-and-cheese portions.
  5. Top with salsa, onion, cilantro, and lime.
