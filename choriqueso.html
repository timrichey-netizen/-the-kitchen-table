CHEESE · MEXICAN

Choriqueso

Bubbling melted cheese topped with browned Mexican chorizo, served with warm tortillas.

25 minServes 6

Photo: SnapCalorie

Ingredients

  • 12 oz Mexican chorizo
  • 12 oz Oaxaca or Monterey Jack cheese, shredded
  • 1 poblano, roasted and diced, optional
  • 1/4 white onion, diced
  • Warm tortillas
  • Pico de gallo, optional

Preparation

  1. Brown chorizo and drain excess fat.
  2. Cook onion and poblano in the same skillet.
  3. Top with cheese.
  4. Bake or broil until bubbling and melted.
  5. Spoon chorizo over the cheese and serve immediately with tortillas.
