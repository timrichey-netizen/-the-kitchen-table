Lemon Squares

Bright lemon bars with a buttery shortbread crust and silky citrus filling.

1 hr + coolServes 8

Ingredients

  • 1 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • ½ cup powdered sugar
  • 2 cups flour, divided
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1½ cups sugar
  • ⅔ cup fresh lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp lemon zest
  • Powdered sugar for finishing

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 350°F. Beat butter, powdered sugar, and 1¾ cups flour; press into a lined pan.
  2. Bake crust 18–20 minutes.
  3. Whisk eggs, sugar, remaining flour, lemon juice, and zest.
  4. Pour over warm crust and bake 20–24 minutes.
  5. Cool completely, dust with powdered sugar, and cut into squares.
