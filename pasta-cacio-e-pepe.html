PASTA · ROMAN

Pasta Cacio e Pepe

A Roman classic of pasta, Pecorino Romano, black pepper, and starchy pasta water.

25 minServes 4

PASTA GUIDE

Recommended pasta: Tonnarelli or spaghetti

See pasta shapes, sauces & pairings →

Ingredients

  • 12 oz tonnarelli or spaghetti
  • 1½ cups finely grated Pecorino Romano
  • 2 tsp freshly cracked black pepper
  • Kosher salt

Preparation

  1. Cook pasta in lightly salted water until just shy of al dente; reserve 1½ cups pasta water.
  2. Toast black pepper in a wide skillet and add about ½ cup pasta water.
  3. Add pasta and toss over medium-low heat.
  4. Remove from heat and gradually add Pecorino, tossing vigorously until creamy and emulsified.
Pasta-water technique: Before draining, reserve at least 1 cup of the hot, starchy cooking water. Add it to the sauce a tablespoon or splash at a time while tossing the pasta. The starch helps emulsify fat, cheese, and sauce so it clings to the pasta and becomes glossy rather than watery.
