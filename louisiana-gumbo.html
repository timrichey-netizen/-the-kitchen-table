Louisiana Gumbo

Louisiana gumbo with a dark roux, the holy trinity, andouille, chicken, and okra served with rice.

Louisiana Creole/CajunLouisiana, United States2 hr

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup oil
  • 3/4 cup flour
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 green bell pepper, diced
  • 2 celery ribs, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 lb andouille, sliced
  • 1 1/2 lb chicken thighs
  • 6 cups chicken stock
  • 2 cups sliced okra
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • cayenne, salt, black pepper
  • cooked white rice

Preparation

  1. Cook oil and flour over medium-low heat, stirring constantly, until roux is deep chocolate brown, 30–45 minutes.
  2. Add onion, bell pepper, and celery and cook 5 minutes; add garlic.
  3. Gradually stir in stock. Add andouille, chicken, bay, thyme, and seasoning. Simmer 45 minutes.
  4. Add okra and simmer 20 minutes more. Remove chicken bones if needed and adjust seasoning. Serve over rice.
