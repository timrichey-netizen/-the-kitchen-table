SAUCE · VELOUTÉ DAUGHTER SAUCE

Suprême

Chicken velouté enriched with cream, butter, and a touch of lemon for a delicate poultry sauce.

25 minMakes about 2 cups

Photo: TasteAtlas

Ingredients

  • 2 cups chicken velouté
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 tsp fresh lemon juice, plus more to taste
  • Salt and white pepper
  • 2 tbsp mushroom cooking liquid, optional

Preparation

  1. Warm the chicken velouté over medium-low heat.
  2. Add the cream and optional mushroom liquid and simmer gently until slightly reduced.
  3. Remove from heat and whisk in the butter.
  4. Season with lemon juice, salt, and white pepper.
  5. Strain if desired and serve with poultry.
