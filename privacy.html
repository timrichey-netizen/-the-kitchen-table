Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

The Kitchen Table is a recipe website hosted through GitHub Pages. This page describes the website features and the proposed advertising system. Review this policy for accuracy before enabling advertising.

Local browser storage

The website uses browser storage for features such as saved favorite recipes, personal ratings, meal selections, and language preferences. Data stored in your browser generally stays on that device unless a feature explicitly sends it to a service.

Accounts and saved favorites

Account sign-in is currently disabled. If enabled, a third-party authentication service (Supabase) will process email addresses, account identifiers, authentication sessions, and public-key credentials for passkeys. Biometric data such as Face ID remains on your device. Saved favorites will be associated with your authenticated account and stored with user-specific access rules. Local favorites are not automatically imported into an account.

Optional activity-based recommendations

When a signed-in member separately opts in, this site can store limited recipe interaction events in that device's browser: recipes viewed or favorited, search use (but not search terms), filter choices, printing, sharing, and which recommendations were displayed. The purpose is to improve suggestions and avoid repeating the same recommended recipes for 21 days. This activity history is not uploaded to Google Sheets while the account sync service is unconfigured. Members can disable collection and erase this browser history from My Account. Public recipe access does not require agreeing to this tracking.

Optional member recipe emails

When account services are activated, members who expressly opt in can choose delivery frequency, recipe categories, and the number of recipe links per email. Subscription settings and the authenticated account email are stored in a private Google Sheet and processed by Google Apps Script. Members may stop future sends by signing in and disabling the email opt-in. Email delivery is currently disabled until configured and tested.

Ratings and reviews

Community ratings and reviews are currently not enabled. If these features are activated, this notice must be updated to identify the service, information collected, retention, and moderation processes.

Advertising

Display advertisements are currently disabled. No Google AdSense advertising script is loaded by this website's advertising module while advertising is disabled. If ads are enabled in the future, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies for ad delivery, measurement, and personalization, subject to applicable consent requirements. This policy and any necessary consent controls will be updated before activation.

Visitor analytics

Google Analytics 4 (GA4) visitor tracking is currently disabled. The analytics module does not load Google Analytics or send tracking events unless the site owner explicitly enables it, supplies a valid Measurement ID, and completes the required consent configuration. Before activation, this policy will be updated to describe analytics data collection, retention, and visitor choices.

External links and hosting

Following external links may take you to independently operated websites with their own policies. GitHub Pages provides hosting infrastructure.

Regional privacy rights

Applicable privacy rights may include access, correction, deletion, portability, objection to processing, withdrawal of consent, and opting out of sale, sharing, targeted advertising or profiling. Relevant laws may include GDPR, UK GDPR, California CCPA/CPRA, Virginia VCDPA, and other state privacy statutes. Coverage depends on the actual operator, business activities and statutory thresholds. Contact details and a verifiable request procedure must be established before rights can be exercised through the website.

Retention and processors

When activated, account, hosting, email, advertising and analytics providers may process data for their disclosed functions. Retention periods, service provider details, international data transfers and account-deletion procedures must be verified before the respective features are activated.

Children and COPPA

This is a general-audience recipe website. It is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without any necessary parental consent. Before accounts are offered to children, age-assurance, COPPA compliance and removal procedures must be assessed.

Cookie choices and commercial disclosures

Read our Cookie & Storage Notice and Advertising, Affiliate & AI Disclosures. Publishing these notices does not itself create the opt-out or consent controls that may be required before nonessential tracking or ads are activated.

Updates

This policy may be revised as the website and its services change.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the website owner's contact information when published. No contact address has been supplied for this policy yet.
