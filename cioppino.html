SEAFOOD · ITALIAN-AMERICAN

Cioppino

A San Francisco Italian-American seafood stew with tomato, wine, shellfish, and fish.

1 hrServes 4

Ingredients

  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion and 1 fennel bulb, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • ½ tsp red-pepper flakes
  • 1 cup white wine
  • 1 can (28 oz) tomatoes
  • 3 cups seafood stock
  • ½ lb firm white fish
  • ½ lb shrimp
  • 1 lb mussels
  • 1 lb clams
  • Parsley

Preparation

  1. Cook onion and fennel in olive oil; add garlic and red pepper.
  2. Add wine, tomatoes, and seafood stock; simmer 20 minutes.
  3. Add clams and mussels; cover until opening. Add fish and shrimp and cook just through.
  4. Discard unopened shellfish and finish with parsley.
