American (San Francisco Italian-American)

Cioppino

A San Francisco seafood stew of Dungeness crab, firm white fish, shrimp, mussels, and clams simmered in a tomato-wine broth.

American (San Francisco Italian-American)San Francisco, California, United States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 1 Dungeness crab, cleaned and cracked
  • 1/2 lb firm white fish, cubed
  • 1/2 lb shrimp, peeled
  • 1/2 lb mussels, scrubbed
  • 1/2 lb clams, scrubbed
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 1 cup dry white wine
  • 1 28-oz can crushed tomatoes
  • 2 cups fish stock
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • salt and black pepper
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley

Preparation

  1. Heat olive oil; soften onion 5 minutes. Add garlic and pepper flakes for 30 seconds.
  2. Add wine and reduce by half. Add tomatoes, stock, bay leaf, oregano, salt, and pepper; simmer 20 minutes.
  3. Add clams and mussels, cover, and cook 4 minutes.
  4. Add crab, fish, and shrimp; simmer gently until fish and shrimp are cooked and shells open, 4–6 minutes. Discard unopened shellfish.
  5. Finish with parsley and serve immediately.
