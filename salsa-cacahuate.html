Salsa de Cacahuate

A spicy, nutty peanut salsa with chile de árbol, roasted tomato, garlic, and toasted peanuts.

20 minMakes about 2 cups

Photo: Kvalifood

Ingredients

  • 1 cup roasted unsalted peanuts
  • 6 dried chiles de árbol
  • 2 Roma tomatoes
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 tsp kosher salt

Preparation

  1. Toast chiles briefly and set aside.
  2. Roast tomatoes and garlic until charred and soft.
  3. Blend peanuts, chiles, tomatoes, garlic, salt, and water until creamy but slightly textured.
  4. Heat oil in a skillet and cook the salsa for 3 to 5 minutes.
  5. Thin with water if needed and adjust salt.
