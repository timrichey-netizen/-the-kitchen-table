California Avocado Toast

California-style avocado toast featuring ripe avocado, lemon, olive oil, and chile flakes on crisp artisan bread.

American (California)California, United States15 min

Ingredients

  • 4 thick slices sourdough or artisan bread
  • 2 ripe California avocados
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
  • black pepper

Preparation

  1. Toast bread until crisp outside but still tender within.
  2. Halve and pit avocados; scoop flesh into a bowl. Add lemon juice, salt, and pepper and mash roughly, retaining texture.
  3. Divide avocado over hot toast.
  4. Drizzle with olive oil and sprinkle with red pepper flakes. Serve immediately.
