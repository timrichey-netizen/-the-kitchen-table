Tacos de Pulpo a la Parrilla

Tender octopus grilled over high heat until smoky and charred, then served with avocado, onion, cilantro, and lime.

1 hr 10 minMakes 10 tacos

Photo: Gastrolab

Ingredients

  • 2 lb cleaned octopus
  • 1 onion, halved
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 10 tortillas
  • Avocado
  • Onion
  • Cilantro
  • Lime
  • Salsa verde or salsa macha

Preparation

  1. Simmer octopus gently until tender, 40 to 60 minutes.
  2. Cool and dry thoroughly.
  3. Toss with olive oil, paprika, and salt.
  4. Grill over very high heat until charred and crisp at the edges.
  5. Slice and serve in tortillas with avocado, onion, cilantro, lime, and salsa.
