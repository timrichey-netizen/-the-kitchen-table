New Orleans BBQ Shrimp

Head-on shrimp cooked in New Orleans’ famous buttery, peppery Worcestershire-lemon sauce—no barbecue sauce required.

30 minServes 4

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

Ingredients

  • 2 lb large shell-on shrimp, preferably head-on
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 6 tbsp unsalted butter, divided
  • 5 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tbsp Creole seasoning
  • 1 tsp coarsely ground black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 cup seafood or chicken stock
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 1 tbsp hot sauce, optional
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • Crusty French bread, for serving

Preparation

  1. Heat olive oil and 2 tablespoons butter in a very large skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. Add shrimp and cook about 1 minute per side.
  3. Add garlic, Worcestershire, Creole seasoning, black pepper, paprika, stock, lemon juice, and hot sauce.
  4. Simmer, tossing the shrimp, until just cooked through and the sauce begins to reduce.
  5. Remove from heat and swirl in the remaining cold butter until glossy.
  6. Finish with parsley and serve immediately with plenty of French bread for the sauce.
