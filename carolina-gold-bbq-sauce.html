American BBQ

Carolina Gold BBQ Sauce

A South Carolina, United States sauce built around yellow mustard, cider vinegar, honey, brown sugar, prepared in its recognizable regional style.

American BBQSouth Carolina, United States30 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup yellow mustard
  • 1/2 cup cider vinegar
  • 1/4 cup honey
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire
  • 1 tsp hot sauce
  • black pepper

Preparation

  1. Combine all ingredients in a saucepan.
  2. Bring to a gentle simmer over medium-low heat, stirring often.
  3. Cook 15–25 minutes until flavors meld and the sauce reaches its characteristic consistency; for fresh-fruit sauces, simmer until fruit is soft and blend smooth.
  4. Cool, adjust salt, acid, sweetness, and heat, and refrigerate in a covered container.
