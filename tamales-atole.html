Tamales con Atole

Warm Mexican tamales served with creamy atole for a classic breakfast pairing.

20 min if tamales preparedServes 4

Photo: Depositphotos

Ingredients

  • 8 prepared tamales
  • 4 cups milk or water
  • 1/2 cup masa harina
  • 1/3 cup piloncillo or brown sugar
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • Pinch of salt

Preparation

  1. Steam tamales until hot.
  2. Whisk masa harina with 1 cup cool milk or water until smooth.
  3. Heat remaining liquid with piloncillo and cinnamon.
  4. Whisk in masa mixture and simmer until thickened, 8 to 10 minutes.
  5. Add vanilla and salt and serve hot with tamales.
