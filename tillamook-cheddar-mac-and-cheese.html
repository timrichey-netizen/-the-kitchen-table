American (Oregon)

Tillamook Cheddar Mac and Cheese

Creamy baked macaroni and cheese centered on sharp Tillamook cheddar from Oregon.

American (Oregon)Tillamook, Oregon, United States45 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lb elbow macaroni
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 1/4 cup flour
  • 3 cups whole milk
  • 12 oz sharp Tillamook cheddar, grated
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 cup panko
  • 1 tbsp melted butter

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 375°F. Cook macaroni 2 minutes shy of package directions; drain.
  2. Melt 4 tbsp butter, whisk in flour, and cook 1 minute. Gradually whisk in milk and simmer until thickened.
  3. Remove from heat and melt in cheddar; add mustard, salt, and pepper.
  4. Fold in macaroni and transfer to baking dish.
  5. Toss panko with melted butter and scatter on top. Bake 20–25 minutes until bubbling and browned.
