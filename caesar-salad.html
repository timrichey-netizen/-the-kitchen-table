Canadian (Alberta)

Caesar Salad

A Alberta, Canada side centered on romaine, parmesan, croutons, caesar dressing, prepared in its recognizable Canadian regional style.

Canadian (Alberta)Alberta, Canada45 min

Ingredients

  • 2 large romaine hearts, chopped
  • 1 cup croutons
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 2 anchovy fillets
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1/3 cup olive oil
  • black pepper

Preparation

  1. Mash anchovies and garlic to a paste. Whisk with egg yolk, lemon, and Dijon. Slowly whisk in olive oil to emulsify. Stir in half the Parmesan and black pepper.
  2. Toss romaine with enough dressing to coat.
  3. Add croutons and remaining Parmesan. Serve immediately.
