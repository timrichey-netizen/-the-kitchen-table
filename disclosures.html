Advertising, Affiliates & AI Disclosures

Updated October 8, 2026

Advertising

Advertising placements have been prepared but Google AdSense remains disabled. If activated, paid placements will be identifiable as advertising and separated from editorial content.

Affiliate relationships

If an article or recipe contains a compensated or affiliate link, a clear disclosure will appear close to that link. A purchase through such a link may result in compensation to the site operator. This page does not represent that any particular affiliate program is currently active.

Sponsored content and reviews

Sponsored articles, gifted products, and any material relationships affecting recommendations will be clearly disclosed. User reviews and endorsements should reflect genuine experiences; compensation or other relevant connections must be disclosed. Editorial independence is a goal, not a guarantee of any commercial relationship.

AI-assisted content

Some website text, images, recipes, or support interactions may be prepared with AI assistance. AI-generated or AI-assisted material can contain inaccuracies. Product, safety, and historical claims should be checked against reliable sources. An interactive AI assistant, if introduced, will be identified as such.
