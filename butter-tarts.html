Canadian (Ontario)

Butter Tarts

A Ontario, Canada dessert centered on pastry, brown sugar, butter, eggs, raisins, prepared in its recognizable Canadian regional style.

Canadian (Ontario)Ontario, Canada1 hr

Ingredients

  • 12 tart shells
  • 1/2 cup butter, melted
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/3 cup corn syrup or maple syrup
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1/2 cup raisins, optional

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 400°F. Arrange tart shells in muffin cups.
  2. Whisk butter, brown sugar, eggs, syrup, vanilla, and salt until smooth; stir in raisins if using.
  3. Fill shells about two-thirds full.
  4. Bake 15–18 minutes until pastry is golden and filling is bubbling but still slightly soft in the center. Cool before serving.
