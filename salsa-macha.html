Salsa Macha

A Veracruz-style chile oil salsa with dried chiles, garlic, nuts or seeds, and vinegar.

25 minMakes about 1 1/2 cups

Photo: The Kitchn / Yahoo

Ingredients

  • 1 cup neutral oil
  • 6 dried guajillo chiles, cut into pieces
  • 8 dried chiles de árbol
  • 4 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1/3 cup peanuts
  • 2 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt

Preparation

  1. Heat oil gently and fry garlic until pale golden; remove.
  2. Fry nuts and sesame until lightly toasted; remove.
  3. Turn heat low and briefly fry the dried chiles until fragrant, taking care not to burn them.
  4. Cool slightly, then pulse chiles, garlic, nuts, seeds, vinegar, salt, and enough oil to form a textured sauce.
  5. Store chilled and stir before serving.
