SALAD · PERUVIAN

Ensalada Criolla

A crisp Peruvian onion-and-tomato salad with lime, cilantro, and ají, ideal beside grilled meats and stews.

15 minServes 4

Photo: Comedera

Ingredients

  • 1 large red onion, very thinly sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, cut into wedges
  • 1 ají amarillo or jalapeño, thinly sliced
  • 1/3 cup cilantro leaves
  • Juice of 2 limes
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Kosher salt

Preparation

  1. Soak sliced onion in cold water 10 minutes if you prefer a milder bite; drain well.
  2. Combine onion, tomato, chile, and cilantro.
  3. Add lime juice, olive oil, and salt.
  4. Toss gently and serve immediately.
