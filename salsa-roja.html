SAUCE · MEXICAN

Salsa Roja

A roasted red Mexican salsa with tomatoes, dried chiles, onion, garlic, and cilantro.

30 minMakes about 2 cups

Photo: My Home Made Recipe

Ingredients

  • 5 Roma tomatoes
  • 2 dried guajillo chiles, stemmed and seeded
  • 2 dried chiles de árbol
  • 1/4 white onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1/4 cup cilantro
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tbsp lime juice

Preparation

  1. Toast the dried chiles briefly in a dry skillet until fragrant; do not burn.
  2. Roast tomatoes, onion, and garlic until softened and charred in spots.
  3. Soak the toasted chiles in hot water for 10 minutes.
  4. Blend tomatoes, onion, garlic, softened chiles, cilantro, salt, and lime until smooth or slightly textured.
  5. Heat oil in a skillet and fry the salsa 5 to 8 minutes, stirring, until flavors deepen. Adjust salt and heat.
