Parmesan Broth

A savory broth made from Parmesan rinds, ideal for beans, risotto, pasta, and vegetables.

Broth & StockItalian

Ingredients

  • 6–8 oz Parmesan rinds
  • 1 onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 8 cups water

Preparation

  1. Scrub rinds and cut into manageable pieces.
  2. Combine with onion, garlic, bay, and water.
  3. Barely simmer 1 1/2–2 hours, stirring occasionally to prevent sticking.
  4. Strain while hot.
