Mole Poblano con Pollo

Tender chicken covered in rich mole poblano with dried chiles, nuts, seeds, spices, tomato, and chocolate.

2 hrServes 6

Photo: Mexican Food XP

Ingredients

  • 6 chicken thighs or mixed chicken pieces
  • 4 ancho chiles
  • 3 mulato chiles
  • 2 pasilla chiles
  • 2 Roma tomatoes
  • 1/2 onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1/4 cup almonds
  • 1/4 cup peanuts
  • 2 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 1 corn tortilla
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • Pinch of cloves and anise
  • 3 cups chicken stock
  • 1 oz Mexican chocolate
  • 2 tbsp lard or neutral oil
  • Salt

Preparation

  1. Poach or roast chicken until nearly cooked through; reserve stock if poaching.
  2. Toast and soak dried chiles. Toast nuts, sesame, tortilla, and spices separately.
  3. Roast tomatoes, onion, and garlic.
  4. Blend all mole ingredients with enough stock to make a smooth sauce.
  5. Fry mole paste in lard or oil, then gradually add stock and simmer 45 minutes. Stir in chocolate.
  6. Add chicken and simmer gently until fully cooked and coated. Serve with sesame seeds and rice.
