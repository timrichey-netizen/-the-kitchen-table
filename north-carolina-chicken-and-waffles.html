North Carolina Chicken and Waffles

Crisp Southern fried chicken served over hot buttermilk waffles with butter and maple syrup.

Southern American (North Carolina)North Carolina, United States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb boneless chicken thighs
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • 1 1/2 cups flour
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • salt and pepper
  • frying oil
  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 tbsp baking powder
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 3/4 cups buttermilk
  • 6 tbsp melted butter
  • maple syrup

Preparation

  1. Marinate chicken in buttermilk 1 hour. Dredge in seasoned flour and fry at 350°F until crisp and 165°F inside.
  2. For waffles, whisk flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt. Whisk eggs, buttermilk, and butter separately; combine just until mixed.
  3. Cook in hot waffle iron until crisp.
  4. Place fried chicken over waffles and serve with maple syrup.
