Tennessee Banana Pudding

Classic Southern banana pudding layered with vanilla wafers, sliced bananas, and rich vanilla custard.

Southern American (Tennessee)Tennessee, United States4 hr

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1/3 cup flour
  • pinch salt
  • 3 cups whole milk
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tsp vanilla
  • 4 ripe bananas, sliced
  • 1 box vanilla wafers
  • whipped cream, optional

Preparation

  1. Whisk sugar, flour, and salt in saucepan; gradually whisk in milk. Cook over medium heat until steaming and thickened.
  2. Temper yolks with hot mixture, return to pan, and cook 2 minutes. Remove from heat; add butter and vanilla.
  3. Layer wafers, bananas, and warm custard in serving dish, repeating layers.
  4. Chill at least 3 hours. Top with whipped cream if desired.
