RICE · MEXICAN

Arroz Rojo

Fluffy Mexican red rice toasted in oil and simmered with tomato, onion, garlic, broth, peas, and carrots.

40 minServes 6

Photo: Eureka Recetas

Ingredients

  • 2 cups long-grain white rice
  • 3 tbsp neutral oil
  • 4 Roma tomatoes
  • 1/4 white onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 3 cups chicken or vegetable broth
  • 1/2 cup peas
  • 1 small carrot, diced
  • 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt

Preparation

  1. Rinse rice and drain thoroughly.
  2. Toast rice in oil until lightly golden.
  3. Blend tomatoes, onion, garlic, and 1 cup broth until smooth.
  4. Pour tomato mixture over rice and cook 2 minutes.
  5. Add remaining broth, peas, carrot, and salt; cover and simmer on low until tender.
  6. Rest 10 minutes, then fluff.
