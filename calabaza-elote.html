SQUASH · MEXICAN

Calabaza con Elote

Mexican squash and corn sautéed with tomato, peppers, onion, and herbs.

30 minServes 6

Photo: Hogarmania

Ingredients

  • 4 zucchini or Mexican squash, diced
  • 2 cups corn kernels
  • 1/2 onion, diced
  • 2 Roma tomatoes, diced
  • 1 poblano or bell pepper, diced
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1/2 tsp oregano
  • Salt and pepper

Preparation

  1. Cook onion and pepper in oil until soft.
  2. Add squash and corn and sauté 5 minutes.
  3. Add tomatoes, oregano, salt, and pepper.
  4. Cook until squash is tender but still holds shape.
