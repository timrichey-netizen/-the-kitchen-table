Zucchini Risotto with Shrimp

Creamy risotto with tender zucchini, sautéed shrimp, lemon, and herbs.

45 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups Arborio rice
  • 1 lb shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 2 medium zucchini, diced
  • 1 shallot, minced
  • 4 cups warm seafood or chicken stock
  • ½ cup dry white wine
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • ½ cup grated Parmigiano-Reggiano
  • 1 lemon
  • Parsley
  • Salt and pepper

Preparation

  1. Sauté shrimp briefly in olive oil until just cooked; remove.
  2. Cook shallot and zucchini until tender. Add rice and toast 2 minutes.
  3. Add wine and cook until absorbed, then add warm stock gradually while stirring.
  4. When rice is creamy and al dente, fold in butter, Parmigiano, lemon zest, and parsley.
  5. Return shrimp to the risotto and serve immediately.
