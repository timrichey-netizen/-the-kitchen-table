CORN · MEXICAN

Esquites

Mexican street-corn cups with sautéed kernels, crema, mayonnaise, cotija, chile, cilantro, and lime.

30 minServes 6

Photo: Pinterest

Ingredients

  • 5 cups corn kernels
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 jalapeño, minced
  • 1/3 cup mayonnaise
  • 1/3 cup Mexican crema
  • 1 cup cotija
  • 1 tsp chile powder
  • 1/2 cup cilantro
  • Juice of 2 limes
  • Salt

Preparation

  1. Cook corn in butter over medium-high heat until lightly charred.
  2. Add jalapeño and cook 1 minute.
  3. Divide into cups.
  4. Top with mayonnaise, crema, cotija, chile powder, cilantro, lime, and salt.
  5. Serve warm.
