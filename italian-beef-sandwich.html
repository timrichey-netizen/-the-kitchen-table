American (Illinois)

Italian Beef Sandwich

A Illinois main centered on roast beef, italian rolls, giardiniera, beef jus, prepared in its recognizable Midwestern regional style.

American (Illinois)Illinois, United States45 min

Ingredients

  • 3 lb beef chuck or top round
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp Italian seasoning
  • salt and pepper
  • 4 cups beef stock
  • 8 Italian rolls
  • 1 cup giardiniera
  • sweet peppers, optional

Preparation

  1. Season beef and roast at 325°F until tender enough to slice thinly; cool slightly and slice paper-thin.
  2. Simmer stock with roasting juices, garlic, and Italian seasoning to make jus.
  3. Warm sliced beef gently in jus without boiling.
  4. Pile beef into sturdy Italian rolls, spoon over jus or dip the sandwich, and top with giardiniera and optional sweet peppers.
