PASTA · ROMAN

Spaghetti Carbonara

Traditional Roman carbonara with guanciale, egg, Pecorino Romano, and black pepper—no cream.

30 minServes 4

PASTA GUIDE

Recommended pasta: Spaghetti or rigatoni

See pasta shapes, sauces & pairings →

Ingredients

  • 12 oz spaghetti
  • 5 oz guanciale, cut into lardons
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 1 whole egg
  • 1 cup finely grated Pecorino Romano
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • Kosher salt

Preparation

  1. Cook spaghetti until al dente, reserving 1 cup pasta water.
  2. Cook guanciale over medium-low heat until crisp and rendered; remove from heat.
  3. Whisk yolks, egg, Pecorino, and black pepper.
  4. Toss hot pasta with guanciale, then off heat stir in egg mixture, loosening with pasta water until glossy and creamy.
Pasta-water technique: Before draining, reserve at least 1 cup of the hot, starchy cooking water. Add it to the sauce a tablespoon or splash at a time while tossing the pasta. The starch helps emulsify fat, cheese, and sauce so it clings to the pasta and becomes glossy rather than watery.
