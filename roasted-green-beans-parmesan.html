Roasted Green Beans with Parmesan

Crisp-edged green beans roasted with garlic and finished with finely grated Parmesan.

25 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb green beans, trimmed
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, finely grated
  • ½ cup finely grated Parmesan
  • Salt and black pepper
  • ½ tsp lemon zest, optional

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 450°F.
  2. Toss green beans with olive oil, garlic, salt, and pepper.
  3. Roast 14–18 minutes until blistered and crisp-tender.
  4. Transfer to a platter and immediately shower with Parmesan and optional lemon zest.
