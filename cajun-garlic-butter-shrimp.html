Cajun Garlic Butter Shrimp

Juicy shrimp seared with Cajun seasoning and finished in garlicky browned butter and lemon.

20 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb large shrimp
  • 2 tsp Cajun seasoning
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 5 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 lemon
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • Salt and black pepper

Preparation

  1. Pat shrimp dry and season with Cajun seasoning.
  2. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high and sear shrimp 1–2 minutes per side; remove.
  3. Lower heat and melt butter. Add garlic and cook until fragrant and lightly golden.
  4. Return shrimp to the skillet, add lemon juice and parsley, and toss until coated.
  5. Serve immediately with extra lemon.
