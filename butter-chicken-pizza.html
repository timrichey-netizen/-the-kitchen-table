Canadian Fusion

Butter Chicken Pizza

A Ontario, Canada main centered on pizza dough, butter chicken, mozzarella, cilantro, prepared in its recognizable Canadian regional style.

Canadian FusionOntario, Canada45 min

Ingredients

  • 1 large pizza dough ball
  • 2 cups cooked butter chicken with thick sauce
  • 8 oz mozzarella, shredded
  • 1/4 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp chopped cilantro
  • 1 tbsp oil

Preparation

  1. Heat oven with pizza stone or steel to 500°F for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Stretch dough into a 12–14-inch round. Spread a thin layer of butter chicken sauce over dough and distribute chicken pieces evenly.
  3. Top with mozzarella and red onion.
  4. Bake 7–10 minutes until crust is browned and cheese bubbles.
  5. Finish with cilantro and serve hot.
