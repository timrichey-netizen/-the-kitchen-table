APPETIZER · PERUVIAN

Tamal Peruano

Peruvian corn tamales seasoned with ají panca, filled with pork or chicken, olives, egg, and wrapped in banana leaves.

2 hrMakes 10–12

Photo: Eat Peru

Ingredients

  • 4 cups fresh corn kernels or prepared masa
  • 1/2 cup lard or neutral oil
  • 3 tbsp ají panca paste
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 2 cups chicken stock
  • 1 1/2 lb cooked pork or chicken, cut into pieces
  • Black olives
  • 2 hard-boiled eggs, sliced
  • Roasted peanuts, optional
  • Banana leaves or corn husks
  • Salt and pepper

Preparation

  1. Cook onion in lard or oil until soft; add garlic, ají panca, and cumin.
  2. Stir in corn or masa and enough stock to make a thick spoonable mixture. Cook until it pulls slightly from the pan.
  3. Lay out softened banana leaves or soaked husks and spoon masa in the center.
  4. Add meat, olive, egg, and peanut if using. Cover with more masa and wrap securely.
  5. Steam 60 to 75 minutes until firm and aromatic.
  6. Rest 10 minutes before opening and serving.
