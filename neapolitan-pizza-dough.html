Italian (Neapolitan)

Neapolitan Pizza Dough

A traditional Neapolitan-style dough with flour, water, salt, and a tiny amount of yeast, fermented slowly for a tender, extensible crust with a puffy cornicione.

Italian (Neapolitan)Naples, Campania, Italy24 hr

Ingredients

  • For 4 dough balls (about 250 g each):
  • 620 g Italian 00 pizza flour
  • 385 g cool water (62% hydration)
  • 18 g fine sea salt
  • 0.8 g instant dry yeast

Preparation

  1. Dissolve the salt in most of the water. Mix the yeast into the flour. Add flour gradually to the salted water, mixing until a shaggy dough forms; add the remaining water as needed.
  2. Knead 10–15 minutes until smooth, elastic, and moderately strong, keeping the dough cool.
  3. Cover and rest at room temperature 2 hours.
  4. Divide into four approximately 250 g portions and shape into tight balls without tearing the surface.
  5. Place in covered dough boxes or containers and ferment 18–24 hours at cool room temperature, about 65–70°F (18–21°C), until relaxed and well aerated. Alternatively refrigerate after an initial rise and bring to room temperature before use.
  6. To shape, place a dough ball on lightly floured surface. Press from the center outward, leaving a 1/2-inch rim untouched. Stretch gently by hand to about 10–12 inches; do not use a rolling pin.
  7. Top sparingly and bake on a fully heated pizza stone/steel at the oven’s highest setting, ideally 550°F (290°C) or hotter, until the rim is puffed and spotted. In a dedicated pizza oven, bake near 800–900°F (425–480°C) for roughly 60–90 seconds.
