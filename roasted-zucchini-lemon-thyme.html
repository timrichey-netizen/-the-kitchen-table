Roasted Zucchini with Lemon and Thyme

Golden roasted zucchini finished with lemon zest, fresh thyme, and olive oil.

30 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 4 medium zucchini, cut into spears
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tbsp fresh thyme leaves
  • 1 garlic clove, finely grated
  • Salt and black pepper

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 450°F.
  2. Toss zucchini with olive oil, garlic, thyme, salt, and pepper.
  3. Arrange cut-side down on a sheet pan and roast 18–22 minutes until browned.
  4. Finish with lemon zest and a squeeze of lemon juice.
