POBLANO · MEXICAN

Rajas con Crema

Roasted poblano strips with onion, corn, crema, and melting cheese.

35 minServes 6

Photo: Recetas Mexas

Ingredients

  • 6 poblano peppers
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 white onion, sliced
  • 1 cup corn kernels
  • 1 cup Mexican crema
  • 6 oz Oaxaca or Monterey Jack cheese
  • Salt and pepper

Preparation

  1. Char poblanos, steam, peel, seed, and slice into strips.
  2. Cook onion in butter until soft.
  3. Add rajas and corn and cook 3 minutes.
  4. Stir in crema and simmer gently.
  5. Add cheese and cook until melted; season and serve.
