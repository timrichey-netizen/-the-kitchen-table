Matambre Arrollado

Rolled beef layered with vegetables, spinach, herbs, and hard-boiled egg, gently cooked and sliced into colorful rounds.

2½ hr + chillServes 6–8

Ingredients

  • 2½–3 lb flank steak or matambre, butterflied and pounded thin
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • Kosher salt and black pepper
  • 1 red bell pepper, cut into strips
  • 2 carrots, cut into batons
  • 4 hard-boiled eggs, quartered lengthwise
  • 2 cups spinach leaves
  • 1 tsp dried oregano

Preparation

  1. Lay the meat flat and season with garlic, parsley, salt, pepper, and oregano.
  2. Arrange the bell pepper, carrots, eggs, and spinach in rows over the meat.
  3. Roll tightly from a long side and tie securely with kitchen twine.
  4. Simmer gently in salted water or beef stock for 1½–2 hours.
  5. Cool under a light weight, then refrigerate until firm.
  6. Remove the twine, slice into rounds, and serve chilled or at room temperature.
