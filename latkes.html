American (New York)

Latkes

A New York side preparation centered on potatoes, onion, eggs, presented in its recognizable regional style.

American (New York)New York, United States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 2 lb russet potatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/4 cup matzo meal or flour
  • 1 1/2 tsp salt
  • black pepper
  • frying oil
  • applesauce and sour cream for serving

Preparation

  1. Grate potatoes and onion; squeeze extremely dry.
  2. Mix with eggs, matzo meal, salt, and pepper.
  3. Fry flattened spoonfuls in 1/4 inch hot oil until crisp and deeply golden, 3–4 minutes per side.
  4. Drain and serve hot with applesauce and sour cream.
