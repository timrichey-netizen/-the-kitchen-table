Cocadas

Chewy golden Mexican coconut sweets with crisp toasted edges.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups shredded coconut
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 3 egg whites
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • Pinch salt

Method

  1. Mix coconut, sugar, egg whites, vanilla, and salt.
  2. Scoop compact mounds onto a lined baking sheet.
  3. Bake at 325°F until golden at the edges.
  4. Cool completely before removing.
