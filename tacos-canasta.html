Tacos de Canasta

Soft steamed basket tacos filled with beans, potatoes, chicharrón, or adobo and bathed in seasoned chile oil.

1 hrMakes 20 tacos

Photo: DiDi Food

Ingredients

  • 20 corn tortillas
  • 2 cups refried beans
  • 2 cups mashed potatoes with chile
  • 2 cups chicharrón in salsa, optional
  • 1/2 cup neutral oil
  • 2 guajillo chiles
  • 1/2 onion, sliced
  • Salt

Preparation

  1. Warm tortillas until pliable.
  2. Fill and fold tortillas with your chosen fillings.
  3. Heat oil with guajillo and onion until fragrant.
  4. Layer tacos tightly in a towel-lined basket, spooning chile oil between layers.
  5. Wrap tightly and let steam 30 minutes before serving.
