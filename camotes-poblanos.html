Camotes Poblanos

Puebla-style sweet potato confections scented with citrus and shaped into soft candies.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb sweet potatoes
  • 1 1/2 cups sugar
  • 1/2 cup water
  • Zest of 1 orange
  • 1 tsp vanilla

Method

  1. Bake or boil sweet potatoes until tender; peel and mash smooth.
  2. Cook sugar and water to a syrup; stir in sweet potato, zest, and vanilla.
  3. Cook, stirring, until thick enough to hold shape.
  4. Cool, shape into small logs, and wrap.
