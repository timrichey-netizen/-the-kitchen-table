Louisiana Creole

Beignets

Pillowy New Orleans yeast-raised beignets fried until golden and buried under powdered sugar.

Louisiana CreoleNew Orleans, Louisiana, United States3 hr

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup warm water
  • 2 1/4 tsp active dry yeast
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 cup evaporated milk
  • 3 1/2 cups flour
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp shortening or butter
  • frying oil
  • 1 1/2 cups powdered sugar

Preparation

  1. Dissolve yeast and a pinch of sugar in warm water for 5–10 minutes.
  2. Mix in sugar, egg, milk, flour, salt, and shortening; knead into a soft dough. Cover and rise until doubled, 1–2 hours.
  3. Roll dough about 1/4 inch thick and cut into 2 1/2-inch squares.
  4. Fry at 360°F until puffed and golden, turning once. Drain briefly and coat heavily with powdered sugar while hot.
