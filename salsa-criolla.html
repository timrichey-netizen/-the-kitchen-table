Salsa Criolla

A bright onion-and-pepper relish with lime, vinegar, cilantro, and a touch of chile.

15 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 1 large red onion, very thinly sliced
  • 1 red bell pepper, thinly sliced
  • 1 small tomato, seeded and sliced
  • ¼ cup chopped cilantro
  • Juice of 2 limes
  • 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • ½ tsp salt
  • Black pepper
  • 1 small chile, thinly sliced, optional

Preparation

  1. Rinse sliced onion briefly under cold water and drain well.
  2. Combine onion, bell pepper, tomato, cilantro, and optional chile.
  3. Whisk lime juice, vinegar, olive oil, salt, and pepper.
  4. Toss with vegetables and let stand 10 minutes before serving.
