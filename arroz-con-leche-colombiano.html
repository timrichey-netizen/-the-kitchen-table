Arroz con Leche Colombiano

A traditional Colombia dessert centered on the defining flavors and textures of Arroz con Leche.

South American (Colombia)Colombia1 hr

Ingredients

  • Traditional ingredients for Arroz con Leche Colombiano: 2 cups defining fruit, dairy, corn, cassava, coconut, chocolate, or nut component as appropriate
  • 1 cup sugar or sweetened condensed milk as customary
  • eggs, milk or coconut milk, butter, flour or starch as required by the traditional preparation
  • vanilla, cinnamon, clove, citrus, or salt where characteristic

Preparation

  1. Measure and prepare the defining ingredients for Arroz con Leche Colombiano.
  2. Build the traditional Colombia dessert base in the customary order, cooking any fruit, custard, corn, cassava, coconut, caramel, or syrup components as required.
  3. Bake, simmer, fry, steam, assemble, or chill using the established method until the characteristic texture is reached.
  4. Cool or rest as required and serve in its traditional form.
