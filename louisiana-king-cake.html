Louisiana Creole

King Cake

New Orleans Mardi Gras king cake made from enriched cinnamon-filled yeast dough and finished with glaze and purple, green, and gold sugar.

Louisiana CreoleNew Orleans, Louisiana, United States4 hr

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup warm milk
  • 2 1/4 tsp yeast
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 3 1/2 cups flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 eggs
  • 6 tbsp butter
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 2 tsp cinnamon
  • 2 tbsp melted butter
  • 1 cup powdered sugar
  • 2 tbsp milk
  • purple, green, and gold sanding sugar

Preparation

  1. Bloom yeast in warm milk. Mix with sugar, flour, salt, eggs, and butter; knead until smooth. Rise until doubled, 1–2 hours.
  2. Roll into a rectangle, brush with melted butter, and sprinkle with brown sugar and cinnamon. Roll into a log and shape into an oval ring, sealing ends.
  3. Rise 45–60 minutes. Bake at 350°F for 25–30 minutes until golden and cooked through.
  4. Cool, glaze with powdered sugar and milk, then stripe with purple, green, and gold sugar.
