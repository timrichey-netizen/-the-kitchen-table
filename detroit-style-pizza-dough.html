American (Detroit)

Detroit-Style Pizza Dough

A high-hydration Detroit-style dough designed for a rectangular metal pan, yielding an airy interior and crisp, fried bottom beneath caramelized cheese edges.

American (Detroit)Detroit, Michigan, United States4–24 hr

Ingredients

  • For one 10 x 14-inch Detroit-style pan:
  • 300 g bread flour
  • 225 g water (75% hydration)
  • 6 g fine sea salt
  • 3 g instant dry yeast
  • 10 g olive oil, plus oil for the pan

Preparation

  1. Mix flour, yeast, and salt. Add water and 10 g olive oil and stir until a sticky dough forms.
  2. Rest 10 minutes. Stretch and fold the dough several times until it begins to smooth and strengthen.
  3. Cover and rise 1–2 hours at room temperature, or refrigerate 12–24 hours and bring toward room temperature before panning.
  4. Generously oil a 10 x 14-inch dark metal Detroit-style pan. Place dough in the pan, turn to coat, and gently stretch toward the corners.
  5. Rest 15–20 minutes, then stretch again until the dough reaches the corners. Cover and proof until puffy, about 1–2 hours.
  6. Top in Detroit style, bringing cheese all the way to the pan edges when making the finished pizza so it caramelizes against the metal.
  7. Bake at 500–550°F (260–290°C) on a low rack until the bottom is deeply browned and crisp and the edges are caramelized, about 12–16 minutes. Loosen and remove promptly from the pan.
