Tres Leches Cake

A light sponge soaked in three milks and topped with softly whipped cream.

Ingredients

  • 5 eggs, separated
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/3 cup whole milk
  • 1 can evaporated milk
  • 1 can sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • Vanilla and cinnamon

Method

  1. Whip egg whites to soft peaks; gradually beat in sugar, then yolks.
  2. Fold in flour and baking powder, followed by whole milk. Bake at 350°F until springy.
  3. Pierce the cooled cake and slowly pour over evaporated milk, condensed milk, and cream.
  4. Chill at least 4 hours. Finish with whipped cream and cinnamon.
