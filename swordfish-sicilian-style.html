Swordfish Sicilian-Style

Seared swordfish with cherry tomatoes, olives, capers, garlic, lemon, and herbs.

35 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 4 swordfish steaks
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 2 cups cherry tomatoes
  • ½ cup Kalamata olives
  • 2 tbsp capers
  • ½ cup dry white wine
  • 1 lemon
  • Parsley or oregano

Preparation

  1. Season swordfish and sear in olive oil until golden and just cooked through; remove.
  2. Cook garlic briefly, then add tomatoes, olives, and capers.
  3. Add wine and simmer until tomatoes soften and sauce reduces slightly.
  4. Return swordfish to the pan, finish with lemon juice and herbs, and serve.
