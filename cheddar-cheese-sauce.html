Cheddar Cheese Sauce

A Béchamel-based cheddar sauce for vegetables, potatoes, burgers, pasta, and baked dishes.

20 minMakes about 2 cups

Photo: PEKIS Recettes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups prepared Béchamel
  • 1 1/2 cups sharp cheddar, freshly grated
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1/4 tsp smoked paprika, optional
  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter
  • Salt and black pepper

Preparation

  1. Warm the Béchamel over low heat until steaming.
  2. Remove from direct heat and add cheddar gradually, whisking until smooth.
  3. Whisk in Dijon, paprika if using, and butter.
  4. Return to very low heat only if needed; do not boil once the cheese is added.
  5. Season with salt and pepper and serve immediately.
