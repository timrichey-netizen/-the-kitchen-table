Lemon & Tomatillo Salsa Verde

A bright salsa verde of roasted tomatillos, lemon, cilantro, garlic, and green chile.

20 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 8 tomatillos, husked
  • 1 serrano or jalapeño chile
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • ½ cup cilantro
  • Zest and juice of 1 lemon
  • ¼ small white onion
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt

Preparation

  1. Broil tomatillos, chile, garlic, and onion until softened and lightly charred.
  2. Transfer to a blender with cilantro, lemon zest, lemon juice, olive oil, and salt.
  3. Pulse to a chunky salsa rather than a smooth purée.
  4. Taste and adjust salt and lemon before serving.
