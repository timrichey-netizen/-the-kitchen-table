SAUCE · HOLLANDAISE DAUGHTER SAUCE

Dijon

Hollandaise sharpened with Dijon mustard for fish, pork, chicken, eggs, and vegetables.

10 minMakes about 1 1/2 cups

Photo: Für Sie

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups prepared Hollandaise
  • 1 1/2 to 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp whole-grain mustard, optional
  • 1 tsp lemon juice, optional
  • Salt and white pepper

Preparation

  1. Warm the Hollandaise gently over very low heat.
  2. Whisk in Dijon mustard until completely smooth.
  3. Add whole-grain mustard if desired.
  4. Adjust with lemon juice, salt, and white pepper.
  5. Serve immediately.
