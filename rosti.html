POTATO · SWISS

Rösti

Classic Swiss shredded-potato rösti with a deeply crisp golden crust and tender center.

45 minServes 4

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

Ingredients

  • 2 lb Yukon Gold or russet potatoes
  • 3 tbsp clarified butter or unsalted butter
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • ½ tsp freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 small onion, finely grated, optional

Preparation

  1. Parboil the whole potatoes until just barely tender, about 8 to 10 minutes. Cool completely, preferably several hours or overnight.
  2. Peel if desired, then grate the potatoes on the large holes of a box grater.
  3. Toss gently with salt, pepper, and grated onion if using.
  4. Heat half the butter in a 10-inch nonstick or well-seasoned skillet. Add the potatoes and press into an even cake.
  5. Cook over medium heat until the underside is deeply golden, 10 to 15 minutes. Slide onto a plate, invert, add remaining butter, and cook the second side until crisp and golden.
  6. Cut into wedges and serve hot.
