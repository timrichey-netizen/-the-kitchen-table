Seaweed Broth

Clean mineral-rich seaweed broth suitable for soups, noodles, and vegetable dishes.

Broth & StockVegetable

Ingredients

  • 8 cups water
  • 1 oz mixed dried seaweed or kombu
  • 2 dried shiitake, optional
  • 2 scallions

Preparation

  1. Rinse seaweed briefly if sandy.
  2. Soak in cold water 30 minutes.
  3. Warm slowly; remove delicate seaweed before boiling.
  4. Add optional mushrooms and scallions, simmer 10 minutes, and strain.
