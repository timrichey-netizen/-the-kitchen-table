Mushroom Broth

Earthy mushroom broth with dried shiitake for extra umami.

Broth & StockVegetable

Ingredients

  • 1 lb fresh mushrooms
  • 1 oz dried shiitake
  • 1 onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce, optional
  • 2 qt water

Preparation

  1. Brown fresh mushrooms deeply.
  2. Add onion and garlic; cook until softened.
  3. Add shiitake and water; simmer 45 minutes.
  4. Strain, season, and cool.
