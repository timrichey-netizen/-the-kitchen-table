Rigatoni with Pecorino and Crispy Guanciale

Rigatoni tossed in a creamy Pecorino emulsion and topped with deeply crisp guanciale.

30 minServes 4

PASTA GUIDE

Recommended pasta: Rigatoni

See pasta shapes, sauces & pairings →

Ingredients

  • 12 oz rigatoni
  • 5 oz guanciale, diced
  • 1¼ cups Pecorino Romano
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • Salt

Preparation

  1. Render guanciale until crisp; reserve the fat.
  2. Cook rigatoni al dente, reserving pasta water.
  3. Toss pasta in the guanciale fat with pepper and a splash of pasta water.
  4. Off heat add Pecorino until creamy; top with crisp guanciale.
