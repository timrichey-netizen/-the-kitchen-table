Loco Moco

A Hawaiʻi local favorite of white rice topped with hamburger patties, rich brown gravy, and fried eggs.

Hawaiʻi LocalHilo, Hawaiʻi, United States45 min

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb ground beef
  • salt and pepper
  • 4 cups cooked white rice
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 2 cups beef stock
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp soy sauce

Preparation

  1. Form beef into 4 patties and season. Sear in skillet until browned and cooked through; remove.
  2. Melt butter in pan, whisk in flour 1 minute, then gradually whisk in stock, Worcestershire, and soy. Simmer until thickened.
  3. Fry eggs sunny-side up.
  4. Divide rice among plates, top with patties, spoon over gravy, and finish each with an egg.
