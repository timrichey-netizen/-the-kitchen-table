APPETIZER · PERUVIAN

Papa Rellena

Crisp golden potato croquettes filled with seasoned beef, onion, olives, egg, and raisins.

1 hr 15 minMakes 8

Photo: Eat Peru

Ingredients

  • 2 lb starchy potatoes
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp ají panca paste
  • 1/2 tsp cumin
  • 1/3 cup chopped black olives
  • 2 hard-boiled eggs, chopped
  • 2 tbsp raisins, optional
  • Flour for dusting
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • Neutral oil, for frying
  • Salt and pepper

Preparation

  1. Boil potatoes until tender, peel, mash, and mix with butter, egg yolk, and salt. Cool.
  2. Brown beef, then add onion, garlic, ají panca, cumin, olives, egg, and raisins. Cool filling.
  3. Flatten a portion of potato in your hand, add filling, and seal into an oval shape.
  4. Dust lightly with flour and dip in beaten egg.
  5. Fry at 350°F until deeply golden. Drain and serve with salsa criolla.
