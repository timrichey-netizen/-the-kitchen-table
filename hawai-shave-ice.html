Hawaiʻi Shave Ice

Hawaiʻi-style ultra-fine shave ice saturated with colorful fruit syrups, optionally finished with condensed milk.

Hawaiʻi LocalHawaiʻi, United States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 6 cups very finely shaved ice
  • 1/2 cup strawberry syrup
  • 1/2 cup pineapple syrup
  • 1/2 cup lilikoi syrup
  • 1/3 cup sweetened condensed milk, optional

Preparation

  1. Prepare very fine, snow-like shaved ice and pack lightly into cups or bowls.
  2. Pour syrups slowly over separate sections so they penetrate the ice.
  3. Add a light drizzle of condensed milk if desired.
  4. Serve immediately before melting.
