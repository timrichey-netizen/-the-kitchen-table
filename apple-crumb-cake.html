Apple Crumb Cake

Tender cinnamon apple cake topped with a generous buttery brown-sugar crumb.

1 hr 20 minServes 9 to 12

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 3/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 3/4 cup sour cream
  • 2 apples, peeled and diced
  • For crumb: 3/4 cup flour
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 5 tbsp melted butter

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 350°F and grease an 8-inch square pan.
  2. Whisk flour, leaveners, salt, and cinnamon.
  3. Beat butter and sugar until fluffy; beat in eggs and vanilla. Mix in sour cream, then fold in dry ingredients and apples.
  4. Spread batter in pan. Mix crumb ingredients until clumpy and scatter evenly over batter.
  5. Bake 45 to 55 minutes until a tester comes out clean. Cool before slicing.
