Gorditas de Desayuno

Thick masa pockets griddled until golden and filled with beans, eggs, cheese, or chorizo.

45 minMakes 8

Photo: Pinterest

Ingredients

  • 2 cups masa harina
  • 1 1/2 cups warm water
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Refried beans
  • Scrambled eggs
  • Queso fresco
  • Salsa roja or salsa verde

Preparation

  1. Mix masa harina, salt, and water into a soft dough.
  2. Divide into 8 balls and pat into thick discs.
  3. Griddle over medium heat until browned and cooked through.
  4. Split each gordita to form a pocket.
  5. Fill with beans, eggs, cheese, and salsa.
