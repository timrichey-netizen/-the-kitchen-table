Tacos de Mantaraya

Tender shredded ray simmered in a tomato-chile sauce and served with onion, cilantro, lime, and salsa.

50 minMakes 10 tacos

Photo: Muy Delish

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb cleaned skate or ray wing
  • Oil
  • Onion
  • 3 Roma tomatoes
  • Garlic
  • 1 guajillo
  • 1 jalapeño
  • Oregano
  • 10 tortillas
  • Cilantro and lime

Preparation

  1. Poach ray gently until tender and shred.
  2. Toast and soak guajillo.
  3. Blend tomatoes, garlic, guajillo, jalapeño, and oregano.
  4. Cook sauce in oil.
  5. Fold in ray and serve in tortillas.
