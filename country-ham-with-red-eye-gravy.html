Country Ham with Red-Eye Gravy

Pan-seared country ham with classic red-eye gravy made by deglazing the skillet with strong coffee.

Southern American (North Carolina)North Carolina, United States20 min

Ingredients

  • 4 slices country ham
  • 1 tsp butter or ham fat
  • 3/4 cup strong black coffee
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 1 tsp brown sugar, optional

Preparation

  1. If ham is extremely salty, soak briefly in water and pat dry.
  2. Heat skillet with butter or ham fat and sear ham 1–2 minutes per side; remove.
  3. Pour coffee and water into hot skillet and scrape up browned bits. Add sugar if desired.
  4. Simmer 2–3 minutes until slightly reduced. Spoon thin red-eye gravy over ham.
