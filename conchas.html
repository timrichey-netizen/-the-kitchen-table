Conchas

Classic Mexican pan dulce with a tender enriched bun and crisp shell-pattern topping.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups bread flour
  • 2 1/4 tsp yeast
  • 3/4 cup warm milk
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 6 tbsp butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • Topping: 1/2 cup butter, 1/2 cup sugar, 1 cup flour

Method

  1. Knead flour, yeast, milk, sugar, eggs, butter, and vanilla until smooth; rise until doubled.
  2. Divide and shape into buns.
  3. Mix topping ingredients into a paste, flatten over buns, and score a shell pattern.
  4. Proof until puffy; bake at 350°F until lightly golden.
