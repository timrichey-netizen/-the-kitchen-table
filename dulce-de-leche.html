Dulce de Leche

Slow-cooked milk caramel with a deep dairy sweetness and silky texture.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups whole milk
  • 1 1/4 cups sugar
  • 1/4 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp vanilla

Method

  1. Combine milk, sugar, and baking soda in a heavy pot.
  2. Simmer very gently, stirring increasingly often as it darkens.
  3. Cook until thick, glossy, and caramel-colored.
  4. Remove from heat and stir in vanilla; cool.
