BEANS · MEXICAN

Frijoles Charros

Pinto beans simmered with bacon, chorizo, tomato, jalapeño, onion, and cilantro.

1 hrServes 8

Photo: Don Zabor

Ingredients

  • 4 cups cooked pinto beans with broth
  • 6 oz bacon, chopped
  • 6 oz Mexican chorizo
  • 1/2 onion, diced
  • 2 Roma tomatoes, diced
  • 1 jalapeño, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1/2 cup cilantro
  • Salt

Preparation

  1. Cook bacon until crisp; add chorizo and brown.
  2. Add onion, jalapeño, and garlic and cook until soft.
  3. Add tomatoes and cook until juicy.
  4. Stir in beans and enough broth for a soupy consistency.
  5. Simmer 20 minutes and finish with cilantro.
