Georgia Peach Cobbler

Classic Southern cobbler filled with ripe Georgia peaches beneath a buttery golden biscuit topping.

Southern American (Georgia)Georgia, United States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 8 cups sliced ripe peaches
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp cornstarch
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 1/2 cups flour
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 6 tbsp cold butter
  • 3/4 cup buttermilk

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 375°F. Toss peaches with sugar, lemon, cornstarch, and cinnamon; place in baking dish.
  2. Mix flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt; cut in butter. Stir in buttermilk just until combined.
  3. Spoon dough over peaches.
  4. Bake 40–45 minutes until deeply golden and bubbling. Rest 15 minutes before serving.
