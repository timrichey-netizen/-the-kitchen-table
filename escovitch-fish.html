Caribbean (Jamaica)

Escovitch Fish

A Jamaica main built around whole fish, vinegar, scotch bonnet, onion, carrot, prepared in its recognizable regional style.

Caribbean (Jamaica)Jamaica1 hr

Ingredients

  • 2 whole snapper, cleaned
  • salt and pepper
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • frying oil
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 carrot, julienned
  • 1 bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 Scotch bonnet
  • 1 cup white vinegar
  • 1 tsp allspice berries
  • 1 tsp sugar

Preparation

  1. Season fish, dust lightly with flour, and fry until crisp and cooked through.
  2. Sauté onion, carrot, pepper, and Scotch bonnet briefly. Add vinegar, allspice, sugar, and salt; simmer 2 minutes.
  3. Pour hot pickled vegetables and liquid over fried fish and rest 10 minutes before serving.
