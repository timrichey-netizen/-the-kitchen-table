MEXICAN · DESSERTS & DRINKS

Jericalla

Guadalajara-style baked custard with vanilla, cinnamon, and a deeply browned top.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups whole milk
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 6 eggs
  • 1 tbsp vanilla

Method

  1. Heat milk with cinnamon and sugar; cool slightly and remove cinnamon.
  2. Whisk eggs and vanilla, then slowly whisk in warm milk.
  3. Pour into ramekins and bake in a water bath at 350°F until set.
  4. Broil briefly to blister and brown the tops.
