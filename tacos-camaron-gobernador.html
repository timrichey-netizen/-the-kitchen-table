Tacos de Camarón Gobernador

Sinaloa-style shrimp tacos with poblano, onion, tomato, and melted cheese folded into crisped tortillas.

30 minMakes 10 tacos

Photo: Goya

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb shrimp, chopped
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1/2 white onion, diced
  • 1 poblano pepper, diced
  • 2 Roma tomatoes, diced
  • 8 oz Oaxaca or Monterey Jack cheese
  • 10 corn tortillas
  • Salt and pepper
  • Salsa verde or salsa de aguacate

Preparation

  1. Cook onion and poblano in butter until soft.
  2. Add tomatoes and cook until juicy.
  3. Add shrimp and cook just until opaque.
  4. Place filling and cheese on tortillas and fold.
  5. Griddle until tortillas are lightly crisp and cheese is melted.
