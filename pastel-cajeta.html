Pastel de Cajeta

Moist vanilla cake layered and glazed with rich goat-milk cajeta.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 3/4 cup butter
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1 cup cajeta

Method

  1. Whisk flour, baking powder, and salt.
  2. Cream butter and sugar; beat in eggs, then alternate dry ingredients with milk and vanilla.
  3. Bake at 350°F until done and cool completely.
  4. Layer and glaze generously with cajeta.
