Native Hawaiian

Haupia

A traditional Hawaiian coconut pudding set with starch and cut into cool, creamy squares.

Native HawaiianHawaiʻi, United States20 min + chill

Ingredients

  • 2 cups full-fat coconut milk
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1/2 cup cornstarch
  • pinch of salt

Preparation

  1. Lightly grease or wet an 8-inch square pan.
  2. Whisk cornstarch with water until smooth.
  3. Heat coconut milk, sugar, and salt over medium heat until steaming.
  4. Whisk in cornstarch slurry and cook, stirring constantly, until very thick, glossy, and pulling from pan, 3–5 minutes.
  5. Spread evenly in pan. Cool, then refrigerate at least 2 hours until firm. Cut into squares and serve chilled.
