PORK · MEXICAN

Pozole Rojo

A hearty red pozole of hominy and pork simmered with guajillo and ancho chile broth, served with crisp fresh garnishes.

3 hrServes 8

Photo: El Gráfico

Ingredients

  • 3 lb pork shoulder
  • 2 cans (25 oz each) hominy, drained and rinsed
  • 5 guajillo chiles
  • 3 ancho chiles
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1/2 onion
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 8 cups chicken or pork stock
  • 2 bay leaves
  • Salt
  • Shredded cabbage or lettuce
  • Sliced radishes
  • Chopped onion
  • Lime wedges
  • Tostadas

Preparation

  1. Simmer pork in stock with bay leaves until beginning to tenderize.
  2. Toast and soak chiles, then blend with garlic, onion, oregano, cumin, and some broth. Strain.
  3. Add red chile sauce to the pot and simmer until pork is very tender.
  4. Shred pork into bite-size pieces and return to the broth.
  5. Add hominy and simmer 20 to 30 minutes more; adjust salt.
  6. Serve with cabbage, radishes, onion, lime, oregano, and tostadas.
