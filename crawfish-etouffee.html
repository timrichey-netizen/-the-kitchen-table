SEAFOOD · CAJUN / CREOLE

Crawfish Étouffée

Crawfish tails smothered in a buttery, lightly thickened Cajun-Creole sauce with the holy trinity and warm spices.

45 minServes 4–6

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

Ingredients

  • 6 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 1 green bell pepper, diced
  • 2 celery ribs, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 cups seafood or chicken stock
  • 1 tbsp Creole seasoning
  • 1/2 tsp dried thyme
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne, optional
  • 1 1/2 lb Louisiana crawfish tails with fat, if available
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Cooked white rice, for serving

Preparation

  1. Melt butter in a wide saucepan. Stir in flour and cook 3 to 5 minutes to make a blond roux.
  2. Add onion, bell pepper, and celery and cook until tender; add garlic.
  3. Slowly stir in stock, Creole seasoning, thyme, and cayenne. Simmer until lightly thickened.
  4. Fold in crawfish tails and their fat and cook gently for 5 to 7 minutes; do not boil hard.
  5. Stir in scallions and parsley and adjust salt and pepper.
  6. Spoon generously over hot white rice.
