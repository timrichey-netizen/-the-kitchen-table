Crawfish Étouffée

Louisiana crawfish tails smothered in a buttery roux-based sauce with the holy trinity and served over rice.

Louisiana Cajun/CreoleLouisiana, United States45 min

Ingredients

  • 6 tbsp butter
  • 1/4 cup flour
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1/2 green bell pepper, diced
  • 2 celery ribs, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 cups seafood stock
  • 1 lb Louisiana crawfish tail meat with fat
  • 1 tsp Cajun seasoning
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne
  • 2 scallions
  • 2 tbsp parsley
  • cooked rice

Preparation

  1. Melt butter and stir in flour; cook to a peanut-butter-colored roux.
  2. Add onion, bell pepper, and celery and cook 6 minutes; add garlic.
  3. Gradually whisk in stock and simmer until thickened, 10 minutes.
  4. Add crawfish and seasoning; simmer gently 5–8 minutes, avoiding overcooking.
  5. Finish with scallions and parsley and serve over rice.
