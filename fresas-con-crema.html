Fresas con Crema

Fresh strawberries folded into sweet Mexican crema for a fast chilled dessert.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb strawberries, sliced
  • 1 cup Mexican crema
  • 1/3 cup sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • Pinch cinnamon

Method

  1. Whisk crema, condensed milk, vanilla, and cinnamon.
  2. Fold in sliced strawberries.
  3. Chill 20 minutes.
  4. Serve cold, with extra berries on top.
