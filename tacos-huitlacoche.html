HUITLACOCHE · MEXICAN

Tacos de Huitlacoche

Earthy huitlacoche sautéed with onion, garlic, chile, corn, and epazote, finished with cheese in warm tortillas.

30 minMakes 8 tacos

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

Ingredients

  • 1 lb fresh or frozen huitlacoche
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil or butter
  • 1/2 white onion, sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 serrano chile, sliced
  • 1/2 cup corn kernels
  • 2 tbsp chopped epazote
  • 4 oz queso fresco or Oaxaca cheese
  • 8 corn tortillas
  • Salt

Preparation

  1. Cook onion in oil until translucent, then add garlic and serrano.
  2. Add huitlacoche and corn and cook over medium heat until tender and most moisture has evaporated.
  3. Stir in epazote and season with salt.
  4. Warm tortillas and add the huitlacoche mixture.
  5. Top with cheese and serve hot.
