California Club Sandwich

A fresh California-style club sandwich layered with roasted turkey, crisp bacon, avocado, tomato, lettuce, and mayonnaise on toasted bread.

American (California)California, United States20 min

Ingredients

  • For 4 sandwiches:
  • 12 slices sturdy sourdough or country white bread
  • 8 tbsp mayonnaise, divided
  • 12 oz (340 g) sliced roasted turkey breast
  • 8 slices crisp cooked bacon
  • 2 ripe avocados, sliced
  • 2 medium ripe tomatoes, thinly sliced
  • 8 leaves crisp lettuce, such as romaine or green leaf
  • Kosher salt, to taste
  • Freshly ground black pepper, to taste

Preparation

  1. Toast the bread until lightly golden and crisp at the edges but still tender enough to bite through.
  2. Spread one side of each toast slice with mayonnaise.
  3. For each sandwich, place one toast slice mayonnaise-side up on the work surface. Layer with lettuce, sliced tomato, a light seasoning of salt and pepper, and half of the turkey allocated to that sandwich.
  4. Place a second toast slice on top, mayonnaise-side up. Arrange the avocado slices over it, season lightly with salt and pepper, then add 2 strips of crisp bacon and the remaining turkey.
  5. Cap with the third toast slice, mayonnaise-side down. Press gently to stabilize the layers without crushing the avocado.
  6. Secure the sandwich with four sandwich picks if desired, then cut diagonally into quarters in classic club-sandwich fashion.
  7. Serve immediately while the toast remains crisp, with the turkey, bacon, avocado, tomato, and lettuce clearly layered.
