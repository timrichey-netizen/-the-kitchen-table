New Mexican

Biscochitos

New Mexico's anise-scented, lard-enriched shortbread cookie finished with cinnamon sugar.

New MexicanNew Mexico, United States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 3 cups flour
  • 1 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 1/2 tsp ground anise
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 8 oz lard, softened
  • 1/2 cup plus 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp brandy or sweet white wine
  • 1/4 cup sugar plus 3/4 tsp cinnamon for coating

Preparation

  1. Whisk flour, baking powder, anise, and salt.
  2. Beat lard with sugar until very light, about 8 minutes. Beat in egg and brandy.
  3. Mix in dry ingredients just until a stiff dough forms; chill 15 minutes.
  4. Roll 1/4 inch thick and cut.
  5. Bake at 350°F for 10–12 minutes until just pale golden.
  6. While warm, coat tops in cinnamon sugar and cool.
