Zurich Style Veal in Cream Sauce

A cream-forward Zurich-style veal sauté with white wine, shallots, a touch of lemon, and optional mushrooms.

35 minServes 4

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb veal cutlets, cut into narrow strips
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 small shallot, finely minced
  • ½ cup dry white wine
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • ¼ cup veal or chicken stock
  • 4 oz mushrooms, sliced, optional
  • 1 tsp lemon zest
  • 1 tbsp chopped parsley
  • Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper

Preparation

  1. Season the veal with salt and pepper. Heat oil and 1 tablespoon butter over high heat and sear the veal in batches. Remove to a plate.
  2. Reduce heat to medium. Add remaining butter, shallot, and mushrooms if using; cook until softened.
  3. Add white wine and reduce until nearly syrupy.
  4. Add stock and cream and simmer until the sauce coats the back of a spoon.
  5. Return the veal briefly, add lemon zest, and adjust seasoning. Finish with parsley and serve immediately.
