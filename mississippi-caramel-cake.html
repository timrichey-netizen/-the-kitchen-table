Mississippi Caramel Cake

Tender Southern layer cake covered in old-fashioned cooked caramel icing.

Southern American (Mississippi)Mississippi, United States2 hr

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cake flour
  • 2 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 cup butter
  • 2 cups sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 tsp vanilla
  • 1 cup butter for icing
  • 2 cups brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup evaporated milk
  • 3 cups powdered sugar

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 350°F. Cream butter and sugar; beat in eggs. Alternate flour mixture with milk; add vanilla. Divide between three 9-inch pans and bake 22–27 minutes. Cool.
  2. For icing, melt butter with brown sugar and evaporated milk; boil gently 2 minutes. Cool slightly, then beat in powdered sugar until spreadable.
  3. Quickly frost between layers and over cake before icing sets.
