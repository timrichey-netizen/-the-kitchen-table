Pasta Aglio e Olio

A simple classic of spaghetti, garlic, olive oil, chile, parsley, and pasta water.

20 minServes 4

PASTA GUIDE

Recommended pasta: Spaghetti

See pasta shapes, sauces & pairings →

Ingredients

  • 12 oz spaghetti
  • ⅓ cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • 6 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • ½ tsp red-pepper flakes
  • ½ cup chopped parsley
  • Salt
  • Reserved pasta water

Preparation

  1. Cook spaghetti until al dente, reserving 1 cup pasta water.
  2. Gently cook garlic in olive oil until pale golden, then add red-pepper flakes.
  3. Add spaghetti and a splash of pasta water and toss vigorously until glossy.
  4. Finish with parsley and serve immediately.
Pasta-water technique: Before draining, reserve at least 1 cup of the hot, starchy cooking water. Add it to the sauce a tablespoon or splash at a time while tossing the pasta. The starch helps emulsify fat, cheese, and sauce so it clings to the pasta and becomes glossy rather than watery.
