CHICKEN · MEXICAN

Tacos de Tinga

Smoky shredded chicken tinga with tomato, onion, and chipotle served in warm corn tortillas.

45 minMakes 12 tacos

Photo: Mexico in My Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 3 cups prepared tinga de pollo
  • 12 corn tortillas
  • Shredded lettuce or cabbage, optional
  • Sliced avocado
  • Mexican crema
  • Queso fresco
  • Sliced onion
  • Lime wedges

Preparation

  1. Warm the prepared tinga gently until hot and juicy.
  2. Warm corn tortillas on a dry skillet.
  3. Fill each tortilla with tinga.
  4. Top with avocado, crema, queso fresco, and onion if desired.
  5. Serve with lime.
