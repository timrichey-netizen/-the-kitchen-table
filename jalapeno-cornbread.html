Jalapeño Cornbread

A regionally rooted jalapeño cornbread preparation highlighting the ingredients and cooking style associated with the named place.

Texan / Tex-MexTexas, United States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb primary vegetable, grain, or bean named in the dish
  • 2 tbsp cooking fat
  • 1 small onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • salt and black pepper
  • regional seasoning as appropriate

Preparation

  1. Clean and prepare the primary ingredient.
  2. Heat cooking fat and soften aromatics.
  3. Add the primary ingredient and season appropriately.
  4. Cook using the traditional method until tender and properly textured.
  5. Adjust seasoning and serve at the temperature customary for the dish.
