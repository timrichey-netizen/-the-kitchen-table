Lowcountry Crab Rice

Lowcountry rice enriched with sweet crabmeat, bacon, onion, celery, and bell pepper, reflecting the region's rice-and-seafood tradition.

Lowcountry / Gullah GeecheeSouth Carolina Lowcountry, United States40 min

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups Carolina Gold or long-grain rice
  • 3 cups seafood or chicken stock
  • 4 slices bacon, diced
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 1 celery rib, diced
  • 1/2 green bell pepper, diced
  • 1 lb lump crabmeat
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • salt

Preparation

  1. Cook rice in stock until tender; keep warm.
  2. Crisp bacon in a wide skillet. Add onion, celery, and bell pepper and cook until softened.
  3. Fold in rice and season with pepper and salt.
  4. Gently fold in crabmeat without breaking the lumps and warm through for 2–3 minutes.
  5. Finish with scallions and serve immediately.
