Caribbean (Jamaica)

Oxtail Stew

A Jamaica main built around oxtail, butter beans, thyme, allspice, prepared in its recognizable regional style.

Caribbean (Jamaica)Jamaica1 hr

Ingredients

  • 4 lb oxtails
  • 1 onion
  • 3 scallions
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 1 tsp allspice
  • 2 tbsp browning sauce
  • 1 Scotch bonnet
  • 4 cups beef stock
  • 1 can butter beans
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • salt and pepper

Preparation

  1. Season oxtails with aromatics, thyme, allspice, browning sauce, salt, and pepper. Brown deeply in oil.
  2. Add stock and Scotch bonnet; cover and braise at a gentle simmer 2 1/2–3 hours until tender.
  3. Add butter beans for final 15 minutes and reduce sauce until glossy.
