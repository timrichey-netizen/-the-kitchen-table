Tacos de Mole

Tender shredded chicken coated in mole poblano or mole negro and tucked into warm corn tortillas.

35 minMakes 12 tacos

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

Ingredients

  • 3 cups shredded cooked chicken
  • 2 cups mole poblano or mole negro
  • 1/2 cup chicken stock, as needed
  • 12 corn tortillas
  • Sesame seeds
  • Crumbled queso fresco, optional
  • Pickled onion, optional

Preparation

  1. Warm mole gently, thinning with stock as needed.
  2. Fold in shredded chicken and simmer until coated and hot.
  3. Warm tortillas.
  4. Fill with mole-coated chicken.
  5. Finish with sesame seeds and optional queso or pickled onion.
