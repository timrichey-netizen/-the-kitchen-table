BEEF · MEXICAN

Tacos de Labio

Slow-braised beef lip cooked until exceptionally tender, then chopped for tacos with onion, cilantro, lime, and salsa.

4 hrMakes 14 tacos

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

Ingredients

  • 3 lb cleaned beef lip
  • 1 white onion, quartered
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 3 cups beef stock
  • 14 corn tortillas
  • Chopped onion, cilantro, lime, salsa

Preparation

  1. Rinse and trim the beef lip well.
  2. Place in a Dutch oven with onion, garlic, bay, cumin, oregano, salt, and stock.
  3. Cover and braise at 300°F for 3 to 3 1/2 hours until very tender.
  4. Rest briefly, then chop finely and moisten with a little braising liquid.
  5. Serve in warm tortillas with onion, cilantro, lime, and salsa.
