Tacos de Barbacoa

Tender barbacoa piled into corn tortillas with onion, cilantro, lime, and salsa.

20 min if barbacoa preparedMakes 12 tacos

Photo: Fine Dining Lovers

Ingredients

  • 3 cups prepared barbacoa
  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 1/2 white onion, chopped
  • 1/2 cup cilantro
  • Lime wedges
  • Salsa borracha or salsa verde
  • Consomé, optional

Preparation

  1. Warm barbacoa gently with a little of its cooking liquid.
  2. Warm tortillas.
  3. Fill generously with barbacoa.
  4. Top with onion and cilantro.
  5. Serve with lime, salsa, and consomé if desired.
