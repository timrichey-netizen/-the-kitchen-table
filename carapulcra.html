PORK · PERUVIAN

Carapulcra

An Afro-Peruvian and Andean stew of dried potatoes, pork, ají panca, peanuts, garlic, and warm spices.

2 hr 15 minServes 6

Photo: Los Fogones de Pistachulín

Ingredients

  • 1 lb dried potato (papa seca)
  • 2 lb pork shoulder, cut into chunks
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 3 tbsp ají panca paste
  • 1 tbsp ají amarillo paste
  • 4 cups pork or chicken stock
  • 1/2 cup roasted peanuts, ground
  • 1/2 tsp cumin
  • Pinch of cinnamon
  • 2 cloves, ground
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Cooked white rice and salsa criolla, for serving

Preparation

  1. Rinse dried potatoes and toast lightly in a dry pot until nutty; cover with water and soak 20 minutes, then drain.
  2. Brown pork in oil and remove.
  3. Cook onion until soft; add garlic, ají panca, ají amarillo, cumin, cinnamon, and clove.
  4. Return pork, add potatoes and stock, and simmer gently until pork and potatoes are tender.
  5. Stir in ground peanuts and cook until the stew thickens.
  6. Season and serve with rice and salsa criolla.
