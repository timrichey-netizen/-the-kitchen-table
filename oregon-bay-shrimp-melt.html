Oregon Bay Shrimp Melt

An open-faced Oregon shrimp melt with sweet pink bay shrimp, creamy dressing, and melted cheese on toasted bread.

American (Oregon Coast)Oregon Coast, United States20 min

Ingredients

  • 12 oz cooked Oregon pink bay shrimp, drained
  • 1/3 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp chopped chives
  • black pepper
  • 4 thick bread slices
  • 4 oz sharp cheddar, shredded

Preparation

  1. Heat broiler.
  2. Fold shrimp with mayonnaise, lemon juice, chives, and pepper.
  3. Lightly toast bread.
  4. Divide shrimp mixture over toast and top with cheddar.
  5. Broil until cheese bubbles and browns lightly, 2–4 minutes. Serve immediately.
