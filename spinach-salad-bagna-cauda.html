Spinach Salad with Bagna Càuda Dressing

Baby spinach with warm anchovy-garlic bagna càuda dressing, radish, and shaved Parmigiano.

20 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 8 oz baby spinach
  • 4 anchovy fillets
  • 4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • ⅓ cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 4 radishes, thinly sliced
  • ½ cup shaved Parmigiano-Reggiano
  • Black pepper

Preparation

  1. Warm olive oil and butter over low heat. Add garlic and cook gently until fragrant.
  2. Add anchovies and mash until they dissolve into the oil.
  3. Remove from heat and stir in lemon juice and black pepper.
  4. Place spinach and radishes in a large bowl and toss with enough warm dressing to lightly wilt the leaves.
  5. Finish with shaved Parmigiano and serve immediately.
