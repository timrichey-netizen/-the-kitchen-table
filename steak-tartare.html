BEEF · FRENCH

Steak Tartare

Classic French-style hand-cut beef tartare with shallot, capers, cornichons, Dijon, herbs, and egg yolk.

25 minServes 4 as an appetizer

Ingredients

  • 12 oz very fresh beef tenderloin or top sirloin, well chilled
  • 1 small shallot, very finely minced
  • 2 tbsp cornichons, finely diced
  • 1 tbsp capers, rinsed and chopped
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 tbsp chopped flat-leaf parsley
  • 1 tbsp chopped chives
  • Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
  • 4 pasteurized egg yolks, optional
  • Toasted baguette or Belgian fries, for serving

Preparation

  1. Keep the beef very cold. Trim away all sinew and fat, then cut into small even dice with a sharp knife.
  2. In a chilled bowl, combine shallot, cornichons, capers, Dijon, Worcestershire, olive oil, parsley, and chives.
  3. Fold in the beef gently. Season with salt and pepper and adjust mustard or acidity to taste.
  4. Shape into four portions. Top each with a pasteurized egg yolk if using.
  5. Serve immediately with toasted baguette or fries. Keep refrigerated until the moment of serving.

Notes

Raw or undercooked meat and eggs can carry foodborne-illness risk. Use very fresh beef from a trusted source, keep it cold, and use pasteurized egg yolks when possible.
