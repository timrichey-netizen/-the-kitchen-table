SAUCE · MEXICAN

Adobo

A red Mexican adobo of dried chiles, garlic, vinegar, oregano, cumin, and warm spices for marinating or braising meat.

40 minMakes about 2 cups

Photo: Maricruz Avalos

Ingredients

  • 4 guajillo chiles
  • 2 ancho chiles
  • 2 pasilla chiles
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1/4 onion
  • 1/3 cup apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tsp Mexican oregano
  • 1/2 tsp ground cumin
  • 4 black peppercorns
  • 2 cloves
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 cup water or stock
  • 1 tsp kosher salt

Preparation

  1. Toast chiles briefly, seed them, and soak in hot water until soft.
  2. Toast cumin, peppercorns, and cloves until fragrant.
  3. Blend softened chiles with garlic, onion, vinegar, spices, oregano, water or stock, and salt.
  4. Strain if a very smooth sauce is desired.
  5. Simmer 10 to 15 minutes, then cool before using as a marinade or braising sauce.
