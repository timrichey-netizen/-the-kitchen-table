Southern American (North Carolina)

Sun Drop Cake

Citrusy North Carolina soda cake flavored with Sun Drop and finished with a bright lemon-lime glaze.

Southern American (North Carolina)North Carolina, United States1 hr 15 min

Ingredients

  • 3 cups flour
  • 2 1/2 cups sugar
  • 1 cup butter
  • 5 eggs
  • 1 cup Sun Drop citrus soda
  • 1 tsp lemon zest
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 2 cups powdered sugar
  • 3 tbsp Sun Drop
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 325°F and grease a Bundt pan. Cream butter and sugar until fluffy; beat in eggs one at a time.
  2. Add flour alternately with 1 cup Sun Drop; mix in zest and vanilla.
  3. Bake 55–65 minutes until a tester comes out clean. Cool 15 minutes, then invert.
  4. Whisk powdered sugar with 3 tbsp Sun Drop and lemon juice and drizzle over cooled cake.
