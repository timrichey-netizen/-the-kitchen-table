Turkey Broth

A rich broth that makes excellent use of a roasted turkey carcass.

Broth & StockPoultry

Ingredients

  • 1 turkey carcass
  • 1 onion
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 celery ribs
  • 2 bay leaves
  • Parsley stems
  • 12 peppercorns
  • 4 qt water

Preparation

  1. Break carcass into a stockpot and add vegetables and aromatics.
  2. Cover with cold water and bring slowly to a simmer.
  3. Simmer 3–4 hours without hard boiling.
  4. Strain and chill promptly.
