BREAKFAST · MEXICAN

Migas Mexicanas

Crisp tortilla strips scrambled with eggs, tomato, onion, chile, and cheese.

25 minServes 4

Photo: DiDi Food

Ingredients

  • 6 corn tortillas, cut into strips
  • 8 eggs
  • 1/2 onion, diced
  • 2 Roma tomatoes, diced
  • 1 jalapeño, minced
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1/2 cup queso fresco
  • Salt and pepper

Preparation

  1. Fry tortilla strips until crisp; remove.
  2. Cook onion and jalapeño in the skillet.
  3. Add tomato and cook briefly.
  4. Add beaten eggs and scramble softly.
  5. Fold in tortilla strips and top with queso fresco.
