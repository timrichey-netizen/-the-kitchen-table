CHICKEN · MEXICAN

Tacos de Pollo Asado

Citrus-and-chile marinated grilled chicken chopped for tacos with onion, cilantro, lime, and salsa.

40 min + marinateMakes 12 tacos

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

Ingredients

  • 2 lb boneless chicken thighs
  • 1/4 cup orange juice
  • 1/4 cup lime juice
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp achiote paste or 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 12 tortillas
  • Onion, cilantro, lime, salsa

Preparation

  1. Whisk citrus, oil, garlic, achiote, cumin, oregano, and salt.
  2. Marinate chicken 2 to 6 hours.
  3. Grill over medium-high heat until charred and fully cooked.
  4. Rest 5 minutes and chop.
  5. Serve in warm tortillas with onion, cilantro, lime, and salsa.
