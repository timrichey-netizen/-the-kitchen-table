Vegetable Broth

Versatile vegetable broth with clean sweetness and no meat.

Broth & StockVegetable

Ingredients

  • 2 onions
  • 3 carrots
  • 3 celery ribs
  • 1 leek
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 tomato
  • Parsley stems
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 3 qt water

Preparation

  1. Roughly chop vegetables.
  2. Sweat in a little oil for 10 minutes without browning.
  3. Add herbs and water; simmer 45–60 minutes.
  4. Strain and cool.
