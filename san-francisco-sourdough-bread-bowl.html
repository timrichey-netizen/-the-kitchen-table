San Francisco Sourdough Bread Bowl

A round San Francisco-style sourdough loaf baked with a crisp crust and sturdy crumb, hollowed into an edible soup bowl.

American (San Francisco)San Francisco, California, United States24 hr

Ingredients

  • 500 g bread flour
  • 350 g water
  • 100 g active 100%-hydration sourdough starter
  • 10 g fine sea salt
  • rice flour for dusting

Preparation

  1. Mix flour and 325 g water; rest 30 minutes. Add starter, salt, and remaining water.
  2. Bulk ferment 4–6 hours, performing 3–4 stretch-and-fold sets during first 2 hours.
  3. Shape into a tight round boule and place seam-up in a floured banneton. Refrigerate 8–16 hours.
  4. Heat covered Dutch oven to 475°F. Score loaf and bake covered 20 minutes, then uncovered at 450°F for 20–25 minutes until deeply browned.
  5. Cool completely. Cut a circular lid from top and pull out enough crumb to form a sturdy bowl, leaving roughly 3/4-inch walls.
