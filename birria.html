BEEF · MEXICAN

Birria

Jalisco-style chile-braised beef served tender in a richly spiced red consommé with onion, cilantro, and lime.

3 hr 30 minServes 8

Photo: Cocinista

Ingredients

  • 4 lb beef chuck or short ribs
  • 5 guajillo chiles
  • 3 ancho chiles
  • 2 pasilla chiles
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 white onion
  • 2 Roma tomatoes
  • 1/3 cup apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp Mexican oregano
  • 1/4 tsp cloves
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 4 cups beef stock
  • 2 bay leaves
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Chopped onion, cilantro, lime, and tortillas

Preparation

  1. Toast and soak dried chiles until softened.
  2. Roast tomatoes, onion, and garlic, then blend with chiles, vinegar, spices, and some stock.
  3. Season and brown beef in a Dutch oven.
  4. Pour chile sauce and remaining stock over beef; add bay leaves.
  5. Cover and braise at 325°F until falling apart, about 3 hours.
  6. Shred beef, skim excess fat if desired, and serve with consommé, onion, cilantro, lime, and tortillas.
