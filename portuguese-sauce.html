Portuguese Sauce

A tomato sauce enriched with onions, garlic, parsley, paprika, and a little stock or wine.

40 minMakes about 3 cups

Photo: Punchfork

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 1/2 cup chicken or vegetable stock
  • 1/4 cup dry white wine, optional
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 1 bay leaf
  • Salt and black pepper

Preparation

  1. Cook onion in olive oil until soft and lightly golden.
  2. Add garlic and paprika and cook briefly.
  3. Add tomatoes, stock, wine if using, and bay leaf.
  4. Simmer gently for 25 to 30 minutes until thickened.
  5. Remove bay leaf, stir in parsley, and season.
