Brownies

Dense, fudgy brownies with a crackly top and deep chocolate flavor.

45 minServes 8

Ingredients

  • 10 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 6 oz bittersweet chocolate
  • 1 cup sugar
  • ½ cup brown sugar
  • 2 large eggs plus 1 yolk
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • ¾ cup flour
  • ¼ cup cocoa powder
  • ½ tsp salt

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 350°F and line an 8-inch square pan.
  2. Melt butter and chocolate together.
  3. Whisk in sugars, then eggs, yolk, and vanilla.
  4. Fold in flour, cocoa, and salt.
  5. Bake 25–30 minutes; cool before slicing.
