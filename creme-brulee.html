DESSERT · FRENCH

Crème Brûlée

Silky vanilla custard beneath a thin, glassy layer of caramelized sugar.

1 hr + chillServes 8

Ingredients

  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 1 vanilla bean or 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 5 large egg yolks
  • ⅓ cup sugar, plus more for brûlée
  • Pinch of salt

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 325°F. Warm cream with vanilla until steaming.
  2. Whisk yolks, sugar, and salt; slowly whisk in warm cream.
  3. Strain into ramekins and bake in a hot-water bath 30–40 minutes until barely set.
  4. Chill at least 4 hours.
  5. Top with a thin layer of sugar and caramelize with a torch.
