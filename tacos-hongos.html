MUSHROOM · MEXICAN

Tacos de Hongos

High-heat sautéed mushrooms with garlic, guajillo, epazote, lime, and avocado in warm tortillas.

30 minMakes 8 tacos

Photo: Infobae

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb mixed mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 guajillo chile, toasted and sliced
  • 1 tbsp chopped epazote
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • 8 corn tortillas
  • Avocado or salsa de aguacate
  • Salt and pepper

Preparation

  1. Dry the mushrooms well and heat a wide skillet until hot.
  2. Cook mushrooms in batches with oil so they brown rather than steam.
  3. Add garlic and guajillo once the mushrooms are browned and most moisture has evaporated.
  4. Stir in epazote, lime, salt, and pepper.
  5. Serve in warm tortillas with avocado or salsa.
