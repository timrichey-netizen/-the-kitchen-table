Belgian French Fries

Classic Belgian-style frites: thick-cut potatoes fried twice for a fluffy center and deeply crisp exterior.

1 hrServes 4

Ingredients

  • 2 lb russet or other floury potatoes
  • Neutral frying oil, such as peanut or canola
  • Kosher salt
  • Mayonnaise, aioli, or your preferred dipping sauce

Preparation

  1. Peel potatoes if desired and cut into thick batons, about 3/8 inch wide. Rinse in cold water until the water runs mostly clear, then dry very thoroughly.
  2. Heat frying oil to 300°F. Fry potatoes in batches for 5 to 7 minutes until tender and pale, not browned.
  3. Drain on a rack or paper towels and let cool at least 20 minutes.
  4. Raise the oil temperature to 375°F. Fry the potatoes again in batches for 2 to 4 minutes until deep golden and crisp.
  5. Drain, salt immediately, and serve hot with mayonnaise or aioli.

Notes

The first fry cooks the interior; the hotter second fry creates the crisp shell.
