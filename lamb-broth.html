Lamb Broth

Fragrant lamb broth with enough body for stews, grains, and sauces.

Broth & StockMeat

Ingredients

  • 3 lb lamb necks or bones
  • 1 onion
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 celery ribs
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 tsp coriander seed
  • 3 qt water

Preparation

  1. Brown lamb bones in a hot oven for a deeper broth.
  2. Add to a pot with vegetables, spices, and cold water.
  3. Simmer gently 3–4 hours, skimming occasionally.
  4. Strain and cool.
