Italian (Roman)

Roman al Taglio Pizza Dough

An exceptionally high-hydration Roman pizza al taglio dough with long fermentation, producing a light, open crumb and crisp base for rectangular tray-baked pizza.

Italian (Roman)Rome, Lazio, Italy24–48 hr

Ingredients

  • For one 13 x 18-inch tray:
  • 500 g strong bread or high-protein pizza flour
  • 400 g cold water (80% hydration)
  • 12 g fine sea salt
  • 2 g instant dry yeast
  • 15 g extra-virgin olive oil, plus more for the tray

Preparation

  1. Mix flour and about 350 g of the water until shaggy. Rest 20 minutes.
  2. Add yeast, then gradually work in the remaining water in small additions, allowing each addition to absorb before adding more. Add salt and finally 15 g olive oil.
  3. Develop strength with repeated stretch-and-folds every 20 minutes for about 1 hour; the dough should become elastic while remaining very soft.
  4. Cover and refrigerate 24–48 hours.
  5. Generously oil a 13 x 18-inch tray. Turn the cold dough onto the tray and gently stretch it with oiled fingertips without crushing its gas bubbles. Rest 30–60 minutes if it resists.
  6. Proof until relaxed, bubbly, and airy, about 1–2 hours depending on temperature.
  7. Top lightly and bake at 500–550°F (260–290°C), ideally on a preheated stone/steel or low rack, until the bottom is crisp and the interior is fully set and open, about 12–18 minutes. Cut into rectangular pieces.
