APPETIZER · PERUVIAN

Causa Rellena

Chilled layers of ají amarillo-seasoned yellow potato filled with chicken or tuna salad and avocado.

1 hr + chillServes 6

Photo: Free Walking Tours Peru

Ingredients

  • 2 lb yellow potatoes or Yukon Gold potatoes
  • 3 tbsp ají amarillo paste
  • Juice of 2 limes
  • 3 tbsp neutral oil
  • Salt
  • 2 cups shredded cooked chicken or drained tuna
  • 1/3 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 ripe avocado, sliced
  • 2 hard-boiled eggs, sliced
  • Black olives, for garnish
  • Lettuce, optional

Preparation

  1. Boil potatoes until tender. Peel and pass through a ricer or mash until smooth.
  2. While still warm, mix potatoes with ají amarillo, lime juice, oil, and salt. Cool completely.
  3. Mix chicken or tuna with mayonnaise and season lightly.
  4. Press half the potato mixture into a mold or dish, add avocado and filling, then top with remaining potato.
  5. Chill at least 30 minutes.
  6. Unmold or slice and garnish with egg, olives, and lettuce.
