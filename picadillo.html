Caribbean (Cuba)

Picadillo

A Cuba main built around ground beef, tomato, olives, raisins, prepared in its recognizable regional style.

Caribbean (Cuba)Cuba1 hr

Ingredients

  • 2 lb ground beef
  • 1 onion
  • 1 green bell pepper
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 1/2 cup green olives
  • 1/3 cup raisins
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • salt and pepper

Preparation

  1. Sauté onion and pepper in oil; add garlic.
  2. Brown beef thoroughly. Stir in tomato sauce, olives, raisins, cumin, oregano, salt, and pepper.
  3. Simmer 20 minutes until thick and savory-sweet.
