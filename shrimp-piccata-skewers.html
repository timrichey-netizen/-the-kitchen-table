Shrimp Piccata Skewers

Grilled shrimp skewers finished with bright lemon-caper piccata butter.

30 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 lemons
  • 3 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 2 tbsp capers, drained
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Skewers

Preparation

  1. Heat a grill or grill pan to medium-high. Toss shrimp with olive oil, half the garlic, salt, pepper, and zest from 1 lemon.
  2. Thread shrimp onto skewers and grill 2–3 minutes per side until just opaque.
  3. Melt butter with remaining garlic and capers; cook 30 seconds, then stir in lemon juice and parsley.
  4. Spoon the warm piccata butter over the shrimp and serve immediately.
