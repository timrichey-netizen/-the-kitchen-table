American Barbecue (North Carolina)

North Carolina Pulled Pork Barbecue

Slow-smoked pork shoulder pulled and seasoned with a sharp North Carolina vinegar-pepper sauce.

American Barbecue (North Carolina)North Carolina, United States10 hr

Ingredients

  • 1 pork shoulder (7–8 lb)
  • 2 tbsp kosher salt
  • 1 tbsp black pepper
  • 2 cups apple cider vinegar
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp crushed red pepper
  • 1 tsp hot sauce

Preparation

  1. Season pork with salt and pepper. Smoke at 250°F over hardwood until bark is dark and internal temperature reaches 195–205°F, about 8–10 hours.
  2. Rest wrapped 45 minutes.
  3. Combine vinegar, sugar, crushed pepper, and hot sauce.
  4. Pull pork into strands, discarding excess fat and bone. Toss with enough vinegar sauce to season thoroughly and serve remaining sauce alongside.
