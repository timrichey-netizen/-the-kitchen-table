American (Maryland)

Maryland Crab Cakes

Classic Maryland crab cakes built around sweet jumbo lump blue crab, lightly bound and seasoned with Old Bay for a crisp exterior and moist, crab-forward center.

American (Maryland)Maryland, Chesapeake Bay region, United States25 min

Ingredients

  • For 6 crab cakes:
  • 1 lb (454 g) jumbo lump Maryland blue crabmeat, picked over carefully for shell
  • 1 large egg, lightly beaten
  • 5 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp prepared Dijon or yellow mustard
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tbsp Old Bay Seasoning or J.O. Crab Seasoning
  • 1 tbsp finely chopped fresh flat-leaf parsley
  • 1/2 cup fine plain breadcrumbs
  • Neutral oil, as needed for frying
  • For serving:
  • Lemon wedges, optional

Preparation

  1. Pick through the jumbo lump crabmeat carefully and remove any shell or cartilage without breaking up the large pieces of crab.
  2. In a large bowl, whisk together the egg, mayonnaise, mustard, Worcestershire sauce, Old Bay or J.O. seasoning, parsley, and breadcrumbs until evenly combined.
  3. Add the crabmeat. Using a rubber spatula or your hands, gently fold the crab into the binder just until combined, taking care to keep the jumbo lumps as intact as possible.
  4. Divide the mixture into 6 portions and gently form each into a compact cake about 3 inches wide. Press only enough to hold the cakes together; do not densely pack them.
  5. Pour enough neutral oil into a heavy skillet to create a shallow layer and heat over medium-high heat to about 350°F (175°C).
  6. Working in batches if necessary, carefully add the crab cakes. Fry until deep golden brown and heated through, about 2–3 minutes per side. Turn them gently so the cakes remain intact. Transfer to a rack or paper-towel-lined plate to drain briefly.
  7. Serve immediately while hot and crisp outside and moist inside, with optional lemon wedges.
