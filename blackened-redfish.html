Louisiana Cajun

Blackened Redfish

Redfish fillets coated with Cajun spices and seared in a smoking-hot cast-iron skillet with butter.

Louisiana CajunLouisiana, United States20 min

Ingredients

  • 4 redfish fillets, 6 oz each
  • 6 tbsp melted butter
  • 2 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1/2 tsp cayenne
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper

Preparation

  1. Ventilate kitchen well and heat a cast-iron skillet until extremely hot.
  2. Combine spices. Dip fish in melted butter and coat generously with seasoning.
  3. Place fillets in dry hot skillet and carefully spoon a little butter over top. Cook about 2 minutes per side until a dark spice crust forms and fish reaches 145°F.
  4. Serve immediately.
