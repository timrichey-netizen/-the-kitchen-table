Spaghetti with Mussels

Spaghetti tossed with mussels, garlic, white wine, parsley, and their briny cooking liquor.

35 minServes 4

PASTA GUIDE

Recommended pasta: Spaghetti or linguine

See pasta shapes, sauces & pairings →

Ingredients

  • 12 oz spaghetti
  • 2 lb mussels
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • ½ tsp red-pepper flakes
  • ½ cup white wine
  • ¼ cup parsley
  • 1 lemon
  • Salt

Preparation

  1. Cook spaghetti al dente, reserving pasta water.
  2. Warm olive oil with garlic and red pepper. Add mussels and wine; cover until opened.
  3. Add pasta with parsley and a splash of pasta water.
  4. Toss until glossy and finish with lemon zest and juice.
