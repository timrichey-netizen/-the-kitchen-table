SAUCE · FRENCH MOTHER SAUCE

Velouté

A silky sauce made by thickening light chicken, veal, or fish stock with a blond roux.

25 minMakes about 2 cups

Photo: Cook the Story

Ingredients

  • 3 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 2½ cups hot light chicken, veal, or fish stock
  • Salt and white pepper

Preparation

  1. Melt butter over medium-low heat.
  2. Whisk in flour and cook to a pale blond roux.
  3. Gradually whisk in hot stock.
  4. Simmer 15–20 minutes until velvety.
  5. Season and strain if desired.
