SAUCE · FRENCH MOTHER SAUCE

Sauce Tomate

Classical French tomato sauce slowly cooked with aromatics and stock for a deep, rounded flavor.

1 hr 30 minMakes about 4 cups

Photo: MasterClass

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp butter or olive oil
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 1 small carrot, diced
  • 1 celery rib, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 can whole peeled tomatoes
  • 1–2 cups light stock
  • Bay leaf and thyme

Preparation

  1. Cook the mirepoix until soft.
  2. Add garlic and tomato paste; cook until fragrant.
  3. Add tomatoes, stock, and herbs.
  4. Simmer gently 60–75 minutes.
  5. Remove herbs; blend or mill if desired and season.
