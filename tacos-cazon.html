Tacos de Cazón

Flaked cazón simmered with tomato, onion, garlic, and chile, then served in corn tortillas.

40 minMakes 10 tacos

Photo: Mexico in My Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb cazón or firm white fish
  • Olive oil
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • 3 Roma tomatoes
  • 1 jalapeño
  • Oregano
  • 10 tortillas
  • Cilantro
  • Lime

Preparation

  1. Poach or steam fish until just cooked and flake it.
  2. Cook onion and garlic in oil.
  3. Add tomato, jalapeño, and oregano.
  4. Fold in fish and warm through.
  5. Serve in tortillas with cilantro and lime.
