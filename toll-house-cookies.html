American (Massachusetts)

Toll House Cookies

A classic Massachusetts dessert featuring chocolate chips, butter, brown sugar, prepared in its recognizable regional style.

American (Massachusetts)Massachusetts, United States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 2 1/4 cups flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 cup butter
  • 3/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 2 cups semisweet chocolate chips
  • 1 cup chopped walnuts, optional

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 375°F. Whisk flour, baking soda, and salt.
  2. Cream butter and sugars; beat in eggs and vanilla. Mix in dry ingredients, then chocolate chips and walnuts if using.
  3. Drop rounded tablespoons on baking sheets.
  4. Bake 9–11 minutes until golden at edges and soft in centers; cool on racks.
