Choclo con Queso

Large-kernel Peruvian corn served simply with slabs of salty queso fresco.

20 minServes 4

Photo: Peru For Less

Ingredients

  • 4 ears Peruvian choclo or large-kernel corn
  • 8 oz queso fresco, cut into thick slices
  • Kosher salt
  • Optional: lime wedges or ají sauce

Preparation

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil.
  2. Add corn and cook until tender, 8 to 12 minutes depending on the variety.
  3. Drain well.
  4. Serve hot or warm with thick slices of queso fresco.
  5. Offer lime or ají sauce alongside if desired.
