Tortillas de Maíz Hechas a Mano

Fresh handmade corn tortillas from masa harina, water, and salt, cooked on a hot comal.

45 minMakes 16 tortillas

Photo: Pinterest

Ingredients

  • 2 cups masa harina
  • 1 1/2 cups warm water, plus more as needed
  • 1/2 tsp salt

Preparation

  1. Mix masa harina and salt.
  2. Add warm water gradually until dough is soft and smooth.
  3. Rest covered 15 minutes.
  4. Divide into 16 balls and press between plastic sheets.
  5. Cook on a hot dry comal about 45 seconds per side, then briefly again until puffed.
  6. Keep wrapped in a towel.
