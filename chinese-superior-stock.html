Chinese Superior Stock

Refined Chinese master stock combining chicken and pork for clear, concentrated savoriness.

Broth & StockChinese

Ingredients

  • 3 lb chicken backs and wings
  • 2 lb pork bones
  • 1 lb Jinhua ham or unsmoked ham, optional
  • 2 inches ginger
  • 4 scallions
  • 5 qt water

Preparation

  1. Blanch chicken and pork bones; rinse thoroughly.
  2. Combine with fresh water and slowly bring to a simmer.
  3. Add ginger, scallions, and optional ham.
  4. Simmer gently 4–5 hours and strain carefully.
