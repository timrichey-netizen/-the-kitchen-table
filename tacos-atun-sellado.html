Tacos de Atún Sellado

Rare-seared tuna sliced for tacos with avocado, cabbage, lime, and bright salsa.

20 minMakes 8 tacos

Photo: Feasting at Home

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb sushi-grade tuna
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and pepper
  • 8 tortillas
  • Cabbage
  • Avocado
  • Cilantro
  • Lime
  • Salsa verde or salsa macha

Preparation

  1. Pat tuna dry and season.
  2. Sear 45–60 seconds per side for a rare center.
  3. Rest 2 minutes and slice.
  4. Serve in tortillas with cabbage, avocado, cilantro, lime, and salsa.
