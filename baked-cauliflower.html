Baked Cauliflower

Tender cauliflower baked under a golden, cheesy breadcrumb crust with herbs.

50 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 1 large head cauliflower, cut into florets
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 1½ cups milk
  • 1 cup grated Gruyère or cheddar
  • ½ cup grated Parmesan
  • ½ cup breadcrumbs
  • 1 tsp thyme leaves
  • Salt and black pepper

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 400°F. Toss cauliflower with olive oil, salt, and pepper and roast 15 minutes.
  2. Melt butter in a saucepan, whisk in flour, then gradually whisk in milk until smooth and thickened.
  3. Stir in Gruyère and half the Parmesan until melted.
  4. Transfer cauliflower to a baking dish, pour over cheese sauce, and top with breadcrumbs, remaining Parmesan, and thyme.
  5. Bake 20–25 minutes until bubbling and deeply golden.
