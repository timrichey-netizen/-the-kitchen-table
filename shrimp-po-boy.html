Shrimp Po’ Boy

Crisp cornmeal-crusted shrimp piled onto French bread with lettuce, tomato, pickles, and tangy remoulade.

40 minMakes 4 sandwiches

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb medium-large shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • 1 cup cornmeal
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tbsp Creole seasoning
  • 1/2 tsp cayenne
  • Neutral oil, for frying
  • 4 New Orleans-style French rolls or crusty sub rolls
  • Shredded lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • Dill pickle slices
  • Remoulade or mayonnaise
  • Lemon wedges

Preparation

  1. Soak the shrimp in buttermilk for 10 to 15 minutes.
  2. Combine cornmeal, flour, Creole seasoning, and cayenne in a shallow dish.
  3. Heat 1 1/2 to 2 inches of oil to 350°F.
  4. Drain shrimp, dredge thoroughly in the cornmeal mixture, and fry in batches until golden and crisp, about 2 to 3 minutes.
  5. Split and lightly toast the bread. Spread with remoulade or mayonnaise.
  6. Pile in fried shrimp, lettuce, tomato, and pickles. Serve with lemon.
