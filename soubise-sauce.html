SAUCE · BÉCHAMEL DAUGHTER SAUCE

Soubise

A delicate Béchamel daughter sauce flavored with slowly cooked onions, then blended until silky.

40 minMakes about 2 cups

Photo: Recetas de Cocina

Ingredients

  • 2 cups prepared Béchamel
  • 2 large yellow onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1/4 cup heavy cream, optional
  • Salt and white pepper
  • Pinch of nutmeg

Preparation

  1. Melt butter over low heat and add the onions with a pinch of salt.
  2. Cook slowly, covered, until very soft and translucent without browning, about 20 to 25 minutes.
  3. Stir the onions into the warm Béchamel.
  4. Blend until completely smooth, then pass through a fine sieve if desired.
  5. Finish with cream if using, then season with white pepper and nutmeg.
