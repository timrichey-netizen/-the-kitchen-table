Mole Poblano

A complex Puebla-style mole with dried chiles, nuts, seeds, spices, tomato, tortilla, and chocolate.

2 hrMakes about 6 cups

Photo: Gastrolab

Ingredients

  • 3 ancho chiles
  • 3 mulato chiles
  • 2 pasilla chiles
  • 2 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 1/4 cup almonds
  • 1/4 cup peanuts
  • 2 Roma tomatoes
  • 1/2 onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 corn tortilla, torn
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp cloves
  • 1/2 tsp anise seed
  • 3 cups chicken stock
  • 1 oz Mexican chocolate
  • 2 tbsp lard or neutral oil
  • Salt, to taste

Preparation

  1. Toast and seed the dried chiles, then soak in hot water until soft.
  2. Toast sesame, nuts, tortilla, and spices separately until fragrant.
  3. Roast tomatoes, onion, and garlic.
  4. Blend all toasted and roasted ingredients with enough stock to make a smooth paste.
  5. Fry the paste in lard or oil, stirring constantly, then gradually add remaining stock.
  6. Simmer 45 to 60 minutes, stir in chocolate, and season until balanced between chile, spice, bitterness, sweetness, and salt.
