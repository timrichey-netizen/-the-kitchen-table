South Carolina Sweet Potato Pie

Silky Southern sweet potato custard pie warmly spiced with cinnamon and nutmeg.

Southern American (South Carolina)South Carolina, United States1 hr 30 min

Ingredients

  • 1 unbaked 9-inch pie crust
  • 2 cups mashed cooked sweet potato
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup evaporated milk
  • 4 tbsp melted butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp nutmeg
  • 1/4 tsp salt

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 350°F. Whisk sweet potato, brown sugar, eggs, milk, butter, vanilla, spices, and salt until smooth.
  2. Pour into crust.
  3. Bake 50–60 minutes until edges are set and center has a slight wobble.
  4. Cool completely before slicing.
