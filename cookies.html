Cookie & Storage Notice

Updated October 8, 2026

Advertising and visitor analytics remain disabled in their respective site modules until separately configured. This notice is not a substitute for a consent-management platform.

Browser storage

Features such as favorites, personal ratings, meal lists and language preferences can store information locally in your browser. Optional account features, if activated, may use authentication cookies or similar storage. You may clear local information through your browser settings, although this can remove saved preferences and favorites.

Advertising and analytics

If advertisements or analytics are enabled later, their providers may use cookies or comparable technologies. Before enabling nonessential cookies, the site operator must implement applicable notice and consent mechanisms, including withdrawal and preference controls where required.

Manage your choices

Use browser controls to restrict or delete storage. A dedicated consent preferences interface will be made available before nonessential tracking is activated where legally required.

Regional requirements

Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland may be entitled to prior consent for certain cookies. For personalized Google ads in those regions, Google requires an appropriately certified CMP. US state privacy rights can include opt-out choices for covered data uses. We do not claim that a generic cookie notice alone meets these obligations.
