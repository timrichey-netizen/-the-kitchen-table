Mississippi Fried Catfish

Mississippi catfish coated in seasoned cornmeal and fried until deeply crisp outside and flaky within.

Southern American (Mississippi)Mississippi, United States35 min

Ingredients

  • 2 lb catfish fillets
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • 1 1/2 cups yellow cornmeal
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1/2 tsp cayenne
  • frying oil

Preparation

  1. Soak catfish in buttermilk 20 minutes.
  2. Combine cornmeal, flour, salt, paprika, and cayenne. Dredge fillets thoroughly.
  3. Fry in 350°F oil until golden and crisp and fish reaches 145°F, about 4–6 minutes.
  4. Drain on a rack and serve hot.
