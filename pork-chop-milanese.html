Pork Chop Milanese

Crisp, golden breaded pork chop served with lemon and a peppery arugula salad.

35 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless pork chops, pounded thin
  • Salt and pepper
  • Flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 cups breadcrumbs
  • ½ cup Parmigiano-Reggiano
  • Olive oil
  • Arugula
  • Lemon wedges

Preparation

  1. Season pork. Dredge in flour, egg, then breadcrumbs mixed with Parmigiano.
  2. Shallow-fry until golden and cooked through, about 3–4 minutes per side.
  3. Drain and season lightly.
  4. Serve with arugula dressed with lemon and olive oil.
