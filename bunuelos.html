MEXICAN · DESSERTS & DRINKS

Buñuelos

Thin, crisp Mexican fritters dusted generously with cinnamon sugar.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 egg
  • 3/4 cup warm water
  • 1 tbsp butter, melted
  • Oil
  • Cinnamon sugar

Method

  1. Mix flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt; add egg, water, and butter to form a soft dough.
  2. Rest 30 minutes, divide, and roll into very thin rounds.
  3. Fry at 350°F until crisp and blistered on both sides.
  4. Drain and coat with cinnamon sugar.
