Tacos de Camarón al Pastor

Shrimp marinated with achiote, guajillo, pineapple, and citrus, then seared and served pastor-style.

30 min + marinateMakes 10 tacos

Photo: Muy Delish

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb shrimp
  • 3 guajillo chiles
  • 1 tbsp achiote paste
  • 1/4 cup pineapple juice
  • 2 tbsp orange juice
  • 1 tbsp vinegar
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1/2 tsp oregano
  • 1/2 tsp cumin
  • 10 tortillas
  • Grilled pineapple
  • Onion, cilantro, lime

Preparation

  1. Toast and soak guajillo chiles.
  2. Blend with achiote, pineapple juice, orange juice, vinegar, garlic, oregano, cumin, and salt.
  3. Marinate shrimp 15 to 20 minutes.
  4. Sear over high heat until lightly charred and just cooked.
  5. Serve with pineapple, onion, cilantro, lime, and salsa.
