Papas a la Provenzal

Golden potatoes tossed with the classic Argentine provenzal combination of garlic and fresh parsley.

40 minServes 4

Representative photo: Valenzuela400 / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb small potatoes, halved or quartered
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, very finely minced
  • ½ cup chopped flat-leaf parsley
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice, optional

Preparation

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Toss the potatoes with 2 tablespoons olive oil, salt, and pepper.
  2. Roast cut-side down until browned and tender, 30–35 minutes.
  3. Mix the garlic, parsley, remaining olive oil, and lemon juice, if using.
  4. Toss the hot potatoes immediately with the garlic-parsley mixture.
  5. Taste for salt and serve hot.
