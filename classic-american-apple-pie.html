Classic American Apple Pie

A double-crust apple pie filled with cinnamon-spiced apples and baked until deeply golden.

1 hr 30 minServes 8

Ingredients

  • 2 pie crusts
  • 7 cups sliced apples
  • ¾ cup sugar
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 1½ tsp cinnamon
  • ¼ tsp nutmeg
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 egg for egg wash

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Fit one crust into a 9-inch pie plate.
  2. Toss apples with sugar, flour, cinnamon, nutmeg, and lemon juice; mound into crust and dot with butter.
  3. Top with second crust, seal, vent, and brush with egg wash.
  4. Bake 20 minutes; reduce to 375°F and bake 40–50 minutes more.
  5. Cool at least 2 hours before slicing.
