Gnocchi alla Sorrentina

Tender gnocchi baked with tomato sauce, mozzarella, Parmigiano, and basil.

45 minServes 4

PASTA GUIDE

Recommended pasta: Potato gnocchi

See pasta shapes, sauces & pairings →

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb potato gnocchi
  • 3 cups tomato sauce
  • 12 oz fresh mozzarella
  • ¾ cup Parmigiano-Reggiano
  • Fresh basil
  • Olive oil
  • Salt

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Cook gnocchi just until they float and drain.
  2. Toss gnocchi with tomato sauce, half the mozzarella, basil, and Parmigiano.
  3. Transfer to a baking dish and top with remaining cheese.
  4. Bake 15–20 minutes until bubbling and browned.
Pasta-water technique: Before draining, reserve at least 1 cup of the hot, starchy cooking water. Add it to the sauce a tablespoon or splash at a time while tossing the pasta. The starch helps emulsify fat, cheese, and sauce so it clings to the pasta and becomes glossy rather than watery.
