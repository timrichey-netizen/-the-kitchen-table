Salsa de Chipotle

A smoky tomato-chipotle salsa with garlic, onion, lime, and cilantro.

20 minMakes about 2 cups

Photo: myfoodbook

Ingredients

  • 4 Roma tomatoes
  • 2 chipotle chiles in adobo
  • 1/4 white onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1/4 cup cilantro
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • 1 tsp kosher salt

Preparation

  1. Roast tomatoes, onion, and garlic until softened and blistered.
  2. Blend with chipotles, cilantro, lime, and salt.
  3. Pulse smooth or leave slightly chunky.
  4. Taste for smoke, heat, salt, and acidity.
  5. Serve immediately or chill before serving.
