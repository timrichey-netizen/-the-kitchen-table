Alegrías

Traditional amaranth bars bound with piloncillo or honey and toasted seeds.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups popped amaranth
  • 1/2 cup piloncillo or brown sugar
  • 1/3 cup honey
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • 1/3 cup pumpkin seeds
  • 1/3 cup peanuts

Method

  1. Toast seeds and combine with popped amaranth.
  2. Cook piloncillo, honey, and lime juice to a thick syrup.
  3. Quickly mix syrup into amaranth and press firmly into a lined pan.
  4. Cool and cut into bars.
