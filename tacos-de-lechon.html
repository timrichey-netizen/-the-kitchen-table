PORK · MEXICAN

Tacos de Lechón

Slow-roasted pork with crisp skin and tender meat, chopped for tacos with citrus, onion, cilantro, and salsa.

4 hrMakes 16 tacos

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

Ingredients

  • 4 lb skin-on pork shoulder or leg
  • 2 tbsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 5 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp oregano
  • Juice of 2 oranges
  • Juice of 2 limes
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Tortillas, onion, cilantro, salsa

Preparation

  1. Rub pork with salt, pepper, garlic, oregano, citrus, and oil; refrigerate overnight.
  2. Roast covered at 300°F until tender.
  3. Increase heat to 450°F and crisp skin and edges.
  4. Rest 15 minutes.
  5. Chop meat and crackling and serve in tortillas.
