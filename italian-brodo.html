Italian Brodo

Gentle Italian meat broth traditionally served with filled pasta or used in risotto and soups.

Broth & StockItalian

Ingredients

  • 2 lb beef shank
  • 1 small chicken
  • 1 onion
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 celery ribs
  • 1 tomato
  • Parsley stems
  • 4 qt water

Preparation

  1. Cover meats with cold water and slowly bring to a simmer.
  2. Skim carefully.
  3. Add vegetables and simmer very gently 3 hours.
  4. Strain; season only after reducing or serving.
