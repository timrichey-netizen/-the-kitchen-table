PORK · MEXICAN

Tacos de Adobada

Chile-marinated pork grilled or seared until caramelized, then chopped for tacos with onion, cilantro, lime, and salsa.

45 min + marinateMakes 12 tacos

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

Ingredients

  • 2 lb pork shoulder, thinly sliced
  • 4 guajillo chiles
  • 2 ancho chiles
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1/4 cup white vinegar
  • 1/4 cup orange juice
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • Corn tortillas
  • Onion, cilantro, lime

Preparation

  1. Toast and soak the dried chiles.
  2. Blend chiles with garlic, vinegar, orange juice, oregano, cumin, and salt.
  3. Marinate pork at least 4 hours.
  4. Grill or sear over high heat until browned and cooked through.
  5. Chop and serve in tortillas with onion, cilantro, lime, and salsa.
