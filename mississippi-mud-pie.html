Mississippi Mud Pie

Intensely chocolate Mississippi pie with a cookie crust, dense chocolate layer, pudding, and whipped cream.

Southern American (Mississippi)Mississippi, United States5 hr

Ingredients

  • 24 chocolate sandwich cookies, crushed
  • 5 tbsp melted butter
  • 8 oz dark chocolate
  • 6 tbsp butter
  • 3 eggs
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 2 tbsp cocoa
  • 2 cups chocolate pudding
  • 1 1/2 cups whipped cream

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 350°F. Mix cookie crumbs and butter; press into 9-inch pie plate and bake 8 minutes.
  2. Melt chocolate and butter. Whisk eggs, sugar, and cocoa; whisk in chocolate. Pour into crust and bake 25–30 minutes until just set. Cool completely.
  3. Spread chocolate pudding over baked layer. Chill at least 3 hours.
  4. Top with whipped cream before serving.
