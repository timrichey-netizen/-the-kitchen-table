Venetian Shrimp with Polenta

Garlicky shrimp served over soft Venetian-style polenta with white wine and herbs.

40 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 1 lb large shrimp
  • 1 cup polenta
  • 4 cups water or light stock
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • ½ cup Parmigiano-Reggiano
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • ½ cup dry white wine
  • 1 small tomato, diced
  • Parsley
  • Salt and pepper

Preparation

  1. Cook polenta slowly in water or stock until creamy. Stir in butter and Parmigiano.
  2. Sauté garlic in olive oil, add shrimp, and cook briefly.
  3. Add white wine and tomato and simmer until shrimp are just cooked and sauce is lightly reduced.
  4. Spoon creamy polenta into bowls and top with shrimp, sauce, and parsley.
