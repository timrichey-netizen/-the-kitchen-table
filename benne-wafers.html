Lowcountry / Gullah Geechee

Benne Wafers

Charleston's delicate, crisp sesame cookies, rooted in the Lowcountry's West African benne tradition.

Lowcountry / Gullah GeecheeCharleston, South Carolina, United States35 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup sesame seeds, preferably benne seeds
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar
  • 4 tbsp butter, softened
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 350°F. Toast sesame seeds lightly and cool.
  2. Cream brown sugar and butter; beat in egg and vanilla. Stir in flour, salt, and sesame seeds.
  3. Drop small teaspoonfuls widely apart on parchment-lined sheets and flatten slightly.
  4. Bake 8–10 minutes until thin and deeply golden at edges. Cool briefly on sheet, then transfer to rack to crisp.
