American (Vermont)

Sugar on Snow

A classic Vermont dessert featuring maple syrup, snow, prepared in its recognizable regional style.

American (Vermont)Vermont, United States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 2 cups pure Vermont maple syrup
  • clean packed snow or finely crushed ice
  • dill pickles and plain doughnuts, traditional accompaniments

Preparation

  1. Pack clean snow firmly into shallow dishes and keep frozen.
  2. Boil maple syrup without stirring to about 235°F.
  3. Immediately drizzle hot syrup in ribbons over snow, where it will thicken into chewy maple candy.
  4. Serve at once, traditionally with pickles and plain doughnuts alongside.
