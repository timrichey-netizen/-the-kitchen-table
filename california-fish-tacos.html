California Fish Tacos

Cali-Baja fish tacos with crisp battered white fish, cabbage, creamy lime sauce, salsa, and corn tortillas.

American (Cali-Baja)Southern California / Baja California influence40 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lb cod or other firm white fish, strips
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 cup cold Mexican lager or sparkling water
  • oil for frying
  • 8 corn tortillas
  • 2 cups shredded cabbage
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • juice of 1 lime
  • 1/2 cup fresh salsa
  • lime wedges

Preparation

  1. Whisk sour cream, mayonnaise, and half the lime juice.
  2. Heat frying oil to 350°F. Whisk flour, baking powder, salt, and cold beer into a light batter.
  3. Dip fish in batter and fry until crisp and cooked through, 3–4 minutes. Drain.
  4. Warm tortillas.
  5. Fill each with fish, cabbage, lime crema, and salsa. Serve with lime.
