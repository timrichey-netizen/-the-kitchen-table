American (Maryland)

Crab Imperial

A Maryland main preparation centered on lump crabmeat, mayonnaise, egg, old bay, presented in its recognizable regional style.

American (Maryland)Maryland, United States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 1 lb jumbo lump crabmeat
  • 1/3 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire
  • 1 tsp Old Bay
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp minced parsley
  • 2 tbsp breadcrumbs
  • 1 tbsp melted butter

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 400°F. Gently pick over crab.
  2. Whisk mayonnaise, egg, mustard, Worcestershire, Old Bay, lemon, and parsley. Fold in crab.
  3. Spoon into ramekins or shells. Mix breadcrumbs with butter and scatter lightly over top.
  4. Bake 12–15 minutes until hot and lightly browned.
