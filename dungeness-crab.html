Pacific Northwest (Washington)

Dungeness Crab

Whole Dungeness crab simply cooked and served to showcase the sweet meat prized along Washington's coast.

Pacific Northwest (Washington)Washington Coast / Puget Sound, United States30 min

Ingredients

  • 2 live or freshly cooked Dungeness crabs, about 2 lb each
  • 1/4 cup kosher salt per gallon cooking water
  • 1 lemon, wedges
  • 4 tbsp melted butter

Preparation

  1. Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a rolling boil.
  2. If using live crab, carefully add crab, cover, and return to boil. Cook about 12–15 minutes for 2-lb crabs.
  3. Transfer to an ice bath briefly to stop cooking.
  4. Remove apron and top shell; discard gills and viscera, rinse lightly, and crack legs and body sections.
  5. Serve with lemon and melted butter.
