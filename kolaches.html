American (Nebraska)

Kolaches

A Nebraska dessert centered on yeast dough, fruit or poppy seed filling, prepared in its recognizable Midwestern regional style.

American (Nebraska)Nebraska, United States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 4 cups flour
  • 2 1/4 tsp yeast
  • 3/4 cup warm milk
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 8 tbsp butter
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 cups fruit, poppy seed, or sweet cheese filling

Preparation

  1. Mix yeast dough with flour, yeast, milk, sugar, eggs, butter, and salt; knead until smooth. Rise until doubled.
  2. Divide into small balls and place on baking sheets; rise again.
  3. Press a deep indentation into each center and fill.
  4. Bake at 375°F for 12–16 minutes until lightly golden.
