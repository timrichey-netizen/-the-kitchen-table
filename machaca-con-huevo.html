BREAKFAST · NORTHERN MEXICAN

Machaca con Huevo

Northern Mexican dried shredded beef scrambled with eggs, tomato, onion, and chile, served with beans and tortillas.

25 minServes 4

Photo: Radio Fórmula

Ingredients

  • 8 oz machaca
  • 8 eggs
  • 1/2 onion, diced
  • 2 Roma tomatoes, diced
  • 1 jalapeño or serrano, minced
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and pepper
  • Refried beans
  • Warm tortillas

Preparation

  1. Cook onion and chile in oil until softened.
  2. Add tomato and cook until juicy.
  3. Stir in machaca and warm through.
  4. Add beaten eggs and scramble gently until softly set.
  5. Serve with refried beans and warm tortillas.
