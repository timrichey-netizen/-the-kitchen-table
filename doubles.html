Caribbean (Trinidad and Tobago)

Doubles

A Trinidad and Tobago main built around bara flatbreads, curried chickpeas, tamarind, pepper sauce, prepared in its recognizable regional style.

Caribbean (Trinidad and Tobago)Trinidad and Tobago1 hr

Ingredients

  • Bara: 3 cups flour
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 2 tsp yeast
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 1/4 cups warm water
  • frying oil. Channa: 2 cans chickpeas
  • 1 onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 2 cups water
  • salt. Tamarind sauce and pepper sauce for serving

Preparation

  1. Mix bara dough, rise 1 hour, divide and stretch into small rounds. Fry briefly until soft and puffed.
  2. Sauté onion and garlic with curry and cumin; add chickpeas and water and simmer until soft and saucy.
  3. Sandwich channa between two bara and finish with tamarind and pepper sauces.
