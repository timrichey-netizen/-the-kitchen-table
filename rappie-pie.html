Acadian

Rappie Pie

A Nova Scotia, Canada side centered on grated potatoes, chicken, broth, onion, prepared in its recognizable Canadian regional style.

AcadianNova Scotia, Canada45 min

Ingredients

  • 5 lb russet potatoes, peeled
  • 1 whole chicken
  • 8 cups water
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 tsp salt
  • black pepper
  • 2 tbsp butter

Preparation

  1. Simmer chicken with water, onion, salt, and pepper until tender; remove meat and reserve hot broth.
  2. Finely grate potatoes and squeeze out as much liquid as possible.
  3. Gradually beat hot broth into dry potato pulp until fluffy and porridge-like.
  4. Spread half in a buttered baking dish, layer with shredded chicken, then cover with remaining potato mixture.
  5. Bake at 400°F for 2–2 1/2 hours until top is deeply browned and crisp.
