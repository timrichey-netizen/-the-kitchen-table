Tacos de Pescado a la Plancha

Simply seasoned fish seared hard on a hot plancha and served with avocado, cabbage, lime, and salsa verde.

25 minMakes 10 tacos

Photo: Muy Delish

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb firm white fish
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 10 corn tortillas
  • Shredded cabbage
  • Avocado
  • Cilantro
  • Salsa verde
  • Lime

Preparation

  1. Pat fish dry and season with oil, cumin, chili powder, salt, and pepper.
  2. Heat a plancha or cast-iron skillet until very hot.
  3. Sear fish without crowding until browned and just cooked through.
  4. Rest briefly and break into large flakes.
  5. Serve in tortillas with cabbage, avocado, cilantro, salsa verde, and lime.
