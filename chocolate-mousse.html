Chocolate Mousse

A classic airy chocolate mousse with deep bittersweet chocolate flavor and a silky finish.

25 min + chillServes 6

Ingredients

  • 7 oz bittersweet chocolate, chopped
  • 3 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 3 large eggs, separated
  • 2 tbsp granulated sugar
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Pinch of salt
  • Chocolate shavings, optional

Preparation

  1. Melt chocolate and butter gently over a double boiler; cool slightly.
  2. Whisk egg yolks into the chocolate mixture one at a time.
  3. Beat egg whites with salt until foamy, then gradually add sugar and beat to soft glossy peaks.
  4. Whip cream with vanilla to soft peaks.
  5. Fold the egg whites into the chocolate in two additions, then fold in the whipped cream until no streaks remain.
  6. Divide among serving glasses and chill at least 3 hours. Garnish with chocolate shavings if desired.
