Chile Relleno

Roasted poblano peppers stuffed with cheese, dipped in a fluffy egg batter, fried, and served in warm tomato sauce.

1 hrServes 4

Photo: Revista Travel

Ingredients

  • 4 large poblano peppers
  • 8 oz Oaxaca, Monterey Jack, or queso fresco
  • 4 large eggs, separated
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • Neutral oil, for frying
  • 2 cups salsa ranchera or tomato sauce
  • Kosher salt

Preparation

  1. Char poblanos until blistered. Steam in a covered bowl 10 minutes, peel carefully, and make one slit in each.
  2. Remove seeds and stuff with cheese; close with toothpicks if needed.
  3. Separate eggs. Beat whites to stiff peaks, then gently fold in yolks and a pinch of salt.
  4. Dust stuffed peppers lightly with flour, dip in egg batter, and fry until golden on all sides.
  5. Drain briefly and serve in warm ranchera sauce.
