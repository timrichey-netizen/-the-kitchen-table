American (Massachusetts)

Boston Cream Pie

A classic Massachusetts dessert featuring sponge cake, pastry cream, chocolate glaze, prepared in its recognizable regional style.

American (Massachusetts)Massachusetts, United States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups cake flour
  • 1 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 2 cups pastry cream
  • 6 oz semisweet chocolate
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 350°F. Make a light sponge batter with flour, baking powder, salt, eggs, sugar, hot milk, butter, and vanilla; bake in two 9-inch layers 20–25 minutes. Cool.
  2. Place pastry cream between layers.
  3. Heat cream and pour over chocolate; stir smooth.
  4. Pour glaze over top, allowing it to drip slightly down sides. Chill briefly to set.
