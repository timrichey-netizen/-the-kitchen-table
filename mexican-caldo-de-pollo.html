Mexican Caldo de Pollo

Mexican chicken broth with vegetables, cilantro, lime, and tender bone-in chicken.

Broth & StockMexican

Ingredients

  • 3 lb bone-in chicken
  • 1 onion
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 zucchini
  • 2 potatoes
  • 2 ears corn
  • Cilantro
  • 3 qt water

Preparation

  1. Simmer chicken, onion, garlic, and salt until chicken is tender.
  2. Skim as needed.
  3. Add firm vegetables first, then zucchini and corn; cook until tender.
  4. Finish with cilantro and serve with lime.
