Tortellini Broth

Traditional brodo-style broth intended for serving tortellini in brodo.

Broth & StockItalian

Ingredients

  • 8 cups Italian brodo
  • Parmigiano rind, optional
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh tortellini for serving

Preparation

  1. Bring brodo to a gentle simmer with optional Parmesan rind.
  2. Simmer 20 minutes and remove rind.
  3. Cook tortellini directly in the broth until tender.
  4. Serve immediately with Parmigiano.
