Louisiana Creole

Bananas Foster

New Orleans bananas caramelized in butter, brown sugar, cinnamon, and rum, served warm over vanilla ice cream.

Louisiana CreoleNew Orleans, Louisiana, United States15 min

Ingredients

  • 4 firm-ripe bananas, halved lengthwise and crosswise
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/4 cup banana liqueur
  • 1/4 cup dark rum
  • 1 pint vanilla ice cream

Preparation

  1. Melt butter in a wide skillet. Add brown sugar and cinnamon and cook until bubbling.
  2. Add bananas and gently cook 1–2 minutes.
  3. Add banana liqueur. Remove pan from direct flame, add rum, then carefully ignite with a long lighter if desired; let flame subside.
  4. Spoon hot bananas and sauce over vanilla ice cream and serve immediately.
