BREAKFAST · YUCATECAN

Huevos Motuleños

Crisp tortillas with black beans, fried eggs, tomato sauce, peas, ham, plantain, and queso fresco.

40 minServes 4

Photo: DiDi Food

Ingredients

  • 8 corn tortillas
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 cups black refried beans
  • 2 cups tomato-ranchera sauce
  • 1/2 cup peas
  • 4 oz ham, diced
  • 1 ripe plantain, sliced and fried
  • 1/2 cup queso fresco
  • Oil

Preparation

  1. Lightly fry tortillas until just crisp.
  2. Spread with black beans.
  3. Top each portion with a fried egg.
  4. Spoon over tomato-ranchera sauce.
  5. Add peas, ham, fried plantain, and queso fresco.
