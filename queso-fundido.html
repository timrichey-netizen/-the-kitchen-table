Queso Fundido

A skillet of bubbling Oaxaca and Monterey Jack cheese with roasted poblano and onion, ready for tortillas.

20 minServes 6

Photo: SnapCalorie

Ingredients

  • 8 oz Oaxaca cheese, shredded
  • 8 oz Monterey Jack cheese, shredded
  • 1 poblano, roasted and diced
  • 1/2 white onion, sliced
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • Warm tortillas
  • Salsa, for serving

Preparation

  1. Cook onion and poblano in butter until soft.
  2. Transfer to a small cast-iron skillet if needed.
  3. Top with both cheeses.
  4. Bake at 400°F until melted and bubbling, 8 to 10 minutes.
  5. Serve immediately with tortillas and salsa.
