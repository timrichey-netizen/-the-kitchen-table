PASTA · ROMAN

Penne all’Arrabbiata

A fiery Roman pasta with tomato, garlic, chile, olive oil, and parsley.

30 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 12 oz penne
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1–2 tsp red-pepper flakes
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • ¼ cup chopped parsley
  • Pecorino, optional
  • Salt

Preparation

  1. Cook garlic and red pepper gently in olive oil.
  2. Add tomatoes and simmer 15–20 minutes.
  3. Cook penne al dente and toss with sauce and a splash of pasta water.
  4. Finish with parsley and optional Pecorino.
