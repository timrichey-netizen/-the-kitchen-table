PASTA · ROMAN

Penne all’Arrabbiata

A fiery Roman pasta with tomato, garlic, chile, olive oil, and parsley.

30 minServes 4

PASTA GUIDE

Recommended pasta: Penne rigate

See pasta shapes, sauces & pairings →

Ingredients

  • 12 oz penne
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1–2 tsp red-pepper flakes
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • ¼ cup chopped parsley
  • Pecorino, optional
  • Salt

Preparation

  1. Cook garlic and red pepper gently in olive oil.
  2. Add tomatoes and simmer 15–20 minutes.
  3. Cook penne al dente and toss with sauce and a splash of pasta water.
  4. Finish with parsley and optional Pecorino.
Pasta-water technique: Before draining, reserve at least 1 cup of the hot, starchy cooking water. Add it to the sauce a tablespoon or splash at a time while tossing the pasta. The starch helps emulsify fat, cheese, and sauce so it clings to the pasta and becomes glossy rather than watery.
