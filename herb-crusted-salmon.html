Herb-Crusted Salmon

Roasted salmon with a crisp herb, garlic, and breadcrumb crust and fresh lemon.

30 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 4 salmon fillets
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • ¾ cup panko breadcrumbs
  • ¼ cup chopped parsley
  • 2 tbsp chopped dill
  • 1 tbsp chopped chives
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon
  • Salt and pepper

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Season salmon with salt and pepper and brush tops with Dijon.
  2. Mix breadcrumbs, herbs, garlic, olive oil, and lemon zest.
  3. Press the herb mixture onto the salmon.
  4. Roast 10–14 minutes, depending on thickness, until just cooked.
  5. Serve with lemon wedges.
