DESSERT · ARGENTINIAN

Rogel

A showstopping Argentine cake of many crisp pastry layers, dulce de leche, and Italian meringue.

2 hr + chillServes 10

Photo source: Infobae.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 6 large egg yolks
  • 1 large egg
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter, melted
  • ⅓ cup water
  • 1 tbsp brandy or cognac, optional
  • Pinch of salt
  • 2½ cups dulce de leche
  • 4 large egg whites
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • ¼ cup water, for meringue syrup

Preparation

  1. Combine flour and salt. Add yolks, egg, melted butter, water, and brandy and knead until smooth. Rest 30 minutes.
  2. Heat oven to 400°F. Divide the dough into 10–12 pieces and roll each paper-thin into an 8-inch circle. Prick with a fork.
  3. Bake the layers individually for 5–7 minutes until crisp and lightly golden. Cool completely.
  4. Stack the pastry layers, spreading a thin, even layer of dulce de leche between each one.
  5. For the meringue, whip egg whites to soft peaks. Boil sugar with ¼ cup water to 240°F, then slowly stream the hot syrup into the whites while beating. Continue until glossy and cool.
  6. Pile or pipe the meringue over the top. Chill briefly before serving so the layers settle while retaining some crispness.
