Roasted Broccoli with Lemon & Almonds

Charred broccoli finished with lemon zest, lemon juice, and toasted sliced almonds.

30 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 2 large heads broccoli, cut into florets
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper
  • ½ cup sliced almonds
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 garlic clove, finely grated
  • Pinch of red-pepper flakes, optional

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 450°F. Toss broccoli with olive oil, salt, and pepper.
  2. Roast 18–22 minutes until charred at the edges and tender.
  3. Toast almonds in a dry skillet until golden.
  4. Toss hot broccoli with lemon zest, lemon juice, garlic, and optional red pepper.
  5. Finish with toasted almonds and serve.
