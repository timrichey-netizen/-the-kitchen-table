Hawaiian Teriyaki-Pineapple BBQ Sauce

A Hawaiʻi, United States sauce built around pineapple juice, soy sauce, brown sugar, ketchup, prepared in its recognizable regional style.

American BBQHawaiʻi, United States30 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup pineapple juice
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/3 cup brown sugar
  • 1/4 cup ketchup
  • 2 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch

Preparation

  1. Combine all ingredients in a saucepan.
  2. Bring to a gentle simmer over medium-low heat, stirring often.
  3. Cook 15–25 minutes until flavors meld and the sauce reaches its characteristic consistency; for fresh-fruit sauces, simmer until fruit is soft and blend smooth.
  4. Cool, adjust salt, acid, sweetness, and heat, and refrigerate in a covered container.
