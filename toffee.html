Toffee

Buttery hard-crack toffee topped with dark chocolate and toasted nuts.

35 min + coolMakes about 1 1/2 lb

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

Ingredients

  • 1 cup unsalted butter
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 1/4 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 6 oz semisweet or dark chocolate, chopped
  • 3/4 cup toasted almonds or pecans, chopped

Preparation

  1. Line a baking sheet with parchment or a silicone mat.
  2. Combine butter, sugar, water, and salt in a heavy saucepan. Cook over medium heat, stirring until melted.
  3. Continue cooking, stirring steadily, until the mixture reaches 300°F and turns deep amber.
  4. Remove from heat, stir in vanilla carefully, and immediately spread thinly on the prepared pan.
  5. Scatter chocolate over the hot toffee. Let soften, then spread evenly and sprinkle with nuts.
  6. Cool completely, then break into pieces.
