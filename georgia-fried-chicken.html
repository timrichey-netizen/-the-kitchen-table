Southern American (Georgia)

Georgia Fried Chicken

Southern buttermilk-marinated chicken with a deeply seasoned, crisp flour crust.

Southern American (Georgia)Georgia, United States4 hr

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken, cut into 8 pieces
  • 2 cups buttermilk
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 tbsp paprika
  • 2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp cayenne
  • frying oil

Preparation

  1. Marinate chicken in buttermilk and 1 tsp salt for at least 3 hours.
  2. Mix flour with remaining salt and spices. Drain chicken lightly and dredge thoroughly in seasoned flour.
  3. Heat 1 inch oil in a heavy skillet to 325–350°F. Fry in batches, turning as needed, until crust is deep golden and chicken reaches 165°F at the thickest part, about 12–18 minutes depending on piece size.
  4. Drain on a rack and rest 5 minutes.
