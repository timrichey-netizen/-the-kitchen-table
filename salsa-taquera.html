Salsa Taquera

A smooth, chile-forward taquería salsa built for tacos, grilled meats, and tortas.

25 minMakes about 2 cups

Photo: Pinterest / Sauce Chick

Ingredients

  • 4 Roma tomatoes
  • 4 dried chiles de árbol
  • 2 dried guajillo chiles
  • 1/4 white onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp apple cider vinegar

Preparation

  1. Toast dried chiles briefly until fragrant, then soak 10 minutes in hot water.
  2. Roast tomatoes, onion, and garlic until charred.
  3. Blend everything with salt and vinegar until very smooth.
  4. Heat oil and fry the salsa 5 minutes to deepen the flavor.
  5. Thin with a little water if needed.
