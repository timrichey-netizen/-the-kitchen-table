Alberta Beef Steak

A Alberta, Canada main centered on alberta beef steak, salt, black pepper, butter, prepared in its recognizable Canadian regional style.

Canadian (Alberta)Alberta, Canada45 min

Ingredients

  • 4 Alberta beef ribeye or strip steaks, 10–12 oz each
  • 2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp coarse black pepper
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 2 tbsp butter

Preparation

  1. Pat steaks dry and season generously with salt and pepper 30 minutes before cooking.
  2. Heat a cast-iron skillet or grill over high heat. Add oil if pan-searing.
  3. Sear steaks until deeply browned, turning once, and cook to desired doneness; about 125–130°F for medium-rare.
  4. Add butter during final minute if pan-searing and baste.
  5. Rest 8–10 minutes before serving.
