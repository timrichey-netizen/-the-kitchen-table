Key Lime Pie

Tangy key lime custard in a crisp graham-cracker crust with whipped cream.

45 min + chillServes 8

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups graham cracker crumbs
  • 6 tbsp melted butter
  • ¼ cup sugar
  • 4 large egg yolks
  • 1 can sweetened condensed milk
  • ½ cup key lime juice
  • 1 tbsp lime zest
  • Whipped cream

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 350°F. Mix crumbs, butter, and sugar and press into a pie plate.
  2. Bake crust 8 minutes.
  3. Whisk yolks, condensed milk, lime juice, and zest.
  4. Bake 15–17 minutes until just set.
  5. Cool, chill at least 3 hours, and serve with whipped cream.
