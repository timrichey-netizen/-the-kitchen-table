Key Lime Pie

Florida Keys pie with a tart, creamy Key lime filling in a crisp graham-cracker crust.

Floridian / Florida KeysFlorida Keys, United States4 hr

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups graham cracker crumbs
  • 3 tbsp sugar
  • 6 tbsp melted butter
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 1 can (14 oz) sweetened condensed milk
  • 1/2 cup fresh Key lime juice
  • 1 tbsp Key lime zest
  • whipped cream, optional

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 350°F. Mix crumbs, sugar, and butter; press into a 9-inch pie plate and bake 8 minutes.
  2. Whisk yolks until slightly thickened. Whisk in condensed milk, lime juice, and zest.
  3. Pour into crust and bake 15–17 minutes until edges are set and center still has a slight wobble.
  4. Cool, then refrigerate at least 3 hours. Serve chilled, optionally with whipped cream.
