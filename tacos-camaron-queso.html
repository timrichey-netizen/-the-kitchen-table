Tacos de Camarón con Queso

Juicy sautéed shrimp with melted Oaxaca cheese, peppers, onion, and warm tortillas.

25 minMakes 10 tacos

Photo: Muy Delish

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb shrimp
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1/2 white onion, sliced
  • 1 poblano or bell pepper, sliced
  • 8 oz Oaxaca or Monterey Jack cheese
  • 10 tortillas
  • Salt and pepper
  • Salsa roja or salsa de aguacate

Preparation

  1. Cook onion and pepper in oil until tender and lightly charred.
  2. Add shrimp, season, and cook until nearly done.
  3. Scatter cheese over and let melt.
  4. Warm tortillas.
  5. Fill with shrimp and cheese and serve with salsa.
