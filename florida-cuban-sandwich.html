Cuban American (Florida)

Cuban Sandwich

Florida Cuban sandwich pressed crisp with roast pork, ham, Swiss cheese, pickles, and mustard on Cuban bread.

Cuban American (Florida)Florida, United States20 min

Ingredients

  • 1 loaf Cuban bread, about 24 inches
  • 2 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 8 oz sliced roast pork
  • 8 oz sliced ham
  • 6 oz Swiss cheese
  • 8 dill pickle slices
  • 2 tbsp butter

Preparation

  1. Split Cuban bread lengthwise and spread mustard inside. Layer roast pork, ham, Swiss, and pickles evenly. Close and cut into portions.
  2. Lightly butter outside.
  3. Press in a plancha or sandwich press over medium heat until bread is crisp and flattened and cheese is melted, 4–6 minutes. Serve hot.
