Papas al Plomo

Whole potatoes wrapped in foil and cooked slowly in the coals until fluffy, then split and dressed with butter and herbs.

1 hrServes 4

Representative foil-baked potato photo: Macgills2 / Wikimedia Commons.

Ingredients

  • 4 large baking potatoes, scrubbed
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Kosher salt
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • Freshly ground black pepper

Preparation

  1. Prepare a grill with a steady bed of medium coals.
  2. Rub the potatoes with olive oil and salt, then wrap each tightly in heavy-duty aluminum foil.
  3. Nestle the potatoes near or partly in the coals and cook 45–60 minutes, turning every 15 minutes, until easily pierced with a skewer.
  4. Carefully unwrap, split open, and fluff the centers with a fork.
  5. Top with butter, parsley, black pepper, and additional salt to taste.
