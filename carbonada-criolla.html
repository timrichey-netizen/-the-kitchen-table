STEW · ARGENTINIAN

Carbonada Criolla

A sweet-savory Argentine beef stew with potatoes, pumpkin, corn, tomato, and fruit.

1 hr 30 minServes 6

Ingredients

  • 2 lb beef chuck, cubed
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 cups beef stock
  • 2 potatoes, cubed
  • 1 sweet potato, cubed
  • 2 cups pumpkin or squash, cubed
  • 1 ear corn, cut into rounds
  • 1 peach or ½ cup dried apricots, chopped
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp ground cumin
  • Kosher salt and black pepper

Preparation

  1. Brown the beef in oil in batches and set aside.
  2. Cook the onion and bell pepper until soft, then add the tomatoes, paprika, cumin, salt, and pepper.
  3. Return the beef and add the stock. Simmer for 40 minutes.
  4. Add the potatoes, sweet potato, pumpkin, corn, and fruit.
  5. Continue simmering about 30 minutes, until the vegetables are tender and the broth is slightly thickened.
