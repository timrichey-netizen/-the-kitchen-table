American (Chicago)

Chicago Deep-Dish Pizza Dough

A rich Chicago deep-dish dough with cornmeal, butter, and oil, formulated to press easily into a deep pan and bake into a tender, crisp, biscuit-like crust.

American (Chicago)Chicago, Illinois, United States3 hr

Ingredients

  • For one 12-inch deep-dish pizza:
  • 360 g all-purpose flour
  • 40 g fine yellow cornmeal
  • 230 g warm water
  • 7 g instant dry yeast
  • 8 g granulated sugar
  • 8 g fine sea salt
  • 30 g unsalted butter, melted and cooled
  • 25 g olive oil, plus more for the pan

Preparation

  1. Combine flour, cornmeal, yeast, sugar, and salt. Add warm water, melted butter, and olive oil.
  2. Mix and knead gently for 3–5 minutes until cohesive and smooth. Avoid excessive kneading; the goal is a tender rather than highly chewy crust.
  3. Cover and let rise until roughly doubled, 60–90 minutes.
  4. Generously oil a 12-inch deep-dish pan. Degas the dough gently and press it evenly across the bottom and about 1 1/2–2 inches up the sides.
  5. Rest 15–20 minutes while the oven heats to 425°F (220°C).
  6. Fill in Chicago deep-dish order, typically cheese first, then fillings, with tomato sauce on top.
  7. Bake 25–35 minutes until the crust is deeply golden and cooked through. Rest several minutes before removing or slicing.
