Chocolate Babka

A rich, tender yeast loaf twisted with ribbons of dark chocolate filling and finished with a light syrup glaze.

4 hrMakes 1 loaf

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

Ingredients

  • 3 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/3 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 1/4 tsp instant yeast
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup whole milk
  • 8 tbsp unsalted butter, softened
  • For filling: 6 oz dark chocolate, chopped
  • 1/3 cup unsalted butter
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 1/4 cup cocoa powder
  • For syrup: 1/4 cup sugar
  • 1/4 cup water

Preparation

  1. Mix flour, sugar, yeast, salt, eggs, and milk until a rough dough forms. Knead in butter gradually until smooth and elastic.
  2. Cover and let rise until doubled, about 1 1/2 to 2 hours.
  3. Melt filling ingredients together until smooth, then cool to spreadable consistency.
  4. Roll dough into a rectangle, spread with chocolate filling, roll tightly into a log, and slice lengthwise. Twist the two halves together and place in a loaf pan.
  5. Proof 45 to 60 minutes. Bake at 350°F for 35 to 40 minutes.
  6. Boil syrup ingredients briefly and brush over the hot loaf. Cool before slicing.
