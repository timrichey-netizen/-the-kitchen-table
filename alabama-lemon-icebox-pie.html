Southern American (Alabama)

Lemon Icebox Pie

Cool Southern lemon icebox pie with a creamy condensed-milk filling in a graham-cracker crust.

Southern American (Alabama)Alabama, United States5 hr

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups graham crumbs
  • 3 tbsp sugar
  • 6 tbsp melted butter
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 1 can (14 oz) sweetened condensed milk
  • 1/2 cup fresh lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp lemon zest
  • whipped cream

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 350°F. Mix crumbs, sugar, and butter and press into a 9-inch pie plate; bake 8 minutes.
  2. Whisk yolks, condensed milk, lemon juice, and zest until smooth. Pour into crust.
  3. Bake 15 minutes until just set. Cool completely, then refrigerate at least 4 hours.
  4. Top with whipped cream just before serving.
