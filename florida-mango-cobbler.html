Florida Mango Cobbler

Juicy ripe mangoes baked beneath a tender golden cobbler topping.

American (Florida)Florida, United States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 6 cups diced ripe mango
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • 1 1/2 cups flour
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 6 tbsp cold butter
  • 3/4 cup milk

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 375°F. Toss mango with sugar, lime juice, and cornstarch; place in baking dish.
  2. Mix flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt. Cut in butter, then stir in milk just until a soft dough forms.
  3. Drop topping over fruit.
  4. Bake 35–40 minutes until topping is golden and mango filling bubbles. Cool 15 minutes before serving.
