American (Santa Maria)

Santa Maria-Style Tri-Tip

California Central Coast tri-tip grilled over live fire with a simple garlic, salt, and pepper seasoning.

American (Santa Maria)Santa Maria Valley, California, United States1 hr 30 min

Ingredients

  • 2 1/2 lb tri-tip roast
  • 2 tsp kosher salt
  • 2 tsp coarse black pepper
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Preparation

  1. Trim excess surface fat. Rub tri-tip with oil, salt, pepper, and garlic powder; rest 30–60 minutes.
  2. Prepare a two-zone charcoal or wood fire.
  3. Sear tri-tip over direct high heat until browned, 3–5 minutes per side.
  4. Move to indirect heat, cover, and cook to 125–130°F for medium-rare, about 20–30 minutes.
  5. Rest 10–15 minutes. Slice thinly across the changing grain.
