SAUCE · VELOUTÉ DAUGHTER SAUCE

Hungarian Sauce

Velouté flavored with onion, paprika, white wine, and cream for a warm, savory finish.

30 minMakes about 2 cups

Photo: Zentrum der Gesundheit

Ingredients

  • 2 cups chicken or veal velouté
  • 1 small onion, finely minced
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp sweet Hungarian paprika
  • 1/3 cup dry white wine
  • 1/4 cup heavy cream
  • Salt and black pepper

Preparation

  1. Cook the onion in butter over medium-low heat until soft without browning.
  2. Stir in paprika for 20 to 30 seconds.
  3. Add white wine and reduce by half.
  4. Whisk in the velouté and simmer gently for 5 minutes.
  5. Finish with cream and season with salt and pepper.
