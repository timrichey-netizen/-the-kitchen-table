American (South Carolina)

Chicken Bog

Pee Dee–style one-pot chicken, rice, and smoked sausage cooked until rich, tender, and characteristically moist.

American (South Carolina)Pee Dee region, South Carolina, United States1 hr 30 min

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken (3–4 lb)
  • 8 cups water
  • 1 onion, quartered
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 lb smoked sausage, sliced
  • 2 cups long-grain white rice

Preparation

  1. Simmer chicken with water, onion, salt, and pepper until tender, about 45 minutes. Remove chicken; strain and reserve 4 cups broth.
  2. When cool enough, remove meat from bones and shred.
  3. Bring reserved broth to a boil with sausage. Stir in rice, cover, and simmer on low 15 minutes.
  4. Fold in chicken, cover, and cook 5–10 minutes more until rice is tender but the mixture remains moist. Rest 5 minutes before serving.
