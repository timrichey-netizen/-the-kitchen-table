Canadian (Manitoba)

Pickled Beets

A Manitoba, Canada side centered on beets, vinegar, sugar, pickling spices, prepared in its recognizable Canadian regional style.

Canadian (Manitoba)Manitoba, Canada45 min

Ingredients

  • 2 lb beets
  • 1 1/2 cups white vinegar
  • 1 cup water
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp pickling spice

Preparation

  1. Simmer whole beets until tender, 30–45 minutes. Cool enough to handle, peel, and slice.
  2. Bring vinegar, water, sugar, salt, and pickling spice to a boil.
  3. Pack beets into clean jars and pour hot brine over them. Cool, cover, and refrigerate at least 24 hours before serving.
