New Mexico Sopaipillas

Puffy New Mexican fried breads with a hollow center, served hot as a side or with honey.

New MexicanNew Mexico, United States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup whole wheat flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 2 tsp shortening or oil
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • about 1/2 cup lukewarm water
  • frying oil

Preparation

  1. Mix flours, salt, and baking powder; work in shortening. Add milk and enough water for a soft dough.
  2. Knead 1 minute; rest covered 30 minutes.
  3. Roll about 1/4 inch thick and cut into wedges or rectangles.
  4. Heat 3 inches oil to 400°F. Fry a few pieces at a time, spooning oil over them so they balloon; turn and cook just until golden. Drain and serve immediately.
