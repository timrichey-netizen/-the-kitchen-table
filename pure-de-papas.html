SIDE · ARGENTINIAN

Puré de Papas

Silky Argentine-style mashed potatoes enriched with butter, warm milk, and a light touch of nutmeg.

35 minServes 4–6

Photo: Horacio Cambeiro / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 4.0.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb Yukon Gold or russet potatoes, peeled and cubed
  • 5 tbsp unsalted butter
  • ¾ cup whole milk, warmed
  • Kosher salt
  • White or black pepper
  • Pinch of freshly grated nutmeg

Preparation

  1. Cover the potatoes with cold salted water and bring to a gentle boil.
  2. Cook until completely tender, 15–20 minutes, then drain very well.
  3. Return the potatoes to the warm pot for 1 minute to evaporate excess moisture.
  4. Mash or rice the potatoes, then fold in butter and enough warm milk to make them smooth and creamy.
  5. Season with salt, pepper, and a small pinch of nutmeg.
