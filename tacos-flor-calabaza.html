Tacos de Flor de Calabaza

Tender squash blossoms sautéed with onion, corn, epazote, chile, and melting cheese in warm corn tortillas.

30 minMakes 8 tacos

Photo: Animal Gourmet / Yahoo

Ingredients

  • 24 squash blossoms, cleaned
  • 1 tbsp butter or oil
  • 1/3 white onion, sliced
  • 1/2 cup corn kernels
  • 1 serrano chile, sliced
  • 1 tbsp chopped epazote
  • 4 oz Oaxaca or panela cheese
  • 8 corn tortillas
  • Salt

Preparation

  1. Remove stems and stamens from squash blossoms and gently clean them.
  2. Cook onion in butter or oil until soft.
  3. Add corn, serrano, blossoms, epazote, and a pinch of salt; cook just until blossoms wilt.
  4. Warm tortillas and add the filling with cheese.
  5. Fold and heat until the cheese softens; serve immediately.
