BREAKFAST · MEXICAN

Entomatadas

Soft corn tortillas bathed in a mild tomato sauce and topped with crema, queso fresco, onion, and optional shredded chicken.

35 minServes 4

Photo: DiDi Food

Ingredients

  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 4 Roma tomatoes
  • 1/4 white onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 serrano, optional
  • 1 cup chicken stock
  • 2 cups shredded chicken, optional
  • 1/2 cup crema
  • 3/4 cup queso fresco
  • Sliced onion
  • Salt

Preparation

  1. Blend tomatoes, onion, garlic, serrano, stock, and salt.
  2. Simmer sauce 10 minutes.
  3. Warm tortillas briefly in a little oil.
  4. Dip tortillas in tomato sauce, fill with chicken if using, and fold.
  5. Top with more sauce, crema, queso fresco, and onion.
