Nashville Hot Chicken

Nashville's fiery fried chicken brushed with cayenne-spiced hot oil and traditionally served with white bread and pickles.

American (Nashville)Nashville, Tennessee, United States5 hr

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken, cut into 8 pieces
  • 2 cups buttermilk
  • 2 tsp hot sauce
  • 2 cups flour
  • 2 tsp salt
  • frying oil
  • 3 tbsp cayenne pepper
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 cup hot frying oil
  • white bread and dill pickle slices

Preparation

  1. Marinate chicken in buttermilk and hot sauce at least 4 hours.
  2. Mix flour and salt; dredge chicken thoroughly.
  3. Fry at 325–350°F until deeply golden and each piece reaches 165°F, 12–18 minutes depending on size.
  4. Carefully whisk 1 cup hot frying oil with cayenne, brown sugar, paprika, garlic powder, and pepper.
  5. Brush spicy oil generously over hot chicken. Serve on white bread with pickles.
