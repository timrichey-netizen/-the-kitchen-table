Whole Wheat Pizza Dough

A flavorful whole wheat pizza dough balanced with bread flour for nutty whole-grain character while retaining enough gluten strength for an extensible, chewy crust.

International / GeneralNot specifically stated / general3–24 hr

Ingredients

  • For 2 medium pizzas:
  • 250 g whole wheat flour
  • 250 g bread flour
  • 350 g water (70% hydration)
  • 10 g fine sea salt
  • 4 g instant dry yeast
  • 15 g olive oil
  • 10 g honey, optional

Preparation

  1. Combine whole wheat flour and bread flour with 325 g water. Mix until shaggy and rest 20–30 minutes to hydrate the bran.
  2. Add yeast, salt, remaining 25 g water, olive oil, and honey if using. Knead 6–8 minutes until elastic; the dough will remain slightly tacky and less silky than white-flour dough.
  3. Cover and rise at room temperature until noticeably expanded, about 1–2 hours. For better flavor, refrigerate 12–24 hours after the initial rise.
  4. Divide into 2 equal balls and shape gently. If refrigerated, bring to room temperature 1–2 hours before shaping.
  5. Hand-stretch each ball to a 12–14-inch round. Work gently because the whole-grain bran can make the dough more prone to tearing.
  6. Top without overloading.
  7. Bake on a fully preheated stone or steel at 500–550°F (260–290°C) until browned underneath and the rim is crisp outside and chewy within, usually 6–10 minutes.
