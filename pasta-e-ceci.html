PASTA · ITALIAN

Pasta e Ceci

A comforting Roman-style pasta and chickpea dish scented with rosemary and garlic.

45 minServes 4

PASTA GUIDE

Recommended pasta: Ditalini or small tubetti

See pasta shapes, sauces & pairings →

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 rosemary sprig
  • 2 cans chickpeas, drained
  • 3 cups stock
  • 8 oz ditalini
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • Salt and pepper
  • Pecorino for serving

Preparation

  1. Warm olive oil with garlic and rosemary; stir in tomato paste.
  2. Add chickpeas and stock and simmer 15 minutes; mash some chickpeas.
  3. Add pasta and cook until tender, adding water as needed for a loose creamy texture.
  4. Season and serve with olive oil and Pecorino.
Pasta-water technique: Before draining, reserve at least 1 cup of the hot, starchy cooking water. Add it to the sauce a tablespoon or splash at a time while tossing the pasta. The starch helps emulsify fat, cheese, and sauce so it clings to the pasta and becomes glossy rather than watery.
