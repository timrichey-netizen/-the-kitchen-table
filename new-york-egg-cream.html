American (New York)

New York Egg Cream

A New York main preparation centered on milk, seltzer, chocolate syrup, presented in its recognizable regional style.

American (New York)New York, United States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup very cold whole milk
  • 1 cup very cold plain seltzer
  • 3 tbsp chocolate syrup

Preparation

  1. Pour cold milk into a tall glass.
  2. Add seltzer in a strong stream while stirring briskly to build a foamy head.
  3. Add chocolate syrup down the side and stir the lower portion while preserving foam on top. Serve immediately.
