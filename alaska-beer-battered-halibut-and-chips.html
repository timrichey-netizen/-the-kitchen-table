Alaska Beer-Battered Halibut and Chips

Wild Alaska halibut in a crisp beer batter served with hot fries in a classic fish-and-chips preparation.

American (Alaska)Alaska, United States45 min

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb Pacific halibut, portions
  • 1 cup flour plus 1/2 cup for dredging
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 cup cold lager
  • 2 lb russet potatoes, cut into fries
  • frying oil
  • lemon wedges

Preparation

  1. Soak cut potatoes in cold water 30 minutes; dry thoroughly.
  2. Heat oil to 325°F and fry potatoes until tender but pale, 4–6 minutes; drain. Raise oil to 375°F.
  3. Whisk flour, baking powder, salt, and cold beer into batter.
  4. Dredge halibut lightly in flour, dip in batter, and fry until crisp and fish reaches 145°F, 4–6 minutes.
  5. Fry potatoes again until golden, 2–3 minutes. Salt and serve with halibut and lemon.
