Roasted Tomato Caprese Salad

Warm roasted tomatoes with fresh mozzarella, basil, olive oil, and balsamic.

35 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 2 pints cherry or small tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 12 oz fresh mozzarella
  • 1 cup fresh basil leaves
  • 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar or glaze
  • Extra-virgin olive oil for finishing

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Toss tomatoes with olive oil, salt, and pepper.
  2. Roast 18–22 minutes until blistered and beginning to collapse.
  3. Arrange warm tomatoes with torn or sliced mozzarella on a platter.
  4. Add basil and drizzle with extra-virgin olive oil and balsamic.
  5. Serve warm or at room temperature.
