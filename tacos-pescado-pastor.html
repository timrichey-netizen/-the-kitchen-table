Tacos de Pescado al Pastor

Fish marinated with achiote, guajillo, pineapple, and citrus, then seared and served pastor-style.

35 min + marinateMakes 10 tacos

Photo: Muy Delish

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb firm fish fillets
  • 3 guajillo chiles
  • 1 tbsp achiote paste
  • 1/4 cup pineapple juice
  • 2 tbsp orange juice
  • 1 tbsp vinegar
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1/2 tsp oregano
  • 1/2 tsp cumin
  • 10 tortillas
  • Grilled pineapple
  • Onion, cilantro, lime

Preparation

  1. Toast and soak guajillo chiles.
  2. Blend with achiote, pineapple juice, orange juice, vinegar, garlic, oregano, cumin, and salt.
  3. Marinate fish 20 to 30 minutes.
  4. Sear or grill over high heat until lightly charred and just cooked.
  5. Serve in tortillas with grilled pineapple, onion, cilantro, lime, and salsa.
