Newfoundland and Labrador

Jiggs Dinner

A Newfoundland and Labrador, Canada main centered on salt beef, cabbage, potatoes, carrots, turnip, pease pudding, prepared in its recognizable Canadian regional style.

Newfoundland and LabradorNewfoundland and Labrador, Canada45 min

Ingredients

  • 2 lb salt beef, soaked overnight if very salty
  • 1 cup yellow split peas tied in cheesecloth or pudding bag
  • 1 small turnip, chunks
  • 6 carrots, chunks
  • 6 potatoes, peeled
  • 1 small cabbage, quartered

Preparation

  1. Place salt beef in a large pot and cover with fresh water. Bring to a simmer and cook 1 1/2 hours.
  2. Add the bag of split peas and simmer 45 minutes.
  3. Add turnip and carrots; cook 20 minutes. Add potatoes and cabbage and cook 20–25 minutes more until all vegetables are tender.
  4. Remove pea bag, mash peas with a little cooking liquid into pease pudding.
  5. Slice salt beef and serve with vegetables and pease pudding.
