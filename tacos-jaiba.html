Tacos de Jaiba

Sweet crab meat warmed with garlic, chile, and lime, served with cabbage, pickled onion, cilantro, and crema.

30 minMakes 10 tacos

Photo: Honest Crab

Ingredients

  • 1 lb lump crabmeat, picked over
  • 1 tbsp butter or olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 serrano chile, minced
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • 10 corn tortillas
  • Shredded cabbage
  • Pickled red onion
  • Cilantro
  • Mexican crema

Preparation

  1. Heat butter or oil and gently cook garlic and serrano.
  2. Fold in crabmeat and warm just until heated through.
  3. Finish with lime and a pinch of salt.
  4. Warm tortillas.
  5. Fill with cabbage and crab, then top with pickled onion, cilantro, and crema.
