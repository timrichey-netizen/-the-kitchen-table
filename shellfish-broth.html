Shellfish Broth

Roasted shellfish-shell broth with concentrated ocean flavor.

Broth & StockSeafood

Ingredients

  • 2 lb mixed shrimp, crab, or lobster shells
  • 1 onion
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 celery rib
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1/2 cup white wine
  • 2 qt water

Preparation

  1. Roast or sauté shells until aromatic and red.
  2. Add vegetables and tomato paste; cook until lightly caramelized.
  3. Deglaze with wine, add water, and simmer 45 minutes.
  4. Crush shells lightly, strain, and cool.
