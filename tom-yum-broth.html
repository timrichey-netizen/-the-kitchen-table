Tom Yum Broth

Hot-sour Thai broth fragrant with lemongrass, galangal, makrut lime, and chile.

Broth & StockThai

Ingredients

  • 6 cups chicken or shrimp broth
  • 2 lemongrass stalks
  • 6 slices galangal
  • 6 makrut lime leaves
  • 4 Thai chiles
  • 3 tbsp fish sauce
  • 3 tbsp lime juice

Preparation

  1. Bruise lemongrass and tear lime leaves.
  2. Simmer broth with lemongrass, galangal, leaves, and chiles 10 minutes.
  3. Add fish sauce.
  4. Remove from heat before adding lime juice; strain or serve rustic.
