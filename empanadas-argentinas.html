Empanadas Argentinas

Baked Argentine beef empanadas filled with onion, warm spices, green olives, and hard-boiled egg.

1 hrMakes about 12

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • ½ tsp ground cumin
  • ¼ tsp chile flakes
  • ½ cup chopped green olives
  • 2 hard-boiled eggs, chopped
  • Kosher salt and black pepper
  • 12 empanada discs
  • 1 egg, beaten, for glazing

Preparation

  1. Cook the onion in olive oil over medium heat until soft.
  2. Add the beef and cook gently, breaking it apart, until just browned.
  3. Stir in both paprikas, cumin, chile flakes, salt, and pepper.
  4. Remove from the heat and fold in the olives and chopped hard-boiled eggs. Cool completely.
  5. Place filling in the center of each empanada disc, fold over, seal, and crimp the edges.
  6. Brush with beaten egg and bake at 400°F for 20–25 minutes, until deeply golden.
