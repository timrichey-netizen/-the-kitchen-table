Gelatina de Mosaico

Colorful cubes of fruit gelatin suspended in a creamy vanilla milk gelatin.

Ingredients

  • 4 flavors fruit gelatin
  • 1 can evaporated milk
  • 1 can sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 3 tbsp unflavored gelatin
  • 1 tsp vanilla

Method

  1. Prepare fruit gelatins separately using slightly less water than package directions; chill firm.
  2. Cut into cubes and combine in a mold.
  3. Bloom unflavored gelatin; dissolve and whisk into evaporated milk, condensed milk, whole milk, and vanilla.
  4. Pour over cubes and chill until fully set.
