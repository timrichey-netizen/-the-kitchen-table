Tacos de Camarón a la Diabla

Shrimp tossed in a fiery chile de árbol and guajillo sauce, served with avocado and lime.

30 minMakes 10 tacos

Photo: Gastrolab

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb shrimp
  • 5 chiles de árbol
  • 3 guajillo chiles
  • 2 Roma tomatoes
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1/4 onion
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 10 corn tortillas
  • Avocado
  • Lime wedges

Preparation

  1. Toast and soak dried chiles.
  2. Blend chiles with tomatoes, garlic, onion, and salt.
  3. Cook sauce in oil for 5 to 7 minutes.
  4. Add shrimp and simmer just until cooked.
  5. Serve in tortillas with avocado and lime.
