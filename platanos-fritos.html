PLANTAIN · MEXICAN

Plátanos Fritos

Ripe plantains fried until deeply caramelized and served with crema or queso fresco.

20 minServes 4

Photo: Conociendo México

Ingredients

  • 3 very ripe plantains
  • Neutral oil
  • Mexican crema, optional
  • Queso fresco, optional

Preparation

  1. Peel plantains and slice diagonally.
  2. Fry in shallow oil until golden and caramelized.
  3. Drain briefly.
  4. Serve warm with crema or queso fresco.
