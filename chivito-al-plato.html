Chivito al Plato

The classic chivito served without bread: steak, ham, mozzarella, bacon, fried egg, salad, and fries.

30 minServes 4

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

Ingredients

  • 4 thin beef steaks
  • 4 slices cooked ham
  • 4 slices mozzarella
  • 8 slices bacon
  • 4 large eggs
  • 4 cups French fries
  • Lettuce leaves
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • Mayonnaise, optional
  • Salt, pepper, and olive oil

Preparation

  1. Cook the bacon until crisp and keep warm.
  2. Season the steaks and sear in a very hot skillet or on a griddle until browned and cooked to your liking.
  3. Top each steak with ham and mozzarella and cook just until the cheese melts.
  4. Fry the eggs to your preferred doneness.
  5. Arrange fries, lettuce, and tomato on four plates. Add the steak, bacon, and fried egg.
  6. Serve immediately, with mayonnaise on the side if desired.
