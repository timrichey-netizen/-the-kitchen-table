Strawberry Tart

A crisp buttery tart shell filled with vanilla pastry cream and topped with glossy fresh strawberries.

1 hr + chillServes 8

Ingredients

  • 1 baked 9-inch sweet tart shell, cooled
  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1 vanilla bean or 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 4 large egg yolks
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/4 cup cornstarch
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 1/2 lb fresh strawberries, hulled and halved
  • 1/4 cup apricot jam
  • 1 tbsp water

Preparation

  1. Heat the milk with vanilla until steaming.
  2. Whisk yolks, sugar, and cornstarch until smooth. Slowly whisk in the hot milk, then return everything to the saucepan.
  3. Cook over medium heat, whisking constantly, until thick and bubbling. Remove from heat and whisk in butter.
  4. Transfer pastry cream to a bowl, cover directly with plastic wrap, and chill completely.
  5. Spread the chilled pastry cream in the tart shell and arrange strawberries over the top.
  6. Warm apricot jam with water and brush lightly over the berries. Chill 30 minutes before slicing.
