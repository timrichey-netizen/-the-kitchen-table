Tacos de Pescado Ahumado

Smoked fish warmed with onion, tomato, chile, and lime, then served with avocado and fresh salsa.

25 minMakes 10 tacos

Photo: Nutrium

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb smoked white fish, flaked
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 onion, diced
  • 2 Roma tomatoes, diced
  • 1 serrano chile, minced
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • 10 corn tortillas
  • Avocado
  • Pico de gallo
  • Cilantro

Preparation

  1. Cook onion and serrano in olive oil until soft.
  2. Add tomato and cook until juicy.
  3. Fold in smoked fish and heat gently.
  4. Finish with lime juice.
  5. Serve in tortillas with avocado, pico de gallo, and cilantro.
