Bife de Chorizo con Chimichurri

Thick Argentine-style strip steaks seared over high heat and finished with bright, garlicky chimichurri.

30 minServes 4

STEAK TECHNIQUE

Meat tip: Salt ahead when possible, dry the surface well, and use very high heat for a strong crust.

See the Meat Cooking Guide →

Ingredients

  • 4 New York strip steaks, 10–12 oz each
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Coarse salt
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • ½ cup chimichurri

Preparation

  1. Let the steaks stand at room temperature for 20–30 minutes, then pat them dry.
  2. Season generously with coarse salt and black pepper.
  3. Heat a grill or cast-iron skillet until very hot and rub the steaks lightly with olive oil.
  4. Cook 4–5 minutes per side for medium-rare, adjusting for thickness and preferred doneness.
  5. Rest 7–10 minutes before slicing across the grain.
  6. Spoon chimichurri over the steaks just before serving.
