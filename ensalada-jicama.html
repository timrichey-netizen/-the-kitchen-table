Ensalada de Jícama

Crisp jícama salad with cucumber, lime, cilantro, chile, and a touch of Tajín.

20 minServes 6

Photo: Jewish Food Society

Ingredients

  • 1 large jícama, peeled and diced
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • 1/4 red onion, finely sliced
  • 1/2 cup cilantro
  • Juice of 2 limes
  • 1 tsp Tajín or chile powder
  • Salt

Preparation

  1. Combine jícama, cucumber, onion, and cilantro.
  2. Add lime, Tajín, and salt.
  3. Toss well and chill 10 minutes before serving.
