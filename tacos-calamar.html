Tacos de Calamar

Quick-seared squid with garlic, chile, lime, and cilantro in warm tortillas.

30 minMakes 10 tacos

Photo: Serious Eats

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb cleaned squid
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 serrano
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • Salt
  • 10 tortillas
  • Cilantro
  • Salsa verde
  • Avocado

Preparation

  1. Pat squid very dry.
  2. Sear quickly in a smoking-hot skillet.
  3. Add garlic and serrano briefly.
  4. Finish with lime and salt.
  5. Serve in tortillas with cilantro, salsa verde, and avocado.
