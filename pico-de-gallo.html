Pico de Gallo

Fresh chopped tomato salsa with white onion, jalapeño, cilantro, lime, and salt.

15 minMakes about 3 cups

Photo: Mommy Plates

Ingredients

  • 5 ripe Roma tomatoes, diced
  • 1/2 white onion, finely diced
  • 1 jalapeño, finely chopped
  • 1/2 cup chopped cilantro
  • Juice of 2 limes
  • 1 tsp kosher salt

Preparation

  1. Combine tomatoes, onion, jalapeño, and cilantro.
  2. Add lime juice and salt and toss gently.
  3. Let rest 10 to 15 minutes.
  4. Taste and adjust lime, chile, and salt.
  5. Drain excess liquid before using as a topping if needed.
