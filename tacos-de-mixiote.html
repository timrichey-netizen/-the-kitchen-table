LAMB · MEXICAN

Tacos de Mixiote

Adobo-marinated lamb steamed in parchment or banana leaf until tender, then served in tortillas.

3 hr 30 minServes 6

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

Ingredients

  • 3 lb lamb shoulder, chunks
  • 5 guajillo chiles
  • 2 ancho chiles
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1/4 onion
  • 1/4 cup vinegar
  • Cumin, oregano, thyme
  • Bay leaves
  • Salt
  • Parchment or banana leaves
  • Corn tortillas

Preparation

  1. Toast and soak chiles.
  2. Blend with garlic, onion, vinegar, spices, and salt.
  3. Marinate lamb at least 4 hours.
  4. Seal portions in parchment or banana leaves.
  5. Steam until very tender, 2 1/2 to 3 hours, then serve in tortillas.
