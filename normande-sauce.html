SAUCE · VELOUTÉ DAUGHTER SAUCE

Normande

Fish velouté enriched with cream, egg yolk, butter, and optional mushroom or oyster liquor.

30 minMakes about 2 cups

Photo: Pavillon France

Ingredients

  • 2 cups fish velouté
  • 1/4 cup mushroom cooking liquid
  • 1/4 cup fish fumet or oyster liquor, optional
  • 1/3 cup heavy cream
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • Salt and white pepper

Preparation

  1. Combine the fish velouté, mushroom liquid, and optional fumet or oyster liquor and reduce gently by about one-quarter.
  2. Whisk together cream and egg yolks.
  3. Temper the yolk mixture with hot sauce, then whisk it back into the pan.
  4. Cook gently until the sauce coats a spoon; do not boil.
  5. Remove from heat and finish with butter, lemon juice, salt, and white pepper.
