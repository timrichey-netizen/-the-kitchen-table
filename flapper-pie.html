Canadian Prairie

Flapper Pie

A Manitoba, Canada dessert centered on graham crust, vanilla custard, meringue, prepared in its recognizable Canadian regional style.

Canadian PrairieManitoba, Canada1 hr

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups graham cracker crumbs
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • 6 tbsp melted butter
  • 3 cups milk
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1/4 cup cornstarch
  • 3 egg yolks
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tsp vanilla
  • 3 egg whites
  • 1/4 cup sugar

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 350°F. Mix crumbs, sugar, and butter; press into pie plate and bake 8 minutes.
  2. Cook milk, sugar, and cornstarch until thick; temper yolks, return to pan, cook 2 minutes, then add butter and vanilla. Pour into crust.
  3. Beat whites to soft peaks, gradually add sugar, and beat glossy. Spread over hot custard.
  4. Bake 10–12 minutes until meringue is golden. Cool, then chill.
