Hawaiʻi Local (Portuguese influence)

Malasadas

Hawaiʻi's Portuguese-influenced sugar-dusted fried yeast doughnuts, crisp outside and fluffy within.

Hawaiʻi Local (Portuguese influence)Hawaiʻi, United States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 4 cups flour
  • 2 1/4 tsp active dry yeast
  • 1/3 cup sugar plus more for coating
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 cup warm milk
  • 4 tbsp melted butter
  • frying oil

Preparation

  1. Dissolve yeast in warm milk with a pinch of sugar.
  2. Mix flour, sugar, and salt; add yeast mixture, eggs, and butter and beat into a soft sticky dough.
  3. Cover and rise until doubled, 1–2 hours.
  4. Portion dough into small rounds. Fry at 350°F until deeply golden and cooked through, turning once.
  5. Drain briefly and roll generously in sugar while warm.
