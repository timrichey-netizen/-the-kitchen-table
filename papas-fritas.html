Papas Fritas

Crisp, hand-cut Argentine-style fries with fluffy centers and plenty of salt.

45 minServes 4

Photo: Horacio Cambeiro / Wikimedia Commons.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb russet potatoes
  • Neutral frying oil
  • Kosher salt

Preparation

  1. Cut the potatoes into ⅜-inch batons. Rinse well in cold water and dry completely.
  2. Heat oil to 325°F. Fry the potatoes in batches for 4–5 minutes until tender but still pale.
  3. Drain and let cool for at least 15 minutes.
  4. Raise the oil to 375°F and fry again for 2–4 minutes until crisp and golden.
  5. Drain well and season immediately with salt.
