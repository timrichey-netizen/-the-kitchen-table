MEAT · URUGUAYAN

Matambre Relleno

Rolled flank steak filled with egg, vegetables, herbs, and peppers, cooked until tender and sliced into spirals.

2 hrServes 8

Photo: Montevideo Portal

Ingredients

  • 1 large matambre or flank steak, about 2 1/2 to 3 lb
  • 4 hard-boiled eggs
  • 2 carrots, cooked until just tender and cut into strips
  • 1 roasted red pepper, cut into strips
  • 2 cups baby spinach or chopped parsley
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp dried oregano
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Butcher’s twine

Preparation

  1. Place the beef flat on a work surface and pound any thick areas so it is an even thickness. Season with salt, pepper, garlic, and oregano.
  2. Arrange spinach or parsley, carrots, roasted pepper, and whole or halved hard-boiled eggs over the meat, leaving a border around the edges.
  3. Roll the meat tightly into a cylinder and tie securely with butcher’s twine every 1 to 2 inches.
  4. Place in a large pot of gently simmering salted water or broth and cook until tender, about 1 1/2 hours. Alternatively roast, tightly wrapped, at 350°F until tender.
  5. Cool slightly under a light weight if you want compact slices. Remove the twine and cut into thick rounds.
  6. Serve warm or chilled, with salad, potatoes, or chimichurri.
