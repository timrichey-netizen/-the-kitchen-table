Shrimp Cakes

Crisp pan-fried shrimp cakes with tender pieces of shrimp, scallions, fresh herbs, lemon, and a light panko crust.

American / GeneralNot specifically stated / general45 min

Ingredients

  • For 8 shrimp cakes:
  • 1 lb (454 g) raw large shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 2 scallions, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp finely chopped fresh cilantro or flat-leaf parsley
  • 1 tsp finely grated lemon zest
  • 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/4 tsp freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 large egg, lightly beaten
  • 1/2 cup panko breadcrumbs, plus 1/2 cup for coating
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 2 to 3 tbsp neutral cooking oil, for pan-frying
  • For serving:
  • Lemon wedges

Preparation

  1. Pat the shrimp very dry. Roughly chop about two-thirds of the shrimp into small pieces. Finely chop the remaining third until it becomes sticky and paste-like; this portion helps bind the cakes while leaving plenty of distinct shrimp pieces.
  2. In a bowl, combine all of the shrimp with the scallions, cilantro or parsley, lemon zest, lemon juice, Dijon mustard, salt, black pepper, egg, 1/2 cup panko, and mayonnaise. Fold until evenly combined.
  3. Divide the mixture into 8 portions. With damp hands, gently form compact cakes about 2 1/2 to 3 inches wide. Place the remaining 1/2 cup panko on a plate and lightly press each cake into the crumbs on both sides.
  4. Refrigerate the formed cakes for 15–20 minutes to firm them and help them hold together during cooking.
  5. Heat 2 tablespoons neutral oil in a large skillet over medium heat until shimmering. Add the shrimp cakes without crowding the pan; work in batches and add the remaining oil as needed.
  6. Pan-fry until crisp and golden brown on the first side, about 3 minutes. Turn carefully and cook the second side until golden and the shrimp is opaque and cooked through, about 2–3 minutes more. The centers should reach 145°F (63°C).
  7. Transfer to a rack or paper-towel-lined plate for 1–2 minutes. Serve hot with lemon wedges.
