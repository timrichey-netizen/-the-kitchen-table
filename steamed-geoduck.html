Steamed Geoduck

Tender Washington geoduck gently steamed and sliced, highlighting the clam's clean briny flavor.

Pacific Northwest (Washington)Puget Sound, Washington, United States20 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cleaned geoduck clam
  • 2 tbsp Shaoxing wine or dry sherry
  • 1 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp finely julienned ginger
  • 2 scallions, julienned
  • 1 tsp sesame oil

Preparation

  1. Separate cleaned siphon and body meat; slice thinly across the grain.
  2. Arrange in a shallow heatproof dish with ginger and wine.
  3. Steam over rapidly boiling water just until opaque and tender, 2–4 minutes; avoid overcooking.
  4. Drizzle with soy sauce and sesame oil and scatter scallions over top. Serve immediately.
