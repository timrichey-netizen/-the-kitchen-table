BEEF · PERUVIAN

Seco de Res

A deeply savory Peruvian beef stew braised with cilantro, ají amarillo, dark beer, peas, carrots, and potatoes.

2 hr 30 minServes 6

Photo: Poyoteca / Toast

Ingredients

  • 3 lb beef chuck, cut into large cubes
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp ají amarillo paste
  • 1 bunch cilantro, blended with 1/2 cup stock
  • 1 cup dark beer
  • 2 cups beef stock
  • 2 carrots, cut into chunks
  • 2 potatoes, cut into chunks
  • 1 cup peas
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Cooked white rice, for serving

Preparation

  1. Season and brown beef deeply in batches; remove.
  2. Cook onion until soft, then add garlic, ají amarillo, and cumin.
  3. Add cilantro puree and beer and simmer briefly.
  4. Return beef with stock, cover, and braise gently about 1 1/2 hours.
  5. Add carrots and potatoes and cook until tender; stir in peas near the end.
  6. Adjust seasoning and serve with white rice.
