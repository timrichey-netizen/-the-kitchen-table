MEAT · URUGUAYAN

Pamplona de Carne

Uruguayan stuffed beef rolls filled with ham, melting cheese, and roasted peppers, then grilled or roasted.

55 minServes 4

Photo: FoodMap

Ingredients

  • 4 thin beef steaks, pounded to about 1/4 inch
  • 4 slices cooked ham
  • 4 oz mozzarella or provolone, cut into strips
  • 1 roasted red pepper, cut into strips
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Kosher salt and black pepper
  • Butcher’s twine or toothpicks

Preparation

  1. Lay the beef steaks flat and season lightly with salt and pepper.
  2. Place ham, cheese, roasted pepper, and parsley across the center of each steak.
  3. Roll each steak tightly around the filling and secure with butcher’s twine or toothpicks.
  4. Brush lightly with olive oil. Grill over medium-high heat, turning to brown all sides, until the beef is cooked and the cheese is melted, about 12 to 16 minutes total. Alternatively roast at 425°F until done.
  5. Rest 5 minutes, remove the ties or picks, and slice into thick rounds for serving.
