American (New York)

Pastrami on Rye

A New York sandwich preparation centered on pastrami, rye bread, mustard, presented in its recognizable regional style.

American (New York)New York, United States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb sliced hot pastrami
  • 8 slices Jewish rye bread
  • 1/3 cup spicy brown mustard
  • dill pickles for serving

Preparation

  1. Steam pastrami gently until hot and tender.
  2. Spread rye bread with mustard.
  3. Pile hot pastrami generously onto four slices and close with remaining bread.
  4. Cut in half and serve immediately with pickles.
