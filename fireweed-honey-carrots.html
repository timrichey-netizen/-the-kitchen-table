American (Alaska)

Fireweed Honey Carrots

Roasted carrots glazed with Alaska fireweed honey and butter for a glossy sweet-savory side.

American (Alaska)Alaska, United States30 min

Ingredients

  • 2 lb carrots, peeled and cut evenly
  • 2 tbsp butter, melted
  • 2 tbsp Alaska fireweed honey
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 425°F.
  2. Toss carrots with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Roast 15 minutes.
  3. Stir melted butter with fireweed honey. Toss carrots with glaze.
  4. Return to oven and roast 8–12 minutes until tender and caramelized, turning once. Serve hot.
