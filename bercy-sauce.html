SAUCE · VELOUTÉ DAUGHTER SAUCE

Bercy

Fish velouté sharpened with shallots, dry white wine, lemon, butter, and parsley.

25 minMakes about 2 cups

Photo: QOOQ

Ingredients

  • 2 cups fish velouté
  • 2 shallots, finely minced
  • 1/2 cup dry white wine
  • 1/2 cup fish fumet or stock
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • Salt and white pepper

Preparation

  1. Combine shallots, white wine, and fish fumet in a saucepan and reduce by about two-thirds.
  2. Whisk in the fish velouté and simmer until the sauce is lightly thickened.
  3. Remove from heat and whisk in the butter.
  4. Add lemon juice and parsley.
  5. Season with salt and white pepper.
