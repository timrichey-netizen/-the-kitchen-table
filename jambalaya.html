Louisiana Creole

Jambalaya

Louisiana jambalaya of rice cooked with andouille, chicken, tomato, and the holy trinity in a deeply seasoned one-pot dish.

Louisiana CreoleLouisiana, United States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 1 lb chicken thighs, diced
  • 12 oz andouille, sliced
  • 1 onion
  • 1 bell pepper
  • 2 celery ribs, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
  • 2 cups long-grain rice
  • 4 cups chicken stock
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • cayenne
  • 2 bay leaves
  • oil, salt, pepper

Preparation

  1. Brown chicken in oil; remove. Brown andouille.
  2. Add onion, pepper, and celery; cook until soft, then add garlic.
  3. Stir in tomatoes, rice, stock, thyme, paprika, cayenne, bay, chicken, and sausage.
  4. Bring to boil, cover, and cook over low heat 20–25 minutes without stirring until rice is tender. Rest 10 minutes, fluff, and serve.
