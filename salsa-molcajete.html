Salsa de Molcajete

A rustic hand-crushed roasted salsa made in a molcajete with tomato, tomatillo, chiles, garlic, onion, and cilantro.

25 minMakes about 2 cups

Photo: Tara's Multicultural Table

Ingredients

  • 3 Roma tomatoes
  • 3 tomatillos
  • 1 jalapeño
  • 1 serrano
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1/4 white onion
  • 1/4 cup cilantro
  • 1 tsp coarse salt

Preparation

  1. Roast tomatoes, tomatillos, chiles, garlic, and onion until blistered and soft.
  2. Grind garlic, chiles, and salt in a molcajete to a paste.
  3. Add tomatoes and tomatillos one at a time, crushing to a coarse texture.
  4. Work in the onion and cilantro.
  5. Taste and adjust salt; serve directly from the molcajete.
