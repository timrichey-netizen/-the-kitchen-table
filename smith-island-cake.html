American (Pennsylvania)

Smith Island Cake

A Maryland dessert preparation centered on yellow cake, chocolate fudge frosting, presented in its recognizable regional style.

American (Pennsylvania)Maryland, United States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 4 cups flour
  • 1 tbsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 1/2 cups butter
  • 3 cups sugar
  • 6 eggs
  • 2 cups milk
  • 2 tsp vanilla
  • 3 cups sugar for frosting
  • 1 1/2 cups evaporated milk
  • 6 oz unsweetened chocolate
  • 6 tbsp butter

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 350°F. Make a classic yellow cake batter from flour, baking powder, salt, butter, sugar, eggs, milk, and vanilla.
  2. Bake batter in 8–10 very thin layers, about 8–10 minutes each. Cool.
  3. Cook frosting sugar, evaporated milk, chocolate, and butter until smooth and slightly thickened.
  4. Stack thin layers with warm fudge frosting between each and over top and sides; cool until set.
