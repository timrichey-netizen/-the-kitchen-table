SAUCE · HOLLANDAISE DAUGHTER SAUCE

Foyot

Béarnaise enriched with meat glaze for an exceptionally savory sauce for steaks, chops, and roasted meats.

15 minMakes about 1 1/2 cups

Photo: Unilever Food Solutions

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups prepared Béarnaise
  • 2 to 3 tbsp warm demi-glace or concentrated meat glaze
  • 1 tsp chopped tarragon, optional
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Preparation

  1. Warm the Béarnaise gently over very low heat.
  2. Whisk in the warm meat glaze one spoonful at a time.
  3. Keep the sauce below a simmer to protect the emulsion.
  4. Add tarragon if desired.
  5. Taste and season lightly before serving.
