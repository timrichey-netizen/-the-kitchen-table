Pan Dulce con Café de Olla

Assorted Mexican pan dulce served with spiced café de olla brewed with cinnamon and piloncillo.

25 minServes 4

Photo: Recetas Mexas

Ingredients

  • 4 to 8 pieces assorted pan dulce, such as conchas or cuernitos
  • 4 cups water
  • 4 tbsp ground coffee
  • 3 to 4 oz piloncillo
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1 star anise, optional
  • 1 strip orange peel, optional

Preparation

  1. Bring water, piloncillo, cinnamon, star anise, and orange peel to a gentle simmer until piloncillo dissolves.
  2. Add coffee, stir, and simmer very gently 3 to 4 minutes.
  3. Remove from heat and steep 5 minutes.
  4. Strain through a fine sieve or cloth.
  5. Serve hot with assorted pan dulce.
