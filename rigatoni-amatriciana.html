PASTA · ROMAN

Rigatoni Amatriciana

Rigatoni coated in tomato, guanciale, Pecorino Romano, and chile.

40 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 12 oz rigatoni
  • 5 oz guanciale, diced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • ½ tsp red-pepper flakes
  • 1 can (28 oz) whole peeled tomatoes, crushed
  • ¾ cup Pecorino Romano
  • Salt

Preparation

  1. Render guanciale until crisp; add red pepper.
  2. Add tomatoes and simmer 15–20 minutes.
  3. Cook rigatoni al dente, reserving pasta water.
  4. Toss pasta with sauce and a splash of pasta water; finish off heat with Pecorino.
