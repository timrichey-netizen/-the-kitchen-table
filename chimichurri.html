SAUCE · ARGENTINIAN

Chimichurri

Argentina’s essential herb sauce with parsley, garlic, oregano, red-wine vinegar, chile, and olive oil.

10 min + restMakes about 1 cup

Ingredients

  • 1 cup finely chopped flat-leaf parsley
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp fresh oregano or 1 tsp dried oregano
  • ½ tsp red-pepper flakes
  • 2 tbsp red-wine vinegar
  • ½ cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • ½ tsp kosher salt
  • Freshly ground black pepper

Preparation

  1. Combine the parsley, garlic, oregano, red-pepper flakes, vinegar, salt, and black pepper.
  2. Stir in the olive oil until evenly combined.
  3. Let stand for at least 20–30 minutes so the flavors meld.
  4. Taste and adjust the vinegar, salt, or chile to preference.
  5. Serve with grilled beef, chicken, sausages, or roasted vegetables. Refrigerate leftovers and bring toward room temperature before serving.
