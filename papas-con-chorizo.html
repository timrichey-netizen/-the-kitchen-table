Papas con Chorizo

Tender potatoes cooked with Mexican chorizo, onion, and chile—ideal as a side or taco filling.

35 minServes 6

Photo: Piloncillo y Vainilla

Ingredients

  • 2 lb potatoes, peeled and diced
  • 12 oz Mexican chorizo
  • 1/2 white onion, diced
  • 1 jalapeño, minced, optional
  • 1 tbsp oil if needed
  • Salt
  • Cilantro

Preparation

  1. Boil potatoes until just tender; drain.
  2. Brown chorizo in a skillet, breaking it up.
  3. Add onion and jalapeño and cook until soft.
  4. Fold in potatoes and cook until lightly crisp and coated in chorizo fat.
  5. Season and finish with cilantro.
