Eastern North Carolina Vinegar BBQ Sauce

A Eastern North Carolina, United States sauce built around apple cider vinegar, white vinegar, brown sugar, red pepper flakes, prepared in its recognizable regional style.

American BBQEastern North Carolina, United States30 min

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups apple cider vinegar
  • 1/2 cup white vinegar
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp red pepper flakes
  • 1 tsp hot sauce
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper

Preparation

  1. Combine all ingredients in a saucepan.
  2. Bring to a gentle simmer over medium-low heat, stirring often.
  3. Cook 15–25 minutes until flavors meld and the sauce reaches its characteristic consistency; for fresh-fruit sauces, simmer until fruit is soft and blend smooth.
  4. Cool, adjust salt, acid, sweetness, and heat, and refrigerate in a covered container.
