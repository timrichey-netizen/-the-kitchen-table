Maple Taffy on Snow

A Quebec, Canada dessert centered on maple syrup, snow, prepared in its recognizable Canadian regional style.

QuébécoisQuebec, Canada1 hr

Ingredients

  • 2 cups pure maple syrup
  • clean packed snow or finely crushed ice

Preparation

  1. Pack clean snow firmly into shallow dishes and keep frozen.
  2. Boil maple syrup without stirring until it reaches about 235°F.
  3. Immediately pour thin ribbons over the snow.
  4. Let set for a few seconds, then roll the chewy maple taffy around wooden sticks and serve immediately.
