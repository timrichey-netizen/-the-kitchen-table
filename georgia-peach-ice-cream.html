Georgia Peach Ice Cream

Creamy churned ice cream loaded with ripe Georgia peaches.

Southern American (Georgia)Georgia, United States5 hr

Ingredients

  • 4 cups peeled diced ripe peaches
  • 3/4 cup sugar, divided
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • pinch salt

Preparation

  1. Toss peaches with 1/4 cup sugar and lemon juice; macerate 30 minutes. Mash half and leave half chunky.
  2. Whisk cream, milk, remaining sugar, vanilla, and salt until sugar dissolves. Stir in mashed peaches. Chill thoroughly.
  3. Churn according to ice-cream maker directions, adding chunky peaches near the end.
  4. Freeze 3–4 hours until scoopable.
