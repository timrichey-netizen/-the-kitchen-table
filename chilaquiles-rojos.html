Chilaquiles Rojos

Crisp tortilla chips tossed in warm red chile sauce and topped with crema, queso fresco, onion, avocado, and eggs.

30 minServes 4

Photo: DiDi Food

Ingredients

  • 8 cups sturdy tortilla chips
  • 3 cups salsa roja
  • 4 eggs
  • 1/2 cup Mexican crema
  • 3/4 cup queso fresco
  • 1/4 white onion, sliced
  • 1 avocado
  • Cilantro

Preparation

  1. Warm salsa roja in a wide skillet.
  2. Add tortilla chips and toss briefly so some remain crisp.
  3. Divide onto plates.
  4. Top with fried eggs, crema, queso fresco, onion, avocado, and cilantro.
