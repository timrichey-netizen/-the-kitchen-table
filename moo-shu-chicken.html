Moo Shu Chicken

A fast stir-fry of chicken, vegetables, mushrooms, and egg served with hoisin and Mandarin pancakes.

Total 40 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lb chicken breast, thinly sliced
  • 3 cups shredded cabbage
  • 1 cup sliced mushrooms
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 green onions
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • Hoisin sauce
  • Mandarin pancakes

Preparation

  1. Scramble eggs quickly in a hot wok; remove.
  2. Stir-fry chicken until nearly cooked.
  3. Add mushrooms and cabbage and cook until just tender.
  4. Return eggs to the pan and season with soy sauce and sesame oil.
  5. Serve in warm pancakes with hoisin sauce.
