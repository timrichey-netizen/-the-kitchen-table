American (Washington)

Walla Walla Sweet Onion Rings

Thick rings of famously mild Walla Walla sweet onion fried in a light, crisp batter.

American (Washington)Walla Walla, Washington, United States30 min

Ingredients

  • 2 large Walla Walla sweet onions
  • 1 1/2 cups buttermilk
  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup cornstarch
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • frying oil

Preparation

  1. Slice onions into 1/2-inch rings and separate. Soak in buttermilk 20 minutes.
  2. Heat oil to 350°F.
  3. Combine flour, cornstarch, baking powder, salt, and pepper.
  4. Lift rings from buttermilk, dredge thoroughly in flour mixture, and fry in batches until crisp and golden, 2–3 minutes.
  5. Drain and season immediately.
