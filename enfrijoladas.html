BEANS · MEXICAN

Enfrijoladas

Corn tortillas dipped in a smooth black bean sauce, filled with cheese or chicken, and topped with crema, onion, and queso fresco.

35 minServes 4

Photo: Mexico in My Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cooked black beans with some cooking liquid
  • 1/4 white onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 chipotle chile in adobo, optional
  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 2 cups shredded chicken or crumbled queso fresco
  • Neutral oil
  • Mexican crema
  • Sliced onion
  • Crumbled queso fresco

Preparation

  1. Blend beans, onion, garlic, chipotle if using, and enough bean liquid to make a smooth sauce.
  2. Simmer bean sauce 8 to 10 minutes and season.
  3. Warm tortillas briefly in a little oil.
  4. Dip each tortilla in bean sauce, fill with chicken or cheese, and fold.
  5. Spoon over more sauce.
  6. Top with crema, onion, and queso fresco.
