Shrimp and Grits

South Carolina Lowcountry shrimp and creamy stone-ground grits with bacon, scallion, lemon, and a savory pan sauce.

Lowcountry / American (South Carolina)South Carolina Lowcountry, United States45 min

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 2 cups water
  • 1 cup stone-ground grits
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 cup sharp cheddar, grated
  • 6 slices bacon, chopped
  • 1 1/2 lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 3 scallions, sliced
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp hot sauce
  • black pepper

Preparation

  1. Bring milk, water, and salt to a gentle simmer. Whisk in grits and cook over low heat, stirring often, until tender and creamy, 25–35 minutes. Stir in butter and cheddar.
  2. Crisp bacon in a skillet; remove, leaving about 2 tbsp drippings.
  3. Season shrimp with pepper and sauté in drippings until just opaque, 2–3 minutes per side. Add garlic and scallions for the final minute.
  4. Add lemon juice, hot sauce, and bacon. Spoon shrimp and pan juices over hot grits.
