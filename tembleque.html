Caribbean (Puerto Rico)

Tembleque

A Puerto Rico dessert built around coconut milk, cornstarch, cinnamon, prepared in its recognizable regional style.

Caribbean (Puerto Rico)Puerto Rico1 hr

Ingredients

  • 2 cans coconut milk
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup cornstarch
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • ground cinnamon

Preparation

  1. Whisk one cup coconut milk with cornstarch until smooth.
  2. Heat remaining coconut milk with sugar, salt, and cinnamon stick. Whisk in slurry and cook until very thick and glossy.
  3. Pour into molds, chill at least 4 hours, unmold, and dust with cinnamon.
