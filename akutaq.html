Alaska Native

Akutaq

Traditional Alaska Native whipped-fat preparation with fish and berries; methods vary among Yup'ik, Iñupiaq and Athabascan communities.

Alaska NativeAlaska, United States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 1 cooked whitefish, bones removed and flesh squeezed dry
  • 1 1/2 cups rendered tallow or other culturally appropriate traditional fat
  • 1/2 cup sugar, optional
  • 4 cups Alaska berries

Preparation

  1. Cook fish fully, remove every bone, and squeeze flesh very dry.
  2. Work the fat until light and aerated.
  3. Beat in fish gradually until evenly incorporated.
  4. Add sugar if using, then gently fold in berries.
  5. Chill or serve according to the community/family tradition being followed; recognize that Akutaq formulations vary significantly among Alaska Native cultures.
