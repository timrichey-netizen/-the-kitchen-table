PORK · MEXICAN

Tacos al Pastor

Chile-and-achiote marinated pork with pineapple, onion, cilantro, and corn tortillas, adapted for a home grill or broiler.

1 hr + marinateMakes 12–16 tacos

Photo: UnoTV

Ingredients

  • 2 lb pork shoulder, sliced very thin
  • 4 guajillo chiles, seeded
  • 2 ancho chiles, seeded
  • 2 tbsp achiote paste
  • 1/3 cup pineapple juice
  • 1/4 cup white vinegar
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • Pineapple slices
  • Corn tortillas
  • Chopped white onion and cilantro
  • Lime wedges

Preparation

  1. Toast dried chiles briefly and soak until soft.
  2. Blend chiles with achiote, pineapple juice, vinegar, garlic, oregano, cumin, pepper, and salt.
  3. Coat pork thoroughly and marinate at least 4 hours, preferably overnight.
  4. Grill or broil pork over high heat until browned and cooked through; char pineapple alongside.
  5. Chop pork and pineapple finely.
  6. Serve in warm corn tortillas with onion, cilantro, lime, and salsa.
