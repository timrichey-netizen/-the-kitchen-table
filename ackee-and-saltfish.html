Caribbean (Jamaica)

Ackee and Saltfish

A Jamaica main built around ackee, salt cod, onion, scotch bonnet, tomato, prepared in its recognizable regional style.

Caribbean (Jamaica)Jamaica1 hr

Ingredients

  • 1 lb salted cod
  • 1 can (19 oz) ackee, drained
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 2 scallions
  • 1/2 Scotch bonnet
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • black pepper

Preparation

  1. Soak salt cod overnight, changing water, then simmer 15 minutes; drain, flake, and remove bones.
  2. Sauté onion, scallion, Scotch bonnet, tomato, and thyme in oil.
  3. Add cod and cook 3–4 minutes.
  4. Fold in ackee gently and warm through without breaking it. Season with pepper.
