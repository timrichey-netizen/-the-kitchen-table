Salsa Ranchera

Smoky roasted tomato salsa with chiles, onion, garlic, and herbs, ideal for eggs, beans, and grilled foods.

30 minMakes about 2 cups

Photo: Latinas Que Comen

Ingredients

  • 5 Roma tomatoes
  • 2 serrano or jalapeño chiles
  • 1/3 white onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp Mexican oregano
  • 1/4 cup cilantro
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 tsp kosher salt

Preparation

  1. Roast tomatoes, chiles, onion, and garlic until blistered and softened.
  2. Blend to a coarse texture with oregano, cilantro, and salt.
  3. Heat oil in a skillet and cook the salsa 5 to 8 minutes.
  4. Adjust seasoning and add a splash of water if too thick.
  5. Serve warm or at room temperature.
