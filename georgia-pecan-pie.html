Southern American (Georgia)

Georgia Pecan Pie

Rich Southern pecan pie with toasted Georgia pecans suspended in a glossy brown-sugar filling.

Southern American (Georgia)Georgia, United States1 hr 20 min

Ingredients

  • 1 unbaked 9-inch pie crust
  • 2 cups pecan halves
  • 3 eggs
  • 3/4 cup light corn syrup
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar
  • 4 tbsp melted butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1/2 tsp salt

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 350°F. Arrange pecans in pie shell.
  2. Whisk eggs, corn syrup, brown sugar, butter, vanilla, and salt until smooth. Pour over pecans.
  3. Bake 50–60 minutes until edges are set and center jiggles only slightly. Shield crust if browning too quickly.
  4. Cool completely before slicing.
