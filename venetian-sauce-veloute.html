SAUCE · VELOUTÉ DAUGHTER SAUCE

Venetian Sauce

Velouté brightened with shallot, tarragon, chervil, and butter for poultry or delicate fish.

25 minMakes about 2 cups

Photo: Picnic

Ingredients

  • 2 cups chicken or fish velouté
  • 1 shallot, finely minced
  • 1/3 cup dry white wine
  • 1 tbsp chopped fresh tarragon
  • 1 tbsp chopped fresh chervil
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • Salt and white pepper

Preparation

  1. Combine shallot and white wine and reduce by about half.
  2. Whisk in the velouté and simmer gently for 5 to 7 minutes.
  3. Remove from heat and whisk in the butter.
  4. Stir in tarragon, chervil, and lemon juice.
  5. Season with salt and white pepper.
