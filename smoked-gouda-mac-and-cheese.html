American

Smoked Gouda Mac and Cheese

A United States pasta built around macaroni, smoked gouda, cheddar, prepared in its recognizable regional style.

AmericanUnited States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 1 lb elbow macaroni
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 1/4 cup flour
  • 3 cups whole milk
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 4 cups smoked Gouda, cheddar
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • salt and black pepper
  • 1 cup panko and 2 tbsp melted butter for baked versions

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 375°F and cook macaroni 2 minutes shy of al dente.
  2. Melt butter, whisk in flour, then gradually whisk in milk and cream; simmer until thick.
  3. Remove from heat, melt in cheeses, Dijon, and recipe-specific additions.
  4. Fold in macaroni, transfer to baking dish, top with buttered panko, and bake 20–25 minutes until bubbling and browned.
