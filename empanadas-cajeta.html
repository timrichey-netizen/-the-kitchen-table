Empanadas de Cajeta

Flaky baked turnovers filled with thick, caramel-like cajeta.

Ingredients

  • 2 1/2 cups flour
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 8 tbsp cold butter
  • 4 oz cream cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 3/4 cup thick cajeta
  • Cinnamon sugar

Method

  1. Cut butter and cream cheese into flour and salt; add egg and bring together.
  2. Chill dough, then roll and cut rounds.
  3. Fill each with cajeta, fold, crimp, and vent.
  4. Bake at 375°F until golden; dust with cinnamon sugar.
