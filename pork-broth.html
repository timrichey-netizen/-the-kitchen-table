BROTH & STOCK · MEAT

Pork Broth

Savory pork broth for noodles, beans, braises, and Mexican or Asian soups.

Broth & StockMeat

Ingredients

  • 3 lb pork neck bones or ribs
  • 1 onion
  • 1 carrot
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 scallions
  • 1 tsp peppercorns
  • 3 qt water

Preparation

  1. Blanch bones 5 minutes and rinse for a cleaner broth.
  2. Combine with aromatics and fresh water.
  3. Simmer gently 3–4 hours.
  4. Strain, cool, and skim fat.
