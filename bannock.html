Canadian / Indigenous-influenced Prairie

Bannock

A Saskatchewan, Canada side centered on flour, baking powder, fat, prepared in its recognizable Canadian regional style.

Canadian / Indigenous-influenced PrairieSaskatchewan, Canada45 min

Ingredients

  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tbsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 3 tbsp melted butter or lard
  • 1 1/4 cups water or milk

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 400°F. Mix flour, baking powder, and salt.
  2. Stir in melted fat and enough liquid to form a soft dough.
  3. Pat into a 1-inch-thick round on a greased pan.
  4. Bake 25–30 minutes until golden and cooked through. Cool slightly and cut into wedges.
