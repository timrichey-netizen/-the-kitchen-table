Saskatoon Berry Pie

A Saskatchewan, Canada dessert centered on saskatoon berries, pastry, sugar, prepared in its recognizable Canadian regional style.

Canadian (Saskatchewan)Saskatchewan, Canada1 hr

Ingredients

  • 1 double-crust 9-inch pie pastry
  • 5 cups Saskatoon berries
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 3 tbsp cornstarch
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tbsp butter

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Toss berries with sugar, cornstarch, lemon, and cinnamon.
  2. Line pie plate with bottom crust, add filling, dot with butter, and cover with top crust; seal and vent.
  3. Bake 20 minutes, reduce to 375°F, and bake 35–40 minutes more until deeply golden and bubbling. Cool at least 2 hours.
