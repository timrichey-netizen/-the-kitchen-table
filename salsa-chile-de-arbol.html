Salsa de Chile de Árbol

A fiery, smooth salsa centered on toasted chile de árbol with tomato, garlic, and a touch of acid.

20 minMakes about 1 1/2 cups

Photo: Pinterest / Pizca de Sabor

Ingredients

  • 12 dried chiles de árbol
  • 3 Roma tomatoes
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1/4 white onion
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 tbsp vinegar or lime juice
  • 1 tsp kosher salt

Preparation

  1. Toast the chiles briefly in a dry pan; remove before they darken too much.
  2. Roast tomatoes, garlic, and onion until softened.
  3. Blend with chiles, vinegar, and salt until smooth.
  4. Fry in hot oil for 3 to 5 minutes.
  5. Cool slightly and use sparingly; this salsa is intentionally hot.
