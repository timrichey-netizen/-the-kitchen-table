Canadian (Prairie / Eastern European)

Perogies

A Manitoba, Canada side centered on potatoes, cheddar, onion, dumpling dough, prepared in its recognizable Canadian regional style.

Canadian (Prairie / Eastern European)Manitoba, Canada45 min

Ingredients

  • 3 cups flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup warm water
  • 2 tbsp sour cream
  • 2 lb potatoes
  • 1 1/2 cups sharp cheddar, grated
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 3 tbsp butter

Preparation

  1. Mix flour and salt; add egg, water, and sour cream to form a soft dough. Knead briefly, cover, and rest 30 minutes.
  2. Boil potatoes until tender, mash with cheddar. Brown onion in butter and stir half into filling.
  3. Roll dough thin, cut rounds, fill, fold, and seal.
  4. Boil in salted water until they float plus 1–2 minutes.
  5. Toss with remaining buttered onions; pan-fry if desired.
