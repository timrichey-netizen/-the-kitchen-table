Salsa de Tomatillo

A tangy green salsa of tomatillos, serrano, onion, garlic, cilantro, and lime.

20 minMakes about 2 cups

Photo: MX Foods

Ingredients

  • 1 lb tomatillos, husked
  • 1 to 2 serrano chiles
  • 1/4 white onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1/2 cup cilantro
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • 1 tsp kosher salt

Preparation

  1. Roast or simmer tomatillos and serranos until softened.
  2. Blend with onion, garlic, cilantro, lime, and salt.
  3. Adjust texture with a splash of water if necessary.
  4. Taste and adjust acidity and salt.
  5. Serve chilled or at room temperature.
