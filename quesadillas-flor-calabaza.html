Quesadillas de Flor de Calabaza

Corn-tortilla quesadillas filled with squash blossoms, onion, epazote, chile, and melting cheese.

30 minMakes 8

Photo: Gastrolab

Ingredients

  • 24 squash blossoms
  • 1/3 onion, sliced
  • 1 serrano, sliced
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp epazote
  • 8 oz Oaxaca cheese
  • 8 corn tortillas
  • Salt
  • Salsa verde

Preparation

  1. Clean squash blossoms and remove stamens.
  2. Cook onion and serrano in oil.
  3. Add blossoms, epazote, and salt and cook just until wilted.
  4. Fill tortillas with blossoms and cheese.
  5. Griddle until cheese melts and serve with salsa verde.
