Salsa Morita

A smoky salsa made with morita chiles, roasted tomato, garlic, onion, and warm spices.

25 minMakes about 2 cups

Photo: Gastrolab

Ingredients

  • 5 dried morita chiles
  • 4 Roma tomatoes
  • 1/4 white onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 black peppercorns
  • Pinch of ground clove
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 tsp kosher salt

Preparation

  1. Toast morita chiles briefly, then soak until pliable.
  2. Roast tomatoes, onion, and garlic until charred.
  3. Blend or grind all ingredients with peppercorns, clove, and salt.
  4. Heat oil and cook the salsa for 4 to 5 minutes.
  5. Adjust salt and texture with a splash of water if needed.
