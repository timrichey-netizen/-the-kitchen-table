Tacos de Mariscos Mixtos

Mixed shrimp, squid, scallops, and fish quickly sautéed with garlic, chile, lime, and herbs.

35 minMakes 12 tacos

Photo: Muy Delish

Ingredients

  • 1/2 lb shrimp
  • 1/2 lb squid
  • 1/2 lb scallops
  • 1/2 lb white fish
  • Olive oil
  • Garlic
  • Serrano
  • Juice of 2 limes
  • 12 tortillas
  • Cabbage
  • Cilantro
  • Salsa verde or salsa macha

Preparation

  1. Pat seafood very dry.
  2. Cook in batches over very high heat, starting with fish and scallops.
  3. Add shrimp and squid last.
  4. Add garlic and serrano near the end.
  5. Finish with lime and serve with cabbage, cilantro, and salsa.
