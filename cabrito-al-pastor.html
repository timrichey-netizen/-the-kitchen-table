GOAT · MEXICAN

Cabrito al Pastor

Northern Mexican young goat roasted slowly over fire or in the oven with salt and simple aromatics until crisp outside and tender within.

3 hrServes 6

Photo: México Desconocido

Ingredients

  • 1 young goat leg or shoulder, 4–5 lb
  • 2 tbsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 4 garlic cloves, crushed
  • 1 tbsp dried oregano
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • Juice of 2 limes
  • 1 cup light beer or stock, optional
  • Corn tortillas and salsa, for serving

Preparation

  1. Rub goat with salt, pepper, garlic, oregano, oil, and lime juice. Marinate 2 to 4 hours.
  2. Prepare a grill for indirect medium-low heat, or heat oven to 325°F.
  3. Cook slowly, turning occasionally if grilling, until tender and well browned, about 2 1/2 to 3 hours.
  4. Use beer or stock to baste lightly if desired.
  5. Rest 15 minutes before carving.
  6. Serve with tortillas, lime, and salsa.
