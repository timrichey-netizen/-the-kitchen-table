RICE · MEXICAN

Arroz Verde

Green rice flavored with poblano, cilantro, parsley, jalapeño, onion, and garlic.

40 minServes 6

Photo: Eat the Gains

Ingredients

  • 2 cups long-grain rice
  • 2 poblano peppers, roasted and peeled
  • 1 jalapeño
  • 1 cup cilantro
  • 1/2 cup parsley
  • 1/4 onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 3 cups chicken or vegetable broth
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 1/2 tsp salt

Preparation

  1. Blend poblanos, jalapeño, cilantro, parsley, onion, garlic, and 1 cup broth.
  2. Toast rice in oil until lightly golden.
  3. Stir in green purée and cook 2 minutes.
  4. Add remaining broth and salt.
  5. Cover and simmer until rice is tender.
  6. Rest 10 minutes and fluff.
