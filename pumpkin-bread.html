Pumpkin Bread

Tender pumpkin quick bread scented with cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg, and cloves.

1 hr 15 minMakes 1 loaf

Ingredients

  • 1 3/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp ginger
  • 1/4 tsp nutmeg
  • Pinch of cloves
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 cup pumpkin puree
  • 2 large eggs
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup neutral oil
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 350°F and grease a 9-by-5-inch loaf pan.
  2. Whisk flour, leaveners, spices, and salt.
  3. In another bowl whisk pumpkin, eggs, brown sugar, oil, milk, and vanilla.
  4. Fold dry ingredients into wet just until combined.
  5. Bake 50 to 60 minutes until a tester comes out clean. Cool in pan 15 minutes, then transfer to a rack.
