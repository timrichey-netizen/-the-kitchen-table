Zucchini “Lasagna”

A lighter lasagna layered with thin zucchini, ricotta, tomato sauce, mozzarella, and basil.

1 hr 15 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 4 medium zucchini, sliced lengthwise
  • 2 cups ricotta
  • 1 egg
  • ½ cup grated Parmigiano-Reggiano
  • 3 cups tomato sauce
  • 12 oz mozzarella, shredded
  • 2 tbsp chopped basil
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Olive oil

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 400°F. Lightly salt zucchini and let stand 15 minutes; blot dry.
  2. Mix ricotta, egg, Parmigiano, basil, salt, and pepper.
  3. Layer tomato sauce, zucchini, ricotta mixture, and mozzarella in a baking dish.
  4. Repeat layers and finish with mozzarella.
  5. Bake 35–40 minutes until bubbling and browned. Rest 15 minutes before slicing.
