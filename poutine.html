Québécois

Poutine

A Quebec, Canada main centered on french fries, cheese curds, brown gravy, prepared in its recognizable Canadian regional style.

QuébécoisQuebec, Canada45 min

Ingredients

  • 4 large russet potatoes, cut into fries
  • frying oil
  • 2 cups fresh white cheese curds
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 2 cups beef or chicken stock
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • salt and black pepper

Preparation

  1. Soak cut potatoes in cold water 30 minutes, drain, and dry thoroughly.
  2. Fry at 325°F until tender but pale; drain. Raise oil to 375°F and fry again until crisp and golden.
  3. Melt butter, whisk in flour, and cook 1 minute. Gradually whisk in stock and Worcestershire; simmer until glossy and thick enough to coat a spoon. Season.
  4. Divide hot fries among bowls, scatter generously with cheese curds, and ladle hot gravy over top so some curds soften while retaining shape. Serve immediately.
