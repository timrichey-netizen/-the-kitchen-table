Neapolitan Sauce

A bright southern Italian tomato sauce with olive oil, garlic, onion, basil, and ripe tomatoes.

45 minMakes about 3 cups

Photo: NEWS.ru

Ingredients

  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 can (28 oz) whole peeled tomatoes, crushed
  • 1 small handful fresh basil
  • 1 tsp sugar, optional
  • Salt and black pepper

Preparation

  1. Cook onion in olive oil until soft.
  2. Add garlic and cook briefly.
  3. Add tomatoes and bring to a gentle simmer.
  4. Cook 30 minutes until slightly thickened.
  5. Stir in basil and season; add a pinch of sugar only if the tomatoes are sharply acidic.
