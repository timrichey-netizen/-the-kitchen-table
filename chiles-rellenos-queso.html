POBLANO · MEXICAN

Chiles Rellenos de Queso

Roasted poblano peppers stuffed with melting cheese, battered, fried, and served with warm tomato sauce.

1 hrServes 4

Photo: Revista Travel

Ingredients

  • 4 large poblanos
  • 10 oz Oaxaca or Monterey Jack cheese
  • 4 eggs, separated
  • 1/4 cup flour
  • Neutral oil, for frying
  • 2 cups salsa ranchera or tomato sauce
  • Salt

Preparation

  1. Char poblanos, steam, peel, slit, and seed.
  2. Stuff with cheese.
  3. Beat egg whites to stiff peaks and fold in yolks with a pinch of salt.
  4. Dust peppers lightly with flour and coat in egg batter.
  5. Fry until golden and serve with warm salsa ranchera.
