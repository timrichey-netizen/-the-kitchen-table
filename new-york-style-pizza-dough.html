American (New York)

New York-Style Pizza Dough

A cold-fermented New York-style dough with bread flour, a touch of sugar and oil, producing a thin foldable slice with a crisp underside and chewy rim.

American (New York)New York City, New York, United States24–72 hr

Ingredients

  • For 3 dough balls (about 330 g each):
  • 600 g bread flour
  • 390 g cool water (65% hydration)
  • 12 g fine sea salt
  • 6 g granulated sugar
  • 3 g instant dry yeast
  • 12 g olive oil

Preparation

  1. Combine flour, sugar, salt, and yeast. Add the cool water and mix until no dry flour remains.
  2. Rest 15 minutes, then knead until moderately smooth and elastic, about 5–8 minutes. Knead in the olive oil until fully incorporated.
  3. Divide into 3 equal balls, lightly oil covered containers, and refrigerate 24–72 hours for cold fermentation.
  4. Remove dough from the refrigerator 1 1/2–2 hours before baking.
  5. On a lightly floured surface, press and stretch one ball into a 14–16-inch round, retaining a modest rim. Avoid a rolling pin if a more open rim is desired.
  6. Top in classic New York proportions without overloading.
  7. Bake on a thoroughly preheated steel or stone at 500–550°F (260–290°C) until the bottom is crisp and browned and the rim is chewy and blistered, generally 5–8 minutes depending on the oven.
