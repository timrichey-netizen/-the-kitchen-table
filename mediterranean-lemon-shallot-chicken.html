Mediterranean Lemon Shallot Chicken with Fresh Herbs

A bright, savory chicken dish with caramelized shallots, garlic, lemon, white wine, and plenty of fresh herbs. The pan sauce is especially good with crusty bread, roasted potatoes, rice, or couscous.

Prep 15 minCook 30–35 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts or 6–8 boneless chicken thighs
  • Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • ½ teaspoon smoked or sweet paprika
  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • 4 large shallots, thinly sliced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • ½ cup dry white wine
  • ¾ cup chicken stock
  • 1 large lemon
  • 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
  • 1 tablespoon unsalted butter
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh dill
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh oregano
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh basil
  • 1 teaspoon fresh thyme leaves
  • 2 tablespoons capers, drained (optional)
  • ¼ teaspoon red-pepper flakes (optional)

Preparation

  1. Season the chicken. Pat the chicken dry. Season generously with salt, pepper, oregano, and paprika. Zest the lemon and reserve the zest.
  2. Brown the chicken. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the chicken and cook until deeply golden, about 4–5 minutes per side. It does not need to be completely cooked through yet. Transfer to a plate.
  3. Caramelize the shallots. Reduce the heat to medium. Add the shallots to the same skillet with a small pinch of salt. Cook for 5–7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until soft and lightly caramelized. Add the garlic and red-pepper flakes and cook for about 30 seconds.
  4. Build the lemon-shallot sauce. Add the white wine and scrape up the browned bits from the bottom of the skillet. Simmer for 2–3 minutes, until reduced by roughly half. Add the chicken stock, Dijon, lemon zest, lemon juice, and capers.
  5. Finish the chicken. Return the chicken and any accumulated juices to the skillet. Reduce the heat to medium-low, cover partially, and simmer for 8–12 minutes, depending on thickness, until the chicken reaches 165°F / 74°C.
  6. Add the fresh herbs. Transfer the chicken to a serving platter. Swirl the butter into the sauce. Remove from the heat and stir in the parsley, dill, oregano, basil, and thyme. Taste and adjust with additional lemon juice, salt, or pepper. Spoon the warm lemon-shallot-herb sauce generously over the chicken.
Mediterranean variation: Add a handful of halved Castelvetrano or Kalamata olives with the chicken stock. A few crumbles of feta added just before serving also work beautifully.
