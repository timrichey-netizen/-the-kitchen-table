Tonkotsu Broth

Opaque, emulsified pork-bone ramen broth produced by a vigorous long boil.

Broth & StockJapanese

Ingredients

  • 5 lb pork neck and femur bones
  • 1 lb pork feet
  • 1 onion
  • 1 head garlic
  • 2 inches ginger
  • Water as needed

Preparation

  1. Blanch bones 10 minutes; drain and scrub thoroughly.
  2. Cover with fresh water and boil vigorously, not gently.
  3. Boil 10–12 hours, topping up water and stirring periodically.
  4. Add aromatics for the final 1–2 hours; strain.
