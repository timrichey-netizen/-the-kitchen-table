Frijoles Refritos

Creamy refried pinto or black beans cooked with onion, garlic, and lard or oil.

25 minServes 6

Photo: Recetas Mexas

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cooked pinto or black beans
  • 1/3 cup bean cooking liquid
  • 2 tbsp lard or neutral oil
  • 1/4 white onion, finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1/2 tsp cumin, optional
  • Salt

Preparation

  1. Cook onion in lard or oil until soft.
  2. Add garlic and cook briefly.
  3. Add beans and mash with a potato masher.
  4. Add bean liquid gradually until creamy.
  5. Cook until thickened and season with salt.
