Coconut Curry Steamed Mussels

Mussels steamed in a fragrant coconut curry broth with ginger, garlic, lime, chile, and fresh herbs.

30 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 2 lb mussels, scrubbed and debearded
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 large shallot, sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
  • 2 tbsp red or yellow curry paste
  • 1 can (13.5 oz) full-fat coconut milk
  • 1/2 cup seafood stock or water
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tsp brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp fresh lime juice
  • 1 small chile, thinly sliced
  • 1/3 cup cilantro leaves
  • Lime wedges, for serving

Preparation

  1. Discard cracked mussels and any open mussels that do not close when tapped.
  2. Heat oil in a large covered pot. Cook shallot until softened, then add garlic, ginger, and curry paste and cook until fragrant.
  3. Add coconut milk, stock, fish sauce, and brown sugar. Bring to a simmer and cook 3 minutes.
  4. Add mussels, cover, and steam 4 to 6 minutes until opened, shaking the pot occasionally.
  5. Discard unopened mussels. Stir in lime juice, chile, and cilantro. Serve immediately with lime wedges.

Notes

Rice or crusty bread is useful for soaking up the coconut curry broth.
