CHICKEN · MEXICAN

Enchiladas Rojas

Corn tortillas dipped in red guajillo chile sauce, filled with chicken or cheese, and topped with crema, cheese, onion, and radish.

50 minServes 4

Photo: Cucina by Elena

Ingredients

  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 3 cups cooked shredded chicken or 2 cups queso fresco
  • 6 guajillo chiles
  • 2 chiles de árbol, optional
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1/4 onion
  • 2 cups chicken stock
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • Neutral oil
  • Mexican crema
  • Crumbled queso fresco
  • Sliced onion and radishes

Preparation

  1. Toast and soak chiles, then blend with garlic, onion, stock, oregano, and salt. Strain and simmer 10 minutes.
  2. Warm a thin layer of oil in a skillet.
  3. Dip tortillas briefly in red sauce, then lightly fry until pliable.
  4. Fill with chicken or cheese and fold or roll.
  5. Spoon over more sauce and top with crema, queso fresco, onion, and radishes.
