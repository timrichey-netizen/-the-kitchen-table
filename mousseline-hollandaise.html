SAUCE · HOLLANDAISE DAUGHTER SAUCE

Mousseline

Hollandaise lightened with softly whipped cream for an airy sauce ideal with delicate fish and vegetables.

15 minMakes about 2 cups

Photo: Le Point

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups prepared Hollandaise, warm not hot
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream, whipped to soft peaks
  • 1 tsp fresh lemon juice, optional
  • Salt and white pepper

Preparation

  1. Prepare the Hollandaise and let it cool slightly so it is warm rather than hot.
  2. Whip the cream to soft peaks.
  3. Fold one-third of the cream into the Hollandaise to lighten it.
  4. Gently fold in the remaining cream without deflating it.
  5. Adjust with lemon, salt, and white pepper and serve immediately.
