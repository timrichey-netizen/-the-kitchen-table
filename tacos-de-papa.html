POTATO · MEXICAN

Tacos de Papa

Crisp potato-filled tacos with seasoned mashed potatoes, lettuce, crema, cheese, and salsa.

45 minMakes 12 tacos

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

Ingredients

  • 2 lb Yukon Gold potatoes
  • 2 tbsp butter or oil
  • 1/2 tsp cumin
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 12 corn tortillas
  • Neutral oil, for frying
  • Shredded lettuce
  • Mexican crema
  • Crumbled queso fresco
  • Salsa roja or salsa verde

Preparation

  1. Boil potatoes until tender, then mash with butter, cumin, garlic powder, and salt.
  2. Warm tortillas until pliable.
  3. Fill with potato and fold.
  4. Shallow-fry until crisp and golden on both sides.
  5. Drain and serve with lettuce, crema, queso fresco, and salsa.
