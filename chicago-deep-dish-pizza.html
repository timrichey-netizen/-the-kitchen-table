American (Illinois)

Chicago Deep-Dish Pizza

A Illinois main centered on pizza dough, mozzarella, italian sausage, tomatoes, prepared in its recognizable Midwestern regional style.

American (Illinois)Illinois, United States45 min

Ingredients

  • 1 deep-dish pizza dough
  • 12 oz sliced mozzarella
  • 1 lb Italian sausage
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan
  • 2 tbsp olive oil

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Oil a 12-inch deep-dish pan and press dough across bottom and up sides.
  2. Layer mozzarella directly on dough, then raw sausage in small pieces.
  3. Cover with seasoned crushed tomatoes and Parmesan; drizzle with oil.
  4. Bake 30–35 minutes until crust is deeply golden and sausage is cooked through. Rest 10 minutes before slicing.
