Tuscan White Bean Soup

A rustic Tuscan soup with cannellini beans, kale, tomato, aromatics, and olive oil.

50 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 cans cannellini beans, drained
  • 1 can diced tomatoes
  • 5 cups chicken or vegetable stock
  • 1 bunch kale, chopped
  • 1 rosemary sprig
  • Salt and pepper
  • Parmigiano rind, optional

Preparation

  1. Cook onion, celery, carrot, and garlic in olive oil until tender.
  2. Add beans, tomatoes, stock, rosemary, and optional Parmigiano rind; simmer 25 minutes.
  3. Mash a small portion of beans for body.
  4. Add kale and simmer until tender, about 8 minutes.
  5. Season and finish with olive oil and black pepper.
