PORK · MEXICAN

Carnitas

Michocán-style pork slowly cooked until tender, then crisped at the edges and served with tortillas, onion, cilantro, and salsa.

3 hrServes 8

Photo: Mexico Insider

Ingredients

  • 4 lb pork shoulder, cut into large chunks
  • 2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 cup lard or neutral oil
  • 1 orange, halved
  • 1 onion, quartered
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 1/2 tsp cumin
  • 1 cup water or stock
  • Corn tortillas
  • Chopped onion, cilantro, lime, and salsa

Preparation

  1. Season pork with salt and pepper.
  2. Add pork, lard or oil, orange, onion, garlic, oregano, cumin, and water to a heavy pot.
  3. Cook covered over very low heat or in a 300°F oven until pork is very tender, about 2 1/2 hours.
  4. Uncover and let liquid reduce.
  5. Shred into large pieces and crisp under the broiler or in a skillet.
  6. Serve with tortillas, onion, cilantro, lime, and salsa.
