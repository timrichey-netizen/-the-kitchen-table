Oregon Hazelnut-Crusted Green Beans

Tender-crisp green beans finished with toasted Oregon hazelnuts, butter, lemon, and crisp breadcrumbs.

American (Oregon)Oregon, United States25 min

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb green beans, trimmed
  • 1/2 cup Oregon hazelnuts, toasted and chopped
  • 1/3 cup panko breadcrumbs
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 1 tsp lemon zest
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • salt and pepper

Preparation

  1. Blanch green beans in salted boiling water until tender-crisp, 3–4 minutes; drain well.
  2. Melt butter in skillet. Add breadcrumbs and hazelnuts and toast until golden, 2–3 minutes. Add garlic for final 30 seconds.
  3. Add beans and toss until hot.
  4. Finish with lemon zest, lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
