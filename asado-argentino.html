MEAT · ARGENTINIAN

Asado Argentino

A classic Argentine mixed grill of beef ribs, steak, and chorizo cooked slowly over live fire and served with chimichurri.

About 2 hrServes 6–8

Ingredients

  • 2 lb flanken-cut beef short ribs
  • 2 lb skirt or flank steak
  • 1½ lb Argentine-style chorizo sausages
  • Coarse salt
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • Lemon wedges
  • Chimichurri, for serving

Preparation

  1. Prepare a charcoal or wood fire and let it burn down to steady medium-hot coals.
  2. Season the meats generously with coarse salt.
  3. Start the ribs over moderate heat, bone-side down, and cook slowly for 45–60 minutes, turning once.
  4. Add the chorizo and cook 20–25 minutes, turning regularly.
  5. Grill the steak last, about 4–6 minutes per side depending on thickness and desired doneness.
  6. Rest the meats 5–10 minutes, slice, and serve family-style with lemon wedges and chimichurri.
