Caribbean (Guadeloupe)

Tarte à la Noix de Coco

A Guadeloupe dessert built around tarte à la noix de coco, prepared in its recognizable regional style.

Caribbean (Guadeloupe)Guadeloupe1 hr

Ingredients

  • 2 cups primary ingredient appropriate to Tarte à la Noix de Coco
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour or starch as traditionally required
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup butter or coconut fat
  • 2 eggs or traditional setting ingredient as appropriate
  • milk or coconut milk as required
  • vanilla, spice, and salt to taste

Preparation

  1. Prepare the traditional base ingredients for Tarte à la Noix de Coco.
  2. Combine the dry and wet components in the customary sequence, preserving the dish's characteristic texture.
  3. Shape, layer, cook, bake, fry, steam, or chill using the traditional method until fully set and properly textured.
  4. Cool or rest as required and serve in the customary Guadeloupe style.
