DESSERT · ARGENTINIAN

Pastafrola

A classic Argentine lattice tart filled with smooth quince paste and a tender, buttery crust.

1 hr 15 minServes 8

Photo source: Vía País.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp kosher salt
  • ½ cup unsalted butter, softened
  • ½ cup granulated sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 large egg yolk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tsp finely grated lemon zest
  • 14 oz quince paste
  • 2–4 tbsp water
  • 1 egg, beaten, for glazing

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 350°F. Butter a 9-inch tart pan.
  2. Whisk flour, baking powder, and salt. Cream butter and sugar, then beat in egg, yolk, vanilla, and lemon zest.
  3. Mix in the dry ingredients until a soft dough forms. Reserve about one-third for the lattice and press the rest into the tart pan.
  4. Warm quince paste with enough water to make it spreadable, then spread evenly over the crust.
  5. Roll the reserved dough into strips and arrange in a lattice over the filling. Brush with beaten egg.
  6. Bake 35–40 minutes until golden. Cool before slicing.
