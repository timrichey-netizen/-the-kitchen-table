MEAT · URUGUAYAN

Chivito

Uruguay’s iconic steak sandwich layered with ham, mozzarella, bacon, egg, lettuce, tomato, and mayonnaise.

30 minServes 4

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

Ingredients

  • 4 thin beef steaks, about 4 oz each
  • 4 crusty sandwich rolls or ciabatta rolls
  • 4 slices cooked ham
  • 4 slices mozzarella
  • 8 slices bacon, cooked crisp
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 tomato, sliced
  • Lettuce leaves
  • Mayonnaise
  • Kosher salt and black pepper
  • Olive oil
  • French fries, for serving

Preparation

  1. Season the steaks generously with salt and pepper. Heat a skillet or griddle over high heat with a thin film of olive oil and cook the steaks quickly, about 1 to 2 minutes per side.
  2. Split and lightly toast the rolls. Spread both sides with mayonnaise.
  3. Top each steak with ham and mozzarella and let the cheese melt briefly in the hot pan.
  4. Fry the eggs sunny-side up or over-easy.
  5. Build each sandwich with lettuce, tomato, steak, ham, mozzarella, bacon, and a fried egg.
  6. Close the sandwiches and serve immediately with French fries.
