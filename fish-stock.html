Fish Stock

A clean, delicate fish stock made with white-fish bones, aromatics, herbs, and white wine.

50 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 2 lb non-oily white-fish bones and trimmings
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 small fennel bulb, sliced
  • 1 celery stalk, sliced
  • 1 small carrot, sliced
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • ½ cup dry white wine
  • 8 cups cold water
  • Parsley stems
  • 2 thyme sprigs
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 6 black peppercorns

Preparation

  1. Rinse fish bones under cold water and drain.
  2. Combine fish bones, vegetables, herbs, peppercorns, wine, and cold water in a stockpot.
  3. Bring just to a gentle simmer, skimming foam as needed.
  4. Simmer 30–35 minutes without boiling vigorously.
  5. Strain through a fine sieve and cool quickly.
