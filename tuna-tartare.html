Tuna Tartare

Clean, bright tuna tartare with avocado, scallion, sesame, citrus, and a light soy dressing.

20 minServes 4 as an appetizer

Ingredients

  • 12 oz sushi-grade tuna, well chilled
  • 1 ripe avocado, diced
  • 2 scallions, thinly sliced
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tsp toasted sesame oil
  • 2 tsp fresh lime juice
  • 1 tsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp finely grated fresh ginger
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds
  • Pinch of chile flakes or finely sliced fresh chile
  • Cilantro or chives, for garnish
  • Crisp wontons, cucumber rounds, or toast, for serving

Preparation

  1. Cut the chilled tuna into small even dice and keep refrigerated.
  2. Whisk soy sauce, sesame oil, lime juice, rice vinegar, ginger, and chile.
  3. Just before serving, gently toss the tuna with the dressing and scallions.
  4. Fold in the avocado carefully so it keeps its shape.
  5. Spoon into chilled bowls or mold into rounds. Finish with sesame seeds and herbs and serve immediately.

Notes

Use tuna specifically sold for raw preparation and keep it refrigerated until serving.
