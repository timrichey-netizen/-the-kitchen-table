American (Massachusetts)

Boston Baked Beans

A classic Massachusetts side featuring navy beans, molasses, salt pork, prepared in its recognizable regional style.

American (Massachusetts)Massachusetts, United States45 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lb dried navy beans, soaked overnight
  • 6 oz salt pork
  • 1 onion
  • 1/3 cup molasses
  • 1/4 cup brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp dry mustard
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 3 cups hot water

Preparation

  1. Drain soaked beans and simmer in fresh water until barely tender, 45–60 minutes; drain.
  2. Heat oven to 300°F. Put onion in bean pot and add beans and salt pork.
  3. Mix molasses, brown sugar, mustard, salt, and hot water; pour over beans.
  4. Cover and bake 5–6 hours, uncovering for the final hour, adding hot water if needed, until dark and tender.
