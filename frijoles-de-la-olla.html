Frijoles de la Olla

Simple pot beans slowly simmered with onion, garlic, and salt until tender and brothy.

2 hrServes 8

Photo: Pinterest

Ingredients

  • 1 lb dried pinto, black, or peruano beans
  • 1/2 white onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 8 cups water, plus more as needed
  • 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • Epazote, optional

Preparation

  1. Rinse and sort beans.
  2. Combine beans, onion, garlic, and water in a heavy pot.
  3. Simmer gently until beans are tender, adding water as needed.
  4. Add salt during the final 30 minutes.
  5. Serve in their broth, with epazote if desired.
