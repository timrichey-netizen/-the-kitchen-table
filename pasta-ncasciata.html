PASTA · SICILIAN

Pasta ’Ncasciata

A Sicilian baked pasta layered with eggplant, meat ragù, cheese, and tomato.

1 hr 30 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 12 oz rigatoni or maccheroni
  • 1 eggplant, sliced or cubed
  • 2 tbsp olive oil plus more for eggplant
  • ½ lb ground beef
  • 2 cups tomato sauce
  • 8 oz caciocavallo or mozzarella
  • ½ cup grated Pecorino
  • 2 hard-boiled eggs, sliced, optional
  • Fresh basil
  • Salt and pepper

Preparation

  1. Fry or roast the eggplant until golden.
  2. Brown beef, add tomato sauce, and simmer 20 minutes.
  3. Cook pasta just shy of al dente and toss with some ragù.
  4. Layer pasta, eggplant, ragù, cheese, optional egg, and basil in a baking dish.
  5. Bake at 375°F for 30–35 minutes until bubbling and browned.
