American BBQ

Kansas City-Style BBQ Sauce

A Kansas City, Missouri, United States sauce built around ketchup, brown sugar, molasses, apple cider vinegar, prepared in its recognizable regional style.

American BBQKansas City, Missouri, United States30 min

Ingredients

  • 2 cups ketchup
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 1/3 cup molasses
  • 1/3 cup apple cider vinegar
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire
  • 1 tbsp yellow mustard
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1/2 tsp cayenne

Preparation

  1. Combine all ingredients in a saucepan.
  2. Bring to a gentle simmer over medium-low heat, stirring often.
  3. Cook 15–25 minutes until flavors meld and the sauce reaches its characteristic consistency; for fresh-fruit sauces, simmer until fruit is soft and blend smooth.
  4. Cool, adjust salt, acid, sweetness, and heat, and refrigerate in a covered container.
