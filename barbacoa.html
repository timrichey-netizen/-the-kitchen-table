BEEF · MEXICAN

Barbacoa

Slow-cooked beef with dried chiles, garlic, vinegar, and warm spices, served with consommé, tortillas, onion, and cilantro.

4 hrServes 8

Photo: Infobae

Ingredients

  • 4 lb beef cheek, chuck, or lamb shoulder
  • 4 guajillo chiles
  • 2 ancho chiles
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1/2 onion
  • 1/4 cup apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 1/4 tsp cloves
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 3 cups beef stock
  • Salt and pepper
  • Corn tortillas, onion, cilantro, and lime

Preparation

  1. Toast and soak chiles until soft.
  2. Blend chiles with garlic, onion, vinegar, cumin, oregano, cloves, and some stock.
  3. Season meat and place in a Dutch oven or slow cooker.
  4. Pour chile mixture and remaining stock over meat; add bay leaves.
  5. Cook covered at 300°F for 3 1/2 to 4 hours, or until fork-tender.
  6. Shred and serve with consommé, tortillas, onion, cilantro, lime, and salsa.
