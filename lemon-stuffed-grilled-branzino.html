Lemon-Stuffed Grilled Branzino

Whole branzino stuffed with lemon and herbs, grilled until crisp-skinned and tender.

35 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 2 whole branzino, cleaned
  • 2 lemons, sliced
  • 4 garlic cloves, sliced
  • Parsley and thyme
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and pepper

Preparation

  1. Heat grill to medium-high and oil the grates.
  2. Season fish inside and out; stuff with lemon, garlic, parsley, and thyme.
  3. Brush with olive oil and grill 5–7 minutes per side until crisp-skinned and cooked through.
  4. Serve with lemon and olive oil.
