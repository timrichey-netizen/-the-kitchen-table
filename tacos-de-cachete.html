BEEF · MEXICAN

Tacos de Cachete

Slow-braised beef cheek cooked until meltingly tender, then shredded for tacos.

4 hrMakes 14 tacos

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

Ingredients

  • 3 lb beef cheeks
  • 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 onion
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 3 cups beef stock
  • 14 tortillas
  • Onion, cilantro, lime, salsa

Preparation

  1. Season beef cheeks and brown lightly if desired.
  2. Braise with onion, garlic, bay, cumin, oregano, and stock at 300°F.
  3. Cook 3 to 3 1/2 hours until very tender.
  4. Shred or chop and moisten with braising liquid.
  5. Serve in warm tortillas with onion, cilantro, lime, and salsa.
