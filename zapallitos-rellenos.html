Zapallitos Rellenos

Tender round zucchini filled with savory beef, onion, herbs, breadcrumbs, and melted cheese.

1 hr 10 minServes 4

Photo: Wikimedia Commons — Zapallitos rellenos collection.

Ingredients

  • 4 round zucchini (zapallitos)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • ½ lb ground beef
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • ½ cup fresh breadcrumbs
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • Kosher salt and black pepper
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella or queso cremoso

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 375°F. Cut the tops from the zucchini and scoop out the centers, leaving a sturdy shell. Chop the scooped flesh.
  2. Blanch the shells in simmering salted water for 4 minutes, then drain upside down.
  3. Cook onion in olive oil until soft. Add beef and brown, then stir in garlic and chopped zucchini flesh.
  4. Remove from heat and mix in breadcrumbs, parsley, paprika, salt, pepper, and half the cheese.
  5. Fill the zucchini shells, top with remaining cheese, and bake 25–30 minutes until tender and browned.
