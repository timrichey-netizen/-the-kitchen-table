CHICKEN · URUGUAYAN

Pollo a la Parrilla

Uruguayan-style grilled chicken cooked slowly over embers until smoky, deeply browned, and juicy.

1 hr 15 minServes 4–6

Photo: LA NACION

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken, 4 to 5 lb, butterflied or quartered
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • 4 garlic cloves, finely minced
  • 1 tbsp chopped fresh parsley
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika
  • 1 1/2 tsp coarse salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • Chimichurri and lemon wedges, for serving

Preparation

  1. Pat the chicken dry. Combine olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, parsley, oregano, paprika, salt, and pepper and rub all over the chicken. Marinate at least 30 minutes, or refrigerate up to 8 hours.
  2. Prepare a wood or charcoal grill for medium-low indirect heat, with a steady bed of embers rather than strong flames.
  3. Place the chicken skin-side up over the cooler part of the grill. Cook slowly for 30 to 40 minutes, rotating as needed for even heat.
  4. Turn the chicken skin-side down and continue grilling until the skin is crisp and well browned and the thickest part reaches 165°F, about 15 to 25 minutes more.
  5. Rest the chicken for 10 minutes before cutting into serving pieces.
  6. Serve with chimichurri, lemon wedges, and simple grilled vegetables or fries.
