Eggplant Parmesan

Crisp eggplant layered with tomato sauce, mozzarella, Parmigiano, and basil.

1 hr 30 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 2 large eggplants, sliced
  • Salt
  • Flour, beaten eggs, and breadcrumbs
  • Olive oil for frying
  • 3 cups tomato sauce
  • 12 oz mozzarella
  • 1 cup Parmigiano-Reggiano
  • Fresh basil

Preparation

  1. Salt eggplant 30 minutes and blot dry. Bread with flour, egg, and breadcrumbs.
  2. Fry until golden and drain.
  3. Layer sauce, eggplant, mozzarella, Parmigiano, and basil.
  4. Bake at 375°F for 30–35 minutes; rest 15 minutes before serving.
