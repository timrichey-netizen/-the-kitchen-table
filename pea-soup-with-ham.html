Pea Soup with Ham

A Quebec, Canada main centered on yellow split peas, ham, onion, carrot, celery, prepared in its recognizable Canadian regional style.

QuébécoisQuebec, Canada45 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lb dried yellow split peas, rinsed
  • 1 meaty ham hock
  • 8 cups water or stock
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 celery ribs, diced
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 tsp dried savory
  • black pepper
  • salt as needed

Preparation

  1. Combine peas, ham hock, water, onion, carrots, celery, bay, savory, and pepper. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a gentle simmer.
  2. Cook partially covered 1 1/2–2 hours, stirring occasionally, until peas are very soft.
  3. Remove ham hock; discard skin and bone, shred meat, and return it to soup.
  4. Adjust thickness with water if needed and season carefully with salt. Serve hot.
