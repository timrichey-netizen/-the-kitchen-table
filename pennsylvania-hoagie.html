American (Pennsylvania)

Pennsylvania Hoagie

A Pennsylvania sandwich preparation centered on italian rolls, salami, ham, provolone, lettuce, tomato, presented in its recognizable regional style.

American (Pennsylvania)Pennsylvania, United States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 4 long hoagie rolls
  • 8 oz deli ham
  • 6 oz Genoa salami
  • 6 oz capicola
  • 8 oz provolone
  • shredded iceberg lettuce
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 1 onion, thinly sliced
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • dried oregano
  • salt and pepper

Preparation

  1. Split rolls lengthwise without cutting through.
  2. Layer ham, salami, capicola, and provolone.
  3. Top with lettuce, tomato, and onion.
  4. Drizzle with oil and vinegar; season with oregano, salt, and pepper. Close firmly and serve.
