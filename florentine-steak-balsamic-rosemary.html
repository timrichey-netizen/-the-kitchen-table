Balsamic and Rosemary-Marinated Florentine Steak

A thick-cut Florentine-style steak marinated with balsamic, rosemary, garlic, and olive oil.

1 hrServes 4

STEAK TECHNIQUE

Meat tip: For a thick steak, dry-brine ahead and consider a two-zone grill or reverse sear for a more even interior.

See the Meat Cooking Guide →

Ingredients

  • 1 thick porterhouse or T-bone steak
  • 3 tbsp balsamic vinegar
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 rosemary sprigs
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • Salt and pepper
  • Lemon wedges

Preparation

  1. Combine balsamic, oil, rosemary, and garlic. Marinate steak 30–60 minutes; pat dry.
  2. Season generously and grill over very high heat until charred and medium-rare.
  3. Rest 10 minutes and slice across the grain.
  4. Drizzle with olive oil and serve with lemon and flaky salt.
