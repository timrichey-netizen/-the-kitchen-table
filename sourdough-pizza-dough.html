Sourdough Pizza Dough

A naturally leavened pizza dough fermented with active sourdough starter for a complex flavor, extensible texture, crisp base, and airy rim.

International / SourdoughNot specifically stated / general24–48 hr

Ingredients

  • For 3 dough balls (about 300 g each):
  • 500 g bread flour
  • 325 g water
  • 100 g active 100%-hydration sourdough starter, at or near peak
  • 11 g fine sea salt
  • 10 g olive oil, optional

Preparation

  1. Mix flour with 300 g water until no dry flour remains. Rest 20–30 minutes.
  2. Add the active starter and mix thoroughly. Dissolve salt in the remaining 25 g water and work it into the dough. Add olive oil if using.
  3. Over the next 2 hours, perform 3–4 sets of stretch-and-folds about 30 minutes apart.
  4. Continue bulk fermentation until the dough has expanded roughly 50% and shows bubbles, usually 3–6 hours total depending on starter strength and room temperature.
  5. Divide into 3 equal portions and shape into tight dough balls. Place in covered containers and refrigerate 12–36 hours.
  6. Remove 1 1/2–3 hours before baking so the dough becomes relaxed and extensible.
  7. Hand-stretch each ball into a pizza round, preserving the airy rim. Top as desired and bake on a thoroughly preheated stone or steel at the oven’s highest setting until blistered, crisp underneath, and fully baked.
