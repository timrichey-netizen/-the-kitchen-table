Spaghetti with Shrimp, Lemon, Mint, and Pecorino

A fresh spaghetti with sautéed shrimp, lemon zest, mint, and salty Pecorino.

35 minServes 4

PASTA GUIDE

Recommended pasta: Spaghetti or linguine

See pasta shapes, sauces & pairings →

Ingredients

  • 12 oz spaghetti
  • 1 lb shrimp
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 lemon
  • ½ cup fresh mint
  • ¾ cup Pecorino Romano
  • Salt, pepper, red-pepper flakes

Preparation

  1. Cook spaghetti al dente, reserving pasta water.
  2. Sauté shrimp with olive oil, garlic, seasoning, and red pepper until just cooked.
  3. Add spaghetti, lemon zest, lemon juice, and a splash of pasta water.
  4. Off heat fold in mint and Pecorino.
Pasta-water technique: Before draining, reserve at least 1 cup of the hot, starchy cooking water. Add it to the sauce a tablespoon or splash at a time while tossing the pasta. The starch helps emulsify fat, cheese, and sauce so it clings to the pasta and becomes glossy rather than watery.
