Causa Limeña

A Lima-style chilled potato terrine seasoned with ají amarillo and lime, layered with avocado and chicken or tuna.

1 hr + chillServes 6

Photo: 196 Flavors

Ingredients

  • 2 lb yellow potatoes or Yukon Gold
  • 3 tbsp ají amarillo paste
  • Juice of 2 limes
  • 3 tbsp neutral oil
  • Salt
  • 2 cups shredded chicken or tuna
  • 1/3 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 2 hard-boiled eggs
  • Black olives

Preparation

  1. Boil potatoes until tender, peel, and mash or rice while warm.
  2. Mix with ají amarillo, lime juice, oil, and salt; cool completely.
  3. Mix chicken or tuna with mayonnaise.
  4. Layer half the potato mixture, avocado, filling, then remaining potato in a mold.
  5. Chill 30 minutes, unmold, and garnish with egg and olives.
