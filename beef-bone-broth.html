Beef Bone Broth

Long-cooked beef bone broth with roasted marrow and joint bones.

Broth & StockMeat

Ingredients

  • 5 lb beef marrow and joint bones
  • 2 tbsp cider vinegar
  • 1 onion
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 celery ribs
  • 5 qt water

Preparation

  1. Roast bones at 425°F until deeply browned.
  2. Place in a stockpot with vinegar, vegetables, and water.
  3. Simmer 10–12 hours, skimming and topping up water as needed.
  4. Strain and chill.
