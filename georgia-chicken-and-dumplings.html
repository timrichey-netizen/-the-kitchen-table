Southern American (Georgia)

Chicken and Dumplings

Tender chicken in rich broth with soft rolled Southern dumplings.

Southern American (Georgia)Georgia, United States1 hr 30 min

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken (3–4 lb)
  • 8 cups water
  • 1 onion
  • 2 celery ribs
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 3/4 cup buttermilk

Preparation

  1. Simmer chicken with water, onion, celery, and salt until tender, about 45 minutes. Remove chicken, strain broth, and shred meat.
  2. Mix flour, baking powder, and salt; cut in butter and add buttermilk to form dough. Roll thin and cut into strips.
  3. Bring 6 cups broth to a simmer. Drop dumplings in individually and simmer 12–15 minutes until tender.
  4. Return chicken to pot and heat through.
