Canadian (Alberta)

Bison Burger

A Alberta, Canada main centered on ground bison, burger buns, cheddar, onion, prepared in its recognizable Canadian regional style.

Canadian (Alberta)Alberta, Canada45 min

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb ground bison
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 4 burger buns
  • 4 slices cheddar
  • 1 small red onion, sliced
  • lettuce and tomato, optional

Preparation

  1. Divide bison into 4 loosely packed patties and season just before cooking.
  2. Grill or sear over medium-high heat about 3–4 minutes per side, avoiding overcooking because bison is lean.
  3. Add cheddar during final minute to melt.
  4. Rest briefly, then serve on buns with onion and optional lettuce and tomato.
