American (New York)

Manhattan Clam Chowder

A New York soup preparation centered on clams, tomatoes, potatoes, celery, presented in its recognizable regional style.

American (New York)New York, United States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 3 dozen cherrystone clams or 3 cups chopped clams with juice
  • 4 slices bacon
  • 1 onion
  • 2 celery ribs
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 potatoes
  • 1 can (28 oz) tomatoes
  • 3 cups clam juice
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 1 bay leaf
  • salt and pepper

Preparation

  1. Crisp bacon; cook onion, celery, and carrots in fat.
  2. Add potatoes, tomatoes, clam juice, thyme, and bay. Simmer until potatoes are tender, 20 minutes.
  3. Add chopped clams and simmer only 3–5 minutes until tender.
  4. Season and serve with bacon.
