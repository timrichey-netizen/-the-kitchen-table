DESSERT · ARGENTINIAN

Torta Balcarce

A rich Argentine celebration cake layered with sponge, dulce de leche, whipped cream, meringue, and chocolate.

2 hr + chillServes 10

Photo source: Cocineros Argentinos.

Ingredients

  • 1 prepared 9-inch vanilla sponge cake, split into 3 layers
  • 1½ cups dulce de leche
  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 3 tbsp powdered sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 cup crushed meringue cookies
  • ½ cup chopped walnuts, optional
  • ½ cup chopped or grated semisweet chocolate
  • 1 tbsp cocoa powder
  • Powdered sugar, for finishing

Preparation

  1. Whip the cream with powdered sugar and vanilla to firm peaks.
  2. Place the first sponge layer on a serving plate and spread with half the dulce de leche, followed by a layer of whipped cream, crushed meringue, walnuts if using, and chocolate.
  3. Add the second sponge layer and repeat the fillings. Top with the final cake layer.
  4. Cover the top and sides with the remaining whipped cream.
  5. Press crushed meringue onto the exterior and finish with chocolate, cocoa, and powdered sugar.
  6. Refrigerate at least 4 hours before slicing.
