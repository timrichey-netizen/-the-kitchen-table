Plátanos Fritos con Crema

Caramelized ripe plantains served warm with cool crema and cinnamon.

Ingredients

  • 3 very ripe plantains
  • 2 tbsp butter or neutral oil
  • Mexican crema
  • Cinnamon
  • Optional condensed milk

Method

  1. Peel and slice plantains on the bias.
  2. Fry over medium heat until deeply caramelized on both sides.
  3. Drain briefly.
  4. Serve warm with crema, cinnamon, and optional condensed milk.
