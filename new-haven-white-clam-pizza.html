American (Connecticut)

New Haven White Clam Pizza

A classic Connecticut main featuring pizza dough, clams, garlic, pecorino, olive oil, prepared in its recognizable regional style.

American (Connecticut)Connecticut, United States45 min

Ingredients

  • 1 ball thin pizza dough
  • 1 dozen littleneck clams, shucked and chopped, liquor reserved
  • 2 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1/3 cup grated pecorino Romano
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • black pepper

Preparation

  1. Heat pizza stone or steel at the oven's maximum temperature for at least 45 minutes.
  2. Stretch dough very thin. Brush with olive oil and a little clam liquor; scatter garlic, clams, pecorino, oregano, and pepper.
  3. Bake until crust is blistered and crisp and clams are just cooked, 5–8 minutes in a very hot home oven.
  4. Finish with a light drizzle of olive oil.
