BROTH & STOCK · FRENCH

Court-Bouillon

Acidulated aromatic poaching broth for fish and shellfish.

Broth & StockFrench

Ingredients

  • 2 qt water
  • 1 cup dry white wine
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 carrot, sliced
  • 1 celery rib
  • 1 lemon, sliced
  • 1 bay leaf
  • Parsley stems
  • Peppercorns

Preparation

  1. Combine all ingredients.
  2. Simmer 20–30 minutes to extract aromatics.
  3. Strain if a clean poaching liquid is desired.
  4. Use for gently poaching fish or shellfish.
