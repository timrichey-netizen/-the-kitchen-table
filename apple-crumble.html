Apple Crumble

Warm cinnamon apples beneath a crisp brown-sugar oat crumble.

55 minServes 8

Ingredients

  • 6 apples, peeled and sliced
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • ⅓ cup sugar
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 cup flour
  • ¾ cup rolled oats
  • ¾ cup brown sugar
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 8 tbsp cold butter, cubed

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 375°F. Toss apples with lemon, sugar, and cinnamon.
  2. Mix flour, oats, brown sugar, and salt; cut in butter until crumbly.
  3. Scatter topping over apples.
  4. Bake 35–40 minutes until bubbling and golden.
  5. Serve warm.
