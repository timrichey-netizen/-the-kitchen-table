Southern American (Alabama)

Lane Cake

Alabama's classic layered white cake filled with a rich bourbon-raisin-pecan custard.

Southern American (Alabama)Alabama, United States3 hr

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cake flour
  • 1 tbsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 cup butter
  • 2 cups sugar
  • 8 egg whites
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 8 egg yolks
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1 cup raisins
  • 1 cup chopped pecans
  • 1 cup shredded coconut
  • 1/3 cup bourbon

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 350°F. Prepare three greased 9-inch pans. Cream butter and sugar; alternate dry ingredients with milk, then fold in beaten egg whites and vanilla. Divide among pans and bake 20–25 minutes. Cool.
  2. For filling, cook yolks, sugar, and butter over low heat until thickened. Stir in raisins, pecans, coconut, and bourbon; cool.
  3. Stack cake layers with filling between and over the top. Let flavors settle several hours before slicing.
