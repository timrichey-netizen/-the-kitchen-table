Sautéed Spinach with Garlic

Silky sautéed spinach with golden garlic, olive oil, and a touch of lemon.

15 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb fresh spinach
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 5 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • Pinch of red-pepper flakes, optional

Preparation

  1. Heat olive oil in a wide skillet over medium heat.
  2. Add garlic and cook until lightly golden.
  3. Add spinach in batches, tossing until just wilted.
  4. Season with salt, pepper, lemon juice, and optional red pepper.
