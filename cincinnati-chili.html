American (Ohio)

Cincinnati Chili

A Ohio main centered on ground beef, tomato, chili spices, spaghetti, cheddar, prepared in its recognizable Midwestern regional style.

American (Ohio)Ohio, United States45 min

Ingredients

  • 2 lb ground beef
  • 4 cups water
  • 1 onion, minced
  • 1 can tomato sauce
  • 2 tbsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp cloves
  • 1/4 tsp allspice
  • 1 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire
  • 1 tbsp cider vinegar
  • salt
  • cooked spaghetti
  • finely shredded cheddar
  • kidney beans and diced onion, optional

Preparation

  1. Crumble raw beef into water and bring to a gentle simmer, stirring until very fine.
  2. Add onion, tomato sauce, chili powder, cinnamon, cloves, allspice, cocoa, Worcestershire, vinegar, and salt.
  3. Simmer uncovered 1 1/2–2 hours, skimming fat as needed.
  4. Serve over spaghetti with cheddar; add beans and/or raw onion for traditional ways.
