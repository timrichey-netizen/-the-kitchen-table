Roasted Eggplant & Cherry Tomatoes

Caramelized eggplant and blistered cherry tomatoes finished with garlic, basil, and olive oil.

40 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 2 medium eggplants, cut into 1-inch pieces
  • 2 pints cherry tomatoes
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • ½ cup fresh basil
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 1 tbsp red wine vinegar, optional

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 425°F.
  2. Toss eggplant and tomatoes with olive oil, garlic, oregano, salt, and pepper.
  3. Roast 30–35 minutes, stirring once, until eggplant is browned and tomatoes blister.
  4. Finish with basil and optional red wine vinegar.
