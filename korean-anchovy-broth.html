Korean Anchovy Broth

Korean myeolchi-yuksu made from dried anchovies, kelp, and radish.

Broth & StockKorean

Ingredients

  • 12 large dried anchovies, heads and guts removed
  • 1 piece dried kelp
  • 6 oz Korean radish
  • 1/2 onion
  • 8 cups water

Preparation

  1. Toast anchovies briefly in a dry pot, optional.
  2. Add water, radish, onion, and kelp; soak 20 minutes.
  3. Heat and remove kelp just before a full boil.
  4. Simmer anchovies and vegetables 15 minutes; strain.
