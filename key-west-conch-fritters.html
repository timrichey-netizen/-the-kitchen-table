Key West Conch Fritters

Key West–style fritters packed with chopped conch, peppers, onion, and herbs in a crisp golden batter.

Floribbean / Florida KeysKey West, Florida, United States35 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lb cleaned conch meat, finely chopped
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/2 onion, finely diced
  • 1/2 bell pepper, finely diced
  • 2 scallions
  • 1 tbsp parsley
  • 1 tsp hot sauce
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • frying oil

Preparation

  1. Combine flour, baking powder, and salt. Whisk egg, milk, and hot sauce and stir into dry ingredients.
  2. Fold in conch, onion, pepper, scallions, and parsley.
  3. Heat oil to 350°F. Drop tablespoonfuls of batter into oil and fry, turning, until deep golden and cooked through, 3–4 minutes.
  4. Drain and serve hot.
