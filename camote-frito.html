Camote Frito

Crisp fried sweet-potato wedges with caramelized edges and a soft center, a classic accompaniment to ceviche and grilled meats.

30 minServes 4

Photo: Recetas Nestlé Perú

Ingredients

  • 2 lb sweet potatoes
  • Neutral oil, for frying
  • Kosher salt
  • Lime wedges, optional

Preparation

  1. Peel sweet potatoes and cut into thick wedges or batons.
  2. Heat oil to 340–350°F.
  3. Fry in batches until tender and golden, 5 to 7 minutes depending on thickness.
  4. Drain well and season with salt.
  5. Serve hot with lime if desired.
