Flan Napolitano

Silky Mexican caramel custard enriched with cream cheese.

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 5 eggs
  • 1 can evaporated milk
  • 1 can sweetened condensed milk
  • 4 oz cream cheese
  • 1 tbsp vanilla

Method

  1. Caramelize sugar in a saucepan and pour into a round baking dish.
  2. Blend eggs, both milks, cream cheese, and vanilla until smooth.
  3. Pour over caramel and bake in a water bath at 350°F until just set, about 55 minutes.
  4. Chill thoroughly, loosen the edge, and invert.
