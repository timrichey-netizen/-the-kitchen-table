SALAD · PERUVIAN

Solterito Arequipeño

A colorful Arequipa salad of fava beans, corn, tomato, onion, olives, queso fresco, and a bright vinegar dressing.

25 minServes 6

Photo: Peru Delights

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked fava beans
  • 2 cups cooked choclo or corn
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup queso fresco, cubed
  • 1/2 cup black olives
  • 1 ají amarillo, thinly sliced, optional
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 1 tbsp chopped parsley
  • Salt and black pepper

Preparation

  1. Combine fava beans, corn, tomato, onion, queso fresco, olives, and ají amarillo.
  2. Whisk olive oil, vinegar, parsley, salt, and pepper.
  3. Pour dressing over salad and toss gently.
  4. Let stand 10 minutes before serving.
  5. Serve chilled or at room temperature.
