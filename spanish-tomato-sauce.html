Spanish Sauce

A tomato sauce with onions, peppers, garlic, paprika, and a warm Spanish-style aromatic profile.

40 minMakes about 3 cups

Photo: NEWS.ru

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 1/2 cup stock
  • 1 bay leaf
  • Salt and black pepper

Preparation

  1. Cook onion and bell pepper in olive oil until soft.
  2. Add garlic and smoked paprika and cook 30 seconds.
  3. Add tomatoes, stock, and bay leaf.
  4. Simmer gently for 25 to 30 minutes.
  5. Remove bay leaf and season to taste.
