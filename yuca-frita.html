Yuca Frita

Crisp fried cassava with a fluffy center, served hot with salsa criolla or creamy chile sauce.

45 minServes 4–6

Photo: Goya

Ingredients

  • 2 lb fresh or frozen yuca
  • Kosher salt
  • Neutral oil, for frying
  • Lime wedges
  • Salsa criolla or ají sauce, for serving

Preparation

  1. Peel yuca and cut into thick batons, removing the woody center if present.
  2. Boil in salted water until just tender, 15 to 20 minutes. Drain and cool completely.
  3. Heat oil to 350°F.
  4. Fry yuca in batches until golden and crisp, 4 to 6 minutes.
  5. Drain, salt immediately, and serve with lime and sauce.
