American (Los Angeles)

French Dip Sandwich

The Los Angeles classic of thinly sliced roast beef on a French roll served with savory beef jus for dipping.

American (Los Angeles)Los Angeles, California, United States35 min

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb thinly sliced cooked roast beef
  • 4 French rolls
  • 4 cups beef stock
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • 1 garlic clove, smashed
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp butter

Preparation

  1. Combine stock, Worcestershire, soy sauce, garlic, and pepper; simmer 15 minutes. Remove garlic.
  2. Warm sliced beef gently in the jus without boiling.
  3. Split rolls and lightly butter and toast them.
  4. Pile warm beef into rolls.
  5. Serve immediately with hot jus in individual cups for dipping.
