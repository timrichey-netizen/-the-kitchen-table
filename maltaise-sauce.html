SAUCE · HOLLANDAISE DAUGHTER SAUCE

Maltaise

Hollandaise brightened with blood-orange juice and blanched orange zest.

15 minMakes about 1 1/2 cups

Photo: Essen & Trinken

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups prepared Hollandaise
  • 2 tbsp fresh blood-orange juice
  • 1 tsp finely grated blood-orange zest
  • 1 tsp lemon juice, if needed
  • Salt and white pepper

Preparation

  1. Warm the Hollandaise gently.
  2. Whisk in the blood-orange juice a teaspoon at a time.
  3. Stir in the zest.
  4. Taste and balance with lemon juice if the orange is very sweet.
  5. Season lightly and serve warm.
