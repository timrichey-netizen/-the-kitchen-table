Coleslaw Estilo Baja

Crunchy Baja-style cabbage slaw with carrot, cilantro, lime, crema, and jalapeño.

20 minServes 6

Photo: The Epoch Times

Ingredients

  • 4 cups shredded green cabbage
  • 1 cup shredded red cabbage
  • 1 carrot, julienned
  • 1/3 cup cilantro
  • 1 jalapeño, minced
  • 1/3 cup Mexican crema
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • Juice of 2 limes
  • Salt

Preparation

  1. Combine cabbages, carrot, cilantro, and jalapeño.
  2. Mix crema, mayonnaise, lime, and salt.
  3. Toss just before serving.
