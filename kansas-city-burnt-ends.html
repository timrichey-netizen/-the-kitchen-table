American (Missouri)

Kansas City Burnt Ends

A Missouri main centered on beef brisket point, barbecue rub, barbecue sauce, prepared in its recognizable Midwestern regional style.

American (Missouri)Missouri, United States45 min

Ingredients

  • 1 brisket point, 4–5 lb
  • 1/4 cup barbecue rub
  • 1 cup Kansas City-style barbecue sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp beef tallow or butter

Preparation

  1. Season brisket point generously with rub. Smoke at 250°F until bark is set and internal temperature is about 165°F.
  2. Wrap and continue smoking until probe-tender, about 200–205°F. Rest briefly.
  3. Cut into 1-inch cubes; toss with barbecue sauce, brown sugar, and tallow.
  4. Return uncovered to smoker 1–2 hours until dark, sticky, and caramelized.
