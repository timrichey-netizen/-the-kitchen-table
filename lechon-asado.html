Caribbean (Cuba)

Lechón Asado

A Cuba main built around pork shoulder, garlic, sour orange, oregano, prepared in its recognizable regional style.

Caribbean (Cuba)Cuba1 hr

Ingredients

  • 5 lb pork shoulder
  • 10 garlic cloves
  • 1 cup sour orange juice
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 2 tsp oregano
  • 2 tsp cumin
  • 2 tbsp salt
  • black pepper

Preparation

  1. Blend garlic, sour orange, oil, oregano, cumin, salt, and pepper. Score pork and marinate overnight.
  2. Roast at 325°F, covered for the first several hours, until fork-tender, about 4–5 hours.
  3. Uncover and raise heat to 450°F to crisp exterior. Rest before slicing or pulling.
