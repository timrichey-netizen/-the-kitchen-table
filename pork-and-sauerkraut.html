American (Pennsylvania)

Pork and Sauerkraut

A Pennsylvania main preparation centered on pork shoulder, sauerkraut, apple, caraway, presented in its recognizable regional style.

American (Pennsylvania)Pennsylvania, United States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 3 lb pork shoulder or loin
  • salt and pepper
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 2 lb sauerkraut, drained
  • 1 apple, sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 tsp caraway seeds
  • 1 cup apple cider

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 325°F. Season and brown pork in oil.
  2. Place sauerkraut, apple, onion, caraway, and cider around pork. Cover.
  3. Braise 2–3 hours until pork is tender and reaches a safe internal temperature.
  4. Slice or pull pork and serve with sauerkraut and pan juices.
