Calabacitas a la Mexicana

Zucchini with tomato, onion, serrano, corn, oregano, and optional queso fresco.

30 minServes 4

Photo: Yahoo Creators

Ingredients

  • 5 zucchini, diced
  • 2 Roma tomatoes, diced
  • 1/2 white onion, diced
  • 1 serrano, minced
  • 1 cup corn kernels
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1/2 tsp Mexican oregano
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and pepper
  • Queso fresco, optional

Preparation

  1. Cook onion and garlic in oil until soft.
  2. Add serrano and tomato; cook until tomato begins to break down.
  3. Add zucchini, corn, oregano, salt, and pepper.
  4. Cook until zucchini is tender but not mushy.
  5. Top with queso fresco if desired.
