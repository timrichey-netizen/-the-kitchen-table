Louisiana Creole

Red Beans and Rice

New Orleans-style creamy red beans slowly cooked with smoked sausage and aromatics and served over white rice.

Louisiana CreoleNew Orleans, Louisiana, United States3 hr

Ingredients

  • 1 lb dried red kidney beans, soaked overnight
  • 1 lb andouille, sliced
  • 1 onion
  • 1 bell pepper
  • 2 celery ribs, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 8 cups water or stock
  • salt, pepper, cayenne
  • cooked white rice
  • scallions

Preparation

  1. Brown sausage; remove. Cook onion, pepper, and celery in rendered fat until soft; add garlic.
  2. Add drained beans, sausage, bay, thyme, and water. Bring to boil, then simmer partially covered 2–2 1/2 hours until beans are very tender.
  3. Mash some beans against pot to make the liquid creamy. Season with salt, pepper, and cayenne.
  4. Serve over white rice with scallions.
