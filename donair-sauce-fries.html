Canadian (Nova Scotia)

Donair Sauce Fries

A Nova Scotia, Canada side centered on french fries, sweet donair sauce, prepared in its recognizable Canadian regional style.

Canadian (Nova Scotia)Nova Scotia, Canada45 min

Ingredients

  • 2 lb russet potatoes, cut into fries
  • frying oil
  • 1 can (12 oz) evaporated milk
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1/3 cup white vinegar
  • 1 tsp garlic powder

Preparation

  1. Whisk evaporated milk, sugar, and garlic powder. Gradually whisk in vinegar and refrigerate until thickened, at least 1 hour.
  2. Fry potatoes at 325°F until tender; drain. Fry again at 375°F until crisp and golden.
  3. Salt fries lightly and drizzle or serve with chilled donair sauce.
