Alder-Smoked Salmon

Pacific Northwest salmon brined and slowly smoked over alder wood for a clean, gently sweet smoke flavor.

Pacific Northwest (Washington)Washington, United States6 hr

Ingredients

  • 2 lb salmon fillet
  • 4 cups water
  • 1/3 cup kosher salt
  • 1/3 cup brown sugar
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • alder smoking chips or chunks

Preparation

  1. Dissolve salt and sugar in water; cool. Brine salmon 4 hours refrigerated.
  2. Rinse and pat dry. Refrigerate uncovered 1–2 hours until tacky pellicle forms.
  3. Heat smoker to 180–200°F with alder wood.
  4. Smoke salmon skin-side down until burnished and 135–145°F internally, about 1 1/2–3 hours depending on thickness.
  5. Rest before serving.
