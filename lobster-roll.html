American (Maine)

Lobster Roll

A classic Maine sandwich featuring maine lobster, mayonnaise, lemon, split-top roll, prepared in its recognizable regional style.

American (Maine)Maine, United States45 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lb cooked Maine lobster meat, chilled and cut in chunks
  • 1/4 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp finely diced celery, optional
  • salt and pepper
  • 4 New England split-top hot dog rolls
  • 2 tbsp butter

Preparation

  1. Fold lobster with mayonnaise, lemon, celery if using, salt, and pepper; keep cold.
  2. Butter outer sides of split-top rolls and griddle until golden.
  3. Fill warm toasted rolls generously with chilled lobster salad.
  4. Serve immediately.
