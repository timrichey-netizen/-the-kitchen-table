American (Tavern / Cracker-style)

Thin-Crust Cracker Pizza Dough

A low-hydration thin-crust dough rolled very thin and docked before baking to create a crisp, brittle, cracker-like pizza base.

American (Tavern / Cracker-style)Midwestern United States / general American1–24 hr

Ingredients

  • For two 12-inch pizzas:
  • 300 g all-purpose flour
  • 150 g water (50% hydration)
  • 6 g fine sea salt
  • 2 g instant dry yeast
  • 15 g olive oil
  • 5 g granulated sugar

Preparation

  1. Combine flour, salt, yeast, and sugar. Add water and olive oil and mix to a firm dough.
  2. Knead 5–7 minutes until smooth. Cover and rest 45–60 minutes. For more flavor, refrigerate up to 24 hours and return to cool room temperature before rolling.
  3. Divide dough in half. On a lightly floured surface, roll each portion extremely thin, about 1/16–1/8 inch, into a roughly 12-inch round.
  4. Transfer to parchment or a floured peel and dock the entire surface repeatedly with a fork to limit bubbling.
  5. For maximum crispness, par-bake on a preheated stone/steel at 475–500°F (245–260°C) for 3–4 minutes until dry but not deeply browned.
  6. Add a thin layer of toppings and return to the oven until the crust is deeply crisp and browned, typically another 5–8 minutes.
  7. Cool briefly before cutting; the crust should remain rigid and audibly crisp.
