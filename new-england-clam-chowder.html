American (Massachusetts)

New England Clam Chowder

A classic Massachusetts soup featuring clams, potatoes, salt pork, cream, prepared in its recognizable regional style.

American (Massachusetts)Massachusetts, United States45 min

Ingredients

  • 4 dozen littleneck clams
  • 4 oz salt pork or bacon, diced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 celery ribs, diced
  • 1 1/2 lb potatoes, diced
  • 3 cups clam liquor or bottled clam juice
  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • thyme
  • salt and pepper

Preparation

  1. Steam clams with a splash of water just until opened; strain and reserve liquor, then chop clam meat.
  2. Render salt pork; cook onion and celery in fat until soft. Stir in flour.
  3. Add clam liquor, potatoes, and thyme; simmer until potatoes are tender.
  4. Add milk and cream and heat gently without boiling. Stir in clams for the final 2–3 minutes. Season and serve.
