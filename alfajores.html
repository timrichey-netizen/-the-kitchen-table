DESSERT · ARGENTINIAN

Alfajores

Tender cornstarch sandwich cookies filled generously with dulce de leche and rolled in coconut.

1 hr 15 minMakes about 18

Photo source: LV12.

Ingredients

  • 1⅓ cups cornstarch
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ¼ tsp baking soda
  • ½ cup unsalted butter, softened
  • ½ cup granulated sugar
  • 3 large egg yolks
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tsp finely grated lemon zest
  • 1 tbsp brandy or milk, optional
  • 1¼ cups thick dulce de leche
  • ½ cup unsweetened shredded coconut

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 350°F. Line two baking sheets with parchment.
  2. Whisk together cornstarch, flour, baking powder, and baking soda.
  3. Cream butter and sugar until light. Beat in the egg yolks, vanilla, lemon zest, and brandy or milk if using.
  4. Mix in the dry ingredients just until a soft dough forms. Chill 20 minutes if sticky.
  5. Roll ¼ inch thick and cut into 2-inch rounds. Bake 8–10 minutes; the cookies should remain pale. Cool completely.
  6. Sandwich pairs with dulce de leche and roll the exposed filling edges in shredded coconut.
