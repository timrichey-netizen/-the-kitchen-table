SAUCE · HOLLANDAISE DAUGHTER SAUCE

Choron

A Béarnaise derivative enriched with tomato purée for a rosy, tangy sauce especially suited to steak and grilled meats.

15 minMakes about 1 1/2 cups

Photo: The Daily Meal

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups prepared Béarnaise
  • 2 to 3 tbsp smooth tomato purée or tomato paste thinned with water
  • 1 tsp lemon juice, optional
  • Salt and white pepper

Preparation

  1. Warm the Béarnaise very gently; do not let it get hot enough to split.
  2. Whisk in tomato purée a little at a time until evenly pink and balanced.
  3. Adjust with lemon juice if needed.
  4. Season lightly with salt and white pepper.
  5. Serve warm with grilled steak or other robust meats.
