Alaska King Crab Legs

Sweet Alaska king crab legs gently steamed and served simply with melted butter and lemon.

American (Alaska)Alaska, United States20 min

Ingredients

  • 3 lb cooked Alaska king crab legs, thawed
  • 6 tbsp unsalted butter, melted
  • 1 lemon, wedges

Preparation

  1. Add 1–2 inches water to a large pot and bring to a boil with a steamer insert.
  2. Add crab legs, cover, and steam until heated through, 5–7 minutes.
  3. Split shells with kitchen shears where helpful.
  4. Serve immediately with melted butter and lemon wedges.
