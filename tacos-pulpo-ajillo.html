Tacos de Pulpo al Ajillo

Tender octopus sautéed with garlic, guajillo, olive oil, and lime, then served in corn tortillas.

1 hrMakes 10 tacos

Photo: Gastrolab

Ingredients

  • 2 lb cleaned octopus
  • 1 onion, halved
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 5 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 2 guajillo chiles, sliced
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • 10 corn tortillas
  • Cilantro
  • Salsa macha

Preparation

  1. Simmer octopus gently with onion and bay until tender.
  2. Cool slightly and cut into bite-size pieces.
  3. Warm olive oil and toast garlic and guajillo gently.
  4. Add octopus and sauté until edges begin to crisp.
  5. Finish with lime and serve in tortillas with cilantro and salsa macha.
