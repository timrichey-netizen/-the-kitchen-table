ONION · MEXICAN

Cebollitas Asadas

Mexican green onions grilled until smoky, sweet, and lightly charred, then finished with lime and salt.

15 minServes 4

Photo: Maricruz Avalos

Ingredients

  • 2 bunches Mexican green onions
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 2 limes

Preparation

  1. Trim only the driest root ends and wash onions.
  2. Toss with oil and salt.
  3. Grill over medium-high heat until charred and tender, about 3 to 4 minutes per side.
  4. Serve hot with lime.
