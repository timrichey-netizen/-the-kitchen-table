SAUCE · VELOUTÉ DAUGHTER SAUCE

Poulette

A velouté-based mushroom sauce finished with egg yolk, cream, lemon, and parsley.

30 minMakes about 2 cups

Photo: Meilleur du Chef

Ingredients

  • 2 cups chicken or veal velouté
  • 8 oz mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 1/4 cup heavy cream
  • 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • Salt and white pepper

Preparation

  1. Cook the mushrooms in butter until tender and lightly browned.
  2. Add the velouté and simmer gently for 5 minutes.
  3. Whisk the yolks and cream together and temper with some hot sauce.
  4. Return the liaison to the pan and heat gently without boiling.
  5. Finish with lemon juice, parsley, salt, and white pepper.
