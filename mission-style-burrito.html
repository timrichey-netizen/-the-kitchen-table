American (San Francisco Mexican-American)

Mission-Style Burrito

A San Francisco Mission District burrito packed with carne asada, rice, beans, salsa, cheese, sour cream, and guacamole in a large flour tortilla.

American (San Francisco Mexican-American)Mission District, San Francisco, California, United States45 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lb skirt steak
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • salt and pepper
  • 4 large burrito-size flour tortillas
  • 2 cups cooked Mexican-style rice
  • 2 cups cooked pinto beans, drained
  • 1 cup shredded Monterey Jack
  • 1 cup salsa
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 1 cup guacamole
  • 1/2 cup chopped cilantro

Preparation

  1. Season steak and sear over high heat to desired doneness; rest and chop.
  2. Warm tortillas until pliable.
  3. Layer rice, beans, steak, cheese, salsa, sour cream, guacamole, and cilantro across each tortilla.
  4. Fold sides inward and roll tightly into a large cylinder.
  5. Wrap in foil and serve hot.
