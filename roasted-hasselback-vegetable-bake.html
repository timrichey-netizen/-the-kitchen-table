Roasted Hasselback Root Vegetable & Zucchini Bake

A colorful side dish with crisp edges, tender centers, olive oil, garlic, and herbs.

Cook 55–60 min

Ingredients

  • 2 zucchini
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 parsnips
  • 1 sweet potato
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • Thyme or rosemary
  • Salt and pepper

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 400°F.
  2. Thinly slice vegetables and arrange tightly in a baking dish.
  3. Brush with olive oil, garlic, herbs, salt, and pepper.
  4. Cover and bake 35 minutes. Uncover and roast 20–25 minutes more until browned and tender.
