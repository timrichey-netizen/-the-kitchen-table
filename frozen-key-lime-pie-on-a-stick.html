Frozen Key Lime Pie on a Stick

A Florida Keys treat of frozen Key lime pie wedges on sticks dipped in a crisp chocolate shell.

Floridian / Florida KeysFlorida Keys, United States6 hr

Ingredients

  • 1 prepared 9-inch Key lime pie, thoroughly chilled
  • 8 wooden ice-pop sticks
  • 12 oz semisweet chocolate
  • 2 tbsp coconut oil

Preparation

  1. Cut cold pie into 8 wedges. Insert a wooden stick through the crust into each wedge. Freeze on a lined tray until solid, at least 4 hours.
  2. Melt chocolate with coconut oil until smooth; cool slightly.
  3. Dip or spoon chocolate over each frozen wedge, allowing excess to drip away.
  4. Return to freezer until shell sets, at least 30 minutes. Keep frozen until serving.
