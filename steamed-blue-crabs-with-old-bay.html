Steamed Blue Crabs with Old Bay

A Maryland main preparation centered on blue crabs, old bay, vinegar, beer, presented in its recognizable regional style.

American (Maryland)Maryland, United States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 12 live Maryland blue crabs
  • 1 cup beer
  • 1 cup water
  • 1/2 cup cider vinegar
  • 1/3 cup Old Bay seasoning, plus more as needed

Preparation

  1. Add beer, water, and vinegar beneath a steamer rack in a large pot and bring to a boil.
  2. Layer live crabs on rack, generously seasoning each layer with Old Bay.
  3. Cover and steam 20–30 minutes until shells are bright red and meat is opaque.
  4. Serve immediately with additional seasoning.
