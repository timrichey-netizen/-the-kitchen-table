Beer & Bacon Mussels

Steamed mussels with crisp bacon, shallots, garlic, Belgian-style ale, mustard, and parsley.

30 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 2 lb mussels, scrubbed and debearded
  • 4 oz thick-cut bacon, diced
  • 1 large shallot, finely chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 cup Belgian-style blonde ale or pale ale
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1/3 cup heavy cream, optional
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • Belgian fries or crusty bread, for serving

Preparation

  1. Discard cracked mussels and any open mussels that do not close when tapped.
  2. Cook bacon in a large covered pot until crisp. Transfer most of the bacon to a plate, leaving about 2 tablespoons of fat in the pot.
  3. Add shallot and cook until soft. Add garlic and cook briefly.
  4. Pour in the beer, whisk in Dijon, and bring to a simmer. Add mussels, cover, and steam 4 to 6 minutes until opened.
  5. Stir in cream if using. Return the crisp bacon, add parsley and pepper, discard unopened mussels, and serve hot.

Notes

A malty Belgian blonde keeps the broth balanced; avoid aggressively bitter beer.
