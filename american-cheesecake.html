American Cheesecake

Classic rich American cheesecake with a buttery graham-cracker crust and smooth vanilla cream-cheese filling.

1 hr 30 min + chillServes 10 to 12

Ingredients

  • 2 cups graham-cracker crumbs
  • 5 tbsp unsalted butter, melted
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 32 oz cream cheese, room temperature
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 1 tbsp vanilla extract
  • 4 large eggs, room temperature
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • Pinch of salt

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 325°F. Mix crumbs, melted butter, and 2 tablespoons sugar; press into a 9-inch springform pan and bake 10 minutes.
  2. Beat cream cheese and sugar on low speed until smooth. Blend in sour cream, vanilla, flour, and salt.
  3. Add eggs one at a time, mixing only until incorporated.
  4. Pour over crust. Bake until edges are set but the center still wobbles slightly, 55 to 70 minutes.
  5. Turn off oven, crack the door, and cool inside 1 hour. Refrigerate at least 6 hours before serving.
