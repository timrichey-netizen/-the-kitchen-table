American (New York)

Potato Knish

A New York side preparation centered on potatoes, onion, pastry, presented in its recognizable regional style.

American (New York)New York, United States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 2 lb russet potatoes
  • 2 onions, diced
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • salt and pepper
  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/3 cup oil
  • 1/2 cup warm water
  • 1 egg for wash

Preparation

  1. Boil potatoes until tender and mash. Brown onions deeply in oil and mix with potatoes; season.
  2. Mix flour, baking powder, salt, oil, and water into soft dough; rest 30 minutes.
  3. Roll thin, portion filling, fold and seal into squat rounds.
  4. Brush with egg and bake at 375°F for 30–35 minutes until golden.
