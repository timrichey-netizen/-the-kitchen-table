Frogmore Stew

Classic South Carolina Lowcountry boil of shrimp, smoked sausage, corn, and potatoes seasoned together in one pot.

Lowcountry / American (South Carolina)St. Helena Island / South Carolina Lowcountry, United States40 min

Ingredients

  • 3 qt water
  • 1/3 cup seafood boil seasoning
  • 1 1/2 lb small red potatoes
  • 1 lb smoked sausage, 2-inch pieces
  • 4 ears corn, halved
  • 2 lb shell-on shrimp
  • 1 lemon, halved

Preparation

  1. Bring water and seafood seasoning to a rolling boil. Add potatoes and cook 10 minutes.
  2. Add sausage and cook 5 minutes.
  3. Add corn and cook 5 minutes.
  4. Add shrimp and cook 2–3 minutes, just until pink and opaque.
  5. Drain immediately and spread on a platter or paper-covered table. Squeeze lemon over the boil and serve hot.
