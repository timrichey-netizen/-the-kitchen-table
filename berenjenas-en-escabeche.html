APPETIZER · ARGENTINIAN

Berenjenas en Escabeche

Tangy marinated eggplant with vinegar, garlic, oregano, chile, and olive oil—a classic Argentine antipasto.

45 min + chillServes 6

Representative photo: Badseed / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0.

Ingredients

  • 2 medium eggplants, peeled in stripes and cut into ½-inch slices
  • 1 tbsp kosher salt
  • 1 cup white-wine vinegar
  • 1 cup water
  • 3 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • ½ tsp crushed red-pepper flakes
  • 2 bay leaves
  • ½ cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley

Preparation

  1. Sprinkle the eggplant with salt and let drain for 20 minutes, then rinse and pat dry.
  2. Bring the vinegar and water to a simmer. Cook the eggplant in batches for 3–5 minutes, until tender but not falling apart. Drain well.
  3. Layer the warm eggplant in a clean container with garlic, oregano, chile flakes, bay leaves, and parsley.
  4. Pour over the olive oil and enough of the warm vinegar mixture to keep the eggplant moist and tangy.
  5. Cool, cover, and refrigerate at least overnight before serving. Keep refrigerated; this is a refrigerator pickle, not a shelf-stable preserve.
