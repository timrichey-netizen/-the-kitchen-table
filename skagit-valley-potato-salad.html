American (Washington)

Skagit Valley Potato Salad

A creamy Northwest potato salad showcasing waxy Skagit Valley potatoes with celery, scallion, mustard, and herbs.

American (Washington)Skagit Valley, Washington, United States40 min

Ingredients

  • 2 lb waxy Skagit Valley potatoes, 1-inch pieces
  • 3/4 cup mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 2 celery ribs, diced
  • 4 scallions, sliced
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper

Preparation

  1. Cover potatoes with cold salted water; simmer until just tender, 10–15 minutes. Drain and cool slightly.
  2. Whisk mayonnaise, mustard, vinegar, salt, and pepper.
  3. Fold warm potatoes with dressing, celery, scallions, and parsley.
  4. Chill at least 1 hour before serving; adjust seasoning.
