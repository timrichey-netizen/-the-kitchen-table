SALAD · ARGENTINIAN

Ensalada Rusa

The Argentine version of Russian salad: tender potatoes, carrots, peas, and mayonnaise served well chilled.

35 min + chillServes 6

Photo: Horacio Cambeiro / Wikimedia Commons.

Ingredients

  • 2 lb Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled and cut into ½-inch cubes
  • 3 carrots, peeled and diced
  • 1 cup frozen peas
  • ¾ cup mayonnaise, plus more as needed
  • 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice or white-wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard, optional
  • Kosher salt and black pepper
  • 2 hard-boiled eggs, chopped, optional

Preparation

  1. Cook the potatoes in salted water until just tender, 8–10 minutes. Drain and cool.
  2. Cook the carrots until tender but not mushy, then add the peas for the final minute. Drain and cool.
  3. Whisk mayonnaise with lemon juice or vinegar and Dijon, if using.
  4. Fold the potatoes, carrots, peas, and eggs, if using, into the dressing. Season with salt and pepper.
  5. Chill for at least 1 hour before serving.
