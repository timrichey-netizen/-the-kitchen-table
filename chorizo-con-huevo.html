Chorizo con Huevo

Mexican chorizo and soft scrambled eggs served with tortillas, beans, and fresh salsa.

20 minServes 4

Photo: Teresa's Recipes

Ingredients

  • 10 oz Mexican chorizo
  • 8 eggs
  • 1/4 white onion, diced
  • Salt
  • Warm tortillas
  • Refried beans
  • Pico de gallo or salsa
  • Cilantro

Preparation

  1. Brown chorizo, breaking it into small pieces.
  2. Add onion and cook until soft.
  3. Beat eggs lightly and pour into skillet.
  4. Scramble gently until just set.
  5. Serve with tortillas, refried beans, pico de gallo or salsa, and cilantro.
