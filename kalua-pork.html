Kālua Pork

Hawaiian-style kālua pork cooked slowly until smoky, succulent, and shreddable, adapting the traditional imu result for a home oven.

Native HawaiianHawaiʻi, United States8 hr

Ingredients

  • 5 lb bone-in pork shoulder
  • 1 tbsp coarse Hawaiian sea salt or kosher salt
  • 1 tbsp natural liquid smoke
  • 1/2 cup water

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 300°F. Pierce pork in several places and rub all over with salt and liquid smoke.
  2. Place in a covered Dutch oven with water.
  3. Roast covered 5–6 hours, turning once, until fork-tender and easily shredded.
  4. Rest 20 minutes, remove excess fat and bone, and shred meat into cooking juices.
  5. Taste for salt and serve hot. For an authentic traditional preparation, kālua pig is cooked in an imu.
