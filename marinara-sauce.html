Marinara

A simple Italian tomato sauce of tomatoes, garlic, olive oil, herbs, and a gentle simmer.

35 minMakes about 3 cups

Photo: Pinterest

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 8 basil leaves
  • Pinch of red pepper flakes, optional
  • Salt and black pepper

Preparation

  1. Warm olive oil and gently cook garlic until fragrant.
  2. Add tomatoes, oregano, and red pepper flakes if using.
  3. Simmer uncovered for 20 to 25 minutes.
  4. Stir in basil near the end.
  5. Season with salt and pepper.
