Caribbean (Jamaica)

Jerk Chicken

A Jamaica main built around chicken, scotch bonnet, allspice, thyme, prepared in its recognizable regional style.

Caribbean (Jamaica)Jamaica1 hr

Ingredients

  • 4 lb chicken pieces
  • 6 scallions
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 Scotch bonnet
  • 2 tbsp fresh thyme
  • 2 tsp ground allspice
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp nutmeg
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp lime juice
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • salt

Preparation

  1. Blend scallions, garlic, Scotch bonnet, thyme, allspice, cinnamon, nutmeg, soy, lime, sugar, oil, and salt.
  2. Coat chicken and marinate at least 4 hours, preferably overnight.
  3. Grill over medium charcoal or wood heat, turning often, until charred and chicken reaches 165°F, 35–45 minutes. Rest briefly.
