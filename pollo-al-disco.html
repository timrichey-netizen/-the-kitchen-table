CHICKEN · ARGENTINIAN

Pollo al Disco

Rustic Argentine chicken braised in a wide pan with peppers, onion, white wine, tomato, and peas.

1 hrServes 6

BRAISE & SEAR

Meat tip: Brown the chicken first for flavor, then finish gently in the sauce so the meat stays juicy while the braising liquid concentrates.

See the Meat Cooking Guide →

Ingredients

  • 3 lb chicken thighs and drumsticks
  • Kosher salt and black pepper
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 green bell pepper, sliced
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 cup dry white wine
  • 1½ cups crushed tomatoes
  • 1 cup chicken stock
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp dried oregano
  • 1 cup peas
  • Chopped parsley

Preparation

  1. Season the chicken and brown it well in a wide skillet or discada pan. Remove temporarily.
  2. Add the onion and peppers and sauté until softened, then add the garlic.
  3. Pour in the wine, tomatoes, and stock and stir in paprika and oregano.
  4. Return the chicken to the pan and simmer uncovered for 35–40 minutes, turning occasionally.
  5. Add peas during the final 5 minutes.
  6. Finish with chopped parsley and serve with bread or potatoes.
