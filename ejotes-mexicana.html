GREEN BEANS · MEXICAN

Ejotes a la Mexicana

Blistered green beans with tomato, onion, jalapeño, garlic, and queso fresco.

25 minServes 4

Photo: We All Eat Together

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb green beans
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1/2 onion
  • 2 Roma tomatoes
  • 1 jalapeño
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1/2 cup queso fresco
  • Salt and pepper

Preparation

  1. Blister green beans in a hot skillet; remove.
  2. Cook onion, jalapeño, and garlic.
  3. Add tomato and cook until saucy.
  4. Return beans and cook until tender-crisp.
  5. Finish with queso fresco.
