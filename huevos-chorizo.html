Huevos con Chorizo

Soft scrambled eggs folded with browned Mexican chorizo and served with warm tortillas and salsa.

20 minServes 4

Photo: Santoku Knives

Ingredients

  • 10 oz Mexican chorizo
  • 8 eggs
  • 1/4 white onion, diced
  • 1 tbsp oil if needed
  • Salt
  • Warm tortillas
  • Salsa roja or salsa verde
  • Cilantro

Preparation

  1. Brown chorizo in a skillet, breaking it into pieces.
  2. Add onion and cook until soft.
  3. Beat eggs lightly and pour into skillet.
  4. Scramble gently until just set.
  5. Serve with tortillas, salsa, and cilantro.
