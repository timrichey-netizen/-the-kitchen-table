American (Washington)

Roasted Yakima Asparagus

Fresh Yakima Valley asparagus roasted simply with olive oil, lemon, salt, and black pepper.

American (Washington)Yakima Valley, Washington, United States20 min

Ingredients

  • 2 lb Yakima Valley asparagus, trimmed
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 lemon

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 425°F.
  2. Arrange asparagus in a single layer on sheet pan. Toss with olive oil, salt, and pepper.
  3. Roast until bright green and tender with lightly browned tips, 10–15 minutes depending on thickness.
  4. Finish with finely grated lemon zest and a squeeze of lemon juice.
