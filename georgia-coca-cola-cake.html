Southern American (Georgia)

Coca-Cola Cake

Moist Southern chocolate sheet cake made with Coca-Cola and finished with warm fudgy pecan icing.

Southern American (Georgia)Georgia, United States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 2 cups flour
  • 2 cups sugar
  • 1 cup Coca-Cola
  • 1 cup butter, divided
  • 1/4 cup cocoa plus 3 tbsp for icing
  • 1/2 cup buttermilk
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 4 cups powdered sugar
  • 1/2 cup chopped pecans

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 350°F. Whisk flour and sugar. Bring Coca-Cola, 1/2 cup butter, and 1/4 cup cocoa to a boil; pour over dry ingredients.
  2. Stir in buttermilk, eggs, baking soda, and vanilla. Bake in greased 9x13 pan 30–35 minutes.
  3. For icing, heat remaining butter with 3 tbsp cocoa and 6 tbsp Coca-Cola. Whisk into powdered sugar and pecans.
  4. Spread warm icing over warm cake; cool before slicing.
