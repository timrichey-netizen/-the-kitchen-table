Alaska Wild Rice Pilaf

A hearty Alaska-inspired wild rice pilaf with mushrooms, onion, celery, and herbs.

American (Alaska)Alaska, United States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups wild rice blend
  • 3 cups chicken or vegetable stock
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 8 oz mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 2 celery ribs, diced
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • black pepper
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley

Preparation

  1. Rinse rice. Combine with stock and bring to boil; cover and simmer according to blend directions until tender, about 40–50 minutes.
  2. Melt butter in skillet and sauté mushrooms until browned. Add onion and celery and cook until tender.
  3. Add thyme, salt, and pepper.
  4. Fold vegetables into cooked rice and finish with parsley.
