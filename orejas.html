Orejas

Mexican bakery palmiers with crisp caramelized layers and a distinctive ear shape.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb puff pastry
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1 tsp cinnamon

Method

  1. Mix sugar and cinnamon and coat the work surface generously.
  2. Roll pastry lightly in sugar, fold both long edges toward the center twice, then fold together.
  3. Slice into ears and place on a lined pan.
  4. Bake at 400°F, turning once, until crisp and caramelized.
