Cholla Bud Salad

A Sonoran Desert salad centered on carefully processed, rehydrated cholla buds (ciolim), an important traditional Tohono O'odham food.

Arizona / SonoranArizona, United States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 2 cups commercially cleaned and dried cholla buds
  • water for soaking/cooking
  • 1 tomato, diced
  • 1/4 red onion, finely diced
  • 2 tbsp lime juice
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • salt
  • 2 tbsp cilantro

Preparation

  1. Use only professionally cleaned/de-spined food-grade cholla buds. Soak overnight, then simmer in fresh water until tender, about 45–60 minutes; drain and cool.
  2. Combine buds with tomato and onion.
  3. Dress with lime juice, olive oil, and salt.
  4. Fold in cilantro and chill briefly before serving.
