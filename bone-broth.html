Bone Broth

Long-simmered mixed bone broth with pronounced gelatin and body.

Broth & StockTechnique

Ingredients

  • 4 lb mixed meaty bones
  • 2 tbsp cider vinegar
  • 1 onion
  • 2 carrots
  • 2 celery ribs
  • 4 qt water

Preparation

  1. Roast bones at 425°F until browned.
  2. Transfer to a pot with vinegar, vegetables, and cold water.
  3. Simmer very gently 8–12 hours, adding water if necessary.
  4. Strain and chill quickly.
