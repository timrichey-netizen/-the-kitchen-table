Pumpkin Pie

Classic American pumpkin pie with warm autumn spices in a flaky pastry crust.

1 hr 20 minServes 8

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

Ingredients

  • 1 unbaked 9-inch pie crust
  • 1 can (15 oz) pumpkin puree
  • 3/4 cup packed brown sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 1/4 cups evaporated milk
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp ground ginger
  • 1/4 tsp ground nutmeg
  • 1/8 tsp ground cloves
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • Whipped cream, for serving

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 425°F and fit pie crust into a 9-inch pie plate.
  2. Whisk pumpkin, brown sugar, eggs, evaporated milk, spices, and salt until smooth.
  3. Pour filling into crust. Bake 15 minutes.
  4. Reduce oven to 350°F and continue baking 35 to 45 minutes, until the edges are set and the center jiggles slightly.
  5. Cool completely before slicing. Serve with whipped cream.
