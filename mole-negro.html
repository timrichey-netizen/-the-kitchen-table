Mole Negro

A dark Oaxacan mole with deeply toasted chiles, spices, nuts, seeds, fruit, tortilla, and chocolate.

2 hr 30 minMakes about 6 cups

Photo: Reporte Noticias

Ingredients

  • 3 chilhuacle negro chiles, or substitute ancho and pasilla
  • 3 mulato chiles
  • 2 pasilla chiles
  • 2 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 1/4 cup almonds
  • 1/4 cup raisins
  • 1 corn tortilla
  • 1 slice day-old bread
  • 2 Roma tomatoes
  • 1/2 onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 2 cloves
  • 1/2 tsp oregano
  • 3 cups chicken stock
  • 1 oz Mexican chocolate
  • 2 tbsp lard or neutral oil
  • Salt, to taste

Preparation

  1. Toast chiles carefully until very dark but not burned; soak until soft.
  2. Toast sesame, almonds, raisins, tortilla, bread, and spices separately.
  3. Roast tomatoes, onion, and garlic.
  4. Blend everything with stock until exceptionally smooth.
  5. Fry the mole paste in lard or oil, stirring constantly.
  6. Add remaining stock and simmer gently for at least 1 hour; stir in chocolate and salt, adjusting until dark, savory, and balanced.
