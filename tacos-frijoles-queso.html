Tacos de Frijoles con Queso

Warm corn tortillas filled with creamy refried beans, queso fresco, onion, cilantro, and salsa.

25 minMakes 12 tacos

Photo: IBERO

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked black or pinto beans
  • 1 tbsp lard or neutral oil
  • 1/4 white onion, finely chopped
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 1/2 tsp cumin, optional
  • Salt
  • 12 corn tortillas
  • 6 oz queso fresco, crumbled
  • Cilantro
  • Salsa verde or salsa roja

Preparation

  1. Heat lard or oil and cook onion until soft; add garlic.
  2. Add beans and mash to a creamy but textured consistency, loosening with a little bean liquid as needed.
  3. Season to taste.
  4. Warm tortillas on a comal.
  5. Fill with beans and top with queso fresco, cilantro, and salsa.
