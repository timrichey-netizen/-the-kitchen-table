CORN · MEXICAN

Elote

Charred Mexican street corn coated with crema-mayo, cotija, chile, cilantro, and lime.

25 minServes 6

Photo: Punchfork

Ingredients

  • 6 ears corn
  • 1/3 cup mayonnaise
  • 1/3 cup Mexican crema
  • 1 cup cotija cheese
  • 1 tsp chile powder
  • 1/2 cup cilantro
  • 2 limes
  • Salt

Preparation

  1. Grill corn until tender and charred in spots.
  2. Mix mayonnaise and crema.
  3. Brush corn generously with the mixture.
  4. Roll or sprinkle with cotija and chile powder.
  5. Finish with cilantro, lime, and salt.
