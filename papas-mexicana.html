POTATO · MEXICAN

Papas a la Mexicana

Crisp-tender potatoes with tomato, onion, jalapeño, and cilantro in the colors of the Mexican flag.

35 minServes 6

Photo: Recipe Blossom

Ingredients

  • 2 lb potatoes, diced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 white onion, diced
  • 2 Roma tomatoes, diced
  • 1 jalapeño, sliced
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 1/4 cup cilantro
  • Salt and pepper

Preparation

  1. Parboil potatoes until barely tender; drain.
  2. Heat oil and brown potatoes in a wide skillet.
  3. Add onion, jalapeño, and garlic; cook until fragrant.
  4. Add tomatoes and cook just until softened.
  5. Finish with cilantro, salt, and pepper.
