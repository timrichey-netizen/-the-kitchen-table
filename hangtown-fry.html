American (California Gold Rush)

Hangtown Fry

A Gold Rush-era California omelet combining oysters, eggs, and crisp bacon.

American (California Gold Rush)Placerville, California, United States30 min

Ingredients

  • 8 shucked oysters, drained
  • 6 large eggs
  • 4 bacon strips, chopped
  • 1/3 cup flour
  • salt and pepper
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp chopped parsley

Preparation

  1. Cook bacon until crisp; drain, reserving 1 tbsp fat.
  2. Season oysters and dust lightly with flour. Brown oysters in reserved bacon fat about 1 minute per side.
  3. Beat eggs with salt and pepper.
  4. Melt butter in skillet, add eggs, and cook until nearly set. Scatter oysters and bacon over half.
  5. Fold omelet, finish gently until set, and garnish with parsley.
