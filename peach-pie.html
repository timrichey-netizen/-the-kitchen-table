Peach Pie

Juicy peach pie with warm spice and a flaky golden crust.

1 hr 25 minServes 8

Ingredients

  • 2 pie crusts
  • 6 cups sliced ripe peaches
  • ¾ cup sugar
  • ¼ cup cornstarch
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • Pinch of nutmeg
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1 egg for egg wash

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 425°F and fit one crust into a pie plate.
  2. Toss peaches with sugar, cornstarch, lemon, cinnamon, nutmeg, and vanilla.
  3. Fill crust and top with second crust or lattice; brush with egg wash.
  4. Bake 20 minutes; reduce to 375°F and bake 35–45 minutes.
  5. Cool completely before slicing.
