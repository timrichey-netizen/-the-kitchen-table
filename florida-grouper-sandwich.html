Florida Grouper Sandwich

A Gulf Coast Florida grouper fillet cooked simply and served on a toasted bun with lettuce, tomato, and tartar sauce.

American (Florida Gulf Coast)Florida Gulf Coast, United States25 min

Ingredients

  • 4 grouper fillets, 5–6 oz each
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • salt and pepper
  • 4 sandwich buns
  • 4 lettuce leaves
  • 4 tomato slices
  • 1/2 cup tartar sauce
  • lemon wedges

Preparation

  1. Pat grouper dry and season with paprika, salt, and pepper.
  2. Heat oil in skillet over medium-high heat and cook fish 3–4 minutes per side until opaque and 145°F.
  3. Toast buns. Spread with tartar sauce and layer lettuce, tomato, and grouper.
  4. Close sandwiches and serve with lemon.
