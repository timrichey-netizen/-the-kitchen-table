PASTA · SICILIAN

Pasta alla Norma

Sicilian pasta with fried eggplant, tomato, basil, and salty ricotta salata.

50 minServes 4

PASTA GUIDE

Recommended pasta: Rigatoni or penne

See pasta shapes, sauces & pairings →

Ingredients

  • 12 oz rigatoni or spaghetti
  • 1 large eggplant, cubed
  • Olive oil
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • Fresh basil
  • ¾ cup ricotta salata
  • Salt and pepper

Preparation

  1. Salt eggplant, pat dry, and fry or roast until golden.
  2. Cook garlic in olive oil; add tomatoes and simmer 20 minutes with basil.
  3. Cook pasta al dente and toss with sauce and half the eggplant.
  4. Top with remaining eggplant, ricotta salata, and basil.
