DESSERT · ARGENTINIAN

Flan con Dulce de Leche

Silky caramel custard served the Argentine way with a generous spoonful of dulce de leche.

1 hr 15 min + chillServes 8

Photo source: Infobae.

Ingredients

  • ¾ cup granulated sugar, for caramel
  • 4 large eggs
  • 2 large egg yolks
  • 1 can (14 oz) sweetened condensed milk
  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Pinch of salt
  • ¾ cup dulce de leche, for serving

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 325°F. Place an 8-inch round cake pan inside a larger roasting pan.
  2. Cook the sugar in a saucepan over medium heat until melted and deep amber. Immediately pour into the cake pan and swirl to coat the bottom.
  3. Whisk the eggs and yolks gently. Add condensed milk, whole milk, vanilla, and salt, mixing without incorporating too much air.
  4. Strain the custard into the caramel-lined pan.
  5. Pour hot water into the roasting pan to come halfway up the sides of the flan pan. Bake 50–60 minutes, until the edges are set and the center still jiggles slightly.
  6. Cool, refrigerate at least 4 hours, then invert onto a platter. Serve with dulce de leche.
