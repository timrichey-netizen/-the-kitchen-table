BROTH & STOCK · FRENCH

Consommé

Crystal-clear clarified meat broth made with a traditional egg-white raft.

Broth & StockFrench

Ingredients

  • 2 qt well-flavored cold beef or chicken broth
  • 1 lb lean ground meat matching broth
  • 4 egg whites
  • 1 onion, minced
  • 1 carrot, minced
  • 1 celery rib, minced
  • 1 tomato, chopped

Preparation

  1. Mix cold broth with meat, egg whites, and vegetables.
  2. Heat slowly while stirring until the raft begins to form.
  3. Stop stirring; barely simmer 45–60 minutes, making a small opening in the raft.
  4. Ladle clear liquid through the opening and strain through damp cheesecloth.
