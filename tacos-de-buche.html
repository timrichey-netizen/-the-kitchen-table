PORK · MEXICAN

Tacos de Buche

Tender pork stomach simmered with aromatics, then chopped and crisped for taquería-style tacos.

2 hr 30 minMakes 12 tacos

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

Ingredients

  • 2 lb cleaned pork stomach
  • 1/2 white onion
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 1 tbsp kosher salt
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 12 tortillas
  • Onion, cilantro, radish, lime, salsa

Preparation

  1. Rinse pork stomach thoroughly.
  2. Simmer with onion, garlic, bay, oregano, and salt until very tender.
  3. Drain and cool slightly.
  4. Chop finely and crisp on a hot griddle.
  5. Serve in tortillas with onion, cilantro, radish, lime, and salsa.
