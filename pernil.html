Caribbean (Puerto Rico)

Pernil

A Puerto Rico main built around pork shoulder, garlic, oregano, citrus, prepared in its recognizable regional style.

Caribbean (Puerto Rico)Puerto Rico1 hr

Ingredients

  • 6 lb pork shoulder, skin-on if available
  • 10 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp oregano
  • 2 tbsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 cup sour orange or orange-lime juice
  • 1/4 cup olive oil

Preparation

  1. Make deep cuts in pork. Mash garlic, oregano, salt, pepper, citrus, and oil; rub into cuts and over pork. Marinate overnight.
  2. Roast at 325°F until very tender, 4–5 hours.
  3. Increase oven to 450°F to crisp skin/exterior. Rest 20 minutes before carving.
