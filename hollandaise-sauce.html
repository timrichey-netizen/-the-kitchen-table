SAUCE · FRENCH MOTHER SAUCE

Hollandaise

A warm emulsion of egg yolks, clarified butter, and lemon juice: rich, glossy, and delicately tangy.

15 minMakes about 1½ cups

Photo: Küchengötter

Ingredients

  • 3 large egg yolks
  • 1 tbsp cold water
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 10 tbsp warm clarified butter
  • Pinch cayenne or white pepper
  • Kosher salt

Preparation

  1. Whisk yolks, water, and lemon juice until slightly thickened.
  2. Whisk over barely simmering water until pale and thick.
  3. Slowly drizzle in warm clarified butter while whisking.
  4. Season with salt, cayenne, and more lemon to taste.
  5. Keep warm—not hot—and serve promptly.
