American (Indiana)

Sugar Cream Pie

A Indiana dessert centered on cream, sugar, flour, butter, nutmeg, prepared in its recognizable Midwestern regional style.

American (Indiana)Indiana, United States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 1 unbaked 9-inch pie crust
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1/4 cup flour
  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 2 tbsp butter, melted
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • freshly grated nutmeg

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 375°F. Whisk sugar and flour, then gradually whisk in cream, butter, and vanilla.
  2. Pour into crust and sprinkle generously with nutmeg.
  3. Bake 45–55 minutes until filling is set around edges with a slight center wobble.
  4. Cool completely before slicing.
