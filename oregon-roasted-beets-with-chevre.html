Oregon Roasted Beets with Chèvre

Earthy roasted Oregon beets paired with creamy chèvre, toasted hazelnuts, and a bright vinaigrette.

American (Oregon)Oregon, United States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 2 lb mixed beets
  • 1 tbsp olive oil plus 2 tbsp for dressing
  • salt and pepper
  • 4 oz chèvre
  • 1/3 cup toasted Oregon hazelnuts
  • 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 400°F. Rub beets with 1 tbsp oil, wrap in foil, and roast until knife-tender, 45–60 minutes.
  2. Cool enough to handle, peel, and cut into wedges.
  3. Whisk remaining olive oil, vinegar, mustard, salt, and pepper. Toss beets gently with dressing.
  4. Arrange on platter and finish with chèvre and hazelnuts.
