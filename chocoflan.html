Chocoflan

The 'impossible cake': chocolate cake and caramel flan baked together in dramatic layers.

Ingredients

  • Caramel sauce
  • 1 chocolate cake batter
  • 5 eggs
  • 1 can evaporated milk
  • 1 can condensed milk
  • 4 oz cream cheese
  • 1 tbsp vanilla

Method

  1. Coat a Bundt pan with caramel and add chocolate cake batter.
  2. Blend eggs, milks, cream cheese, and vanilla; carefully pour over batter.
  3. Cover and bake in a water bath at 350°F until set.
  4. Chill completely and invert; the layers will have switched while baking.
