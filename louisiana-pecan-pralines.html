Louisiana Pecan Pralines

Classic New Orleans pralines: creamy, caramelized sugar-and-cream candies packed with toasted pecans.

Louisiana CreoleNew Orleans, Louisiana, United States35 min

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups granulated sugar
  • 3/4 cup light brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 4 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 1/2 cups toasted pecan halves
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1/4 tsp salt

Preparation

  1. Line baking sheets with parchment.
  2. Combine sugars, cream, butter, and salt in a heavy saucepan. Cook over medium heat, stirring, until dissolved, then boil to 236–240°F.
  3. Remove from heat; stir in vanilla and pecans. Beat with a wooden spoon until mixture thickens and loses some gloss.
  4. Quickly drop spoonfuls onto parchment. Cool completely until firm.
