Poke Bowl

A modern Hawaiʻi poke bowl of fresh raw ahi seasoned with shoyu, sesame oil, onion, and scallion over rice.

Hawaiʻi LocalHawaiʻi, United States30 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lb sashimi-grade ahi tuna, 3/4-inch cubes
  • 3 tbsp shoyu/soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1/4 cup thinly sliced sweet onion
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds
  • 4 cups cooked short-grain rice

Preparation

  1. Keep ahi thoroughly chilled.
  2. Combine shoyu, sesame oil, sweet onion, scallions, and sesame seeds.
  3. Fold in tuna gently until evenly coated. Refrigerate 10–15 minutes.
  4. Divide warm or room-temperature rice among bowls.
  5. Spoon poke over rice and serve immediately.
