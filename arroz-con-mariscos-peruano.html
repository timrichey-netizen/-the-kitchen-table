SEAFOOD · PERUVIAN

Arroz con Mariscos

Peruvian seafood rice with shrimp, squid, shellfish, ají panca, ají amarillo, tomato, peas, and cilantro.

50 minServes 6

Photo: DiDi Food Perú

Ingredients

  • 2 cups long-grain rice
  • 1 lb mixed seafood: shrimp, squid, mussels, scallops
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tbsp ají panca paste
  • 1 tbsp ají amarillo paste
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 3 cups seafood stock
  • 1/2 cup peas
  • 1/2 red bell pepper, diced
  • 1/3 cup chopped cilantro
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Lime wedges

Preparation

  1. Sauté seafood briefly in hot oil until just underdone; remove.
  2. Cook onion and bell pepper until soft. Add garlic, ají panca, ají amarillo, and tomato paste.
  3. Stir in rice and coat well, then add seafood stock and season.
  4. Cover and simmer until rice is nearly tender.
  5. Fold in seafood and peas and cook just until seafood is done and rice is tender.
  6. Finish with cilantro and serve with lime.
