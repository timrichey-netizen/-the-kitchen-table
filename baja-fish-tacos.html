Baja Fish Tacos

Crisp beer-battered fish tacos with shredded cabbage, lime crema, pico de gallo, and salsa.

45 minMakes 10 tacos

Photo: Muy Delish

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb firm white fish, cut into strips
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup cornstarch
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 cup cold Mexican lager or sparkling water
  • Neutral oil, for frying
  • 10 corn tortillas
  • Shredded cabbage
  • Lime crema
  • Pico de gallo
  • Lime wedges

Preparation

  1. Pat fish very dry and season lightly.
  2. Whisk flour, cornstarch, baking powder, chili powder, and salt. Add cold beer just before frying to make a thin batter.
  3. Heat oil to 350°F.
  4. Dip fish in batter and fry in batches until crisp and cooked through, about 3 to 4 minutes.
  5. Drain briefly and serve in warm tortillas with cabbage, lime crema, pico de gallo, and lime.
