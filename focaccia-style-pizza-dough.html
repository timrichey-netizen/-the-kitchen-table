Italian (Focaccia-style)

Focaccia-Style Pizza Dough

An olive-oil-rich, high-hydration focaccia-style pizza dough with a soft open crumb, crisp oily base, and characteristic dimpled surface.

Italian (Focaccia-style)Liguria-inspired, Italy4–24 hr

Ingredients

  • For one 9 x 13-inch pan:
  • 450 g bread flour
  • 340 g water (76% hydration)
  • 9 g fine sea salt
  • 4 g instant dry yeast
  • 30 g extra-virgin olive oil, plus 30 g for pan and finishing

Preparation

  1. Combine flour, yeast, and salt. Add water and 30 g olive oil and mix until a wet, sticky dough forms.
  2. Rest 15 minutes, then perform 3–4 rounds of stretch-and-folds at 15-minute intervals until the dough strengthens.
  3. Cover and rise 1–2 hours, or refrigerate 12–24 hours for deeper flavor.
  4. Generously oil a 9 x 13-inch pan. Transfer dough to the pan and gently stretch toward the edges. Rest and repeat if needed.
  5. Proof until very puffy and bubbly, 1–2 hours.
  6. Drizzle the surface with some of the remaining olive oil. With oiled fingertips, press deep dimples throughout without completely deflating the dough.
  7. Add desired pizza toppings sparingly and bake at 450–475°F (230–245°C) for 18–25 minutes until golden, crisp underneath, and airy inside. Finish with remaining olive oil if desired.
