Paletas Mexicanas

Fresh-fruit Mexican ice pops with bright lime and visible pieces of fruit.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups ripe fruit
  • 1/3 cup sugar or honey
  • 1/2 cup water or milk
  • 2 tbsp lime juice
  • Pinch salt

Method

  1. Blend two-thirds of the fruit with sugar, liquid, lime, and salt.
  2. Fold in remaining diced fruit.
  3. Pour into paleta molds and insert sticks.
  4. Freeze until solid, at least 6 hours.
