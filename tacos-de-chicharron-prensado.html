Tacos de Chicharrón Prensado

Pressed pork crackling simmered in a guajillo-red chile sauce until rich and tender, then served in corn tortillas.

40 minMakes 12 tacos

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb chicharrón prensado, chopped
  • 5 guajillo chiles
  • 2 ancho chiles
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1/4 onion
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 2 cups chicken or pork stock
  • 12 corn tortillas
  • Chopped onion, cilantro, lime

Preparation

  1. Toast and soak guajillo and ancho chiles.
  2. Blend with garlic, onion, oregano, salt, and stock until smooth; strain.
  3. Simmer sauce 10 minutes.
  4. Add chopped chicharrón prensado and cook until tender and the sauce clings, 10 to 15 minutes.
  5. Serve in warm tortillas with onion, cilantro, and lime.
