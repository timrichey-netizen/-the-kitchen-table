SAUCE · HOLLANDAISE DAUGHTER SAUCE

Béarnaise

Hollandaise flavored with a shallot, vinegar, tarragon, and chervil reduction—the classic steak sauce.

25 minMakes about 1 1/2 cups

Photo: Food Faith Fitness

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups prepared Hollandaise
  • 1/4 cup white wine vinegar
  • 1/4 cup dry white wine
  • 1 small shallot, minced
  • 1 tsp crushed black peppercorns
  • 1 tbsp chopped fresh tarragon
  • 1 tbsp chopped fresh chervil or parsley
  • Salt, to taste

Preparation

  1. Combine vinegar, wine, shallot, peppercorns, and half the tarragon in a small saucepan.
  2. Reduce until only 2 to 3 tablespoons of liquid remain, then strain and cool slightly.
  3. Whisk the reduction into warm Hollandaise.
  4. Stir in the remaining tarragon and chervil.
  5. Taste for salt and acidity and serve warm.
