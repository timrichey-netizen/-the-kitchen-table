Bucatini Amatriciana

Hollow bucatini coated with guanciale-rich tomato sauce and Pecorino Romano.

40 minServes 4

PASTA GUIDE

Recommended pasta: Bucatini

See pasta shapes, sauces & pairings →

Ingredients

  • 12 oz bucatini
  • 5 oz guanciale, diced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • ½ tsp red-pepper flakes
  • 1 can (28 oz) tomatoes
  • 1 cup Pecorino Romano
  • Salt

Preparation

  1. Render guanciale until crisp and add red pepper.
  2. Add tomatoes and simmer 15–20 minutes.
  3. Cook bucatini al dente and toss with sauce and a splash of pasta water.
  4. Finish off heat with Pecorino.
