MEAT · ARGENTINIAN

Milanesa Napolitana

Crisp breaded beef cutlets topped with tomato sauce, ham, melted mozzarella, and oregano.

45 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 4 thin beef cutlets
  • Kosher salt and black pepper
  • ½ cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 1½ cups breadcrumbs
  • Neutral oil, for shallow frying
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 8 slices ham
  • 8 slices mozzarella
  • Dried oregano

Preparation

  1. Pound the beef to about ¼ inch thick and season with salt and pepper.
  2. Dredge each cutlet in flour, then beaten egg, then breadcrumbs.
  3. Shallow-fry in hot oil until golden on both sides; drain well.
  4. Arrange the cutlets on a baking sheet and top each with tomato sauce, ham, and mozzarella.
  5. Sprinkle with oregano and bake at 425°F for 8–10 minutes, until the cheese is melted and lightly browned.
