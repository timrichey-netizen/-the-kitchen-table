Chocolate Lava Cake

Individual dark chocolate cakes with tender edges and a molten center.

25 minServes 8

Ingredients

  • 4 oz bittersweet chocolate
  • ½ cup unsalted butter
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 egg yolks
  • ¼ cup sugar
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • Pinch of salt
  • Butter and cocoa for ramekins

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Butter and cocoa four ramekins.
  2. Melt chocolate and butter together until smooth.
  3. Whisk eggs, yolks, sugar, and salt; fold in chocolate, then flour.
  4. Divide into ramekins and bake 10–12 minutes.
  5. Rest 1 minute, invert, and serve immediately.
