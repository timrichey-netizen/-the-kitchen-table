Ensalada de Nopales

Tender nopales tossed with tomato, onion, cilantro, jalapeño, lime, and queso fresco.

25 minServes 6

Photo: Pinterest

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb cleaned nopales, diced
  • 2 Roma tomatoes, diced
  • 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 jalapeño, minced
  • 1/2 cup cilantro
  • Juice of 2 limes
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 cup queso fresco
  • Salt

Preparation

  1. Boil or sauté nopales until tender; rinse and cool if boiled.
  2. Combine with tomato, onion, jalapeño, and cilantro.
  3. Dress with lime, oil, and salt.
  4. Fold in queso fresco.
  5. Serve chilled or at room temperature.
