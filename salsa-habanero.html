Salsa Habanero

A fiery Yucatán-style habanero salsa with citrus, onion, garlic, and salt.

20 minMakes about 1 1/2 cups

Photo: Pati Jinich

Ingredients

  • 4 to 6 habanero chiles
  • 1/2 white or red onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1/3 cup sour orange juice, or equal parts orange and lime juice
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 tsp kosher salt

Preparation

  1. Char habaneros, onion, and garlic in a dry skillet or comal.
  2. Cool slightly and remove pepper stems.
  3. Blend or pound with citrus juice and salt.
  4. Whisk in oil if desired for a slightly emulsified texture.
  5. Serve sparingly; this salsa is very hot.
