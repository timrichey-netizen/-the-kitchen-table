Verdolagas

Purslane sautéed with tomato, onion, garlic, and chile for a tangy green side dish.

25 minServes 4

Photo: Secret Recipe Box

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb verdolagas, cleaned
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1/2 onion, diced
  • 2 Roma tomatoes, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 serrano, minced
  • Salt

Preparation

  1. Cook onion and serrano in oil until soft.
  2. Add garlic and tomato and cook until saucy.
  3. Add verdolagas and salt.
  4. Cover and cook until stems are tender.
