Pastel de Elote

Tender Mexican sweet-corn cake with a moist, lightly caramelized crumb.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups corn kernels
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 can sweetened condensed milk
  • 1/2 cup butter, melted
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1 cup flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • Pinch salt

Method

  1. Blend corn, eggs, condensed milk, butter, and vanilla until mostly smooth.
  2. Fold in flour, baking powder, and salt.
  3. Bake in a greased pan at 350°F until golden and a tester emerges clean.
  4. Cool slightly and serve plain or with crema.
