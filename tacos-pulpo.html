Tacos de Pulpo

Tender octopus charred on a hot grill or skillet and served with lime, cilantro, onion, avocado, and salsa.

1 hrMakes 10 tacos

Photo: Gastrolab

Ingredients

  • 2 lb cleaned octopus
  • 1 onion, halved
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 10 corn tortillas
  • Cilantro
  • Diced onion
  • Avocado
  • Lime wedges
  • Salsa verde or salsa macha

Preparation

  1. Simmer octopus gently with onion and bay until tender, 40 to 60 minutes depending on size.
  2. Cool slightly and cut tentacles into bite-size pieces.
  3. Toss with olive oil, paprika, garlic, and salt.
  4. Char quickly on a very hot grill or skillet.
  5. Serve in warm tortillas with cilantro, onion, avocado, lime, and salsa.
