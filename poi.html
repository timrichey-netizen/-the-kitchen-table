Native Hawaiian

Poi

Traditional Hawaiian poi made by mashing cooked kalo (taro) with water to a smooth, elastic consistency.

Native HawaiianHawaiʻi, United States30 min

Ingredients

  • 2 lb fresh taro/kalo corms
  • 1/2 to 1 1/2 cups water, as needed

Preparation

  1. Wearing gloves when handling raw taro, peel and cut corms into chunks.
  2. Steam until completely tender, 30–45 minutes; taro must be thoroughly cooked. Cool slightly.
  3. Traditionally pound cooked kalo on a board; for a home method, mash very thoroughly or process while gradually adding water.
  4. Continue adding water until poi is smooth, cohesive, and reaches desired one-, two-, or three-finger consistency.
  5. Serve fresh, or cover and allow controlled fermentation at cool room temperature for a tangier flavor before refrigerating.
