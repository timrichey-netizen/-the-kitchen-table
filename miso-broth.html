Miso Broth

Quick dashi-based miso broth for soup, noodles, tofu, and vegetables.

Broth & StockJapanese

Ingredients

  • 4 cups dashi
  • 3–4 tbsp miso paste
  • Tofu, wakame, and scallion, optional

Preparation

  1. Warm dashi to just below a simmer.
  2. Place miso in a small bowl and whisk with a ladleful of hot dashi.
  3. Return mixture to pot.
  4. Do not boil after adding miso; add garnishes and serve.
