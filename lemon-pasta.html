Lemon Pasta

Silky lemon pasta with Parmigiano-Reggiano, butter, olive oil, garlic, and plenty of fresh lemon.

25 minServes 4

PASTA GUIDE

Recommended pasta: Spaghetti or linguine

See pasta shapes, sauces & pairings →

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

Ingredients

  • 12 oz spaghetti or linguine
  • Kosher salt
  • 3 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, very thinly sliced
  • 1 tbsp finely grated lemon zest
  • 3 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • 1 cup finely grated Parmigiano-Reggiano
  • 1/2 cup reserved pasta water, plus more as needed
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley or basil

Preparation

  1. Cook pasta in well-salted water until just shy of al dente. Reserve at least 1 cup cooking water.
  2. Melt butter with olive oil in a wide skillet. Add garlic and cook gently until fragrant but not browned.
  3. Add lemon zest and about 1/2 cup pasta water. Add the pasta and toss vigorously.
  4. Remove from heat and add Parmesan a handful at a time, tossing until glossy and emulsified. Add more pasta water as needed.
  5. Stir in lemon juice, black pepper, and herbs. Taste for salt and serve immediately.
Pasta-water technique: Before draining, reserve at least 1 cup of the hot, starchy cooking water. Add it to the sauce a tablespoon or splash at a time while tossing the pasta. The starch helps emulsify fat, cheese, and sauce so it clings to the pasta and becomes glossy rather than watery.
