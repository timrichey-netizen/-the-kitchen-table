AVOCADO · MEXICAN

Guacamole

Classic guacamole with ripe avocado, lime, onion, serrano, cilantro, tomato, and salt.

15 minServes 6

Photo: ItsOnly.Recipes

Ingredients

  • 4 ripe avocados
  • Juice of 2 limes
  • 1/3 white onion, finely diced
  • 1 serrano, minced
  • 1 Roma tomato, diced
  • 1/2 cup cilantro
  • 1 tsp kosher salt

Preparation

  1. Mash avocados to the desired texture.
  2. Fold in lime, onion, serrano, tomato, cilantro, and salt.
  3. Taste and adjust lime and salt.
  4. Serve immediately.
