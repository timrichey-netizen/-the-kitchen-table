Tacos de Machaca

Northern Mexican shredded dried beef sautéed with onion, tomato, chile, and optional egg.

35 minMakes 10 tacos

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

Ingredients

  • 12 oz machaca or dried shredded beef
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1/2 white onion, diced
  • 2 Roma tomatoes, diced
  • 1 jalapeño or serrano
  • 4 eggs, optional
  • 10 tortillas
  • Cilantro and salsa

Preparation

  1. Rinse machaca briefly if excessively salty.
  2. Cook onion and chile in oil.
  3. Add tomato and cook until juicy.
  4. Stir in machaca and crisp lightly.
  5. Add beaten eggs if desired and serve in tortillas.
