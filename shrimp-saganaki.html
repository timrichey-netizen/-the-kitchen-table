Shrimp Saganaki

Greek-style shrimp baked in a garlicky tomato sauce with feta and fresh herbs.

35 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb large shrimp
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • ½ tsp red-pepper flakes
  • 1 can (14 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • ½ cup dry white wine
  • 6 oz feta, crumbled
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • Salt and pepper

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Sauté onion in olive oil until soft; add garlic and red pepper.
  2. Add wine and reduce briefly, then stir in tomatoes and oregano and simmer 10 minutes.
  3. Nestle shrimp into the sauce and scatter feta over the top.
  4. Bake 8–10 minutes until shrimp are just cooked and feta softens.
  5. Finish with parsley and serve immediately.
