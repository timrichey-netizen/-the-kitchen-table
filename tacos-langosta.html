Tacos de Langosta

Baja-style lobster tacos with garlic butter, avocado, lime, cabbage, and green salsa.

35 minMakes 8 tacos

Photo: The Nature of Home

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb cooked lobster meat, cut into chunks
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • 8 corn or flour tortillas
  • Shredded cabbage
  • Avocado
  • Cilantro
  • Salsa verde or salsa de aguacate
  • Salt

Preparation

  1. Melt butter over medium heat and cook garlic briefly.
  2. Add lobster and warm gently just until heated; do not overcook.
  3. Finish with lime and salt.
  4. Warm tortillas.
  5. Fill with cabbage and lobster and top with avocado, cilantro, and salsa.
