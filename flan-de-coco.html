Caribbean (Cuba)

Flan de Coco

A Cuba dessert built around coconut milk, eggs, caramel, prepared in its recognizable regional style.

Caribbean (Cuba)Cuba1 hr

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup sugar for caramel
  • 1 can sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 can coconut milk
  • 5 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • pinch salt

Preparation

  1. Melt sugar to amber caramel and coat flan mold.
  2. Blend condensed milk, coconut milk, eggs, vanilla, and salt; strain into mold.
  3. Bake in a water bath at 325°F for 50–60 minutes until just set. Chill thoroughly and invert.
