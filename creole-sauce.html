Creole Sauce

A Louisiana tomato sauce with onion, celery, bell pepper, garlic, herbs, Worcestershire, and a touch of cayenne.

35 minMakes about 3 cups

Photo: Food Network

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 cup chopped onion
  • 1/2 cup chopped celery
  • 1/2 cup chopped green bell pepper
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 cups chopped tomatoes
  • 2 cups tomato sauce
  • 1 cup chicken stock
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • Cayenne, salt, and black pepper
  • 2 tbsp butter, optional

Preparation

  1. Heat olive oil and cook onion, celery, and bell pepper until softened.
  2. Add garlic, tomatoes, thyme, oregano, Worcestershire, and cayenne.
  3. Add tomato sauce and stock and bring to a gentle simmer.
  4. Cook 15 to 20 minutes until the vegetables are tender and the sauce is slightly reduced.
  5. Finish with butter if desired and adjust seasoning.
