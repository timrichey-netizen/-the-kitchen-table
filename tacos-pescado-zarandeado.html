Tacos de Pescado Zarandeado

Nayarit-style chile-and-citrus marinated fish grilled hard for char, then served with pico de gallo and salsa verde.

1 hrMakes 10 tacos

Photo: Andy Cooks

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb firm white fish fillets
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp achiote paste
  • 1 guajillo chile, softened
  • 1 chipotle chile in adobo
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • Juice of 2 limes
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce or Maggi seasoning
  • 10 corn tortillas
  • Pico de gallo
  • Salsa verde

Preparation

  1. Blend mayonnaise, achiote, guajillo, chipotle, garlic, lime, and soy or Maggi into a smooth marinade.
  2. Coat fish and marinate 30 minutes.
  3. Grill over high heat until charred in spots and just cooked through.
  4. Rest briefly and flake into large pieces.
  5. Serve in tortillas with pico de gallo, salsa verde, and lime.
