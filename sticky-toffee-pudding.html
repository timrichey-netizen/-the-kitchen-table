Sticky Toffee Pudding

A moist date cake drenched in warm buttery toffee sauce, best served with vanilla ice cream or cream.

1 hrServes 8

Ingredients

  • 8 oz pitted dates, chopped
  • 1 cup boiling water
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 6 tbsp unsalted butter, softened
  • 3/4 cup dark brown sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • For sauce: 1 cup dark brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter
  • 3/4 cup heavy cream
  • Pinch of salt

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 350°F and butter an 8-inch square baking dish.
  2. Pour boiling water over dates, stir in baking soda, and let stand 10 minutes. Mash lightly.
  3. Whisk flour, baking powder, and salt. In another bowl beat butter and brown sugar until fluffy, then beat in eggs and vanilla.
  4. Fold in dry ingredients, then the date mixture. Bake 30 to 35 minutes until set.
  5. For the sauce, simmer brown sugar, butter, cream, and salt for 4 to 5 minutes until smooth and glossy.
  6. Poke the warm cake all over and spoon over some sauce. Serve warm with additional sauce.
