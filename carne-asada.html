BEEF · MEXICAN

Carne Asada

Citrus-garlic marinated grilled steak, charred over high heat and sliced thin for tacos or platters.

35 min + marinateServes 6

Photo: Unsplash

Ingredients

  • 2 lb skirt, flank, or flap steak
  • 1/3 cup orange juice
  • 1/4 cup lime juice
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 jalapeño, minced
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • Chopped cilantro and lime wedges

Preparation

  1. Whisk citrus juices, oil, garlic, jalapeño, cumin, oregano, salt, and pepper.
  2. Marinate steak 2 to 8 hours.
  3. Heat grill to very high heat and oil the grates.
  4. Grill steak until deeply charred and cooked to desired doneness, turning once.
  5. Rest 8 to 10 minutes.
  6. Slice thinly against the grain and serve with tortillas, lime, and salsa.
