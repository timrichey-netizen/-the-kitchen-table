Tacos de Marlín Ahumado

Smoked marlin sautéed with tomato, onion, chile, and lime, then topped with avocado in corn tortillas.

30 minMakes 10 tacos

Photo: Nutrium

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb smoked marlin, shredded
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 white onion, diced
  • 2 Roma tomatoes, diced
  • 1 serrano chile, minced
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • 10 corn tortillas
  • Avocado
  • Cilantro

Preparation

  1. Heat oil and cook onion and serrano until softened.
  2. Add garlic and tomato and cook until juicy.
  3. Fold in smoked marlin and heat through without drying it out.
  4. Finish with lime juice.
  5. Serve in warm tortillas with avocado and cilantro.
