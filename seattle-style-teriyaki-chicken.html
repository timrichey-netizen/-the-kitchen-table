American (Seattle Japanese-American)

Seattle-Style Teriyaki Chicken

Seattle's charred chicken teriyaki with a sweet-savory soy glaze, served in the local takeout style.

American (Seattle Japanese-American)Seattle, Washington, United States45 min

Ingredients

  • 2 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • 1 cup soy sauce
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 2 tbsp grated ginger
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch plus 1 tbsp water
  • 4 cups cooked white rice

Preparation

  1. Combine soy sauce, sugar, water, ginger, and garlic; reserve half. Marinate chicken in the remainder 2–8 hours.
  2. Simmer reserved sauce 5 minutes; thicken with cornstarch slurry.
  3. Grill or broil chicken over high heat until charred and 165°F, about 5–7 minutes per side.
  4. Rest and slice.
  5. Glaze with sauce and serve over rice.
