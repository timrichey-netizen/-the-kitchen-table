PASTA · ITALIAN

Bolognese Meat Sauce

A slow-simmered ragù with beef, pork, soffritto, wine, milk, tomato, and Parmigiano.

2 hr 30 minServes 4

PASTA GUIDE

Recommended pasta: Tagliatelle

See pasta shapes, sauces & pairings →

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 onion, finely diced
  • 1 carrot, finely diced
  • 1 celery stalk, finely diced
  • 8 oz ground beef
  • 8 oz ground pork
  • ½ cup dry white wine
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 1 can (14 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • Pinch of nutmeg
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Tagliatelle and Parmigiano-Reggiano for serving

Preparation

  1. Cook onion, carrot, and celery in olive oil and butter until very soft.
  2. Add beef and pork and brown thoroughly.
  3. Add wine and reduce almost completely, then stir in milk and nutmeg and simmer until absorbed.
  4. Add tomatoes and simmer very gently 1½–2 hours, adding a little water if needed.
  5. Season and serve over tagliatelle with Parmigiano-Reggiano.
Pasta-water technique: Before draining, reserve at least 1 cup of the hot, starchy cooking water. Add it to the sauce a tablespoon or splash at a time while tossing the pasta. The starch helps emulsify fat, cheese, and sauce so it clings to the pasta and becomes glossy rather than watery.
