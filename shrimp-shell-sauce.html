Shrimp Shell Sauce

A concentrated shrimp-shell sauce made from roasted shells, aromatics, tomato, wine, and stock.

50 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • Shells and heads from 1½–2 lb shrimp
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 1 carrot, chopped
  • 1 celery stalk, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • ½ cup dry white wine
  • 3 cups water or light fish stock
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 2 thyme sprigs
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • Salt and pepper

Preparation

  1. Heat olive oil in a saucepan and cook shrimp shells until deeply pink and fragrant.
  2. Add onion, carrot, celery, and garlic and cook until softened. Stir in tomato paste.
  3. Add wine and reduce by half. Add stock, bay, and thyme and simmer 25 minutes.
  4. Strain through a fine sieve, pressing the solids well.
  5. Reduce to desired intensity and whisk in butter. Season to taste.
