Nopales Asados

Grilled cactus paddles seasoned simply with oil, salt, lime, and onion.

25 minServes 4

Photo: Real de Oaxaca

Ingredients

  • 6 cleaned nopales
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 red onion, sliced
  • 2 limes
  • Cilantro

Preparation

  1. Brush nopales with oil and season.
  2. Grill over medium-high heat until tender and marked, 4 to 5 minutes per side.
  3. Slice or serve whole.
  4. Top with onion and cilantro.
  5. Finish with lime.
