White Wine & Garlic Mussels

Mussels steamed quickly with garlic, shallot, dry white wine, butter, lemon, and parsley.

25 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 2 lb mussels, scrubbed and debearded
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 large shallot, finely chopped
  • 4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup dry white wine
  • 1/2 cup seafood stock or water
  • 1 tsp lemon zest
  • 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • 1/4 cup chopped flat-leaf parsley
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • Crusty bread, for serving

Preparation

  1. Discard cracked mussels and any open mussels that do not close when tapped.
  2. Melt butter with olive oil in a large covered pot. Cook shallot 2 to 3 minutes, then add garlic and cook 30 seconds.
  3. Add wine and stock and bring to a strong simmer.
  4. Add mussels, cover, and steam 4 to 6 minutes, shaking the pot once or twice, until the mussels open.
  5. Discard any mussels that remain closed. Stir in lemon zest, lemon juice, parsley, and black pepper. Serve immediately with bread.

Notes

Do not overcook the mussels; remove them from heat as soon as they have opened.
