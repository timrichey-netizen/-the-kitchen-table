Tacos de Callo de Hacha

Seared scallops with lime, avocado, chile, and fresh salsa in warm corn tortillas.

20 minMakes 8 tacos

Photo: Feasting at Home

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb sea scallops
  • Oil
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • 8 tortillas
  • Avocado
  • Pico de gallo
  • Cilantro
  • Lime
  • Salsa de aguacate

Preparation

  1. Pat scallops very dry and season.
  2. Sear in a very hot skillet 1 1/2–2 minutes per side.
  3. Rest briefly.
  4. Serve in tortillas with avocado, pico de gallo, cilantro, lime, and salsa.
