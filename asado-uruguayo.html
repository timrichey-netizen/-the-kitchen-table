Asado Uruguayo

Traditional Uruguayan parrilla with beef, short ribs, and chorizo cooked slowly over wood or charcoal.

2–3 hrServes 6

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

Ingredients

  • 2 lb beef short ribs
  • 2 lb ribeye, strip steak, or flank steak
  • 6 fresh chorizos
  • Coarse salt
  • Chimichurri, for serving
  • Grilled peppers or provoleta, optional

Preparation

  1. Build a wood or charcoal fire and allow it to burn down to a steady bed of glowing coals. Arrange the grill for moderate, even heat.
  2. Season the beef generously with coarse salt shortly before cooking.
  3. Start the short ribs first, bone-side down, and cook slowly until deeply browned and tender.
  4. Add chorizos and turn them regularly until cooked through and browned.
  5. Grill steaks to the desired doneness, moving them to cooler areas as needed to control flare-ups.
  6. Rest the beef for 5 to 10 minutes, slice across the grain, and serve with chimichurri and any grilled accompaniments.
