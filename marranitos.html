Marranitos

Pig-shaped Mexican gingerbread cookies sweetened with piloncillo and warm spices.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp ginger
  • 6 tbsp butter
  • 3/4 cup piloncillo syrup
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tbsp milk

Method

  1. Whisk flour, baking soda, and spices.
  2. Beat butter with piloncillo syrup and egg; add dry ingredients and enough milk for a soft dough.
  3. Roll thick and cut with a pig-shaped cutter.
  4. Bake at 350°F until puffed and lightly browned.
