Alaska Sourdough Biscuits

Flaky sourdough biscuits inspired by Alaska's long sourdough tradition, using starter discard for tang and tenderness.

American (Alaska)Alaska, United States30 min

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tbsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 8 tbsp cold unsalted butter, cubed
  • 1 cup sourdough starter discard
  • 1/2 cup cold buttermilk, plus more as needed

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Whisk flour, baking powder, salt, and sugar.
  2. Cut cold butter into flour until pea-sized.
  3. Stir together starter and buttermilk; fold into dry ingredients just until shaggy.
  4. Pat dough into rectangle, fold in thirds twice for layers, then pat 3/4 inch thick.
  5. Cut biscuits straight down without twisting.
  6. Bake 14–18 minutes until risen and golden.
