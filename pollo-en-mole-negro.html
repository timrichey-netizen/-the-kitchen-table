Pollo en Mole Negro

Tender chicken simmered in deep, smoky Oaxacan mole negro with toasted chiles, spices, nuts, fruit, and chocolate.

2 hrServes 6

Photo: México Desconocido

Ingredients

  • 6 chicken thighs or mixed chicken pieces
  • 5 cups prepared mole negro
  • 1 to 2 cups chicken stock
  • Salt
  • Toasted sesame seeds
  • Cooked rice, for serving

Preparation

  1. Season chicken and brown lightly in a heavy pot.
  2. Add enough stock to come partway up the chicken and simmer gently until nearly cooked.
  3. Stir in mole negro and enough additional stock to make a thick, pourable sauce.
  4. Simmer gently until chicken is fully tender and coated.
  5. Taste and adjust salt.
  6. Serve with rice and toasted sesame seeds.
