PASTA · URUGUAYAN

Capeletis a la Caruso

Stuffed pasta in Uruguay’s famous Caruso sauce with cream, ham, mushrooms, and Parmesan.

45 minServes 4

Photo: La Gran Uruguaya

Ingredients

  • 1 lb fresh capeletis or tortellini
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 8 oz mushrooms, sliced
  • 5 oz cooked ham, diced
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup finely grated Parmesan
  • Pinch of freshly grated nutmeg
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Chopped parsley, for serving

Preparation

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil for the pasta.
  2. Melt butter in a wide skillet. Add mushrooms and cook until their moisture evaporates and they begin to brown.
  3. Add ham and cook 1 to 2 minutes. Pour in cream and milk and bring to a gentle simmer.
  4. Stir in Parmesan and nutmeg. Simmer until the sauce lightly coats a spoon. Season with salt and pepper.
  5. Cook the capeletis until tender according to package directions. Drain, reserving a little pasta water.
  6. Toss the pasta with the Caruso sauce, loosening with a splash of pasta water if needed. Finish with parsley and extra Parmesan.
