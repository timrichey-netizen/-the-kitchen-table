Tacos de Pescado Capeado

Mexican-style battered fish tacos with a light, crisp capeado coating, cabbage, crema, and salsa.

40 minMakes 10 tacos

Photo: Muy Delish

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb white fish strips
  • 3/4 cup flour
  • 1/4 cup cornstarch
  • 1 egg
  • 3/4 cup cold sparkling water or beer
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Neutral oil, for frying
  • 10 corn tortillas
  • Cabbage
  • Mexican crema
  • Salsa verde
  • Lime

Preparation

  1. Whisk flour, cornstarch, egg, cold liquid, and salt into a light batter.
  2. Heat oil to 350°F.
  3. Coat fish and fry until puffed, golden, and cooked through.
  4. Drain on a rack or paper towels.
  5. Serve in tortillas with cabbage, crema, salsa verde, and lime.
