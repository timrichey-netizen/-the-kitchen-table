Creamy Seafood Risotto

Silky Arborio rice with shrimp, scallops, mussels, white wine, and seafood stock.

50 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups Arborio rice
  • 4 cups warm seafood stock
  • ½ cup white wine
  • 1 shallot
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • ½ lb shrimp
  • ½ lb scallops
  • 1 lb mussels
  • Parsley and lemon zest

Preparation

  1. Sauté shallot in olive oil and butter; add rice and toast 2 minutes.
  2. Add wine, then warm stock gradually, stirring until creamy and al dente.
  3. Sear scallops and shrimp separately; steam mussels until opened.
  4. Fold seafood into risotto with butter, parsley, and lemon zest.
