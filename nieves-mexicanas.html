Nieves Mexicanas

Refreshing Mexican-style fruit ice churned until light and scoopable.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups ripe fruit puree
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1 cup water
  • 1/4 cup lime juice
  • Pinch salt

Method

  1. Dissolve sugar in water and cool.
  2. Mix with fruit puree, lime, and salt.
  3. Chill thoroughly, then churn in an ice-cream maker.
  4. Freeze 2–3 hours to firm.
