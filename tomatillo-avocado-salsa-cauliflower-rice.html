Tomatillo-Avocado Salsa over Cauliflower Rice

Bright roasted tomatillo-avocado salsa spooned over fluffy cauliflower rice with cilantro and lime.

30 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 1 large head cauliflower, riced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 6 tomatillos, husked
  • 1 jalapeño
  • 1 avocado, diced
  • ¼ red onion, finely diced
  • ½ cup cilantro
  • Juice of 2 limes
  • 1 garlic clove
  • Salt

Preparation

  1. Broil tomatillos, jalapeño, and garlic until softened and lightly charred.
  2. Blend tomatillos, jalapeño, garlic, half the cilantro, lime juice, and salt into a chunky salsa.
  3. Fold in avocado and red onion.
  4. Sauté cauliflower rice in olive oil 4–5 minutes until tender but not mushy.
  5. Spoon tomatillo-avocado salsa over cauliflower rice and finish with cilantro.
