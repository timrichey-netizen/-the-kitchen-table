American (New Jersey)

Salt Water Taffy

A New Jersey dessert preparation centered on sugar, corn syrup, butter, presented in its recognizable regional style.

American (New Jersey)New Jersey, United States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 2 cups sugar
  • 2 tbsp cornstarch
  • 1 cup light corn syrup
  • 3/4 cup water
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp vanilla or flavoring

Preparation

  1. Grease a pan. Combine sugar and cornstarch, then add corn syrup, water, butter, and salt. Cook without stirring after boiling until 255°F.
  2. Pour into pan and cool until safe to handle. Add flavoring.
  3. Pull repeatedly with buttered hands until pale and firm.
  4. Stretch into ropes, cut pieces, and wrap individually in waxed paper.
