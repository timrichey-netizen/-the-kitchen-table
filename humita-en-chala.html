Humita en Chala

Fresh corn, onion, peppers, basil, and optional cheese wrapped in corn husks and gently steamed.

1 hr 15 minServes 6

Ingredients

  • 8 ears fresh corn with husks
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 red bell pepper, finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • ½ cup milk
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp ground cumin
  • ¼ cup chopped fresh basil
  • Kosher salt and black pepper
  • ½ cup queso fresco or mozzarella, optional

Preparation

  1. Carefully remove the husks and reserve the largest intact pieces.
  2. Grate or process the corn kernels into a coarse puree.
  3. Sauté the onion and bell pepper in butter until soft.
  4. Stir in the corn, milk, paprika, cumin, basil, salt, and pepper and cook until thick, about 10 minutes.
  5. Spoon the filling onto overlapping corn husks, adding a little cheese if desired. Fold into packets and tie.
  6. Steam or gently simmer the packets for 25–30 minutes.
