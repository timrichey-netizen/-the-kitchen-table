American (Delaware)

Sussex County Pepper Pot Soup

A Delaware soup preparation centered on sussex, county, pepper, pot, soup, presented in its recognizable regional style.

American (Delaware)Delaware, United States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb defining meat, seafood, or vegetables for Sussex County Pepper Pot Soup
  • 6 cups stock
  • 1 onion
  • 2 celery ribs
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • salt, pepper, and regional seasonings

Preparation

  1. Prepare the defining ingredients.
  2. Cook aromatics in butter until softened.
  3. Add stock and main ingredients.
  4. Simmer gently until tender and flavors are integrated.
  5. Adjust seasoning and serve hot.
