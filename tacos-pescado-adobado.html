Tacos de Pescado Adobado

White fish marinated in red chile adobo, seared until caramelized, and served with onion, cilantro, and avocado salsa.

35 min + marinateMakes 10 tacos

Photo: Muy Delish

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb firm fish
  • 1 cup Mexican adobo
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 10 corn tortillas
  • Chopped onion
  • Cilantro
  • Salsa de aguacate
  • Lime wedges

Preparation

  1. Coat fish with adobo and lime and marinate 20 to 30 minutes.
  2. Heat a skillet or grill over high heat.
  3. Cook fish until charred at edges and just opaque inside.
  4. Rest briefly and flake.
  5. Serve in tortillas with onion, cilantro, avocado salsa, and lime.
