Veal Piccata

Thin veal cutlets in a bright lemon-butter sauce with capers and parsley.

30 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb veal cutlets, pounded thin
  • Salt and black pepper
  • ½ cup flour
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • ½ cup dry white wine
  • ¾ cup chicken stock
  • 2 lemons
  • 3 tbsp capers
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley

Preparation

  1. Season veal and dredge lightly in flour.
  2. Sauté in olive oil and 1 tbsp butter until lightly browned, 1–2 minutes per side; remove.
  3. Deglaze with wine and reduce by half. Add stock, lemon juice, and capers.
  4. Simmer briefly, then whisk in remaining butter.
  5. Return veal to sauce, warm through, and finish with parsley and lemon slices.
