American (Florida Gulf Coast)

Pan-Fried Gulf Shrimp

Florida Gulf shrimp quickly pan-fried with garlic, butter, lemon, and parsley.

American (Florida Gulf Coast)Florida Gulf Coast, United States20 min

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb large Gulf shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • salt and pepper
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 4 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley

Preparation

  1. Pat shrimp dry and season. Heat oil and 1 tbsp butter in a wide skillet over medium-high.
  2. Add shrimp in one layer and cook about 90 seconds per side until just opaque.
  3. Add remaining butter and garlic and cook 30 seconds.
  4. Remove from heat; toss with lemon juice and parsley and serve immediately.
