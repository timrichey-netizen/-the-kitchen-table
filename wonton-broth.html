Wonton Broth

Light, savory broth seasoned for wontons with chicken stock, ginger, scallion, and white pepper.

Broth & StockChinese

Ingredients

  • 6 cups chicken or superior stock
  • 4 slices ginger
  • 3 scallions
  • 1 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • White pepper
  • Salt

Preparation

  1. Simmer stock with ginger and scallion 10 minutes.
  2. Remove aromatics.
  3. Season lightly with soy, sesame oil, white pepper, and salt.
  4. Ladle over freshly cooked wontons.
