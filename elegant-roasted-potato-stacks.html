Elegant Roasted Potato Stacks

Thinly sliced potatoes layered with butter and herbs, roasted into crisp-edged individual stacks.

1 hrServes 4

Ingredients

  • 2 lb Yukon Gold potatoes
  • 4 tbsp melted butter
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tsp thyme leaves
  • 1 garlic clove, finely grated
  • Salt and black pepper
  • ¼ cup finely grated Parmesan, optional

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 400°F and butter a muffin tin.
  2. Slice potatoes very thinly with a mandoline.
  3. Toss slices with butter, olive oil, thyme, garlic, salt, and pepper.
  4. Stack slices tightly in muffin cups and sprinkle with Parmesan if using.
  5. Roast 45–55 minutes until tender inside and deeply crisp at the edges.
