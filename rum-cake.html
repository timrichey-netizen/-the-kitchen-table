Caribbean (Jamaica)

Rum Cake

A Jamaica dessert built around dark rum, dried fruit, butter, brown sugar, prepared in its recognizable regional style.

Caribbean (Jamaica)Jamaica1 hr

Ingredients

  • 2 cups mixed dried fruit
  • 1 cup dark rum
  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 cup butter
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1/2 cup browning sauce
  • additional rum for brushing

Preparation

  1. Soak fruit in rum overnight or longer; puree coarsely.
  2. Heat oven to 325°F. Cream butter and sugar; beat in eggs. Fold in flour, baking powder, cinnamon, fruit, vanilla, and browning.
  3. Bake in greased Bundt or cake pan 60–75 minutes.
  4. Brush warm cake with additional rum and cool completely.
