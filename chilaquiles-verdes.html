Chilaquiles Verdes

Crisp tortilla chips tossed in tomatillo salsa verde with crema, queso fresco, onion, avocado, and eggs.

30 minServes 4

Photo: DiDi Food

Ingredients

  • 8 cups tortilla chips
  • 3 cups salsa verde
  • 4 eggs
  • 1/2 cup Mexican crema
  • 3/4 cup queso fresco
  • 1/4 white onion, sliced
  • 1 avocado
  • Cilantro

Preparation

  1. Warm salsa verde in a wide skillet.
  2. Toss in tortilla chips briefly.
  3. Plate immediately.
  4. Top with eggs, crema, queso fresco, onion, avocado, and cilantro.
