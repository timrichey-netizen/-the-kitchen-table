Steamed Mussels with Thai-Style Coconut-Curry Broth

A Thai-style coconut-curry mussel preparation inspired by J. Kenji López-Alt’s Serious Eats recipe, with green curry, fresh aromatics, fish sauce, lime, and cilantro.

20 minServes 3 to 4

Ingredients

  • 2 lb mussels, scrubbed and debearded
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 2 shallots, thinly sliced
  • 4 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 1 tbsp Thai green curry paste
  • 1 tsp finely grated lime zest
  • 1 can (13.5 to 15 oz) coconut milk
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce, plus more to taste
  • 1 tbsp palm sugar or light brown sugar
  • 1 small Thai or serrano chile, thinly sliced
  • 1/3 cup cilantro leaves and tender stems
  • 1 tbsp fresh lime juice, plus wedges for serving

Preparation

  1. Discard cracked mussels and any open mussels that do not close when tapped.
  2. Heat the oil with a spoonful of the thick coconut cream from the top of the can. Add shallots and garlic and cook until softened.
  3. Add green curry paste and lime zest and cook until very fragrant.
  4. Add the remaining coconut milk, fish sauce, and sugar. Simmer briefly to blend the flavors.
  5. Add mussels, cover, and cook just until they open. Discard any that remain closed.
  6. Finish with fresh chile, cilantro, and lime juice. Serve immediately with lime wedges.

Notes

Inspired by the Serious Eats recipe by J. Kenji López-Alt. View the original recipe at Serious Eats →
