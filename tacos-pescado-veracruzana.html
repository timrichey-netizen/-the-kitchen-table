Tacos de Pescado a la Veracruzana

Fish tacos topped with a Veracruz-style tomato sauce of olives, capers, garlic, oregano, and chile.

35 minMakes 10 tacos

Photo: Mexico in My Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb white fish fillets
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 cups Veracruz-style tomato sauce
  • 1/3 cup sliced green olives
  • 1 tbsp capers
  • 1 jalapeño, sliced
  • 10 corn tortillas
  • Cilantro
  • Lime wedges

Preparation

  1. Season and sear fish in olive oil until nearly cooked.
  2. Add Veracruz-style tomato sauce, olives, capers, and jalapeño and simmer gently until fish is done.
  3. Break fish into large flakes in the sauce.
  4. Warm tortillas.
  5. Fill with fish and sauce and finish with cilantro and lime.
