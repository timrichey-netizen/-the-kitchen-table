PORK · MEXICAN

Tacos de Chicharrón

Crisp pork chicharrón tucked into tortillas with salsa, onion, cilantro, and lime, or softened briefly in salsa roja.

25 minMakes 10 tacos

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

Ingredients

  • 12 oz pork chicharrón
  • 2 cups salsa roja, optional
  • 10 corn tortillas
  • Chopped white onion
  • Chopped cilantro
  • Lime wedges
  • Salsa verde or salsa roja

Preparation

  1. For crisp tacos, break chicharrón into bite-size pieces and warm briefly.
  2. For chicharrón en salsa, simmer salsa roja in a skillet and fold in chicharrón for 3 to 5 minutes until slightly softened.
  3. Warm tortillas.
  4. Fill with chicharrón.
  5. Top with onion, cilantro, lime, and extra salsa.
