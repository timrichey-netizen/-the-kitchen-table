Huevos Rancheros

Fried eggs on warm corn tortillas with ranchera sauce, refried beans, avocado, and queso fresco.

25 minServes 4

Photo: DiDi Food

Ingredients

  • 8 corn tortillas
  • 4 eggs
  • 2 cups salsa ranchera
  • 2 cups frijoles refritos
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 1/2 cup queso fresco
  • Cilantro
  • Oil and salt

Preparation

  1. Warm tortillas and keep covered.
  2. Heat ranchera sauce and refried beans.
  3. Fry eggs sunny-side-up or to preference.
  4. Set tortillas on plates, spread with beans, top with eggs and ranchera sauce.
  5. Garnish with avocado, queso fresco, and cilantro.
