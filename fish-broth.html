Fish Broth

Fast, clean fish broth for chowders, seafood sauces, risotto, and soups.

Broth & StockSeafood

Ingredients

  • 2 lb non-oily white-fish bones and heads, gills removed
  • 1 leek
  • 1 onion
  • 1 celery rib
  • 1/2 cup dry white wine
  • Parsley stems
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 2 qt water

Preparation

  1. Rinse fish bones thoroughly.
  2. Sweat vegetables without browning; add bones and wine.
  3. Add water and simmer only 20–30 minutes.
  4. Strain immediately; do not overcook.
