Tacos de Alambre

Mexico City-style tacos with steak, bacon, peppers, onion, and melted cheese.

35 minMakes 12 tacos

Photo: El Periódico / RecetasGratis

Ingredients

  • 1 lb sirloin or skirt steak, diced
  • 6 oz bacon, chopped
  • 1 green bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 white onion, sliced
  • 6 oz Oaxaca or Monterey Jack cheese
  • 12 tortillas
  • Salsa and lime

Preparation

  1. Cook bacon until crisp.
  2. Sear steak over high heat.
  3. Cook peppers and onion until lightly charred.
  4. Return steak and bacon to pan.
  5. Melt cheese over the mixture and serve in tortillas.
