Crab Broth

Sweet crab-shell broth ideal for crab soups, sauces, and seafood rice.

Broth & StockSeafood

Ingredients

  • 2 lb crab shells and bodies
  • 1 onion
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 celery rib
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 qt water

Preparation

  1. Break shells into manageable pieces and roast or sauté.
  2. Add vegetables and tomato paste; cook until fragrant.
  3. Add water and simmer 45 minutes.
  4. Strain carefully through a fine sieve.
