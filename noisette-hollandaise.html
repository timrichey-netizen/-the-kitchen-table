SAUCE · HOLLANDAISE DAUGHTER SAUCE

Noisette

Hollandaise made with browned butter for a nutty, deeper flavor that pairs beautifully with fish and vegetables.

20 minMakes about 1 1/2 cups

Photo: Arla

Ingredients

  • 12 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 3 large egg yolks
  • 1 tbsp water
  • 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • Pinch of cayenne or white pepper
  • Kosher salt

Preparation

  1. Brown the butter over medium heat until the milk solids turn hazelnut-colored and fragrant; strain if desired and keep warm.
  2. Whisk egg yolks, water, and lemon juice over gentle heat until thick and pale.
  3. Slowly whisk in the warm browned butter to create an emulsion.
  4. Season with cayenne or white pepper and salt.
  5. Serve warm with fish, shellfish, or vegetables.
