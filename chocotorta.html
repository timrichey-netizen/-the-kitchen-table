DESSERT · ARGENTINIAN

Chocotorta

Argentina’s beloved no-bake chocolate cookie cake layered with dulce de leche and cream cheese.

30 min + chillServes 8

Photo: Ezarate / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 3.0.

Ingredients

  • 14 oz chocolate tea biscuits or chocolate wafer cookies
  • 1½ cups dulce de leche
  • 12 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1 cup strong coffee or milk, cooled
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract, optional
  • Unsweetened cocoa or grated chocolate, for finishing

Preparation

  1. Beat the dulce de leche and cream cheese until completely smooth and evenly blended.
  2. Pour the cooled coffee or milk into a shallow dish.
  3. Quickly dip each chocolate biscuit in the liquid without letting it become soggy.
  4. Arrange a single layer of dipped biscuits in an 8-inch square dish, then spread with a layer of the dulce de leche mixture.
  5. Repeat the layers until the biscuits and filling are used, finishing with the cream mixture.
  6. Dust with cocoa or grated chocolate. Cover and refrigerate at least 6 hours, preferably overnight, before slicing.
