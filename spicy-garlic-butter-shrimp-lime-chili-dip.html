Spicy Garlic Butter Shrimp with Lime Chili Dip

Seared shrimp in garlicky chile butter served with a creamy lime-chile dipping sauce.

25 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb large shrimp
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 red chile, thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 lime
  • 2 tbsp chopped cilantro
  • ½ cup mayonnaise or Greek yogurt
  • 1 tbsp chile sauce
  • Salt and pepper

Preparation

  1. Mix mayonnaise or yogurt with lime juice, chile sauce, and a pinch of salt for the dip.
  2. Season shrimp with paprika, salt, and pepper.
  3. Sear shrimp in a hot skillet until nearly cooked; add butter, garlic, and sliced chile.
  4. Toss until glossy and just cooked through.
  5. Finish with cilantro and serve with lime wedges and the lime-chile dip.
