Tom Kha Broth

Coconut-enriched Thai broth balancing galangal, lemongrass, lime, chile, and fish sauce.

Broth & StockThai

Ingredients

  • 4 cups chicken broth
  • 2 cups coconut milk
  • 2 lemongrass stalks
  • 8 slices galangal
  • 6 makrut lime leaves
  • 3 Thai chiles
  • 3 tbsp fish sauce
  • 3 tbsp lime juice

Preparation

  1. Simmer chicken broth with aromatics 10 minutes.
  2. Add coconut milk and return to a gentle simmer.
  3. Season with fish sauce.
  4. Remove from heat and add lime juice; avoid hard boiling.
