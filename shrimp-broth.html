Shrimp Broth

Quick shrimp-shell broth for gumbo, bisque, rice, sauces, and seafood soups.

Broth & StockSeafood

Ingredients

  • Shells from 2 lb shrimp
  • 1 onion
  • 1 celery rib
  • 1 carrot
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 qt water

Preparation

  1. Sauté shells until pink and fragrant.
  2. Add vegetables and tomato paste; cook 5 minutes.
  3. Add water and simmer 30–40 minutes.
  4. Strain through a fine sieve.
