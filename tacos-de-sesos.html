BEEF · MEXICAN

Tacos de Sesos

Traditional beef brain tacos with gently poached sesos, lightly crisped on the griddle and served with onion, cilantro, lime, and salsa.

1 hrMakes 10 tacos

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb cleaned beef brains
  • 1/2 onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 tbsp vinegar
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 10 corn tortillas
  • Chopped onion and cilantro
  • Lime wedges
  • Salsa verde or salsa de chile de árbol

Preparation

  1. Rinse brains carefully and remove any obvious membrane or clots.
  2. Poach gently in barely simmering salted water with onion, garlic, bay, and vinegar for 15 to 20 minutes until set.
  3. Cool slightly and cut into chunks.
  4. Lightly crisp in a hot skillet with oil.
  5. Serve in warm tortillas with onion, cilantro, lime, and salsa.
