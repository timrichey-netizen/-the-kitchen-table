Ensalada de Aguacate

Creamy avocado salad with tomato, red onion, cilantro, lime, and queso fresco.

15 minServes 4

Photo: Ovenspiration

Ingredients

  • 3 avocados, cubed
  • 2 cups cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/2 red onion, sliced
  • 1/2 cup queso fresco
  • 1/3 cup cilantro
  • Juice of 2 limes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and pepper

Preparation

  1. Combine avocado, tomato, onion, queso fresco, and cilantro.
  2. Whisk lime, oil, salt, and pepper.
  3. Dress gently and serve immediately.
