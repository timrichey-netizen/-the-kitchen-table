Tacos de Camarón Empanizado

Breaded shrimp fried until crisp and served with cabbage, crema, lime, and salsa.

35 minMakes 10 tacos

Photo: Muy Delish

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb shrimp
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 1 1/2 cups panko or fine breadcrumbs
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Neutral oil, for frying
  • 10 tortillas
  • Shredded cabbage
  • Crema
  • Salsa verde
  • Lime

Preparation

  1. Season shrimp with salt and chili powder.
  2. Dredge in flour, dip in egg, then coat with breadcrumbs.
  3. Fry at 350°F until crisp and cooked through.
  4. Drain well.
  5. Serve in tortillas with cabbage, crema, salsa verde, and lime.
