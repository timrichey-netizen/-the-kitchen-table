Salsa de Guajillo

A smooth, brick-red salsa built around toasted guajillo chiles with tomato, garlic, onion, and oregano.

25 minMakes about 2 cups

Photo: Maricruz Avalos

Ingredients

  • 8 guajillo chiles, stemmed and seeded
  • 2 Roma tomatoes
  • 1/4 white onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1/2 tsp Mexican oregano
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp apple cider vinegar, optional

Preparation

  1. Toast guajillos briefly on a dry skillet until fragrant, then soak in hot water until pliable.
  2. Roast tomatoes, onion, and garlic until charred in spots.
  3. Blend chiles, vegetables, oregano, salt, and a little soaking water until smooth.
  4. Strain for a silky texture.
  5. Fry the salsa in oil for 5 minutes; finish with vinegar if desired.
