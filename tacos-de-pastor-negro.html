PORK · MEXICAN

Tacos de Pastor Negro

A dark, smoky variation on al pastor made with toasted chiles, achiote, and charred aromatics.

1 hr + marinateMakes 12–16 tacos

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

Ingredients

  • 2 lb pork shoulder, thinly sliced
  • 4 guajillo chiles
  • 2 pasilla chiles
  • 1 ancho chile
  • 1/2 charred onion
  • 4 charred garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp achiote paste
  • 1/4 cup pineapple juice
  • 1/4 cup vinegar
  • Oregano, cumin, salt
  • Tortillas, pineapple, onion, cilantro

Preparation

  1. Toast chiles deeply without burning and soak until soft.
  2. Blend with charred aromatics, achiote, pineapple juice, vinegar, spices, and salt.
  3. Marinate pork at least 4 hours.
  4. Grill or broil until deeply caramelized.
  5. Chop and serve with pineapple, onion, cilantro, and lime.
