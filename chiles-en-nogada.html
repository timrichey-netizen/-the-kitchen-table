POBLANO · MEXICAN

Chiles en Nogada

Roasted poblanos stuffed with sweet-savory picadillo, covered in walnut cream sauce, and garnished with pomegranate and parsley.

1 hr 45 minServes 6

Photo: México Desconocido

Ingredients

  • 6 large poblano peppers
  • 1 lb ground pork
  • 1/2 lb ground beef
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 apple, diced
  • 1 pear, diced
  • 1/4 cup raisins
  • 1/4 cup almonds, chopped
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • Pinch of clove
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 1 1/2 cups walnuts, soaked and peeled if desired
  • 1 cup Mexican crema
  • 4 oz queso fresco
  • 1 tbsp sugar, optional
  • Pomegranate seeds and parsley, for garnish

Preparation

  1. Char poblanos, steam 10 minutes, peel carefully, and slit lengthwise.
  2. Brown pork and beef; add onion, garlic, tomato, fruit, raisins, almonds, cinnamon, clove, salt, and pepper. Cook until the filling is moist but not wet.
  3. Stuff peppers generously with picadillo.
  4. Blend walnuts, crema, queso fresco, and sugar if using until smooth and pourable.
  5. Spoon nogada over peppers.
  6. Garnish with pomegranate seeds and parsley and serve at cool room temperature.
