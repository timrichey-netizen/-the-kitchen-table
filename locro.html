STEW · ARGENTINIAN

Locro

A hearty Argentine hominy and bean stew with pork, chorizo, squash, and warm spices.

About 3 hrServes 8

SLOW-COOKING TECHNIQUE

Meat tip: Pork shoulder benefits from long, gentle cooking; avoid a hard boil, which can toughen meat and muddy the broth.

See the Meat Cooking Guide →

Ingredients

  • 1 lb dried hominy, soaked overnight
  • 1 lb dried white beans, soaked overnight
  • 1 lb pork shoulder, cubed
  • 8 oz bacon, diced
  • 12 oz chorizo, sliced
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 1 leek, chopped
  • 1 small butternut squash, cubed
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • Kosher salt and black pepper
  • Water or stock, as needed

Preparation

  1. Drain the soaked hominy and beans and place them in a large pot with pork, bacon, onion, and leek.
  2. Add enough water or stock to cover generously and simmer gently for about 2 hours.
  3. Add chorizo, squash, cumin, paprika, salt, and pepper.
  4. Continue simmering until the beans and hominy are tender and the squash partially dissolves into the broth.
  5. Adjust seasoning and serve very hot.
