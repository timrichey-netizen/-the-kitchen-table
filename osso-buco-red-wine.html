Osso Buco with Red Wine

Slow-braised veal shanks in red wine, tomato, aromatics, and herbs, finished with gremolata.

3 hrServes 4

BRAISING TECHNIQUE

Meat tip: Brown the shanks deeply, then braise gently with enough liquid to surround—not drown—the meat until collagen turns silky.

See the Meat Cooking Guide →

Ingredients

  • 4 veal shanks
  • Salt, pepper, and flour
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, 1 carrot, 1 celery stalk, diced
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 cup red wine
  • 1 can (14 oz) tomatoes
  • 2 cups beef stock
  • Rosemary and thyme
  • Parsley, lemon zest, and garlic for gremolata

Preparation

  1. Season and flour shanks; brown well and remove.
  2. Cook vegetables and garlic. Deglaze with red wine and reduce by half.
  3. Add tomatoes, stock, herbs, and veal. Cover and braise at 325°F for 2–2½ hours.
  4. Serve with reduced sauce and gremolata.
