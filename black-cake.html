Caribbean (Trinidad and Tobago)

Black Cake

A Trinidad and Tobago dessert built around rum-soaked fruit, browned sugar, warm spices, prepared in its recognizable regional style.

Caribbean (Trinidad and Tobago)Trinidad and Tobago1 hr

Ingredients

  • 3 cups mixed dried fruit
  • 1 1/2 cups dark rum
  • 1/2 cup cherry wine
  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp nutmeg
  • 1 cup butter
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 5 eggs
  • 1/3 cup browning

Preparation

  1. Soak fruit in rum and wine at least overnight and puree.
  2. Heat oven to 300°F. Cream butter and sugar; beat in eggs, then fold in flour, spices, fruit, and browning.
  3. Bake in lined pan 1 1/2–2 hours until set.
  4. Brush with rum and age several days if desired.
