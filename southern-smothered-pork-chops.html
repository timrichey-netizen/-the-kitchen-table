Southern Smothered Pork Chops

Browned pork chops gently braised in onion-rich Southern pan gravy until tender.

Southern American (Mississippi)Mississippi, United States1 hr

Ingredients

  • 4 bone-in pork chops
  • salt and pepper
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 cups chicken stock
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/2 tsp thyme

Preparation

  1. Season chops and dredge lightly in flour, reserving 2 tbsp flour. Brown in oil 3 minutes per side; remove.
  2. Add butter and onion and cook until soft. Stir in reserved flour for 1 minute.
  3. Gradually whisk in stock and milk; add thyme.
  4. Return chops, cover, and simmer gently 25–35 minutes until tender and at least 145°F. Adjust seasoning.
