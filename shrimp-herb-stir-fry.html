Shrimp & Herb Stir-Fry

A fast shrimp stir-fry with snap peas, peppers, onion, garlic, and a generous handful of fresh herbs.

25 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 2 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 small onion, sliced
  • 2 cups snap peas
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • ½ cup mixed cilantro, basil, and mint
  • Black pepper

Preparation

  1. Whisk soy sauce, rice vinegar, and sesame oil together.
  2. Heat a wok or large skillet over high heat. Add oil and sear shrimp until nearly cooked; remove.
  3. Stir-fry onion, bell pepper, snap peas, garlic, and ginger for 2–3 minutes.
  4. Return shrimp, add sauce, and toss until glossy and cooked through.
  5. Remove from heat and fold in the fresh herbs.
