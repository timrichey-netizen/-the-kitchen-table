Cajun Seasoning Mix

A balanced homemade Cajun spice blend with paprika, garlic, herbs, pepper, and cayenne.

5 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp paprika
  • 1 tbsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tbsp garlic powder
  • 1 tbsp onion powder
  • 2 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 tsp dried thyme
  • 2 tsp black pepper
  • 1½ tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp cayenne pepper
  • ½ tsp white pepper

Preparation

  1. Combine all ingredients in a small bowl and whisk thoroughly.
  2. Taste a tiny pinch and adjust salt or cayenne to preference.
  3. Transfer to an airtight jar.
  4. Store in a cool, dry place for up to 6 months.
