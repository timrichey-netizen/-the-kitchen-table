American (Tennessee)

Moon Pie

Homemade interpretation of Chattanooga's iconic marshmallow sandwich cookie with graham-style rounds and chocolate coating.

American (Tennessee)Chattanooga, Tennessee, United States2 hr

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole-wheat pastry flour
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 8 tbsp butter
  • 1/4 cup honey
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • 12 large marshmallows
  • 16 oz milk or semisweet chocolate
  • 1 tbsp coconut oil

Preparation

  1. Heat oven to 350°F. Mix flour, sugar, baking soda, and salt; work in butter, then honey and milk to form dough. Chill 30 minutes.
  2. Roll thin, cut rounds, and bake 10–12 minutes; cool.
  3. Place marshmallow between paired cookies and warm briefly until soft; press gently. Chill sandwiches.
  4. Melt chocolate with coconut oil, coat sandwiches completely, and let set.
