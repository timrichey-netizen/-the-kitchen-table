Pastel de Papa

Argentine shepherd’s pie with seasoned beef, olives, egg, and creamy mashed potatoes baked until golden.

1 hr 15 minServes 6

Ingredients

  • 2 lb potatoes, peeled and cubed
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • ½ cup milk
  • Kosher salt and black pepper
  • 1½ lb ground beef
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • ½ tsp ground cumin
  • ½ cup green olives
  • 3 hard-boiled eggs, chopped
  • ½ cup raisins, optional
  • ½ cup grated cheese, optional

Preparation

  1. Boil the potatoes until tender, then mash with butter, milk, salt, and pepper.
  2. Brown the beef with the onion and bell pepper.
  3. Season with paprika, cumin, salt, and pepper, then fold in the olives, chopped eggs, and raisins if using.
  4. Spread the beef mixture in a baking dish and cover evenly with mashed potatoes.
  5. Sprinkle with cheese if desired and bake at 400°F for 25–30 minutes, until browned on top.
