BEEF · FRENCH

Boeuf Bourguignon

Classic French beef braised slowly in red wine with mushrooms, pearl onions, bacon, and herbs.

3 hr 30 minServes 4

BRAISING TECHNIQUE

Meat tip: Brown in batches, keep the braising liquid below a boil, and give the chuck enough time for connective tissue to melt.

See the Meat Cooking Guide →

MUSHROOM TIP

For better browning: use a wide hot pan, avoid crowding, and let released moisture evaporate before adding sauce ingredients.

See the Mushroom Cooking Guide →

Ingredients

  • 3 lb beef chuck, cut into large cubes
  • 6 oz bacon or lardons
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 2 cups dry red wine
  • 2 cups beef stock
  • 2 carrots, cut into chunks
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 8 oz mushrooms
  • 10 oz pearl onions
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • Thyme, bay leaf, salt, and pepper

Preparation

  1. Cook bacon until browned. Remove, then brown beef in the rendered fat in batches.
  2. Add onion and carrots; cook until lightly browned. Stir in garlic, tomato paste, and flour.
  3. Add wine and stock, scraping up browned bits. Return beef and bacon with herbs.
  4. Cover and braise at 325°F for about 2½ hours.
  5. Sauté mushrooms and pearl onions separately, then fold into the stew before serving.
