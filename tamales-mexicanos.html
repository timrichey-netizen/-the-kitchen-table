MASA · MEXICAN

Tamales

Classic Mexican corn-husk tamales with fluffy masa and a savory pork or chicken filling in red chile sauce.

2 hr 30 minMakes 18–20

Photo: Mexico in My Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 20 dried corn husks
  • 4 cups masa harina for tamales
  • 1 1/3 cups lard or shortening
  • 3 to 4 cups warm chicken stock
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 3 cups shredded pork or chicken
  • 2 cups salsa roja or chile colorado sauce

Preparation

  1. Soak corn husks in hot water until pliable.
  2. Beat lard until fluffy. Mix masa harina, baking powder, and salt; beat in warm stock gradually, then fold into lard until light and spreadable.
  3. Mix shredded meat with red chile sauce.
  4. Spread masa on husks, add filling, fold sides inward, and fold up the narrow end.
  5. Steam upright 60 to 75 minutes until masa pulls cleanly from the husk.
  6. Rest 10 minutes before serving.
