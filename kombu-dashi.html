Kombu Dashi

Minimal vegan dashi extracting clean glutamate-rich flavor from kombu.

Broth & StockJapanese

Ingredients

  • 4 cups water
  • 1 piece kombu, about 4 inches

Preparation

  1. Wipe kombu gently; do not scrub away its white surface bloom.
  2. Soak in cold water 30 minutes to overnight.
  3. Heat slowly and remove kombu just before the water boils.
  4. Use immediately as a soup and sauce base.
