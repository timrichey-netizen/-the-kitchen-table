Québécois

Cretons

A Quebec, Canada side centered on ground pork, onion, milk, warm spices, prepared in its recognizable Canadian regional style.

QuébécoisQuebec, Canada45 min

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground pork
  • 1 small onion, minced
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 1/2 cup fine breadcrumbs
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp cloves
  • 1/4 tsp allspice
  • 1 tsp salt
  • black pepper

Preparation

  1. Combine pork, onion, milk, breadcrumbs, spices, salt, and pepper in a saucepan.
  2. Cook over low heat 45–60 minutes, stirring often, until pork is very tender and mixture is thick and spreadable.
  3. Mash or beat until finely textured.
  4. Pack into a crock, chill several hours, and serve cold or cool with toast.
