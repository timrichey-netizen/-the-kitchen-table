Chayotes con Crema

Tender chayote with butter, onion, cream, and cheese.

30 minServes 4

Photo: TudoReceitas

Ingredients

  • 3 chayotes, peeled and cubed
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1/2 onion, diced
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1/2 cup Mexican crema
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup shredded cheese
  • Salt and pepper

Preparation

  1. Boil chayote until just tender; drain.
  2. Cook onion and garlic in butter.
  3. Add chayote, crema, and milk.
  4. Simmer gently, add cheese, and season.
