PASTA · URUGUAYAN

Ñoquis con Tuco

Potato gnocchi served with a slow-simmered Uruguayan-style tomato and beef tuco.

1 hrServes 4

Photo: La Gran Uruguaya

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb potato gnocchi
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 8 oz ground beef, optional
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 bay leaf
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Grated Parmesan or Pecorino, for serving

Preparation

  1. Heat olive oil in a saucepan. Cook onion until soft, then add garlic and cook briefly.
  2. Add ground beef if using and cook until browned. Stir in tomato paste for 1 minute.
  3. Add crushed tomatoes, oregano, bay leaf, salt, and pepper. Simmer gently for 30 to 40 minutes until rich and thick.
  4. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add gnocchi and cook until they float, then cook about 30 seconds longer.
  5. Drain and toss with enough tuco to coat.
  6. Serve with extra sauce and grated cheese.
