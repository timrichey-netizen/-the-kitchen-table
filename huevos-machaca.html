Huevos con Machaca

Scrambled eggs with shredded dried beef, tomato, onion, and chile.

25 minServes 4

Photo: Santoku Knives

Ingredients

  • 8 oz machaca
  • 8 eggs
  • 1/2 onion, diced
  • 2 Roma tomatoes, diced
  • 1 jalapeño, minced
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Warm flour tortillas
  • Salsa

Preparation

  1. Cook onion and jalapeño in oil.
  2. Add tomato and cook until juicy.
  3. Stir in machaca and warm through.
  4. Add beaten eggs and scramble until softly set.
  5. Serve with flour tortillas and salsa.
