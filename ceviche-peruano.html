SEAFOOD · PERUVIAN

Ceviche Peruano

Fresh white fish briefly cured in lime juice with red onion, cilantro, chile, sweet potato, and Peruvian corn.

25 minServes 4

Photo: Perú Consulta

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb very fresh firm white fish, cut into cubes
  • 3/4 cup fresh lime juice
  • 1 small red onion, very thinly sliced
  • 1 ají limo or serrano, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 cup chopped cilantro
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 small garlic clove, finely grated, optional
  • Cooked sweet potato, for serving
  • Choclo or large-kernel corn, for serving
  • Cancha, optional
  • Lettuce leaves, optional

Preparation

  1. Keep fish cold until ready to assemble.
  2. Place fish in a chilled bowl and season with salt, chile, garlic if using, and half the cilantro.
  3. Add lime juice and toss gently. Let stand only a few minutes, just until the exterior turns opaque while the center remains tender.
  4. Fold in red onion and remaining cilantro.
  5. Taste and adjust salt and lime.
  6. Serve immediately with sweet potato, corn, cancha, and lettuce.
