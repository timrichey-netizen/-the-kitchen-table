Zurich-Style Veal with Creamy Mushroom Sauce

Tender veal strips with mushrooms in a classic Zurich-style white-wine and cream sauce, ideal with rösti.

40 minServes 4

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb veal cutlets, sliced into thin strips
  • 8 oz cremini mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1 small shallot, minced
  • ½ cup dry white wine
  • ¾ cup heavy cream
  • ½ cup veal or chicken stock
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tsp fresh lemon juice
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
  • Kosher salt and black pepper

Preparation

  1. Season the veal with salt and pepper. Heat oil and 1 tablespoon butter in a large skillet over high heat.
  2. Sear the veal quickly in batches until lightly browned but still tender. Transfer to a plate.
  3. Add remaining butter and mushrooms; cook until browned. Add shallot and cook 1 minute.
  4. Deglaze with white wine and reduce by about half. Add stock and cream and simmer until lightly thickened.
  5. Stir in Dijon and lemon juice. Return the veal and any juices to the pan just long enough to warm through. Finish with parsley and serve with rösti.
