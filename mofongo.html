Caribbean (Puerto Rico)

Mofongo

A Puerto Rico main built around green plantains, garlic, pork cracklings, prepared in its recognizable regional style.

Caribbean (Puerto Rico)Puerto Rico1 hr

Ingredients

  • 4 green plantains
  • frying oil
  • 6 garlic cloves
  • 1 cup pork cracklings
  • 1/2 cup warm chicken broth
  • salt

Preparation

  1. Peel plantains, cut into chunks, and fry at 325°F until tender but not browned.
  2. Mash garlic with salt and pork cracklings in a pilón. Add hot plantains in batches and pound, moistening with broth, until cohesive but textured.
  3. Shape into domes and serve hot.
