Spicy Cajun-Style Shrimp & Corn Chowder

Creamy corn chowder with shrimp, potatoes, Cajun spice, aromatics, and a gentle chile heat.

45 minServes 4

Ingredients

  • 1 lb shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 celery stalks, diced
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 medium potatoes, diced
  • 3 cups corn kernels
  • 4 cups seafood or chicken stock
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 2 tsp Cajun seasoning
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika
  • Hot sauce, salt, and pepper
  • Parsley or green onion

Preparation

  1. Cook onion, celery, and bell pepper in butter until soft. Add garlic and seasonings.
  2. Add potatoes, corn, and stock. Simmer until potatoes are tender, about 15 minutes.
  3. Stir in cream and bring to a gentle simmer.
  4. Add shrimp and cook 3–4 minutes until opaque.
  5. Season with hot sauce, salt, and pepper; garnish and serve.
