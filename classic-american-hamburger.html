BEEF · AMERICAN

Classic American Hamburger

A juicy American hamburger made with ground beef mixed with grated onion, Worcestershire, egg, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.

30 minMakes 4 burgers

Photo: Wikimedia Commons

Ingredients

  • 1½ lb ground beef, preferably 80/20
  • ¼ cup finely grated onion, including its juices
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 large egg
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • ½ tsp freshly ground black pepper
  • 4 hamburger buns
  • 4 slices American or cheddar cheese, optional
  • Lettuce, tomato, sliced onion, pickles, ketchup, mustard, and mayonnaise, for serving

Preparation

  1. In a large bowl, combine the ground beef, grated onion, Worcestershire sauce, egg, garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
  2. Mix gently with your hands just until evenly combined; do not overwork the meat.
  3. Divide into four equal portions and form into patties slightly wider than the buns. Press a shallow dimple into the center of each patty.
  4. Grill or pan-sear over medium-high heat until browned and cooked to your preferred doneness. Add cheese during the final minute if using.
  5. Toast the buns and assemble with your preferred toppings and condiments.
