Southern American (North Carolina)

Hatch-Style Catfish Plate

A Carolina-style fried catfish plate with cornmeal crust, hush puppies, slaw, and tartar sauce.

Southern American (North Carolina)North Carolina, United States40 min

Ingredients

  • 4 catfish fillets
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • 1 cup cornmeal
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • frying oil
  • 2 cups prepared coleslaw
  • 8 hush puppies
  • 1/2 cup tartar sauce
  • lemon wedges

Preparation

  1. Soak catfish in buttermilk 20 minutes. Mix cornmeal, flour, salt, and paprika.
  2. Heat oil to 350°F. Dredge fish in cornmeal mixture and fry until crisp and fish reaches 145°F, 4–6 minutes depending on thickness.
  3. Drain well.
  4. Plate each fillet with hush puppies and coleslaw; serve tartar sauce and lemon alongside.
