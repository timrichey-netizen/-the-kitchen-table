Tacos de Camarón al Ajillo

Garlicky shrimp sautéed with guajillo chile, olive oil, and lime for a fast, savory taco filling.

25 minMakes 10 tacos

Photo: Muy Delish

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 5 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  • 2 guajillo chiles, seeded and sliced
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 10 corn tortillas
  • Cilantro
  • Avocado
  • Salsa de aguacate

Preparation

  1. Pat shrimp dry and season with salt.
  2. Warm olive oil and gently toast garlic and guajillo until fragrant.
  3. Add shrimp and cook quickly until just opaque.
  4. Finish with lime juice.
  5. Serve in tortillas with cilantro, avocado, and salsa de aguacate.
