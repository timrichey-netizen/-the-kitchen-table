Fettuccine Alfredo

The original Roman-style Alfredo: fettuccine emulsified with butter, Parmigiano-Reggiano, and pasta water.

20 minServes 4

PASTA GUIDE

Recommended pasta: Fettuccine

See pasta shapes, sauces & pairings →

Ingredients

  • 12 oz fresh fettuccine
  • 6 tbsp unsalted butter, softened
  • 1½ cups finely grated Parmigiano-Reggiano
  • Kosher salt
  • Warm pasta water

Preparation

  1. Cook fettuccine until al dente; reserve pasta water.
  2. Place butter in a warm serving bowl and add drained pasta.
  3. Add Parmigiano gradually with splashes of hot pasta water, tossing until silky and emulsified.
  4. Serve immediately with extra Parmigiano.
