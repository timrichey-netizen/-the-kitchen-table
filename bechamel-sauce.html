SAUCE · FRENCH MOTHER SAUCE

Béchamel

The classic French white sauce: milk thickened with a white roux and gently seasoned with nutmeg.

25 minMakes about 2 cups

Photo: HENSSLERS

Ingredients

  • 3 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 2½ cups whole milk, warmed
  • ½ tsp kosher salt
  • Pinch white pepper
  • Pinch freshly grated nutmeg

Preparation

  1. Melt butter over medium-low heat.
  2. Whisk in flour and cook 2–3 minutes without browning.
  3. Gradually whisk in warm milk.
  4. Simmer gently, whisking often, until the sauce coats a spoon.
  5. Season with salt, pepper, and nutmeg.
