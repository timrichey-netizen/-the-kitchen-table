Cajeta

Traditional Mexican goat-milk caramel, slowly reduced until glossy and deeply flavored.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups goat milk
  • 1 1/4 cups sugar
  • 1/4 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1 small cinnamon stick

Method

  1. Combine goat milk, sugar, vanilla, and cinnamon; bring to a gentle simmer.
  2. Dissolve baking soda in a spoonful of water and stir in carefully.
  3. Cook low and slow, stirring often, until mahogany-colored and thick.
  4. Remove cinnamon and cool.
